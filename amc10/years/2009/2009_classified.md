  - 标签：概率;计数
  - 题面：Rachel and Robert run on a circular track. Rachel runs counterclockwise and completes a lap every 90 seconds, and Robert runs clockwise and completes a lap every 80 seconds. Both start from the start line at the same time. At some random time between 10 minutes and 11 minutes after they begin to run, a photographer standing inside the track takes a picture that shows one- fourth of the track, centered on the starting line. What is the probability that both Rachel and Robert are in the picture? (A) 1 (B) 1 (C) 3 (D) 1 (E) 5 16 8 16 4 16
