2016 AMC 10B Problem 12

2016AMC 10BProblem 12概率与统计组合概率概率计数

Problem Statement

Two different numbers are selected at random from $\{1,2,3,4,5\}$ and multiplied together. What is the probability that the product is even?

Choices

  1. A0.2
  2. B0.4
  3. C0.5
  4. D0.7
  5. E0.8

Answer

D. 0.7

Solution

Step 1: Use the complement

The product is even unless both selected numbers are odd. It is easier to count odd products first.

Step 2: Count all pairs

There are \[\binom52=10\] ways to choose two different numbers from $\{1,2,3,4,5\}$.

Step 3: Count odd-product pairs

The odd numbers are $1,3,5$, so there are \[\binom32=3\] pairs whose product is odd.

Step 4: Find even-product pairs

Therefore $10-3=7$ pairs have even product.

Step 5: Convert to probability

The probability is \[\frac7{10}=0.7.\]

Step 6: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{0.7}$.

Key Idea

Count the complement: products that are odd.
