2020AMC 10AProblem 3代数运算与化简代数化简

Problem Statement

Assuming $a\ne3$, $b\ne4$, and $c\ne5$, what is the value in simplest form of \[\frac{a-3}{5-c}\cdot\frac{b-4}{3-a}\cdot\frac{c-5}{4-b}?\]

Choices

  1. A-1
  2. B1
  3. Cabc
  4. D$1-\frac1{60abc}$
  5. E$1-\frac{abc}{60}$

Answer

A. -1

Solution

Step 1: Look for sign pairs

Each denominator is the negative of a similar numerator: \[3-a=-(a-3),\quad4-b=-(b-4),\quad5-c=-(c-5).\]

Step 2: Rewrite the denominator product

The denominator \[(5-c)(3-a)(4-b)\] equals \[-(c-5)\cdot-(a-3)\cdot-(b-4).\] There are three negative signs, so the denominator is the negative of the numerator product.

Step 3: Cancel safely

Since $a\ne3$, $b\ne4$, and $c\ne5$, none of these factors is zero, so cancellation is valid.

Step 4: Compute the expression

The expression is \[-1.\]

Step 5: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{-1}$.

Key Idea

Pair each factor with its negative counterpart.
