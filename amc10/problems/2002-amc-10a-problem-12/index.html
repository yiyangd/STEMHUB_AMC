2002 AMC 10A Problem 12

2002AMC 10AProblem 12概率与统计平均数与数据集平均数数据

Problem Statement

Mr. Earl E. Bird leaves his house for work at exactly 8:00 A.M. every morning. When he averages 40 miles per hour, he arrives at his workplace three minutes late. When he averages 60 miles per hour, he arrives three minutes early. At what average speed, in miles per hour, should Mr. Bird drive to arrive at his workplace precisely on time?

Choices

  1. A45
  2. B48
  3. C50
  4. D55
  5. E58

Answer

B. 48

Solution

Step 1: Turn minutes into hours

Three minutes is $0.05$ hours. Let the on-time travel time be $t$ hours and the distance be $d$ miles.

Step 2: Write both time equations

At $40$ mph, $d/40=t+0.05$. At $60$ mph, $d/60=t-0.05$.

Step 3: Eliminate $t$

Subtracting gives $d/40-d/60=0.10$. Since $1/40-1/60=1/120$, we get $d=12$.

Step 4: Find the needed speed

The on-time travel time is $12/40-0.05=0.25$ hours. The needed speed is $12/0.25=48$, so the answer is $\boxed{48}$.

Key Idea

把平均数、总和或中位数条件转化为代数约束。
