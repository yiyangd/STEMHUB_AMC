2007 AMC 10A Problem 23

2007AMC 10AProblem 23几何三角形三角形角度

Problem Statement

How many ordered pairs $(m,n)$ of positive integers, with $m>n$, have the property that their squares differ by $96$?

Choices

  1. A$3$
  2. B$4$
  3. C$6$
  4. D$9$
  5. E$12$

Answer

B. 4

Solution

Step 1: Factor the difference of squares

The condition is $m^2-n^2=96$, so $(m-n)(m+n)=96$.

Step 2: Use parity

The two factors $m-n$ and $m+n$ have the same parity. Since their product is even and their sum/difference must give integer $m,n$, both factors must be even.

Step 3: List factor pairs

The positive even factor pairs of $96$ with smaller factor first are $(2,48),(4,24),(6,16),(8,12)$.

Step 4: Check that each pair works

For each pair $(u,v)$, set $m-n=u$ and $m+n=v$. Then $m=\frac{u+v}{2}$ and $n=\frac{v-u}{2}$ are positive integers because $u$ and $v$ are even.

Step 5: Count

There are $4$ such pairs, hence $4$ ordered pairs $(m,n)$ with $m>n$.

Step 6: Answer

The answer is $\boxed{4}$.

Key Idea

Factor the difference of squares and count compatible factor pairs.
