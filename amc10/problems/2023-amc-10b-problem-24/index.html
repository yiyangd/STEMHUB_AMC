2023 AMC 10B Problem 24

2023AMC 10BProblem 24几何面积与相似面积比例

Problem Statement

What is the perimeter of the boundary of the region consisting of all points that can be expressed as \[(2u-3w,\ v+4w)\] with $0\le u\le1$, $0\le v\le1$, and $0\le w\le1$?

Choices

  1. A$10\sqrt3$
  2. B10
  3. C12
  4. D18
  5. E16

Answer

E. 16

Solution

Step 1: Recognize what the parameters do

The point \[(2u-3w,\ v+4w)\] is built by adding independent amounts in three directions. As $u$ varies, it contributes the segment from $(0,0)$ to $(2,0)$. As $v$ varies, it contributes the segment from $(0,0)$ to $(0,1)$. As $w$ varies, it contributes the segment from $(0,0)$ to $(-3,4)$.

Step 2: Think of the region as a vector sum

The whole region is the Minkowski sum of these three segments. In two dimensions, the boundary is a centrally symmetric polygon, often called a zonotope.

Step 3: Use the perimeter fact for a zonotope

For a zonotope generated by planar segments with vectors $v_1,v_2,\ldots,v_m$, the perimeter is \[2\sum_{i=1}^m |v_i|.\] Intuitively, each generating direction contributes one pair of parallel boundary sides.

Step 4: Compute the three lengths

The vector lengths are \[|(2,0)|=2,\quad |(0,1)|=1,\quad |(-3,4)|=5.\]

Step 5: Find the perimeter

Therefore the perimeter is \[2(2+1+5)=16.\]

Step 6: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{16}$.

Key Idea

View the region as a zonotope generated by three vectors.
