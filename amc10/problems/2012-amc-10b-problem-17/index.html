2012 AMC 10B Problem 17

2012AMC 10BProblem 17几何空间几何空间几何体积

Problem Statement

Jesse cuts a circular paper disk of radius $12$ along two radii to form two sectors, the smaller having central angle $120^\circ$. He makes two circular cones, using each sector as the lateral surface of a cone. What is the ratio of the volume of the smaller cone to that of the larger?

Choices

  1. A\frac18
  2. B\frac14
  3. C\frac{\sqrt{10}}{10}
  4. D\frac{\sqrt5}{6}
  5. E\frac{\sqrt{10}}{5}

Answer

C. \frac{\sqrt{10}}{10}

Solution

Step 1: Convert sectors to cone radii

The slant height of both cones is $12$. The $120^\circ$ sector has arc length $8\pi$, so the smaller cone base radius is $4$. The larger sector has arc length $16\pi$, so its base radius is $8$.

Step 2: Find cone heights

The smaller height is $\sqrt{12^2-4^2}=8\sqrt2$. The larger height is $\sqrt{12^2-8^2}=4\sqrt5$.

Step 3: Compare volumes

Volume is proportional to $r^2h$. The ratio is $\frac{4^2\cdot8\sqrt2}{8^2\cdot4\sqrt5}=\frac{\sqrt{10}}{10}$.

Step 4: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{\frac{\sqrt{10}}{10}}$.

Key Idea

Turn sector arc lengths into cone base radii and use slant height 12.
