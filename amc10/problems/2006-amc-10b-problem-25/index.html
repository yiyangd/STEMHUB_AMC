2006 AMC 10B Problem 25

2006AMC 10BProblem 25数论进制与数字数字进制

Problem Statement

Mr. Jones has eight children of different ages. On a family trip his oldest child, who is $9$, spots a license plate with a $4$-digit number in which each of two digits appears two times. \"Look, daddy!\" she exclaims. \"That number is evenly divisible by the age of each of us kids!\" \"That's right,\" replies Mr. Jones, \"and the last two digits just happen to be my age.\" Which of the following is not the age of one of Mr. Jones's children?

Choices

  1. A$4$
  2. B$5$
  3. C$6$
  4. D$7$
  5. E$8$

Answer

B. 5

Solution

Step 1: Translate the ages condition

The oldest child is $9$, and there are eight children of different ages. So the children's ages are eight different numbers chosen from $1,2,\ldots,9$, and the license number is divisible by all eight of them.

Step 2: Use the repeated-digit condition

The license number has four digits with exactly two digits appearing twice. We need such a number that is divisible by $9$ and by seven other ages from $1$ through $8$.

Step 3: Look for the missing age

Testing divisibility is efficient here because the possible ages are only $1$ through $9$. The number $5544$ has two $5$s and two $4$s, and it is divisible by $1,2,3,4,6,7,8,$ and $9$.

Step 4: Check the divisibility

Indeed, $5544$ is even, its last two digits $44$ are divisible by $4$, its digit sum is $18$ so it is divisible by $3$ and $9$, and $5544\div7=792$. Also $5544\div8=693$.

Step 5: Identify the age not included

The divisors from $1$ to $9$ that work are $1,2,3,4,6,7,8,9$. The only missing possible age among the answer choices is $5$.

Step 6: Answer

Therefore $\boxed{5}$ is not the age of one of Mr. Jones's children.

Key Idea

Use divisibility by ages 1 through 9 and the special repeated-digit structure of the license plate.
