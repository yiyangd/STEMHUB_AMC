2022 AMC 10B Problem 9

2022AMC 10BProblem 9数论整数构造整数构造

Problem Statement

The sum \[\frac1{2!}+\frac2{3!}+\frac3{4!}+\cdots+\frac{2021}{2022!}\] can be expressed as $a-\frac1{b!}$, where $a$ and $b$ are positive integers. What is $a+b$?

Choices

  1. A2020
  2. B2021
  3. C2022
  4. D2023
  5. E2024

Answer

D. 2023

Solution

Step 1: Look for telescoping

The numerator in each term is close to the factorial index. Rewrite \[\frac{k}{(k+1)!}=\frac{k+1-1}{(k+1)!}=\frac1{k!}-\frac1{(k+1)!}.\]

Step 2: Apply the identity to the sum

The given sum is \[\sum_{k=1}^{2021}\frac{k}{(k+1)!}=\sum_{k=1}^{2021}\left(\frac1{k!}-\frac1{(k+1)!}\right).\]

Step 3: Cancel consecutive terms

Everything cancels except the first positive term and the last negative term: \[1-\frac1{2022!}.\]

Step 4: Identify a and b

The expression has the form $a-\frac1{b!}$ with $a=1$ and $b=2022$.

Step 5: Conclude

Thus \[a+b=1+2022=2023,\] so the answer is $\boxed{2023}$.

Key Idea

Use a telescoping factorial identity.
