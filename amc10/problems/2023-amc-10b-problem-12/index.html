2023 AMC 10B Problem 12

2023AMC 10BProblem 12代数多项式与根多项式根

Problem Statement

When the roots of \[P(x)=(x-1)^1(x-2)^2(x-3)^3\cdots(x-10)^{10}\] are removed from the real number line, what remains is the union of $11$ disjoint open intervals. On how many of those intervals is $P(x)$ positive?

Choices

  1. A3
  2. B7
  3. C6
  4. D4
  5. E5

Answer

C. 6

Solution

Step 1: Start on the far right

For $x>10$, every factor $x-i$ is positive, so $P(x)>0$ on the interval $(10,\infty)$.

Step 2: Know when signs change

Crossing a root of even multiplicity does not change the sign. Crossing a root of odd multiplicity does change the sign.

Step 3: List odd-multiplicity roots

The odd multiplicities occur at roots $1,3,5,7,9$. The even multiplicities occur at $2,4,6,8,10$.

Step 4: Track positive intervals

Moving left from $(10,\infty)$, the sign stays positive across $10$, changes across $9$, stays across $8$, changes across $7$, and so on. The positive intervals are \[(10,\infty),(9,10),(6,7),(5,6),(2,3),(1,2).\]

Step 5: Count

There are $6$ such intervals.

Step 6: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{6}$.

Key Idea

Track sign changes only at roots of odd multiplicity.
