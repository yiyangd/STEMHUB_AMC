2015 AMC 10A Problem 22

2015AMC 10AProblem 22概率与统计组合概率概率计数

Problem Statement

Eight people are sitting around a circular table, each holding a fair coin. All eight people flip their coins, and those who flip heads stand while those who flip tails remain seated. What is the probability that no two adjacent people will stand?

Choices

  1. A\frac{47}{256}
  2. B\frac3{16}
  3. C\frac{49}{256}
  4. D\frac{25}{128}
  5. E\frac{51}{256}

Answer

A. \frac{47}{256}

Solution

Step 1: Model heads and tails as binary strings

Each coin flip outcome is a circular string of length $8$, where heads means a person stands. There are $2^8=256$ total outcomes.

Step 2: Count by number of standing people

With no adjacent standing people on a circle of $8$, there can be $0$, $1$, $2$, $3$, or $4$ people standing.

Step 3: Use circular spacing counts

The counts are $1$ for zero heads, $8$ for one head, $20$ for two heads, $16$ for three heads, and $2$ for four heads.

Step 4: Add favorable outcomes

The total favorable count is $1+8+20+16+2=47$.

Step 5: Compute probability

The probability is $\frac{47}{256}$.

Step 6: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{\frac{47}{256}}$.

Key Idea

Count binary circular strings of length 8 with no adjacent heads.
