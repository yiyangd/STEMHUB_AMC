Problem Statement

At the beginning of the school year, Lisa’s goal was to earn an A on at least 80% of her 50 quizzes for the year. She earned an A on 22 of the first 30 quizzes. If she is to achieve her goal, on at most how many of the remaining quizzes can she earn a grade lower than an A?

Choices

  1. A1
  2. B2
  3. C3
  4. D4
  5. E5

Answer

B. 2

Solution

Step 1: Convert the goal

Lisa wants an A on at least $80\%$ of $50$ quizzes. That means she needs at least $0.8\cdot50=40$ A grades.

Step 2: Use what she already has

She already has $22$ A grades from the first $30$ quizzes.

Step 3: Find how many more A grades she needs

She needs $40-22=18$ more A grades among the remaining $20$ quizzes.

Step 4: Count allowed non-A grades

If she needs $18$ A grades out of $20$, then at most $2$ of the remaining quizzes can be lower than an A.

Step 5: Answer

The answer is $\boxed{2}$.

Key Idea

Convert a percentage goal into the required number of A grades.
