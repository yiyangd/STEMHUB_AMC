Problem Statement

What is the value of $\frac{2a^{-1}+\frac{a^{-1}}2}{a}$ when $a=\frac12$?

Choices

  1. A1
  2. B2
  3. C5
  4. D10
  5. E20

Answer

D. 10

Solution

Step 1: Substitute the easy part first

When $a=\frac12$, its reciprocal is $a^{-1}=2$. The expression has several layers, so first replace every $a^{-1}$ by $2$.

Step 2: Simplify the numerator

The numerator is \[2a^{-1}+\frac{a^{-1}}2=2\cdot2+\frac22=4+1=5.\]

Step 3: Divide by a

The full expression is \[\frac{5}{a}=\frac{5}{1/2}=10.\]

Step 4: Check the direction

Because $a=\frac12$ is less than $1$, dividing by $a$ should make the numerator larger, not smaller. Getting $10$ from numerator $5$ is reasonable.

Step 5: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{10}$.

Key Idea

Substitute carefully after simplifying negative exponents.
