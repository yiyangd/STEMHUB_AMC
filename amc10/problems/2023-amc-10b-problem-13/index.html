Problem Statement

What is the area of the region in the coordinate plane defined by the inequality \[\left||x|-1\right|+\left||y|-1\right|\le1?\]

Choices

  1. A2
  2. B8
  3. C4
  4. D15
  5. E12

Answer

B. 8

Solution

Step 1: Use symmetry

The expression depends on $|x|$ and $|y|$, so the region is symmetric across both axes.

Step 2: Work in the first quadrant

In the first quadrant, the inequality becomes \[|x-1|+|y-1|\le1.\] This is a diamond centered at $(1,1)$.

Step 3: Find the first-quadrant area

The diamond has diagonals of length $2$ and $2$, so its area is \[\frac12\cdot2\cdot2=2.\] It lies entirely in the first quadrant.

Step 4: Multiply by symmetry

There are four congruent copies, one in each quadrant.

Step 5: Conclude

The total area is \[4\cdot2=8,\] so the answer is $\boxed{8}$.

Key Idea

Use quadrant symmetry and an absolute-value diamond.
