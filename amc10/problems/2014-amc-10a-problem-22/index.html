2014 AMC 10A Problem 22

2014AMC 10AProblem 22几何三角形三角形角度

Problem Statement

In rectangle $ABCD$, $AB=20$ and $BC=10$. Let $E$ be a point on $CD$ such that $\angle CBE=15^\circ$. What is $AE$?

Choices

  1. A\frac{20}{\sqrt3}
  2. B10\sqrt3
  3. C18
  4. D11\sqrt3
  5. E20

Answer

E. 20

Solution

Step 1: Place the rectangle

Let $A=(0,0)$, $B=(20,0)$, $C=(20,10)$, and $D=(0,10)$. Point $E$ lies on the top side $CD$.

Step 2: Use the 15-degree angle

From $B$, segment $BC$ is vertical and has length $10$. Since $\angle CBE=15^\circ$, the horizontal distance from $C$ to $E$ is $10\tan15^\circ$.

Step 3: Use the special tangent value

\[\tan15^\circ=2-\sqrt3.\] Therefore $E$ has $x$-coordinate $20-10(2-\sqrt3)=10\sqrt3$, and $y$-coordinate $10$.

Step 4: Compute AE

Now \[AE=\sqrt{(10\sqrt3)^2+10^2}=\sqrt{300+100}=20.\]

Step 5: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{20}$.

Key Idea

Use the 15-degree angle to locate E, then apply the distance formula.
