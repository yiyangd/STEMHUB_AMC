2006 AMC 10B Problem 9

2006AMC 10BProblem 9代数运算与化简代数化简

Problem Statement

Francesca uses $100$ grams of lemon juice, $100$ grams of sugar, and $400$ grams of water to make lemonade. There are $25$ calories in $100$ grams of lemon juice and $386$ calories in $100$ grams of sugar. Water contains no calories. How many calories are in $200$ grams of her lemonade?

Choices

  1. A$129$
  2. B$137$
  3. C$174$
  4. D$223$
  5. E$411$

Answer

B. 137

Solution

Step 1: Compute the total mass

The lemonade contains $100+100+400=600$ grams in all. The question asks about $200$ grams, which is one third of the total mixture.

Step 2: Compute the total calories

The lemon juice contributes $25$ calories, the sugar contributes $386$ calories, and the water contributes $0$. So the full $600$ grams has $25+386=411$ calories.

Step 3: Scale the calories

Since the ingredients are mixed evenly, a $200$-gram serving has the same fraction of the total calories as its fraction of the mass. That fraction is $\frac{200}{600}=\frac13$.

Step 4: Calculate

Therefore the calories in $200$ grams are \[\frac13\cdot411=137.\]

Step 5: Answer

The answer is $\boxed{137}$.

Key Idea

Use proportional reasoning: compute total calories first, then scale to 200 grams.
