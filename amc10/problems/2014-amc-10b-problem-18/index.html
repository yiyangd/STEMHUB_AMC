2014 AMC 10B Problem 18

2014AMC 10BProblem 18概率与统计平均数与数据集平均数数据

Problem Statement

A list of $11$ positive integers has a mean of $10$, a median of $9$, and a unique mode of $8$. What is the largest possible value of an integer in the list?

Choices

  1. A24
  2. B30
  3. C31
  4. D33
  5. E35

Answer

E. 35

Solution

Step 1: Use the total sum

The mean is $10$ for $11$ numbers, so the total sum is $110$.

Step 2: Understand what to minimize

To make the largest entry as large as possible, we want the other ten entries as small as possible while keeping median $9$ and unique mode $8$.

Step 3: Construct a minimal first ten

One minimal arrangement is \[1,1,8,8,8,9,9,10,10,11.\] Here the sixth number is $9$, and $8$ is the unique mode.

Step 4: Compute the remaining value

The sum of these ten numbers is $75$, so the eleventh number can be $110-75=35$.

Step 5: Check uniqueness

Adding $35$ does not create another value appearing three times, so $8$ remains the unique mode.

Step 6: Conclude

The largest possible value is $\boxed{35}$.

Key Idea

Minimize the first ten entries while preserving median and unique mode.
