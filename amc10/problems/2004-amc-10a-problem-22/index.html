2004 AMC 10A Problem 22

2004AMC 10AProblem 22几何圆与曲线圆曲线

Problem Statement

Square $ABCD$ has side length $2$. A semicircle with diameter $AB$ is constructed inside the square, and the tangent to the semicircle from $C$ intersects side $AD$ at $E$. What is the length of $CE$?

Choices

  1. A$\frac{2+\sqrt5}{2}$
  2. B$\sqrt5$
  3. C$\sqrt6$
  4. D$\frac52$
  5. E$5-\sqrt5$

Answer

D. \frac{5}{2}

Solution

Step 1: Set up coordinates

Place $A=(0,0)$, $B=(2,0)$, $C=(2,2)$, and $D=(0,2)$. The semicircle has center $(1,0)$ and radius $1$. Let $E=(0,e)$ on side $AD$.

Step 2: Write the tangent line

Line $CE$ passes through $(2,2)$ and $(0,e)$. In standard form it is $(2-e)x-2y+2e=0$.

Step 3: Use the tangent condition

A tangent line is exactly one radius away from the center. Thus the distance from $(1,0)$ to the line is $1$: \[\frac{|(2-e)+2e|}{\sqrt{(2-e)^2+4}}=1.\]

Step 4: Solve for E

This becomes $\frac{2+e}{\sqrt{(2-e)^2+4}}=1$. Squaring gives $(2+e)^2=(2-e)^2+4$, so $8e=4$ and $e=\frac12$.

Step 5: Find CE

Now $CE=\sqrt{(2-0)^2+(2-\frac12)^2}=\sqrt{4+\frac94}=\sqrt{\frac{25}{4}}=\frac52$. The answer is $\boxed{\frac52}$.

Key Idea

Use coordinate geometry and the distance from a circle center to a tangent line.
