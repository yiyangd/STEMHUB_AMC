2020 AMC 10A Problem 13

2020AMC 10AProblem 13概率与统计几何概率几何概率面积

Problem Statement

A frog sitting at $(1,2)$ begins random unit jumps parallel to the coordinate axes. Each direction is chosen independently at random. The sequence ends when the frog reaches a side of the square with vertices $(0,0),(0,4),(4,4),(4,0)$. What is the probability that the sequence ends on a vertical side?

Choices

  1. A$\frac12$
  2. B$\frac58$
  3. C$\frac23$
  4. D$\frac34$
  5. E$\frac78$

Answer

B. \frac58

Solution

Step 1: Turn the random walk into probabilities

Let $p(x,y)$ be the probability that the frog eventually exits through a vertical side when starting at $(x,y)$.

Step 2: Use symmetry

By symmetry about the horizontal midline, let \[a=p(1,2),\quad b=p(1,1)=p(1,3),\quad c=p(2,2),\quad d=p(2,1)=p(2,3).\] Also $p=1$ on vertical sides and $p=0$ on horizontal sides.

Step 3: Write averaging equations

At each interior point, the probability is the average of the four neighboring probabilities. Thus \[a=\frac{1+c+2b}{4},\quad b=\frac{1+d+a}{4},\quad c=\frac{a+d}{2},\quad d=\frac{2b+c}{4}.\]

Step 4: Solve the small system

Solving gives \[a=\frac58,\quad b=\frac12,\quad c=\frac12,\quad d=\frac38.\]

Step 5: Use the starting point

The frog starts at $(1,2)$, so the desired probability is $a$.

Step 6: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{\frac58}$.

Key Idea

Solve a small system of harmonic probability equations.
