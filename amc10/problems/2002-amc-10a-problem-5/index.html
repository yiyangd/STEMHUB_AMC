2002 AMC 10A Problem 5

2002AMC 10AProblem 5几何圆与曲线圆曲线

Problem Statement

Each of the small circles in the figure has radius one. The innermost circle is tangent to the six circles that surround it, and each of those circles is tangent to the large circle and to its small-circle neighbors. Find the area of the shaded region.

Choices

  1. Aπ
  2. B1.5π
  3. C2π
  4. D3π
  5. E3.5π

Answer

C. 2\pi

Solution

Step 1: Read the geometry

Each small circle has radius $1$. The six surrounding circles have centers $2$ units from the center of the innermost circle.

Step 2: Find large radius

Each surrounding circle is tangent to the large circle, so the large radius is $2+1=3$.

Step 3: Subtract areas

The large circle has area $9\pi$, and the seven small circles have total area $7\pi$. The shaded area is $9\pi-7\pi=2\pi$.

Step 4: Note

This conclusion depends on the diagram's shaded region. The answer is $\boxed{2\pi}$.

Key Idea

利用圆、切线、曲线方程或几何约束求关键量。
