2023 AMC 10A Problem 21

2023AMC 10AProblem 21数论质因数与整除质因数整除

Problem Statement

Let $P(x)$ be the unique polynomial of minimal degree with the following properties: $P(x)$ has leading coefficient $1$, $1$ is a root of $P(x)-1$, $2$ is a root of $P(x-2)$, $3$ is a root of $P(3x)$, and $4$ is a root of $4P(x)$. The roots of $P(x)$ are integers, with one exception. The non-integer root is $\frac{m}{n}$, where $m$ and $n$ are relatively prime positive integers. What is $m+n$?

Choices

  1. A41
  2. B43
  3. C45
  4. D47
  5. E49

Answer

D. 47

Solution

Step 1: Translate each condition

The condition that $1$ is a root of $P(x)-1$ means $P(1)=1$. The condition that $2$ is a root of $P(x-2)$ means $P(0)=0$. The condition that $3$ is a root of $P(3x)$ means $P(9)=0$. Finally, $4$ is a root of $4P(x)$ means $P(4)=0$.

Step 2: Use the integer roots

Thus $0$, $4$, and $9$ are roots of $P(x)$. The polynomial is monic and has exactly one non-integer root, so the minimal-degree form is \[P(x)=x(x-4)(x-9)(x-r),\] where $r$ is the non-integer root.

Step 3: Use the value at x equals 1

Now substitute $x=1$: \[P(1)=1(1-4)(1-9)(1-r)=24(1-r).\] The condition says this equals $1$.

Step 4: Solve for the exceptional root

So \[24(1-r)=1,\] hence \[1-r=\frac1{24}\] and \[r=\frac{23}{24}.\]

Step 5: Compute the requested sum

The non-integer root is $\frac{m}{n}=\frac{23}{24}$, so \[m+n=23+24=47.\]

Step 6: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{47}$.

Key Idea

Translate the root conditions into required polynomial values.
