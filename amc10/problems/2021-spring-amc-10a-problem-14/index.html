2021 SpringAMC 10AProblem 14数论整数构造整数构造

Problem Statement

All the roots of polynomial \[z^6-10z^5+Az^4+Bz^3+Cz^2+Dz+16\] are positive integers, possibly repeated. What is the value of $B$?

Choices

  1. A-88
  2. B-80
  3. C-64
  4. D-41
  5. E-40

Answer

A. -88

Solution

Step 1: Use Vieta's formulas

The polynomial is monic with positive integer roots. The coefficient of $z^5$ is $-10$, so the six roots have sum $10$. The constant term is $16$, so their product is $16$.

Step 2: Find the roots

Since $16=2^4$, the roots are positive integers built from four factors of $2$ and some $1$s. To have six roots with sum $10$, the only possibility is \[2,2,2,2,1,1.\]

Step 3: Connect B to triples of roots

For a monic degree-$6$ polynomial, the coefficient of $z^3$ is $-e_3$, where $e_3$ is the sum of all products of triples of roots.

Step 4: Compute e3

The triple products are: three $2$s gives $\binom43\cdot8=32$; two $2$s and one $1$ gives $\binom42\binom21\cdot4=48$; one $2$ and two $1$s gives $\binom41\binom22\cdot2=8$. Thus \[e_3=32+48+8=88.\]

Step 5: Conclude

Therefore $B=-88$, so the answer is $\boxed{-88}$.

Key Idea

Use Vieta's formulas and factor the integer constant.
