2008 AMC 10B Problem 10

2008AMC 10BProblem 10几何圆与曲线圆曲线

Problem Statement

Points $A$ and $B$ are on a circle of radius $5$ and $AB=6$. Point $C$ is the midpoint of the minor arc $AB$. What is the length of the line segment $AC$?

Choices

  1. A$\sqrt{10}$
  2. B$\sqrt{7}$
  3. C$\sqrt{14}$
  4. D$\sqrt{15}$
  5. E$4\sqrt{2}$

Answer

A. \sqrt{10}

Solution

Step 1: Relate the chord to a central angle

Let $O$ be the center of the circle. Since $AB=6$ in a circle of radius $5$, half the chord has length $3$. If $\angle AOC=\theta$, then the midpoint $C$ of the minor arc means $\angle AOB=2\theta$.

Step 2: Use the right triangle from the chord

Dropping a perpendicular from $O$ to chord $AB$ gives a right triangle with hypotenuse $5$ and half-chord $3$. Thus $\sin\theta=\frac35$ and $\cos\theta=\frac45$.

Step 3: Find the chord AC

The segment $AC$ is a chord subtending central angle $\theta$, so $AC=2\cdot5\sin(\theta/2)$.

Step 4: Use the half-angle formula

Since $\cos\theta=\frac45$, we get $\sin(\theta/2)=\sqrt{\frac{1-\cos\theta}{2}}=\sqrt{\frac{1/5}{2}}=\frac1{\sqrt{10}}$.

Step 5: Finish

Therefore $AC=10\cdot\frac1{\sqrt{10}}=\sqrt{10}$.

Step 6: Answer

The length of $AC$ is $\boxed{\sqrt{10}}$.

Key Idea

Use chord length and half-angle geometry in the circle.
