Problem Statement

The sum of three numbers is 20. The first is 4 times the sum of the other two. The second is seven times the third. What is the product of all three?

Choices

  1. A28
  2. B40
  3. C100
  4. D400
  5. E800

Answer

A. 28

Solution

Step 1: Set variables

Let the third number be $z$. Then the second is $7z$.

Step 2: Use first condition

The first is $4$ times the sum of the other two, so it is $4(7z+z)=32z$.

Step 3: Use total

The sum is $32z+7z+z=40z=20$, so $z=1/2$.

Step 4: Product

The product is $(32z)(7z)(z)=224z^3=224/8=28$. The answer is $\\boxed{28}$.

Key Idea

通过代数化简、配对或基本运算降低计算量。
