2016 AMC 10B Problem 7

2016AMC 10BProblem 7几何三角形三角形角度

Problem Statement

The ratio of the measures of two acute angles is $5:4$, and the complement of one of these two angles is twice as large as the complement of the other. What is the sum of the degree measures of the two angles?

Choices

  1. A75
  2. B90
  3. C135
  4. D150
  5. E270

Answer

C. 135

Solution

Step 1: Represent the two angles

Let the acute angles be $5x$ and $4x$. Their sum will be $9x$, so our goal is to find $x$.

Step 2: Compare the complements

The smaller angle $4x$ has the larger complement, $90-4x$. The larger angle $5x$ has complement $90-5x$.

Step 3: Use the twice-as-large condition

The larger complement is twice the smaller complement, so \[90-4x=2(90-5x).\]

Step 4: Solve

This gives $90-4x=180-10x$, so $6x=90$ and $x=15$.

Step 5: Find the requested sum

The two angles sum to $9x=9\cdot15=135$ degrees.

Step 6: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{135}$.

Key Idea

Turn the angle ratio and complement condition into an equation.
