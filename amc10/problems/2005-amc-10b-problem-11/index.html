2005AMC 10BProblem 11几何空间几何空间几何体积

Problem Statement

The first term of a sequence is $2005$. Each succeeding term is the sum of the cubes of the digits of the previous term. What is the $2005$th term of the sequence?

Choices

  1. A$29$
  2. B$55$
  3. C$85$
  4. D$133$
  5. E$250$

Answer

E. 250

Solution

Step 1: Compute the first few terms

Start with $2005$. The next term is $2^3+0^3+0^3+5^3=8+125=133$.

Step 2: Look for a cycle

From $133$, the next term is $1^3+3^3+3^3=55$. From $55$, the next term is $5^3+5^3=250$. From $250$, the next term is $2^3+5^3+0^3=133$.

Step 3: Identify the repeating block

After the first term, the sequence repeats $133,55,250$ with period $3$.

Step 4: Use the index

The $2005$th term is $2004$ steps after the first term. Since $2004$ is divisible by $3$, the sequence lands on the third value of the repeating block, $250$.

Step 5: Answer

The $2005$th term is $\boxed{250}$.

Key Idea

Compute early terms until the digit-cube process enters a cycle.
