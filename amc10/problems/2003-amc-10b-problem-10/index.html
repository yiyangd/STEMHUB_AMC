2003 AMC 10B Problem 10

2003AMC 10BProblem 10组合数学递推与序列计数递推序列

Problem Statement

Old license plates consisted of one letter followed by four digits. New license plates consist of three letters followed by three digits. By how many times is the number of possible license plates increased?

Choices

  1. A$26$
  2. B$\\frac{26^2}{10}$
  3. C$\\frac{26^2}{10^2}$
  4. D$\\frac{26^3}{10}$
  5. E$\\frac{26^3}{10^2}$

Answer

B. \frac{26^2}{10}

Solution

Step 1: Count old plates

An old plate has $26\cdot10^4$ possibilities.

Step 2: Count new plates

A new plate has $26^3\cdot10^3$ possibilities.

Step 3: Take ratio

The increase factor is $\\frac{26^3\cdot10^3}{26\cdot10^4}=\\frac{26^2}{10}$.

Step 4: Answer

The answer is $\\boxed{\\frac{26^2}{10}}$.

Key Idea

建立状态递推或寻找周期规律计数。
