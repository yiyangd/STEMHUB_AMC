Problem Statement

Portia's high school has $3$ times as many students as Lara's high school. The two high schools have a total of $2600$ students. How many students does Portia's high school have?

Choices

  1. A600
  2. B650
  3. C1950
  4. D2000
  5. E2050

Answer

C. 1950

Solution

Step 1: Set up the smaller school first

Let Lara's high school have $x$ students. Portia's school has $3x$ students.

Step 2: Use the total

Together they have $x+3x=4x$ students, and this total is $2600$.

Step 3: Solve for x

So \[4x=2600,\] giving \[x=650.\]

Step 4: Find Portia's school size

Portia has \[3x=3\cdot650=1950\] students.

Step 5: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{1950}$.

Key Idea

Use a simple ratio model for the two schools.
