2017 AMC 10B Problem 15

2017AMC 10BProblem 15几何面积与相似面积比例

Problem Statement

Rectangle $ABCD$ has $AB=3$ and $BC=4$. Point $E$ is the foot of the perpendicular from $B$ to diagonal $AC$. What is the area of $\triangle ADE$?

Choices

  1. A1
  2. B$\frac{42}{25}$
  3. C$\frac{28}{15}$
  4. D2
  5. E$\frac{54}{25}$

Answer

E. \frac{54}{25}

Solution

Step 1: Set coordinates

Let $A=(0,0)$, $B=(3,0)$, $C=(3,4)$, and $D=(0,4)$. The diagonal $AC$ lies on the line through $(0,0)$ and $(3,4)$.

Step 2: Find the foot E

The foot from $B$ to $AC$ is the projection of $(3,0)$ onto the line in direction $(3,4)$. This gives \[E=\frac{B\cdot(3,4)}{(3,4)\cdot(3,4)}(3,4)=\frac9{25}(3,4)=\left(\frac{27}{25},\frac{36}{25}\right).\]

Step 3: Use AD as a base

Segment $AD$ is vertical with length $4$. The horizontal distance from $E$ to line $AD$ is the $x$-coordinate of $E$, which is $\frac{27}{25}$.

Step 4: Compute area

\[[ADE]=\frac12\cdot4\cdot\frac{27}{25}=\frac{54}{25}.\]

Step 5: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{\frac{54}{25}}$.

Key Idea

Use coordinates and a projection onto the diagonal.
