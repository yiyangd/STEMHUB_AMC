2016 AMC 10B Problem 6

2016AMC 10BProblem 6数论进制与数字数字进制

Problem Statement

Laura added two three-digit positive integers. All six digits in these numbers are different. Laura's sum is a three-digit number $S$. What is the smallest possible value for the sum of the digits of $S$?

Choices

  1. A1
  2. B4
  3. C5
  4. D15
  5. E21

Answer

B. 4

Solution

Step 1: Find a lower bound for S

To make the sum of two three-digit numbers as small as possible with six distinct digits, use $1$ and $2$ in the hundreds places and use $0,3,4,5$ in the remaining places. The smallest possible sum is at least \[100+200+10(0+3)+(4+5)=339.\]

Step 2: Rule out digit sums below 4

A three-digit number with digit sum less than $4$ is at most $300$, because the largest such number is $300$. But $S$ must be at least $339$.

Step 3: Show digit sum 4 is possible

We need an example, not just a lower bound. The sum \[157+243=400\] uses six different digits in the two addends, and $400$ has digit sum $4$.

Step 4: Conclude minimality

No digit sum below $4$ is possible, and digit sum $4$ is possible.

Step 5: Final answer

The smallest possible digit sum is $\boxed{4}$.

Key Idea

Minimize a three-digit sum using place value and distinct digits.
