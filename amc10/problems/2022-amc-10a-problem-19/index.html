2022 AMC 10A Problem 19

2022AMC 10AProblem 19数论整除与同余同余余数

Problem Statement

Define $L_n$ as the least common multiple of all the integers from $1$ to $n$ inclusive. There is a unique integer $h$ such that \[\frac11+\frac12+\frac13+\cdots+\frac1{17}=\frac{h}{L_{17}}.\] What is the remainder when $h$ is divided by $17$?

Choices

  1. A1
  2. B3
  3. C5
  4. D7
  5. E9

Answer

C. 5

Solution

Step 1: Focus on the numerator modulo 17

When the fractions are put over the common denominator $L_{17}$, the numerator is \[h=\sum_{k=1}^{17}\frac{L_{17}}{k}.\] We only need this modulo $17$.

Step 2: Notice which terms matter

For $k=1,2,\ldots,16$, the number $\frac{L_{17}}{k}$ is still divisible by $17$, because $k$ does not remove the factor $17$. So all those terms are $0\pmod{17}$.

Step 3: Reduce to one term

Only the term for $k=17$ may be nonzero modulo $17$. Therefore \[h\equiv \frac{L_{17}}{17}\pmod{17}.\]

Step 4: Write the needed lcm

After removing the factor $17$, the remaining lcm is \[L_{16}=2^4\cdot3^2\cdot5\cdot7\cdot11\cdot13.\]

Step 5: Compute modulo 17

Modulo $17$, this is \[16\cdot9\cdot5\cdot7\cdot11\cdot13\equiv (-1)\cdot9\cdot5\cdot7\cdot11\cdot13.\] Since $5\cdot7=35\equiv1$, this becomes \[-9\cdot11\cdot13\equiv -99\cdot13\equiv 3\cdot13=39\equiv5.\]

Step 6: Conclude

The remainder is $\boxed{5}$.

Key Idea

Use modular arithmetic with the common denominator $L_{17}$.
