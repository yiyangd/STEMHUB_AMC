2008 AMC 10B Problem 14

2008AMC 10BProblem 14几何坐标几何坐标几何

Problem Statement

Triangle $OAB$ has $O=(0,0)$, $B=(5,0)$, and $A$ in the first quadrant. In addition, $\angle ABO=90^\circ$ and $\angle AOB=30^\circ$. Suppose that $\overline{OA}$ is rotated $90^\circ$ counterclockwise about $O$. What are the coordinates of the image of $A$?

Choices

  1. A$(-\frac{10\sqrt3}{3},5)$
  2. B$(-\frac{5\sqrt3}{3},5)$
  3. C$(\sqrt3,5)$
  4. D$(\frac{5\sqrt3}{3},5)$
  5. E$(\frac{10\sqrt3}{3},5)$

Answer

B. (-\frac{5\sqrt3}{3},5)

Solution

Step 1: Locate point A

Because $\angle ABO=90^\circ$ and $B=(5,0)$, segment $AB$ is vertical, so $A$ has $x$-coordinate $5$.

Step 2: Use the 30-degree angle

The line $OA$ makes a $30^\circ$ angle with the positive $x$-axis. Therefore $\tan30^\circ=\frac{y}{5}$, so $y=5\tan30^\circ=\frac{5\sqrt3}{3}$.

Step 3: Apply the rotation rule

A $90^\circ$ counterclockwise rotation about the origin sends $(x,y)$ to $(-y,x)$.

Step 4: Substitute A's coordinates

Thus $A=(5,\frac{5\sqrt3}{3})$ maps to $(-\frac{5\sqrt3}{3},5)$.

Step 5: Answer

The image is $\boxed{(-\frac{5\sqrt3}{3},5)}$.

Key Idea

Find A by trigonometry, then apply the 90-degree coordinate rotation rule.
