2012 AMC 10A Problem 17

2012AMC 10AProblem 17数论质因数与整除质因数整除

Problem Statement

Let $a$ and $b$ be relatively prime integers with $a>b>0$ and \[\frac{a^3-b^3}{(a-b)^3}=\frac{73}{3}.\] What is $a-b$?

Choices

  1. A1
  2. B2
  3. C3
  4. D4
  5. E5

Answer

C. 3

Solution

Step 1: Factor the numerator

Use $a^3-b^3=(a-b)(a^2+ab+b^2)$. Then \[\frac{a^3-b^3}{(a-b)^3}=\frac{a^2+ab+b^2}{(a-b)^2}=\frac{73}{3}.\]

Step 2: Write a in terms of the gap

Let $d=a-b$. Then $a=b+d$. Substituting into the numerator gives $(b+d)^2+b(b+d)+b^2=3b^2+3bd+d^2$.

Step 3: Use the ratio t=b/d

Divide by $d^2$ and let $t=\frac bd$. We get $3t^2+3t+1=\frac{73}{3}$. Multiplying by $3$ gives $9t^2+9t-70=0$.

Step 4: Solve for t

The positive solution is $t=\frac73$, so $\frac bd=\frac73$. Thus $b=7k$ and $d=3k$.

Step 5: Use relative primality

Then $a=b+d=10k$. Since $a$ and $b$ are relatively prime, $k=1$. Therefore $a-b=d=3$, and the answer is $\boxed{3}$.

Key Idea

Factor the difference of cubes and solve for the ratio $b/(a-b)$.
