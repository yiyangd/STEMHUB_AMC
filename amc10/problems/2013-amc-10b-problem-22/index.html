2013 AMC 10B Problem 22

2013AMC 10BProblem 22组合数学排列组合排列组合计数

Problem Statement

A regular octagon $ABCDEFGH$ has center $J$. Each of the vertices and the center is to be associated with one of the digits $1$ through $9$, with each digit used once, in such a way that the sums of the numbers on the lines $AJE$, $BJF$, $CJG$, and $DJH$ are equal. In how many ways can this be done?

Choices

  1. A384
  2. B576
  3. C1152
  4. D1680
  5. E3546

Answer

C. 1152

Solution

Step 1: Focus on opposite pairs

The equal line sums are $A+J+E$, $B+J+F$, $C+J+G$, and $D+J+H$. Since the same center digit $J$ appears in every sum, the four opposite vertex pairs must all have the same sum.

Step 2: Choose the center digit

The digits $1$ through $9$ have total sum $45$. If the center digit is $c$ and each opposite pair has sum $s$, then $4s=45-c$. Thus $45-c$ must be divisible by $4$, so $c$ can be $1$, $5$, or $9$.

Step 3: Verify the pairings

For $c=1$, the remaining digits pair to sum $11$: $(2,9),(3,8),(4,7),(5,6)$. For $c=5$, they pair to sum $10$: $(1,9),(2,8),(3,7),(4,6)$. For $c=9$, they pair to sum $9$: $(1,8),(2,7),(3,6),(4,5)$.

Step 4: Count arrangements for each center

Once the four pairs are determined, assign them to the four opposite lines in $4!$ ways. Then each pair can be reversed between its two endpoints, giving $2^4$ choices.

Step 5: Multiply

For each of the $3$ possible center digits, there are $4!\cdot2^4=384$ arrangements. The total is $3\cdot384=1152$.

Step 6: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{1152}$.

Key Idea

Use the common line sum to force opposite vertex pairs to have equal sums.
