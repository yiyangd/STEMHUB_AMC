2002 AMC 10B Problem 25

2002AMC 10BProblem 25概率与统计平均数与数据集平均数数据

Problem Statement

When $15$ is appended to a list of integers, the mean is increased by $2$. When $1$ is appended to the enlarged list, the mean of the enlarged list is decreased by $1$. How many integers were in the original list?

Choices

  1. A$4$
  2. B$5$
  3. C$6$
  4. D$7$
  5. E$8$

Answer

A. 4

Solution

Step 1: Set variables

Let the original list have $n$ integers, sum $S$, and mean $m=S/n$.

Step 2: Use adding 15

After appending $15$, the mean is $m+2$, so $S+15=(n+1)(m+2)$. This simplifies to $15=m+2n+2$, or $m=13-2n$.

Step 3: Use adding 1

After appending $1$ to the enlarged list, the new mean is one less than $m+2$, so it is $m+1$. Thus $S+16=(n+2)(m+1)$, which gives $2m+n=14$.

Step 4: Solve

Substitute $m=13-2n$ into $2m+n=14$: $2(13-2n)+n=14$, so $n=4$. The answer is $\\boxed{4}$.

Key Idea

把平均数、总和或中位数条件转化为代数约束。
