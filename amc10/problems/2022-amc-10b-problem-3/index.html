2022 AMC 10B Problem 3

2022AMC 10BProblem 3数论进制与数字数字进制

Problem Statement

How many three-digit positive integers have an odd number of even digits?

Choices

  1. A150
  2. B250
  3. C350
  4. D450
  5. E550

Answer

D. 450

Solution

Step 1: Understand the phrase odd number of even digits

A three-digit number has three digits. An odd number of even digits means either exactly $1$ even digit or exactly $3$ even digits.

Step 2: Count digit choices

For the hundreds digit, there are $4$ even choices $(2,4,6,8)$ and $5$ odd choices. For each of the tens and ones digits, there are $5$ even choices and $5$ odd choices.

Step 3: Count exactly one even digit

If the hundreds digit is the only even digit, there are $4\cdot5\cdot5=100$ numbers. If the even digit is in the tens or ones place, there are $2\cdot5\cdot5\cdot5=250$ numbers.

Step 4: Count exactly three even digits

All three digits even gives \[4\cdot5\cdot5=100\] numbers.

Step 5: Add the cases

The total is \[100+250+100=450.\]

Step 6: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{450}$.

Key Idea

Separate cases with one or three even digits.
