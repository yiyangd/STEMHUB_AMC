2019 AMC 10B Problem 17

2019AMC 10BProblem 17概率与统计独立性独立事件概率

Problem Statement

A red ball and a green ball are randomly and independently tossed into bins numbered with positive integers so that for each ball, the probability that it is tossed into bin $k$ is $2^{-k}$ for $k=1,2,3,\ldots$. What is the probability that the red ball is tossed into a higher-numbered bin than the green ball?

Choices

  1. A$\frac14$
  2. B$\frac27$
  3. C$\frac13$
  4. D$\frac38$
  5. E$\frac37$

Answer

C. \frac13

Solution

Step 1: Use symmetry

Because the red and green balls have the same independent distribution, the probability that red is higher equals the probability that green is higher.

Step 2: Subtract the tie probability

Let $P$ be the probability that the two balls land in the same bin. Then \[P(\text{red higher})=\frac{1-P}{2}.\]

Step 3: Compute the tie probability

The probability both balls land in bin $k$ is \[(2^{-k})^2=4^{-k}.\] Thus \[P=\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}4^{-k}=\frac{1/4}{1-1/4}=\frac13.\]

Step 4: Finish

So \[P(\text{red higher})=\frac{1-\frac13}{2}=\frac13.\]

Step 5: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{\frac13}$.

Key Idea

Use symmetry and subtract the probability of a tie.
