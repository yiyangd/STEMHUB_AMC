2017 AMC 10A Problem 24

2017AMC 10AProblem 24代数多项式与根多项式根

Problem Statement

For certain real numbers $a$, $b$, and $c$, the polynomial $g(x)=x^3+ax^2+x+10$ has three distinct roots, and each root of $g(x)$ is also a root of the polynomial $f(x)=x^4+x^3+bx^2+100x+c$. What is $f(1)$?

Choices

  1. A-9009
  2. B-8008
  3. C-7007
  4. D-6006
  5. E-5005

Answer

C. -7007

Solution

Step 1: Use divisibility of polynomials

The three distinct roots of $g(x)$ are also roots of $f(x)$. Since $g$ is monic cubic and $f$ is monic quartic, we can write \[f(x)=(x+k)g(x)\] for some real number $k$.

Step 2: Expand

\[(x+k)(x^3+ax^2+x+10)=x^4+(a+k)x^3+(1+ak)x^2+(10+k)x+10k.\]

Step 3: Compare coefficients

Matching with $f(x)=x^4+x^3+bx^2+100x+c$, the $x$ coefficient gives $10+k=100$, so $k=90$. Then the $x^3$ coefficient gives $a+k=1$, so $a=-89$.

Step 4: Find b and c

The $x^2$ coefficient is \[b=1+ak=1+(-89)(90)=-8009.\] Also $c=10k=900$.

Step 5: Evaluate f of 1

\[f(1)=1+1+b+100+c=2-8009+100+900=-7007.\]

Step 6: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{-7007}$.

Key Idea

Use polynomial divisibility and compare coefficients.
