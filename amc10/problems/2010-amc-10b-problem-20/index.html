2010 AMC 10B Problem 20

2010AMC 10BProblem 20几何圆与曲线圆曲线

Problem Statement

Two circles lie outside regular hexagon $ABCDEF$. The first is tangent to $\overline{AB}$, and the second is tangent to $\overline{DE}$. Both are tangent to lines $BC$ and $FA$. What is the ratio of the area of the second circle to that of the first circle?

Choices

    Answer

    D. 81

    Solution

    Step 1: Use a convenient scale

    The ratio of areas will not depend on the size of the regular hexagon, so let the side length be $1$. The lines $BC$ and $FA$ meet at a $60^\circ$ angle outside the hexagon.

    Step 2: Find the small circle radius

    The first circle is tangent to lines $BC$ and $FA$ and also to side $AB$. In the $60^\circ$ angle, its center lies on the angle bisector. The distance from the angle vertex to line $AB$ is the altitude of an equilateral triangle of side $1$, namely $\frac{\sqrt3}{2}$. For a circle inside this small triangular region, that distance equals $3r_1$, so $r_1=\frac{\sqrt3}{6}$.

    Step 3: Find the large circle radius

    The second circle is tangent to the same two lines but to the opposite side $DE$. The distance from the angle vertex down to line $DE$ is three times as large, $\frac{3\sqrt3}{2}$. Since this circle lies on the far side of $DE$, the distance to its center is $2r_2$, and the last radius reaches back to $DE$, so $2r_2=\frac{3\sqrt3}{2}+r_2$. Thus $r_2=\frac{3\sqrt3}{2}$.

    Step 4: Compare radii

    The ratio of the radii is \[\frac{r_2}{r_1}=\frac{\frac{3\sqrt3}{2}}{\frac{\sqrt3}{6}}=9.\] Since circle areas scale with the square of the radius, the area ratio is $9^2=81$.

    Step 5: Conclude

    Therefore the ratio of the area of the second circle to that of the first circle is $\boxed{81}$.

    Key Idea

    View the tangent circles as incircles in the same $60^\circ$ angle and compare their radii.
