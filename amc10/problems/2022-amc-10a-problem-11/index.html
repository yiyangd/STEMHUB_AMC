Problem Statement

Ted mistakenly wrote $2^m\cdot\sqrt{\frac{1}{4096}}$ as $2\cdot\sqrt[m]{\frac{1}{4096}}$. What is the sum of all real numbers $m$ for which these two expressions have the same value?

Choices

  1. A5
  2. B6
  3. C7
  4. D8
  5. E9

Answer

C. 7

Solution

Step 1: Turn the strange expressions into powers

The main obstacle is notation, not computation. Since $4096=2^{12}$, both expressions can be written as powers of $2$.

Step 2: Rewrite the left side

The square root contributes an exponent of $\frac12$: \[\sqrt{\frac1{4096}}=(2^{-12})^{1/2}=2^{-6}.\] Thus the left expression is $2^m\cdot2^{-6}=2^{m-6}$.

Step 3: Rewrite the right side

The $m$th root means an exponent of $\frac1m$, so \[2\cdot\sqrt[m]{\frac1{4096}}=2\cdot(2^{-12})^{1/m}=2^{1-12/m}.\]

Step 4: Compare exponents

Because the bases are the same and positive, the exponents must be equal: \[m-6=1-\frac{12}{m}.\] Multiplying by $m$ gives \[m^2-7m+12=0.\]

Step 5: Use the requested sum

The roots are $m=3$ and $m=4$, so the sum of all possible values is $3+4=7$.

Step 6: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{7}$.

Key Idea

Convert radicals and roots to powers of 2, then compare exponents.
