2004 AMC 10A Problem 20

2004AMC 10AProblem 20几何面积与相似面积比例

Problem Statement

Points $E$ and $F$ are located on square $ABCD$ so that $\triangle BEF$ is equilateral. What is the ratio of the area of $\triangle DEF$ to that of $\triangle ABE$?

Choices

  1. A$\frac43$
  2. B$\frac32$
  3. C$\sqrt3$
  4. D$2$
  5. E$1+\sqrt3$

Answer

D. 2

Solution

Step 1: Use a convenient scale

Let the side length of the square be $1$. From the diagram, $E$ lies on $AD$ and $F$ lies on $DC$, with $\triangle BEF$ equilateral.

Step 2: Set a helpful variable

Let $DE=DF=x$. Then $AE=1-x$. Triangle $DEF$ is a right isosceles triangle, so $EF^2=x^2+x^2=2x^2$.

Step 3: Use the equilateral condition

Because $\triangle BEF$ is equilateral, $BE=EF$. Also triangle $ABE$ is right, so $BE^2=AB^2+AE^2=1+(1-x)^2$.

Step 4: Relate x to the square

Set $BE^2=EF^2$: \[1+(1-x)^2=2x^2.\] This simplifies to $x^2=2(1-x)$.

Step 5: Compare areas

Now $[DEF]=\frac{x^2}{2}$ and $[ABE]=\frac{1-x}{2}$. Their ratio is \[\frac{[DEF]}{[ABE]}=\frac{x^2}{1-x}=2.\] The answer is $\boxed{2}$.

Key Idea

Relate the square and equilateral triangle with right triangles and equal side lengths.
