2022 AMC 10B Problem 11

2022AMC 10BProblem 11代数对数与指数对数指数

Problem Statement

All the high schools in a large school district are involved in a fundraiser selling T-shirts. Which choice is logically equivalent to the statement, \"No school bigger than Euclid HS sold more T-shirts than Euclid HS\"?

Choices

  1. AAll schools smaller than Euclid HS sold fewer T-shirts than Euclid HS.
  2. BNo school that sold more T-shirts than Euclid HS is bigger than Euclid HS.
  3. CAll schools bigger than Euclid HS sold fewer T-shirts than Euclid HS.
  4. DAll schools that sold fewer T-shirts than Euclid HS are smaller than Euclid HS.
  5. EAll schools smaller than Euclid HS sold more T-shirts than Euclid HS.

Answer

B. No school that sold more T-shirts than Euclid HS is bigger than Euclid HS.

Solution

Step 1: Name the original statement

The statement says that there is no school satisfying both conditions: bigger than Euclid HS and sold more T-shirts than Euclid HS.

Step 2: Write the logical form

In logical language, it says, \"There does not exist a school that is both bigger than Euclid HS and sold more T-shirts than Euclid HS.\"

Step 3: Move one condition into the subject

An equivalent way to say this is: among the schools that sold more T-shirts than Euclid HS, none of them is bigger than Euclid HS.

Step 4: Compare with the choices

This matches choice B exactly: \"No school that sold more T-shirts than Euclid HS is bigger than Euclid HS.\"

Step 5: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{\text{B}}$.

Key Idea

Rewrite a universal negative statement using its contrapositive form.
