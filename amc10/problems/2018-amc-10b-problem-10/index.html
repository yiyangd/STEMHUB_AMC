2018 AMC 10B Problem 10

2018AMC 10BProblem 10数论进制与数字数字进制

Problem Statement

In a rectangular parallelepiped, $AB=3$, $BC=1$, and $CG=2$. Point $M$ is the midpoint of $FG$. What is the volume of the rectangular pyramid with base $BCHE$ and apex $M$?

Choices

  1. A$\frac12$
  2. B1
  3. C$\frac32$
  4. D$\sqrt2$
  5. E2

Answer

E. 2

Solution

Step 1: Set coordinates for the box

Use coordinates $A=(0,0,0)$, $B=(3,0,0)$, $C=(3,1,0)$, $E=(0,0,2)$, $F=(3,0,2)$, $G=(3,1,2)$, and $H=(0,1,2)$.

Step 2: Locate M

Since $M$ is the midpoint of $FG$, \[M=\left(3,\frac12,2\right).\]

Step 3: Find the base area

The base $BCHE$ is a rectangle with side vectors $\overrightarrow{BC}=(0,1,0)$ and $\overrightarrow{BE}=(-3,0,2)$. Its area is \[\left|\overrightarrow{BC}\times\overrightarrow{BE}\right|=\sqrt{13}.\]

Step 4: Find the height

The distance from $M$ to the plane $BCHE$ is \[\frac{6}{\sqrt{13}}.\]

Step 5: Compute pyramid volume

The volume is \[\frac13\cdot\sqrt{13}\cdot\frac6{\sqrt{13}}=2.\]

Step 6: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{2}$.

Key Idea

Use coordinates for the slanted rectangular base and height.
