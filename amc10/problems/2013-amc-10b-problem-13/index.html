Problem Statement

Jo and Blair take turns counting from $1$ to one more than the last number said by the other person. Jo starts by saying $1$, Blair follows by saying $1,2$, Jo then says $1,2,3$, and so on. What is the $53$rd number said?

Choices

  1. A2
  2. B3
  3. C5
  4. D6
  5. E8

Answer

E. 8

Solution

Step 1: Group by turns

The first turn contributes $1$ number, the second contributes $2$, the third contributes $3$, and so on.

Step 2: Use triangular numbers

After $9$ turns, the total number said is $1+2+\cdots+9=45$. After $10$ turns, the total is $55$.

Step 3: Locate the 53rd number

The $53$rd number is in the $10$th turn. It is the $53-45=8$th number said during that turn.

Step 4: Read the number

During the $10$th turn, the speaker says $1,2,\ldots,10$. The $8$th number is $8$, so the answer is $\boxed{8}$.

Key Idea

Use triangular numbers to locate the 53rd spoken number.
