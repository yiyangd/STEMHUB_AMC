2011 AMC 10B Problem 13

2011AMC 10BProblem 13概率与统计独立性独立事件概率

Problem Statement

Two real numbers are selected independently at random from the interval $[-20,10]$. What is the probability that the product of those numbers is greater than zero?

Choices

  1. A\frac19
  2. B\frac13
  3. C\frac49
  4. D\frac59
  5. E\frac23

Answer

D. \frac{5}{9}

Solution

Step 1: Identify when the product is positive

A product of two real numbers is positive when both numbers are positive or both numbers are negative. The probability of selecting exactly zero is $0$, so it does not affect the answer.

Step 2: Find the interval lengths

The full interval $[-20,10]$ has length $30$. The negative part has length $20$, and the positive part has length $10$.

Step 3: Compute same-sign probability

The probability both numbers are negative is $\left(\frac{20}{30}\right)^2=\frac49$. The probability both are positive is $\left(\frac{10}{30}\right)^2=\frac19$.

Step 4: Add the cases

The total probability is $\frac49+\frac19=\frac59$. The answer is $\boxed{\frac59}$.

Key Idea

The product is positive when both selected numbers have the same sign.
