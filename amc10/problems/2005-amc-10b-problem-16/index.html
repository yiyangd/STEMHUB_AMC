2005 AMC 10B Problem 16

2005AMC 10BProblem 16代数多项式与根多项式根

Problem Statement

The quadratic equation $x^2+mx+n=0$ has roots that are twice those of $x^2+px+m=0$, and none of $m$, $n$, and $p$ is zero. What is the value of $\frac np$?

Choices

  1. A$1$
  2. B$2$
  3. C$4$
  4. D$8$
  5. E$16$

Answer

D. 8

Solution

Step 1: Name the roots

Let the roots of $x^2+px+m=0$ be $r$ and $s$. Then the roots of $x^2+mx+n=0$ are $2r$ and $2s$.

Step 2: Use Vieta on the second equation

For $x^2+px+m=0$, we have $r+s=-p$ and $rs=m$.

Step 3: Use Vieta on the first equation

For $x^2+mx+n=0$, the sum of roots is $-m$. But the roots are $2r$ and $2s$, so $2r+2s=2(r+s)=-2p=-m$. Hence $m=2p$.

Step 4: Use the product

The product of the first equation's roots is $n$. Thus $n=(2r)(2s)=4rs=4m$.

Step 5: Answer

Since $m=2p$, we get $n=4m=8p$, so $\frac np=\boxed{8}$.

Key Idea

Apply Vieta's formulas to two quadratics whose roots differ by a factor of 2.
