Problem Statement

A carton contains milk that is $2\%$ fat, an amount that is $40\%$ less fat than the amount contained in a carton of whole milk. What is the percentage of fat in whole milk?

Choices

  1. A$\frac{12}{5}$
  2. B$3$
  3. C$\frac{10}{3}$
  4. D$\frac{38}{5}$
  5. E$\frac{42}{5}$

Answer

C. \frac{10}{3}

Solution

Step 1: Translate the percent statement

Let $w$ be the fat percentage of whole milk. The $2\%$ milk has $40\%$ less fat, so it has $60\%$ of the whole-milk fat.

Step 2: Set up the equation

Thus $2=0.60w=\frac35w$.

Step 3: Solve

Multiplying by $\frac53$ gives $w=2\cdot\frac53=\frac{10}{3}$.

Step 4: Answer

Whole milk is $\boxed{\frac{10}{3}\%}$ fat.

Key Idea

Translate '40 percent less' into 60 percent of the whole-milk fat percentage.
