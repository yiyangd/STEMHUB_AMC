Problem Statement

The sum of two nonzero real numbers is $4$ times their product. What is the sum of the reciprocals of the two numbers?

Choices

  1. A1
  2. B2
  3. C4
  4. D8
  5. E12

Answer

C. 4

Solution

Step 1: Name the two numbers

Let the two nonzero real numbers be $x$ and $y$. The problem says \[x+y=4xy.\]

Step 2: Recognize the target

The sum of the reciprocals is \[\frac1x+\frac1y=\frac{x+y}{xy}.\]

Step 3: Substitute the given relationship

Since $x+y=4xy$, we get \[\frac{x+y}{xy}=\frac{4xy}{xy}=4.\]

Step 4: Check why nonzero matters

The numbers are nonzero, so dividing by $xy$ is valid.

Step 5: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{4}$.

Key Idea

Divide the given equation by the product.
