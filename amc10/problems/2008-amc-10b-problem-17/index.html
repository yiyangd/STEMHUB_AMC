2008 AMC 10B Problem 17

2008AMC 10BProblem 17概率与统计组合概率概率计数

Problem Statement

A poll shows that $70\%$ of all voters approve of the mayor's work. On three separate occasions a pollster selects a voter at random. What is the probability that on exactly one of these three occasions the voter approves of the mayor's work?

Choices

  1. A$0.063$
  2. B$0.189$
  3. C$0.233$
  4. D$0.333$
  5. E$0.441$

Answer

B. 0.189

Solution

Step 1: Identify success and failure

An approval has probability $0.7$, and a non-approval has probability $0.3$. We want exactly one approval in three selections.

Step 2: Choose where the approval happens

The one approval can occur in any one of the $3$ selections.

Step 3: Compute one pattern

For a fixed pattern such as approve, not approve, not approve, the probability is $0.7(0.3)^2$.

Step 4: Multiply by the number of patterns

Thus the probability is $3\cdot0.7\cdot(0.3)^2=3\cdot0.7\cdot0.09=0.189$.

Step 5: Answer

The probability is $\boxed{0.189}$.

Key Idea

Use the binomial probability for exactly one approval in three independent selections.
