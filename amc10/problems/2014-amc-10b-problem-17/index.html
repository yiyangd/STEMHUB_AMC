2014 AMC 10B Problem 17

2014AMC 10BProblem 17数论质因数与整除质因数整除

Problem Statement

What is the greatest power of $2$ that is a factor of $10^{1002}-4^{501}$?

Choices

  1. A2^{1002}
  2. B2^{1003}
  3. C2^{1004}
  4. D2^{1005}
  5. E2^{1006}

Answer

D. 2^{1005}

Solution

Step 1: Factor out the obvious power of 2

We have $10^{1002}=2^{1002}5^{1002}$ and $4^{501}=2^{1002}$. Therefore \[10^{1002}-4^{501}=2^{1002}(5^{1002}-1).\]

Step 2: Find the extra factors of 2

We need the power of $2$ dividing $5^{1002}-1$. Since the exponent is even, the standard factor rule gives \[v_2(5^n-1)=v_2(5-1)+v_2(5+1)+v_2(n)-1.\]

Step 3: Apply the rule

For $n=1002$, this is $2+1+1-1=3$, because $1002$ has one factor of $2$.

Step 4: Add exponents

The total power of $2$ is $1002+3=1005$.

Step 5: Conclude

The greatest power is $\boxed{2^{1005}}$.

Key Idea

Factor out the obvious power of 2 and use the exponent of 5^n-1.
