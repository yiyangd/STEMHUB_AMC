2009 AMC 10B Problem 18

2009AMC 10BProblem 18几何面积与相似面积比例

Problem Statement

Rectangle $ABCD$ has $AB=8$ and $BC=6$. Point $M$ is the midpoint of diagonal $\overline{AC}$, and $E$ is on $AB$ with $\overline{ME}\perp\overline{AC}$. What is the area of $\triangle AME$?

Choices

  1. A$\frac{65}{8}$
  2. B$\frac{25}{3}$
  3. C$9$
  4. D$\frac{75}{8}$
  5. E$\frac{85}{8}$

Answer

D. \frac{75}{8}

Solution

Step 1: Use coordinates

Place $A=(0,0)$, $B=(8,0)$, and $C=(8,6)$. Then $M$, the midpoint of $AC$, is $(4,3)$.

Step 2: Find the perpendicular line

The slope of $AC$ is $\frac68=\frac34$, so a line perpendicular to $AC$ has slope $- rac43$. Through $M$, it has equation $y-3=-\frac43(x-4)$.

Step 3: Find E

Point $E$ lies on $AB$, so $y=0$. Substituting gives $-3=-\frac43(x-4)$, so $x= rac{25}{4}$. Thus $AE=\frac{25}{4}$.

Step 4: Compute the area

Using base $AE$ on the $x$-axis and height $3$, the area of $\triangle AME$ is $\frac12\cdot\frac{25}{4}\cdot3=\frac{75}{8}$.

Step 5: Answer

The area is $\boxed{\frac{75}{8}}$.

Key Idea

Use coordinates to find the foot of a perpendicular from the midpoint of a diagonal to AB.
