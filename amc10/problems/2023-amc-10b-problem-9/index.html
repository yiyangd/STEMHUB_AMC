2023AMC 10BProblem 9几何三角形三角形角度

Problem Statement

The numbers $16$ and $25$ are a pair of consecutive positive perfect squares whose difference is $9$. How many pairs of consecutive positive perfect squares have a difference less than or equal to $2023$?

Choices

  1. A674
  2. B1011
  3. C1010
  4. D2019
  5. E2017

Answer

B. 1011

Solution

Step 1: Write consecutive squares

A pair of consecutive positive perfect squares has the form $n^2$ and $(n+1)^2$ for some positive integer $n$.

Step 2: Find their difference

The difference is \[(n+1)^2-n^2=2n+1.\]

Step 3: Apply the bound

We need \[2n+1\le2023.\] This gives \[2n\le2022,\] so $n\le1011$.

Step 4: Count possible n

The positive integers $n=1,2,\ldots,1011$ each give one pair of consecutive squares.

Step 5: Conclude

There are $\boxed{1011}$ pairs.

Key Idea

Use the formula for the difference of consecutive squares.
