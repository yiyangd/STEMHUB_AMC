2023 AMC 10A Problem 13

2023AMC 10AProblem 13几何三角形三角形角度

Problem Statement

Abdul and Chiang are standing $48$ feet apart in a field. Bharat is standing in the same field as far from Abdul as possible so that the angle formed by his lines of sight to Abdul and Chiang measures $60^\circ$. What is the square of the distance, in feet, between Abdul and Bharat?

Choices

  1. A1728
  2. B2601
  3. C3072
  4. D4608
  5. E6912

Answer

C. 3072

Solution

Step 1: Draw the locus idea

Fix Abdul and Chiang as two points $48$ feet apart. Points Bharat for which $\angle ABC$ is $60^\circ$ lie on arcs of circles where the segment between Abdul and Chiang is a chord.

Step 2: Use the chord formula

For a circle of radius $R$, a chord that subtends an inscribed angle of $60^\circ$ subtends a central angle of $120^\circ$. Thus \[48=2R\sin60^\circ=R\sqrt3,\] so \[R=16\sqrt3.\]

Step 3: Find the farthest point from Abdul

The farthest point on that circle from Abdul is across the diameter from Abdul, so the maximum distance from Abdul is \[2R=32\sqrt3.\]

Step 4: Square the distance

The question asks for the square of this distance: \[(32\sqrt3)^2=1024\cdot3=3072.\]

Step 5: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{3072}$.

Key Idea

Use the chord of a 60-degree inscribed angle to locate the largest possible radius.
