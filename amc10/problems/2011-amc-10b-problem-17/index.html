2011 AMC 10B Problem 17

2011AMC 10BProblem 17几何圆与曲线圆曲线

Problem Statement

In the given circle, the diameter $\overline{EB}$ is parallel to $\overline{DC}$, and $\overline{AB}$ is parallel to $\overline{ED}$. The angles $\angle AEB$ and $\angle ABE$ are in the ratio $4:5$. What is the degree measure of $\angle BCD$?

Choices

  1. A120
  2. B125
  3. C130
  4. D135
  5. E140

Answer

C. 130

Solution

Step 1: Use the diameter

Since $\overline{EB}$ is a diameter, the inscribed angle $\angle EAB$ is a right angle. That makes triangle $AEB$ easier because the other two angles are in the ratio $4:5$.

Step 2: Find the two acute angles

Let $\angle AEB=4x$ and $\angle ABE=5x$. Then $4x+5x+90=180$, so $9x=90$ and $x=10$. Therefore $\angle ABE=50^\circ$.

Step 3: Use parallel lines

Because $\overline{AB}\parallel\overline{ED}$, the angle $\angle ABE$ equals $\angle BED$ by alternate interior angles. Thus $\angle BED=50^\circ$.

Step 4: Use the cyclic quadrilateral

Points $B,E,D,C$ lie on the circle, so quadrilateral $BEDC$ is cyclic. Opposite angles in a cyclic quadrilateral are supplementary, so $\angle BED+\angle BCD=180^\circ$.

Step 5: Finish

Therefore $\angle BCD=180^\circ-50^\circ=130^\circ$. The answer is $\boxed{130}$.

Key Idea

Use the diameter to get a right angle, then use parallel lines and cyclic quadrilateral angles.
