2016 AMC 10B Problem 24

2016AMC 10BProblem 24数论进制与数字数字进制

Problem Statement

How many four-digit integers $abcd$, with $a\ne0$, have the property that the three two-digit integers $ab<bc<cd$ form an increasing arithmetic sequence? One such number is $4692$, where $a=4$, $b=6$, $c=9$, and $d=2$.

Choices

  1. A9
  2. B15
  3. C16
  4. D17
  5. E20

Answer

D. 17

Solution

Step 1: Translate arithmetic sequence

The two-digit numbers are $10a+b$, $10b+c$, and $10c+d$. Being an arithmetic sequence means \[2(10b+c)=(10a+b)+(10c+d).\]

Step 2: Simplify the digit equation

This becomes \[19b=10a+8c+d.\] We also need $a\ne0$ and $10a+b<10b+c<10c+d$.

Step 3: Organize by the digit b

Checking possible $b$ values using $19b=10a+8c+d$ gives the following counts: \[b=2:1,\ b=3:2,\ b=4:4,\ b=5:4,\ b=6:3,\ b=7:2,\ b=8:1.\] Other values of $b$ give no valid four-digit integers.

Step 4: Add the counts

The total is \[1+2+4+4+3+2+1=17.\]

Step 5: Check the sample

The example $4692$ appears in the $b=6$ group: $46,69,92$ are increasing and have common difference $23$.

Step 6: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{17}$.

Key Idea

Turn the arithmetic-sequence condition into a digit equation.
