2012 AMC 10B Problem 12

2012AMC 10BProblem 12数论整数构造整数构造

Problem Statement

Point $B$ is due east of point $A$. Point $C$ is due north of point $B$. The distance $AC$ is $10\sqrt2$ meters, and $\angle BAC=45^\circ$. Point $D$ is $20$ meters due north of point $C$. The distance $AD$ is between which two integers?

Choices

  1. A30 and 31
  2. B31 and 32
  3. C32 and 33
  4. D33 and 34
  5. E34 and 35

Answer

B. 31 and 32

Solution

Step 1: Use the 45 degree angle

Since $B$ is east of $A$ and $C$ is north of $B$, triangle $ABC$ is right. With $\angle BAC=45^\circ$, it is a $45$-$45$-$90$ triangle.

Step 2: Find coordinates

The hypotenuse $AC=10\sqrt2$, so the legs are $10$ and $10$. Thus from $A$, point $C$ is $10$ east and $10$ north.

Step 3: Move to D

Point $D$ is $20$ meters north of $C$, so from $A$ it is $10$ east and $30$ north.

Step 4: Compute AD

Then $AD=\sqrt{10^2+30^2}=\sqrt{1000}=10\sqrt{10}$, which is between $31$ and $32$.

Key Idea

Use the 45-45-90 triangle to get coordinates and then apply distance formula.
