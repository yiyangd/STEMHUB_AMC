2008 AMC 10A Problem 3

2008AMC 10AProblem 3数论整数构造整数构造

Problem Statement

For the positive integer $n$, let $\langle n\rangle$ denote the sum of all the positive divisors of $n$ with the exception of $n$ itself. For example, $\langle4\rangle=1+2=3$ and $\langle12\rangle=1+2+3+4+6=16$. What is $\langle\langle\langle6\rangle\rangle\rangle$?

Choices

  1. A$6$
  2. B$12$
  3. C$24$
  4. D$32$
  5. E$36$

Answer

A. 6

Solution

Step 1: Evaluate the first layer

The proper positive divisors of $6$ are $1,2,3$, and their sum is $1+2+3=6$. Thus $\langle6\rangle=6$.

Step 2: Notice the fixed value

Since applying the operation to $6$ gives $6$ again, every additional application also gives $6$.

Step 3: Apply all three brackets

Therefore $\langle\langle\langle6\rangle\rangle\rangle=6$.

Step 4: Answer

The answer is $\boxed{6}$.

Key Idea

Iterate the proper-divisor-sum function carefully.
