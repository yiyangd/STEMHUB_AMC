2021 Spring AMC 10B Problem 11

2021 SpringAMC 10BProblem 11几何面积与相似面积比例

Problem Statement

Grandma cuts a rectangular pan into equal rectangular brownies using straight cuts parallel to the sides, each cut going entirely across the pan. She wants the same number of interior pieces as pieces along the perimeter. What is the greatest possible number of brownies she can produce?

Choices

  1. A24
  2. B30
  3. C48
  4. D60
  5. E64

Answer

D. 60

Solution

Step 1: Model the brownie grid

Suppose the pan is cut into $m$ rows and $n$ columns of brownies. Then there are $mn$ total pieces.

Step 2: Count interior pieces

The interior pieces are those not touching the perimeter, so there are \[(m-2)(n-2)\] of them.

Step 3: Count perimeter pieces

The perimeter pieces are the total minus the interior: \[mn-(m-2)(n-2)=2m+2n-4.\]

Step 4: Set the counts equal

We need \[(m-2)(n-2)=2m+2n-4.\] Simplifying gives \[mn-4m-4n+8=0,\] or \[(m-4)(n-4)=8.\]

Step 5: Maximize the product

The factor pair $1\cdot8$ gives $(m,n)=(5,12)$ up to order, producing \[mn=60\] brownies. The other factor pair gives fewer.

Step 6: Conclude

The greatest possible number is $\boxed{60}$.

Key Idea

Relate grid dimensions to interior and perimeter pieces.
