2020 AMC 10A Problem 10

2020AMC 10AProblem 10几何空间几何空间几何体积

Problem Statement

Seven cubes, whose volumes are $1,8,27,64,125,216,$ and $343$ cubic units, are stacked vertically to form a tower in which the volumes decrease from bottom to top. Except for the bottom cube, the bottom face of each cube lies completely on top of the cube below it. What is the total surface area of the tower, including the bottom?

Choices

  1. A644
  2. B658
  3. C664
  4. D720
  5. E749

Answer

B. 658

Solution

Step 1: Find side lengths

The cube volumes are $1^3,2^3,\ldots,7^3$, so the side lengths are $1,2,\ldots,7$. They are stacked with side length $7$ on bottom and $1$ on top.

Step 2: Start with separate surface areas

If the cubes were separate, their total surface area would be \[6(1^2+2^2+\cdots+7^2)=6\cdot140=840.\]

Step 3: Subtract hidden contact faces

When a cube of side $k$ sits on a larger cube, the contact hides area $k^2$ on the bottom of the small cube and $k^2$ on the top of the large cube. For $k=1,2,\ldots,6$, subtract \[2(1^2+2^2+\cdots+6^2)=2\cdot91=182.\]

Step 4: Compute

The visible surface area is \[840-182=658.\]

Step 5: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{658}$.

Key Idea

Add cube surface areas and subtract hidden contact faces.
