2021 Spring AMC 10B Problem 19

2021 SpringAMC 10BProblem 19概率与统计平均数与数据集平均数数据

Problem Statement

Suppose $S$ is a finite set of positive integers. If the greatest integer is removed, the average of the remaining integers is $32$. If the least integer is also removed, the average of the remaining integers is $35$. If the greatest integer is then returned, the average rises to $40$. The greatest integer in the original set is $72$ greater than the least integer. What is the average of all integers in $S$?

Choices

  1. A36.2
  2. B36.4
  3. C36.6
  4. D36.8
  5. E37

Answer

D. 36.8

Solution

Step 1: Name the important quantities

Let the original set have $n$ elements, least element $l$, greatest element $g$, and let $M$ be the sum of the other $n-2$ elements.

Step 2: Write the three average equations

Removing the greatest gives \[M+l=32(n-1).\] Removing both least and greatest gives \[M=35(n-2).\] Returning the greatest while leaving out the least gives \[M+g=40(n-1).\]

Step 3: Solve for l and g in terms of n

Using $M=35(n-2)$, the first equation gives \[l=32(n-1)-35(n-2)=38-3n.\] The third gives \[g=40(n-1)-35(n-2)=5n+30.\]

Step 4: Use the difference

We are told $g-l=72$, so \[(5n+30)-(38-3n)=72.\] Hence $8n-8=72$, and $n=10$.

Step 5: Find the total average

Then $M=35(8)=280$, $l=8$, and $g=80$. The total sum is \[280+8+80=368.\] With $10$ elements, the average is \[36.8.\]

Step 6: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{36.8}$.

Key Idea

Set up equations for the middle sum, least, greatest, and set size.
