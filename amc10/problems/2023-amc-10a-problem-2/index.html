2023 AMC 10A Problem 2

2023AMC 10AProblem 2代数运算与化简代数化简

Problem Statement

The weight of $\frac13$ of a large pizza together with $3\frac12$ cups of orange slices is the same as the weight of $\frac34$ of a large pizza together with $\frac12$ cup of orange slices. A cup of orange slices weighs $\frac14$ of a pound. What is the weight, in pounds, of a large pizza?

Choices

  1. A$1\frac45$
  2. B2
  3. C$2\frac25$
  4. D3
  5. E$3\frac35$

Answer

A. 1\frac45

Solution

Step 1: Name the pizza weight

Let $p$ be the weight of a large pizza, in pounds. One cup of orange slices weighs $\frac14$ pound.

Step 2: Set up the equality

The given balance is \[\frac13p+3\frac12\cdot\frac14=\frac34p+\frac12\cdot\frac14.\]

Step 3: Move the orange terms together

The difference in orange slices is $3$ cups, so the orange-slice weight difference is \[3\cdot\frac14=\frac34.\] This equals the pizza-weight difference \[\frac34p-\frac13p=\frac5{12}p.\]

Step 4: Solve for p

Thus \[\frac5{12}p=\frac34,\] so \[p=\frac34\cdot\frac{12}{5}=\frac95=1\frac45.\]

Step 5: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{1\frac45}$.

Key Idea

Set up a linear equation for pizza and orange-slice weights.
