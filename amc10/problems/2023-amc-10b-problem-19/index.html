2023 AMC 10B Problem 19

2023AMC 10BProblem 19概率与统计几何概率几何概率面积

Problem Statement

Sonya the frog chooses a point uniformly at random in the square $[0,6]\times[0,6]$. She then chooses a distance uniformly at random from $[0,1]$ and a direction uniformly at random from north, south, east, and west. What is the probability that she lands outside the square?

Choices

  1. A$\frac16$
  2. B$\frac1{12}$
  3. C$\frac14$
  4. D$\frac1{10}$
  5. E$\frac19$

Answer

B. \frac1{12}

Solution

Step 1: Use symmetry of directions

By symmetry, the probability of leaving the square is the same for north, south, east, or west. So compute it for one direction, say east.

Step 2: Set up the eastward condition

If Sonya starts at horizontal coordinate $x$, an eastward hop of length $d$ leaves the square exactly when \[d>6-x.\]

Step 3: Average over x and d

This can only happen when $x>5$. For $5\le x\le6$, the probability over $d\in[0,1]$ is \[x-5.\]

Step 4: Integrate over the square width

Since $x$ is uniform on $[0,6]$, the eastward escape probability is \[\frac1{6}\int_5^6(x-5)\,dx=\frac1{6}\cdot\frac12=\frac1{12}.\]

Step 5: Use direction symmetry

Averaging over the four directions gives the same value, because each direction has probability $\frac1{12}$ of escaping conditional on that direction.

Step 6: Conclude

The probability is $\boxed{\frac1{12}}$.

Key Idea

Average the one-direction escape probability by symmetry.
