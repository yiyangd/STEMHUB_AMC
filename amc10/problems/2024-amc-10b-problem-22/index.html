2024AMC 10BProblem 22组合数学排列组合排列组合计数

Problem Statement

A group of $16$ people will be partitioned into $4$ indistinguishable $4$-person committees. Each committee will have one chairperson and one secretary. The number of different ways to make these assignments can be written as $3^rM$, where $r$ and $M$ are positive integers and $M$ is not divisible by $3$. What is $r$?

Choices

  1. A5
  2. B6
  3. C7
  4. D8
  5. E9

Answer

A. 5

Solution

Step 1: Count the committee partitions

First partition the $16$ people into four indistinguishable groups of $4$. This can be done in \[\frac{16!}{(4!)^4\,4!}\] ways.

Step 2: Assign roles inside committees

In each $4$-person committee, choose an ordered pair for chairperson and secretary in \(4\cdot3=12\) ways. Across four committees, this contributes \(12^4\).

Step 3: Write the full count

The total number of assignments is \[\frac{16!\cdot12^4}{(4!)^4\,4!}.\] We only need the exponent of $3$ in this number.

Step 4: Compute powers of 3

Using \(v_3\) for the exponent of $3$, \[v_3(16!)=\left\lfloor\frac{16}{3}\right\rfloor+\left\lfloor\frac{16}{9}\right\rfloor=5+1=6.\] Also \(v_3(12^4)=4\), and the denominator has \(v_3((4!)^4\,4!)=4+1=5\).

Step 5: Subtract denominator exponents

Therefore \[r=6+4-5=5.\]

Step 6: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{5}$.

Key Idea

Compute the exponent of 3 in a committee-counting formula.
