2004 AMC 10B Problem 16

2004AMC 10BProblem 16几何圆与曲线圆曲线

Problem Statement

Three circles of radius $1$ are externally tangent to each other and internally tangent to a larger circle. What is the radius of the large circle?

Choices

  1. A$2+\frac{\sqrt6}{3}$
  2. B$2$
  3. C$\frac{2+3\sqrt2}{3}$
  4. D$\frac{3+2\sqrt3}{3}$
  5. E$\frac{3+\sqrt3}{2}$

Answer

D. \frac{3+2\sqrt3}{3}

Solution

Step 1: Connect the centers

The centers of the three small circles form an equilateral triangle of side $2$, because the circles have radius $1$ and are externally tangent.

Step 2: Find the distance from the triangle center

The center of the large circle is the center of this equilateral triangle. The distance from the center of an equilateral triangle of side $2$ to a vertex is $\frac{2}{\sqrt3}$.

Step 3: Add one small radius

The large circle's radius equals the distance from its center to a small-circle center plus the small radius $1$.

Step 4: Compute

So the large radius is $1+\frac{2}{\sqrt3}=1+\frac{2\sqrt3}{3}=\frac{3+2\sqrt3}{3}$.

Step 5: Answer

The answer is $\boxed{\frac{3+2\sqrt3}{3}}$.

Key Idea

Use the equilateral triangle formed by the three small circle centers.
