2016 AMC 10B Problem 21

2016AMC 10BProblem 21几何面积与相似面积比例

Problem Statement

What is the area of the region enclosed by the graph of the equation $x^2+y^2=|x|+|y|$?

Choices

  1. A$\pi+\sqrt2$
  2. B$\pi+2$
  3. C$\pi+2\sqrt2$
  4. D$2\pi+\sqrt2$
  5. E$2\pi+2\sqrt2$

Answer

B. \pi+2

Solution

Step 1: Work one quadrant at a time

In the first quadrant, $|x|=x$ and $|y|=y$, so the equation becomes \[x^2+y^2=x+y.\] Completing the square gives \[(x-\tfrac12)^2+(y-\tfrac12)^2=\frac12.\]

Step 2: Recognize the arc

In the first quadrant this is a semicircle whose diameter is the segment from $(1,0)$ to $(0,1)$. The same structure appears in each quadrant.

Step 3: See the whole shape

The enclosed region is a diamond with vertices $(1,0)$, $(0,1)$, $(-1,0)$, and $(0,-1)$, plus four outward semicircles.

Step 4: Compute the areas

The diamond has diagonals $2$ and $2$, so its area is $\frac12\cdot2\cdot2=2$. Each semicircle has radius $\frac1{\sqrt2}$, so each has area \[\frac12\pi\left(\frac1{\sqrt2}\right)^2=\frac\pi4.\] Four semicircles have total area $\pi$.

Step 5: Add

The total enclosed area is \[2+\pi.\]

Step 6: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{\pi+2}$.

Key Idea

Decompose the region into a diamond and four semicircles.
