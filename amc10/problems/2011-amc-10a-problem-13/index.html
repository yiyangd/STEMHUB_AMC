2011 AMC 10A Problem 13

2011AMC 10AProblem 13数论进制与数字数字进制

Problem Statement

How many even integers are there between $200$ and $700$ whose digits are all different and come from the set $\{1,2,5,7,8,9\}$?

Choices

  1. A12
  2. B20
  3. C72
  4. D120
  5. E200

Answer

A. 12

Solution

Step 1: Use the range first

The number is between $200$ and $700$, so its hundreds digit can only be $2$ or $5$ from the allowed set. A hundreds digit of $1$ is too small, and $7,8,9$ are too large.

Step 2: Use evenness

The units digit must be even. From the allowed digits, the even choices are $2$ and $8$. Digits also have to be different.

Step 3: Count when the hundreds digit is 2

If the hundreds digit is $2$, then the units digit cannot also be $2$, so it must be $8$. The tens digit can be any of $1,5,7,9$, giving $4$ numbers.

Step 4: Count when the hundreds digit is 5

If the hundreds digit is $5$, then the units digit can be $2$ or $8$. After choosing the units digit, there are $4$ choices left for the tens digit, giving $2\cdot4=8$ numbers.

Step 5: Add the cases

The total is $4+8=12$. The answer is $\boxed{12}$.

Key Idea

Count by hundreds digit and units digit, since the number must be even and in range.
