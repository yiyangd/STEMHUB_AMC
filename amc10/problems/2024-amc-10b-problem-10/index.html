2024 AMC 10B Problem 10

2024AMC 10BProblem 10几何面积与相似面积比例

Problem Statement

Quadrilateral $ABCD$ is a parallelogram, and $E$ is the midpoint of side $\overline{AD}$. Let $F$ be the intersection of lines $EB$ and $AC$. What is the ratio of the area of quadrilateral $CDEF$ to the area of triangle $CFB$?

Choices

  1. A5:4
  2. B4:3
  3. C3:2
  4. D5:3
  5. E2:1

Answer

A. 5:4

Solution

Step 1: Choose convenient coordinates

Because only an area ratio is needed, choose a simple parallelogram: let \[A=(0,0),\quad B=(1,0),\quad D=(0,1),\quad C=(1,1).\] Then $E$, the midpoint of $\overline{AD}$, is \[(0,\tfrac12).\]

Step 2: Find point F

The diagonal $AC$ has equation $y=x$. The line through $E(0,\frac12)$ and $B(1,0)$ has equation \[y=\frac12-\frac12x.\] Solving with $y=x$ gives \[x=\frac13,\quad y=\frac13,\] so $F=(\frac13,\frac13)$.

Step 3: Compute the triangle area

Triangle $CFB$ has base $CB=1$ and horizontal distance from $F$ to the line $x=1$ equal to $\frac23$, so \[[CFB]=\frac12\cdot1\cdot\frac23=\frac13.\]

Step 4: Compute the quadrilateral area

Using the shoelace formula on $C(1,1)$, $D(0,1)$, $E(0,\frac12)$, and $F(\frac13,\frac13)$ gives \[[CDEF]=\frac5{12}.\]

Step 5: Compare

Therefore \[[CDEF]:[CFB]=\frac5{12}:\frac13=5:4.\]

Step 6: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{5:4}$.

Key Idea

Use coordinates to compare parallelogram sub-areas.
