2004 AMC 10A Problem 19

2004AMC 10AProblem 19几何圆与曲线圆曲线

Problem Statement

A white cylindrical silo has diameter $30$ feet and height $80$ feet. A red stripe with a horizontal width of $3$ feet is painted on the silo, making two complete revolutions around it. What is the area of the stripe in square feet?

Choices

  1. A$120$
  2. B$180$
  3. C$240$
  4. D$360$
  5. E$480$

Answer

C. 240

Solution

Step 1: Unroll the cylinder

Imagine cutting the silo vertically and unrolling it into a rectangle. The red stripe becomes a diagonal band crossing the rectangle while wrapping twice around the cylinder.

Step 2: Use the given horizontal width

The stripe has horizontal width $3$ feet. In the unrolled rectangle, a diagonal band of constant horizontal width can be sheared into a vertical rectangle without changing area.

Step 3: Identify the rectangle dimensions

After this shear, the equivalent rectangle has width $3$ feet and height equal to the silo height, $80$ feet. The number of revolutions changes the slant, but not this area calculation when the horizontal width is fixed.

Step 4: Compute the area

The area is $3\cdot80=240$ square feet.

Step 5: Answer

The stripe area is $\boxed{240}$.

Key Idea

Unroll the cylinder and shear the diagonal stripe into a rectangle with the same area.
