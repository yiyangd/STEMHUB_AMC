2012 AMC 10A Problem 16

2012AMC 10AProblem 16代数三角恒等式三角函数恒等式

Problem Statement

Three runners start running simultaneously from the same point on a $500$-meter circular track. They each run clockwise around the course maintaining constant speeds of $4.4$, $4.8$, and $5.0$ meters per second. The runners stop once they are all together again somewhere on the circular course. How many seconds do the runners run?

Choices

  1. A1000
  2. B1250
  3. C2500
  4. D5000
  5. E10000

Answer

C. 2500

Solution

Step 1: Use relative speeds

The fastest runner at $5.0$ m/s must gain whole laps on each of the other two runners. The relative speeds are $5.0-4.8=0.2$ m/s and $5.0-4.4=0.6$ m/s.

Step 2: Write the lap conditions

They are all together again when both relative distances are multiples of $500$ meters. So $0.2t$ and $0.6t$ must both be multiples of $500$.

Step 3: Solve the conditions

The condition $0.2t=500k$ gives $t=2500k$. The condition $0.6t=500m$ gives $t=\frac{2500}{3}m$. The smallest positive time satisfying both is $t=2500$.

Step 4: Check

In $2500$ seconds, the relative gains are $500$ meters and $1500$ meters, both whole laps.

Step 5: Conclude

The runners run for $\boxed{2500}$ seconds.

Key Idea

All runners meet again when each relative distance is a multiple of the track length.
