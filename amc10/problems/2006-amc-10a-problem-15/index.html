2006 AMC 10A Problem 15

2006AMC 10AProblem 15几何圆与曲线圆曲线

Problem Statement

Odell and Kershaw run for 30 minutes on a circular track. Odell runs clockwise at 250 m/min and uses the inner lane with a radius of 50 meters. Kershaw runs counterclockwise at 300 m/min and uses the outer lane with a radius of 60 meters, starting on the same radial line as Odell. How many times after the start do they pass each other?

Choices

  1. A29
  2. B42
  3. C45
  4. D47
  5. E50

Answer

D. 47

Solution

Step 1: Convert speeds to angular speeds

Odell's angular speed is $250/50=5$ radians per minute. Kershaw's angular speed is $300/60=5$ radians per minute.

Step 2: Use opposite directions

Since they run in opposite directions, their relative angular speed is $5+5=10$ radians per minute.

Step 3: Find total relative angle

In $30$ minutes, the relative angular distance is $30\cdot10=300$ radians.

Step 4: Count meetings after the start

They pass whenever the relative angular distance reaches a positive multiple of $2\pi$. The count is $\left\lfloor\frac{300}{2\pi}\right\rfloor$.

Step 5: Answer

Since $\frac{300}{2\pi}\approx47.7$, they pass $\boxed{47}$ times after the start.

Key Idea

Compare angular speeds on two circular lanes moving in opposite directions.
