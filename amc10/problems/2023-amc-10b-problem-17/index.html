2023 AMC 10B Problem 17

2023AMC 10BProblem 17几何面积与相似面积比例

Problem Statement

A rectangular box $P$ has distinct edge lengths $a,b,c$. The sum of the lengths of all $12$ edges is $13$, the sum of the areas of all $6$ faces is $\frac{11}{2}$, and the volume is $\frac12$. What is the length of the longest interior diagonal connecting two vertices of $P$?

Choices

  1. A2
  2. B$\frac38$
  3. C$\frac98$
  4. D$\frac94$
  5. E$\frac32$

Answer

D. \frac94

Solution

Step 1: Translate the box information

The total edge length is $4(a+b+c)=13$, so \[a+b+c=\frac{13}{4}.\] The total face area is $2(ab+bc+ca)=\frac{11}{2}$, so \[ab+bc+ca=\frac{11}{4}.\]

Step 2: Focus on the diagonal

An interior diagonal of a rectangular box has length \[\sqrt{a^2+b^2+c^2}.\]

Step 3: Use symmetric sums

We can compute \[a^2+b^2+c^2=(a+b+c)^2-2(ab+bc+ca).\]

Step 4: Substitute

This gives \[a^2+b^2+c^2=\left(\frac{13}{4}\right)^2-2\cdot\frac{11}{4}=\frac{169}{16}-\frac{88}{16}=\frac{81}{16}.\]

Step 5: Take the square root

The diagonal length is \[\sqrt{\frac{81}{16}}=\frac94.\]

Step 6: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{\frac94}$.

Key Idea

Use symmetric sums of the box edge lengths.
