2021 Spring AMC 10A Problem 19

2021 SpringAMC 10AProblem 19几何面积与相似面积比例

Problem Statement

The area of the region bounded by the graph of \[x^2+y^2=3|x-y|+3|x+y|\] is $m+n\pi$, where $m$ and $n$ are integers. What is $m+n$?

Choices

  1. A18
  2. B27
  3. C36
  4. D45
  5. E54

Answer

E. 54

Solution

Step 1: Split by absolute value signs

The signs of $x-y$ and $x+y$ divide the plane into four regions. In each region, the equation becomes a circle equation.

Step 2: Analyze one region

For example, when $|x-y|=x-y$ and $|x+y|=x+y$, the equation becomes \[x^2+y^2=6x,\] or \[(x-3)^2+y^2=3^2.\]

Step 3: Use symmetry for the other regions

The other sign choices produce circles of radius $3$ centered at \[(-3,0),\quad(0,3),\quad(0,-3).\] Together these arcs enclose a square of side $6$ plus four semicircles of radius $3$.

Step 4: Compute the area

The square contributes $6^2=36$. The four semicircles contribute \[4\cdot\frac12\pi\cdot3^2=18\pi.\]

Step 5: Find m+n

So the area is \[36+18\pi,\] giving $m=36$ and $n=18$.

Step 6: Conclude

The requested sum is \[m+n=54.\] The answer is $\boxed{54}$.

Key Idea

Split by absolute-value signs to identify circular arcs.
