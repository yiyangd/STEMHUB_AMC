2009 AMC 10A Problem 22

2009AMC 10AProblem 22概率与统计组合概率概率计数

Problem Statement

Two cubical dice each have removable numbers $1$ through $6$. The twelve numbers on the two dice are removed, put into a bag, then drawn one at a time and randomly reattached to the faces of the cubes, one number to each face. The dice are then rolled and the numbers on the two top faces are added. What is the probability that the sum is $7$?

Choices

  1. A$\frac19$
  2. B$\frac18$
  3. C$\frac16$
  4. D$\frac{2}{11}$
  5. E$\frac15$

Answer

D. \frac{2}{11}

Solution

Step 1: Model the top labels

After the labels are randomly attached, the two top faces are like two labels chosen from the twelve labels without replacement, one from each die after labeling.

Step 2: Count all ordered possibilities

There are $12$ choices for the first top label and $11$ remaining choices for the second, so $132$ ordered label choices.

Step 3: Count sums of 7

The ordered value pairs that sum to $7$ are $(1,6),(2,5),(3,4),(4,3),(5,2),(6,1)$. For each ordered value pair, there are $2$ copies of the first value and $2$ copies of the second, giving $4$ label choices.

Step 4: Compute favorable choices

There are $6\cdot4=24$ favorable ordered choices.

Step 5: Find the probability

The probability is $\frac{24}{132}=\frac{2}{11}$.

Step 6: Answer

The answer is $\boxed{\frac{2}{11}}$.

Key Idea

Treat the two top faces as two drawn number labels from the twelve labels without replacement.
