2021 Spring AMC 10A Problem 5

2021 SpringAMC 10AProblem 5概率与统计平均数与数据集平均数数据

Problem Statement

The quiz scores of a class with $k>12$ students have a mean of $8$. The mean of a collection of $12$ of these quiz scores is $14$. What is the mean of the remaining quiz scores in terms of $k$?

Choices

  1. A$\frac{14-8}{k-12}$
  2. B$\frac{8k-168}{k-12}$
  3. C$\frac{14}{12}-\frac{8}{k}$
  4. D$\frac{14(k-12)}{k^2}$
  5. E$\frac{14(k-12)}{8k}$

Answer

B. \frac{8k-168}{k-12}

Solution

Step 1: Translate means into totals

A mean is a total divided by the number of scores. Since the whole class has $k$ students with mean $8$, the total score is \[8k.\]

Step 2: Find the known subgroup total

The $12$ selected scores have mean $14$, so their total is \[12\cdot14=168.\]

Step 3: Subtract to get the remaining total

The remaining students therefore have total score \[8k-168.\]

Step 4: Divide by the number remaining

There are $k-12$ remaining students, so their mean is \[\frac{8k-168}{k-12}.\]

Step 5: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{\frac{8k-168}{k-12}}$.

Key Idea

Convert means to total sums.
