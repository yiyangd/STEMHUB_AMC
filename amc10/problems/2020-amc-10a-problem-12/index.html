2020 AMC 10A Problem 12

2020AMC 10AProblem 12概率与统计平均数与数据集平均数数据

Problem Statement

Triangle $AMC$ is isosceles with $AM=AC$. Medians $MV$ and $CU$ are perpendicular to each other, and $MV=CU=12$. What is the area of $\triangle AMC$?

Choices

  1. A48
  2. B72
  3. C96
  4. D144
  5. E192

Answer

C. 96

Solution

Step 1: Set coordinates

Let the base be $MC=b$ on the $x$-axis, with $M=(-b/2,0)$, $C=(b/2,0)$, and $A=(0,h)$. This uses the symmetry from $AM=AC$.

Step 2: Write the median vectors

The midpoint of $AC$ is $V=(b/4,h/2)$, so \[\overrightarrow{MV}=\left(\frac{3b}{4},\frac{h}{2}\right).\] The midpoint of $AM$ is $U=(-b/4,h/2)$, so \[\overrightarrow{CU}=\left(-\frac{3b}{4},\frac{h}{2}\right).\]

Step 3: Use perpendicularity

The medians are perpendicular, so their dot product is $0$: \[-\frac{9b^2}{16}+\frac{h^2}{4}=0.\] Hence \[h^2=\frac{9b^2}{4}.\]

Step 4: Use the median length

Since $MV=12$, \[\left(\frac{3b}{4}\right)^2+\left(\frac{h}{2}\right)^2=144.\] Substituting $h^2=\frac{9b^2}{4}$ gives \[\frac{9b^2}{16}+\frac{9b^2}{16}=144,\] so $b^2=128$.

Step 5: Find the area

The area is \[\frac12 bh.\] Since $h=\frac{3b}{2}$, the area is \[\frac12 b\cdot\frac{3b}{2}=\frac{3b^2}{4}=\frac{3\cdot128}{4}=96.\]

Step 6: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{96}$.

Key Idea

Use coordinates for the perpendicular equal medians.
