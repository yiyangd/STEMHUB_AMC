2023 AMC 10B Problem 16

2023AMC 10BProblem 16数论进制与数字数字进制

Problem Statement

An upno is a positive integer of two or more digits whose digits are strictly increasing from left to right. A downno is a positive integer of two or more digits whose digits are strictly decreasing from left to right. Let $U$ be the total number of upnos and $D$ the total number of downnos. What is $|U-D|$?

Choices

  1. A512
  2. B10
  3. C0
  4. D9
  5. E511

Answer

E. 511

Solution

Step 1: Count upnos by choosing digits

An upno is determined by choosing at least two digits from $1$ through $9$ and writing them in increasing order. A $0$ cannot appear because it would have to be the first digit.

Step 2: Compute U

Thus \[U=\sum_{k=2}^{9}\binom9k=2^9-\binom90-\binom91=512-1-9=502.\]

Step 3: Count downnos by choosing digits

A downno is determined by choosing at least two digits from $0$ through $9$ and writing them in decreasing order. Including $0$ is allowed because it will not be the leading digit unless it is the only digit, which is excluded.

Step 4: Compute D

Therefore \[D=\sum_{k=2}^{10}\binom{10}{k}=2^{10}-1-10=1013.\]

Step 5: Subtract

We get \[|U-D|=|502-1013|=511.\]

Step 6: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{511}$.

Key Idea

Count digit subsets that form increasing or decreasing numbers.
