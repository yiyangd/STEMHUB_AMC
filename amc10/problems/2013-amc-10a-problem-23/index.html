2013 AMC 10A Problem 23

2013AMC 10AProblem 23几何圆与曲线圆曲线

Problem Statement

In $\triangle ABC$, $AB=86$ and $AC=97$. A circle with center $A$ and radius $AB$ intersects $BC$ at points $B$ and $X$. Moreover $BX$ and $CX$ have integer lengths. What is $BC$?

Choices

  1. A11
  2. B28
  3. C33
  4. D61
  5. E72

Answer

D. 61

Solution

Step 1: Use the equal radii

Since $B$ and $X$ lie on the circle centered at $A$, we have $AB=AX=86$. Thus $BX$ is a chord of that circle lying on line $BC$.

Step 2: Name the integer pieces

Let $BX=x$ and $CX=m$, where both are positive integers. Then $BC=x+m$.

Step 3: Compare right triangles

Drop the perpendicular from $A$ to line $BC$. It bisects chord $BX$, so the horizontal distances to $B$ and $X$ are both $x/2$. Comparing $AC^2$ and $AB^2$ gives \[(m+x/2)^2-(x/2)^2=97^2-86^2.\]

Step 4: Factor

The left side is $m(m+x)$, and the right side is $2013=3\cdot11\cdot61$. Thus $m$ and $m+x=BC$ are factor pairs of $2013$.

Step 5: Use triangle inequality

The possible larger factors are $2013,671,183,61$. Since $BC<AB+AC=183$, only $61$ can work. The answer is $\boxed{61}$.

Key Idea

Use equal radii to create a chord and factor the difference of squares.
