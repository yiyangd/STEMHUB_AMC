2011 AMC 10A Problem 11

2011AMC 10AProblem 11几何面积与相似面积比例

Problem Statement

Square $EFGH$ has one vertex on each side of square $ABCD$. Point $E$ is on $\overline{AB}$ with $AE=7\cdot EB$. What is the ratio of the area of $EFGH$ to the area of $ABCD$?

Choices

  1. A\frac{49}{64}
  2. B\frac{25}{32}
  3. C\frac{7\sqrt2}{8}
  4. D\frac{5}{8}
  5. E\frac{14}{4}

Answer

B. \frac{25}{32}

Solution

Step 1: Choose a scale

Because the question asks for an area ratio, we can choose a convenient scale. Let $EB=1$, so $AE=7$ and the side length of square $ABCD$ is $8$.

Step 2: Place the first vertex

Put $A=(0,8)$, $B=(8,8)$, $C=(8,0)$, and $D=(0,0)$. Then $E=(7,8)$. If $F$ lies on side $BC$, the horizontal change from $E$ to $F$ is $1$.

Step 3: Use the square rotation

For the rotated square to land one vertex on each side, the vertical drop from $E$ to $F$ is $7$. Thus the side vector of the inner square can be taken as $(1,-7)$. Its squared side length is $1^2+7^2=50$.

Step 4: Compare areas

The area of square $EFGH$ is $50$. The area of square $ABCD$ is $8^2=64$. So the ratio is $\frac{50}{64}=\frac{25}{32}$.

Step 5: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{\frac{25}{32}}$.

Key Idea

Use coordinates for the rotated inner square and compare side lengths.
