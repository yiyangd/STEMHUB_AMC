2015 AMC 10A Problem 17

2015AMC 10AProblem 17几何面积与相似面积比例

Problem Statement

A line that passes through the origin intersects both the line $x=1$ and the line $y=1+\frac{x}{\sqrt3}$. The three lines create an equilateral triangle. What is the perimeter of the triangle?

Choices

  1. A2\sqrt6
  2. B2+2\sqrt3
  3. C6
  4. D3+2\sqrt3
  5. E6+3\sqrt3

Answer

D. 3+2\sqrt3

Solution

Step 1: Use the angle between the fixed lines

The line $x=1$ is vertical, and the line $y=1+\frac{x}{\sqrt3}$ has slope $\frac1{\sqrt3}$, so it makes a $30^\circ$ angle with the $x$-axis. The angle between these two lines is $60^\circ$.

Step 2: Choose the third line

For the triangle to be equilateral, the third line through the origin must make a $60^\circ$ angle with each of the other two lines. This gives the line $y=-\frac{x}{\sqrt3}$.

Step 3: Find the vertical side length

On $x=1$, the slanted upper line has $y=1+\frac1{\sqrt3}$, while the line through the origin has $y=-\frac1{\sqrt3}$. The side length is \[1+\frac2{\sqrt3}.\]

Step 4: Find the perimeter

The triangle is equilateral, so the perimeter is \[3\left(1+\frac2{\sqrt3}\right)=3+2\sqrt3.\]

Step 5: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{3+2\sqrt3}$.

Key Idea

Use the 60-degree angle between the given lines to identify the third line.
