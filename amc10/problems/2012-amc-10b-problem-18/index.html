2012 AMC 10B Problem 18

2012AMC 10BProblem 18概率与统计组合概率概率计数

Problem Statement

In a population, one of every $500$ people has a disease. A test is always positive for a person with the disease. For a person without the disease, there is a $2\%$ false positive rate. Let $p$ be the probability that a randomly chosen person with a positive test actually has the disease. Which choice is closest to $p$?

Choices

  1. A\frac1{98}
  2. B\frac19
  3. C\frac1{11}
  4. D\frac{49}{99}
  5. E\frac{98}{99}

Answer

C. \frac{1}{11}

Solution

Step 1: Use a population of 500

In $500$ people, expect $1$ person with the disease and $499$ without it.

Step 2: Count positive tests

The diseased person definitely tests positive. Among the $499$ without disease, about $0.02\cdot499=9.98$ test positive falsely.

Step 3: Compute conditional probability

Among positive tests, the probability of actually having the disease is $\frac{1}{1+9.98}\approx0.091$.

Step 4: Match a choice

This is closest to $\frac1{11}$.

Key Idea

Use conditional probability among positive tests.
