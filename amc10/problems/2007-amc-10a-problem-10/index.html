2007 AMC 10A Problem 10

2007AMC 10AProblem 10概率与统计平均数与数据集平均数数据

Problem Statement

The Dunbar family consists of a mother, a father, and some children. The average age of the members of the family is $20$, the father is $48$ years old, and the average age of the mother and children is $16$. How many children are in the family?

Choices

  1. A$2$
  2. B$3$
  3. C$4$
  4. D$5$
  5. E$6$

Answer

E. 6

Solution

Step 1: Let the number of children be $n$

Then the family has $n+2$ members: the children, the mother, and the father.

Step 2: Use the whole-family average

The total age of the whole family is $20(n+2)$.

Step 3: Use the mother-and-children average

The mother and children together have $n+1$ people with average age $16$, so their total age is $16(n+1)$. Adding the father's age gives total family age $16(n+1)+48$.

Step 4: Set the totals equal

So $20(n+2)=16(n+1)+48$. This simplifies to $20n+40=16n+64$, hence $4n=24$ and $n=6$.

Step 5: Answer

There are $\boxed{6}$ children.

Key Idea

Use weighted averages to compare total family age in two ways.
