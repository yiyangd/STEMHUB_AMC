Problem Statement

As shown below, convex pentagon $ABCDE$ has sides $AB=3$, $BC=4$, $CD=6$, $DE=3$, and $EA=7$. The pentagon is originally positioned in the plane with vertex $A$ at the origin and vertex $B$ on the positive $x$-axis. The pentagon is then rolled clockwise to the right along the $x$-axis. Which side will touch the point $x=2009$ on the $x$-axis?

Choices

  1. A$\overline{AB}$
  2. B$\overline{BC}$
  3. C$\overline{CD}$
  4. D$\overline{DE}$
  5. E$\overline{EA}$

Answer

C. \overline{CD}

Solution

Step 1: Use one full rolling cycle

As the pentagon rolls, the side touching the $x$-axis changes in order: $AB,BC,CD,DE,EA$, then repeats.

Step 2: Find the period length

The distance covered in one full cycle is the perimeter: $3+4+6+3+7=23$.

Step 3: Reduce the target position

We need the side touching $x=2009$. Since $2009=23\cdot87+8$, this is the same position as $x=8$ within one cycle.

Step 4: Locate x=8 in the cycle

Within one cycle, $AB$ covers $0$ to $3$, $BC$ covers $3$ to $7$, and $CD$ covers $7$ to $13$. Since $8$ lies between $7$ and $13$, side $CD$ is touching.

Step 5: Answer

The side is $\boxed{\overline{CD}}$.

Key Idea

Rolling the pentagon makes the side lengths repeat along the x-axis with period equal to the perimeter.
