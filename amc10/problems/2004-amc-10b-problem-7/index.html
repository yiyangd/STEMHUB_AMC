2004 AMC 10B Problem 7

2004AMC 10BProblem 7数论进制与数字数字进制

Problem Statement

On a trip from the United States to Canada, Isabella took d U.S. dollars. At the border she ex- changed them all, receiving 10 Canadian dollars for every 7 U.S. dollars. After spending 60 Cana- dian dollars, she had d Canadian dollars left. What is the sum of the digits of d?

Choices

  1. A5
  2. B6
  3. C7
  4. D8
  5. E9

Answer

A. 5

Solution

Step 1: Translate the exchange rate

For every $7$ U.S. dollars, Isabella receives $10$ Canadian dollars. If she starts with $d$ U.S. dollars, she receives $\frac{10}{7}d$ Canadian dollars.

Step 2: Use the spending information

After spending $60$ Canadian dollars, she has $d$ Canadian dollars left. So \[\frac{10}{7}d-60=d.\]

Step 3: Solve for d

Subtracting $d$ gives $\frac{3}{7}d=60$, so $d=140$.

Step 4: Find the digit sum

The sum of the digits of $140$ is $1+4+0=5$.

Step 5: Answer

The answer is $\boxed{5}$.

Key Idea

Set up an exchange-rate equation and solve for the original dollar amount.
