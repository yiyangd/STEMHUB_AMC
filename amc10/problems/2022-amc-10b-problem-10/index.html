2022 AMC 10B Problem 10

2022AMC 10BProblem 10概率与统计平均数与数据集平均数数据

Problem Statement

Camila writes down five positive integers. The unique mode of these integers is $2$ greater than their median, and the median is $2$ greater than their arithmetic mean. What is the least possible value for the mode?

Choices

  1. A5
  2. B7
  3. C9
  4. D11
  5. E13

Answer

D. 11

Solution

Step 1: Translate the statistics

Let the unique mode be $m$. Then the median is $m-2$, and the arithmetic mean is $m-4$.

Step 2: Use the sorted order

Write the five numbers in nondecreasing order. Since the median is $m-2$ and the mode is larger than the median, the two copies of the mode must be the fourth and fifth numbers.

Step 3: Write the sum condition

The total sum is $5(m-4)$. The numbers look like \[a,\ b,\ m-2,\ m,\ m,\] so their sum is \[a+b+3m-2.\] Equating gives \[a+b+3m-2=5m-20,\] hence \[a+b=2m-18.\]

Step 4: Minimize while keeping a unique mode

The numbers $a$ and $b$ must be positive and cannot be equal, because then they would create another mode. The smallest possible distinct positive sum is $1+2=3$.

Step 5: Find the least possible m

We need \[2m-18\ge3,\] so $m\ge10.5$. The least integer candidate is $m=11$.

Step 6: Check that it works

For $m=11$, choose $a=1$ and $b=3$. The list $1,3,9,11,11$ has median $9$, mean $7$, and unique mode $11$. The answer is $\boxed{11}$.

Key Idea

Convert median, mode, and mean conditions into a sum equation.
