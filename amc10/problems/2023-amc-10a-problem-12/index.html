2023 AMC 10A Problem 12

2023AMC 10AProblem 12数论进制与数字数字进制

Problem Statement

How many three-digit positive integers $N$ satisfy both of the following properties? The number $N$ is divisible by $7$, and the number formed by reversing the digits of $N$ is divisible by $5$.

Choices

  1. A13
  2. B14
  3. C15
  4. D16
  5. E17

Answer

B. 14

Solution

Step 1: Use the reversed-number condition first

If the reversed number is divisible by $5$, then its last digit is $0$ or $5$. The last digit of the reversed number is the hundreds digit of $N$, so the hundreds digit of $N$ must be $5$.

Step 2: Write the number

Let \[N=500+10a+b,\] where $a$ and $b$ are digits. We need $N$ to be divisible by $7$.

Step 3: Reduce modulo 7

Since $500\equiv3\pmod7$, the condition is \[10a+b+3\equiv0\pmod7.\] For each tens digit $a$, this determines $b$ modulo $7$.

Step 4: Count digit possibilities

For $a=0,1,\ldots,9$, the required digit $b$ is one of $0$ through $9$ in $14$ total cases. Equivalently, checking the multiples of $7$ from $500$ to $599$ gives $14$ multiples.

Step 5: Conclude

There are $\boxed{14}$ such integers.

Key Idea

Use the last digit of the reversed number and divisibility by 7.
