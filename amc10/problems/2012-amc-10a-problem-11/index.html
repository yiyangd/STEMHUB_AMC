2012 AMC 10A Problem 11

2012AMC 10AProblem 11几何圆与曲线圆曲线

Problem Statement

Externally tangent circles with centers at points $A$ and $B$ have radii of lengths $5$ and $3$, respectively. A line externally tangent to both circles intersects ray $AB$ at point $C$. What is $BC$?

Choices

  1. A4
  2. B4.8
  3. C10.2
  4. D12
  5. E14.4

Answer

D. 12

Solution

Step 1: Identify the homothety point

The external tangent line and the line through the centers meet at $C$. From $C$, the two circles look like scaled copies of each other, so the distances from $C$ to the centers are in the same ratio as the radii.

Step 2: Use the radius ratio

The radii are $5$ and $3$, so $CA:CB=5:3$. Since the circles are externally tangent, $AB=5+3=8$.

Step 3: Place C on the ray

Point $C$ is on ray $AB$ beyond $B$, so $CA=CB+AB=CB+8$.

Step 4: Solve

Now \[\frac{CB+8}{CB}=\frac53.\] Thus $3CB+24=5CB$, so $CB=12$.

Step 5: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{12}$.

Key Idea

Use similar triangles or homothety from the external tangent point.
