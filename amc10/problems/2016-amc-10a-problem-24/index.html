2016 AMC 10A Problem 24

2016AMC 10AProblem 24几何圆与曲线圆曲线

Problem Statement

A quadrilateral is inscribed in a circle of radius $200\sqrt2$. Three of the sides of this quadrilateral have length $200$. What is the length of the fourth side?

Choices

  1. A200
  2. B$200\sqrt2$
  3. C$200\sqrt3$
  4. D$300\sqrt2$
  5. E500

Answer

E. 500

Solution

Step 1: Convert side lengths to central angles

For a chord of length $200$ in a circle of radius $200\sqrt2$, let half of its central angle be $\phi$. Then \[200=2(200\sqrt2)\sin\phi,\] so \[\sin\phi=\frac{1}{2\sqrt2}.\]

Step 2: Understand the remaining side

The three equal sides use three equal central angles, each equal to $2\phi$. The fourth side corresponds to the remaining central angle $2\pi-6\phi$.

Step 3: Use the half-angle of the remaining chord

The fourth chord length is \[2(200\sqrt2)\sin(\pi-3\phi)=400\sqrt2\sin(3\phi).\]

Step 4: Compute sin three phi

Using $\sin(3\phi)=3\sin\phi-4\sin^3\phi$ and $\sin\phi=\frac1{2\sqrt2}$, we get \[\sin(3\phi)=\frac{3}{2\sqrt2}-\frac{4}{16\sqrt2}=\frac5{4\sqrt2}.\]

Step 5: Find the length

Therefore the fourth side is \[400\sqrt2\cdot\frac5{4\sqrt2}=500.\]

Step 6: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{500}$.

Key Idea

Use chord length and central angle relationships.
