2014 AMC 10B Problem 21

2014AMC 10BProblem 21几何三角形三角形角度

Problem Statement

Trapezoid $ABCD$ has parallel sides $AB$ of length $33$ and $CD$ of length $21$. The other two sides have lengths $10$ and $14$. The angles at $A$ and $B$ are acute. What is the length of the shorter diagonal of $ABCD$?

Choices

  1. A10\sqrt6
  2. B25
  3. C8\sqrt{10}
  4. D18\sqrt2
  5. E26

Answer

B. 25

Solution

Step 1: Drop altitudes to the longer base

Let the horizontal projection of the side of length $10$ be $x$, and let the height be $h$. Since the base lengths differ by $33-21=12$, the other side of length $14$ has horizontal projection $12-x$.

Step 2: Use the two right triangles

The side lengths give \[x^2+h^2=10^2\] and \[(12-x)^2+h^2=14^2.\]

Step 3: Solve for the projection

Subtracting the first equation from the second gives $(12-x)^2-x^2=96$, so $144-24x=96$ and $x=2$.

Step 4: Find the shorter diagonal

Then $h^2=10^2-2^2=96$. The shorter diagonal has horizontal distance $21+x=23$, so its length squared is $23^2+96=625$.

Step 5: Conclude

The shorter diagonal has length $\sqrt{625}=\boxed{25}$.

Key Idea

Drop altitudes in the trapezoid and solve for the horizontal projections.
