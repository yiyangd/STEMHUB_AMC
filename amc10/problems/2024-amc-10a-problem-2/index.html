2024AMC 10AProblem 2数论整除与同余同余余数

Problem Statement

A model used to estimate the time it will take to hike to the top of a mountain on a trail is of the form $T=aL+bG$, where $a$ and $b$ are constants, $T$ is the time in minutes, $L$ is the length of the trail in miles, and $G$ is the altitude gain in feet. The model estimates $69$ minutes for a $1.5$-mile trail that ascends $800$ feet and also $69$ minutes for a $1.2$-mile trail that ascends $1100$ feet. How many minutes does the model estimate for a $4.2$-mile trail that ascends $4000$ feet?

Choices

  1. A240
  2. B246
  3. C252
  4. D258
  5. E264

Answer

B. 246

Solution

Step 1: Translate the two estimates into equations

The model is $T=aL+bG$. The two given trails both take $69$ minutes, so \[1.5a+800b=69\] and \[1.2a+1100b=69.\]

Step 2: Subtract to find a relation

Subtracting the second equation from the first gives \[0.3a-300b=0,\] so \[a=1000b.\]

Step 3: Find b and a

Substitute $a=1000b$ into $1.5a+800b=69$: \[1500b+800b=69,\] so \[2300b=69,\quad b=0.03.\] Hence $a=30$.

Step 4: Apply the model to the new trail

For $L=4.2$ and $G=4000$, \[T=30(4.2)+0.03(4000)=126+120=246.\]

Step 5: Conclude

The estimated time is $\boxed{246}$ minutes.

Key Idea

Solve a two-equation linear model efficiently.
