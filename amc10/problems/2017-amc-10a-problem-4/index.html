Problem Statement

Mia is helping her mom pick up $30$ toys that are strewn on the floor. Mia's mom manages to put $3$ toys into the toy box every $30$ seconds, but each time immediately after those $30$ seconds have elapsed, Mia takes $2$ toys out of the box. How much time, in minutes, will it take Mia and her mom to put all $30$ toys into the box for the first time?

Choices

  1. A13.5
  2. B14
  3. C14.5
  4. D15
  5. E15.5

Answer

B. 14

Solution

Step 1: Understand the cycle

Every $30$ seconds, Mia's mom puts in $3$ toys. Then Mia removes $2$ toys. Usually this gives a net gain of $1$ toy per $30$ seconds.

Step 2: Do not overshoot the final moment

The question asks when all $30$ toys are in the box for the first time. This can happen immediately after Mom puts in $3$ toys, before Mia removes any.

Step 3: Get close to 30

After $27$ full cycles, the box has $27$ toys, and $27$ cycles take $27\cdot30$ seconds.

Step 4: Use one final mom move

In the next $30$ seconds, Mom adds $3$ toys, bringing the total to $30$ for the first time.

Step 5: Convert time

The total time is \[28\cdot30=840\text{ seconds}=14\text{ minutes}.\]

Step 6: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{14}$.

Key Idea

Track the net gain per 30 seconds and handle the final moment carefully.
