2006 AMC 10B Problem 14

2006AMC 10BProblem 14代数多项式与根多项式根

Problem Statement

Let $a$ and $b$ be the roots of the equation $x^2-mx+2=0$. Suppose that $a+\frac1b$ and $b+\frac1a$ are the roots of the equation $x^2-px+q=0$. What is $q$?

Choices

  1. A$\frac52$
  2. B$\frac72$
  3. C$4$
  4. D$\frac92$
  5. E$8$

Answer

D. \frac{9}{2}

Solution

Step 1: Record what Vieta gives

Since $a$ and $b$ are roots of $x^2-mx+2=0$, Vieta's formulas tell us that $ab=2$. We do not actually need the value of $m$.

Step 2: Understand what $q$ represents

For the equation $x^2-px+q=0$, the constant term $q$ is the product of its two roots. So \ \[q=\left(a+\frac1b\right)\left(b+\frac1a\right).\]

Step 3: Multiply carefully

Expanding gives \ \[ab+1+1+\frac1{ab}.\] The two middle terms are $a\cdot\frac1a=1$ and $\frac1b\cdot b=1$.

Step 4: Substitute $ab=2$

Thus \ \[q=2+1+1+\frac12=\frac92.\]

Step 5: Answer

The answer is $\boxed{\frac92}$.

Key Idea

Use Vieta's formulas and multiply the transformed roots directly.
