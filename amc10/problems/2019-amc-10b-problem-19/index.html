2019 AMC 10B Problem 19

2019AMC 10BProblem 19数论整数构造整数构造

Problem Statement

Let $S$ be the set of all positive integer divisors of $100000$. How many numbers are the product of two distinct elements of $S$?

Choices

  1. A98
  2. B100
  3. C117
  4. D119
  5. E121

Answer

C. 117

Solution

Step 1: Represent divisors by exponent pairs

Since \[100000=10^5=2^5\cdot5^5,\] every divisor in $S$ has the form $2^a5^b$ with $0\le a,b\le5$.

Step 2: Represent products

The product of two divisors has the form $2^u5^v$, where $u$ and $v$ can range from $0$ to $10$. Without the distinctness restriction, that gives $11\cdot11=121$ possible exponent pairs.

Step 3: Account for distinct divisors

We need the two chosen divisors to be distinct. A product exponent pair is impossible only when it can be made in exactly one way, by multiplying a divisor by itself.

Step 4: Find the impossible corner cases

The only exponent pairs with a unique split in both coordinates are \[(0,0),(0,10),(10,0),(10,10).\] Each would require using the same corner divisor twice, which is not allowed.

Step 5: Subtract

Therefore the number of possible products is \[121-4=117.\]

Step 6: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{117}$.

Key Idea

Count exponent pairs for products of distinct divisors.
