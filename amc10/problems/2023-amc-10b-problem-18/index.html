2023 AMC 10B Problem 18

2023AMC 10BProblem 18数论整数构造整数构造

Problem Statement

Suppose $a,b,c$ are positive integers such that \[\frac{a}{14}+\frac{b}{15}=\frac{c}{210}.\] Which of the following statements are necessarily true? I. If $\gcd(a,14)=1$ or $\gcd(b,15)=1$ or both, then $\gcd(c,210)=1$. II. If $\gcd(c,210)=1$, then $\gcd(a,14)=1$ or $\gcd(b,15)=1$ or both. III. $\gcd(c,210)=1$ if and only if $\gcd(a,14)=\gcd(b,15)=1$.

Choices

  1. AI, II, and III
  2. BI only
  3. CI and II only
  4. DIII only
  5. EII and III only

Answer

E. II and III only

Solution

Step 1: Clear denominators

From \[\frac{a}{14}+\frac{b}{15}=\frac{c}{210},\] multiply by $210$ to get \[15a+14b=c.\]

Step 2: Look prime by prime

The prime divisors of $210$ are $2,3,5,7$. For primes $2$ and $7$, the term $14b$ is divisible by the prime, so $c$ is coprime to those primes exactly when $a$ is.

Step 3: Use the other primes

For primes $3$ and $5$, the term $15a$ is divisible by the prime, so $c$ is coprime to those primes exactly when $b$ is.

Step 4: Interpret the result

Therefore \[\gcd(c,210)=1\] if and only if both \[\gcd(a,14)=1\quad\text{and}\quad\gcd(b,15)=1.\] This makes statement III true.

Step 5: Check statements I and II

Statement II follows from statement III, because if $\gcd(c,210)=1$, then both smaller gcds are $1$, so certainly at least one is $1$. Statement I is false because having only one of the two gcds equal to $1$ is not enough.

Step 6: Conclude

The necessarily true statements are II and III only, so the answer is $\boxed{\text{E}}$.

Key Idea

Analyze prime divisibility in the equation after clearing denominators.
