2010 AMC 10B Problem 14

2010AMC 10BProblem 14概率与统计平均数与数据集平均数数据

Problem Statement

The average of the numbers $1,2,3,\ldots,98,99,$ and $x$ is $100x$. What is $x$?

Choices

    Answer

    B. \frac{50}{101}

    Solution

    Step 1: Translate average into total sum

    An average is total divided by number of terms. Here there are the $99$ numbers from $1$ to $99$, plus the extra number $x$, so there are $100$ terms altogether.

    Step 2: Sum the known numbers

    The sum $1+2+\cdots+99$ is $\frac{99\cdot100}{2}=99\cdot50$. Therefore the total sum of all $100$ terms is $99\cdot50+x$.

    Step 3: Set up the average equation

    Since the average is $100x$, we write \[\frac{99\cdot50+x}{100}=100x.\] Multiplying by $100$ gives $99\cdot50+x=10000x$.

    Step 4: Solve for $x$ cleanly

    Move the $x$ term to the right: $99\cdot50=9999x$. Since $9999=99\cdot101$, we get \[x=\frac{99\cdot50}{99\cdot101}=\frac{50}{101}.\]

    Step 5: Check the size

    The value is about $0.495$, which is plausible because the average $100x$ is about $49.5$, near the average of $1$ through $99$. The answer is $\boxed{\frac{50}{101}}$.

    Key Idea

    Turn the average statement into an equation using the sum of an arithmetic sequence.
