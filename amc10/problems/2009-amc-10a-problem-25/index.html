2009 AMC 10A Problem 25

2009AMC 10AProblem 25数论质因数与整除质因数整除

Problem Statement

For $k>0$, let $I_k=10\ldots064$, where there are $k$ zeros between the $1$ and the $6$. Let $N(k)$ be the number of factors of $2$ in the prime factorization of $I_k$. What is the maximum value of $N(k)$?

Choices

  1. A$6$
  2. B$7$
  3. C$8$
  4. D$9$
  5. E$10$

Answer

B. 7

Solution

Step 1: Write the number algebraically

The number $I_k$ has digits $1$, then $k$ zeros, then $64$, so $I_k=10^{k+2}+64$.

Step 2: Factor powers of 2

This is $2^{k+2}5^{k+2}+2^6$. The number of factors of $2$ depends on which of $k+2$ and $6$ is smaller, except when they are equal.

Step 3: Handle k less than 4

If $k<4$, then $k+2<6$, and factoring out $2^{k+2}$ leaves an odd number. So $N(k)=k+2\le5$.

Step 4: Handle k greater than 4

If $k>4$, factoring out $2^6$ leaves an odd number, so $N(k)=6$.

Step 5: Check the special case

When $k=4$, the two terms have the same power $2^6$: $I_4=2^6(5^6+1)$. Since $5^6+1=15626$ is divisible by $2$ but not by $4$, this gives $N(4)=7$.

Step 6: Answer

The maximum value is $\boxed{7}$.

Key Idea

Use 2-adic valuation of 10^(k+2)+64 and check the special case when powers of 2 match.
