2004 AMC 10A Problem 23

2004AMC 10AProblem 23几何圆与曲线圆曲线

Problem Statement

Circles $A$, $B$, and $C$ are externally tangent to each other and internally tangent to circle $D$. Circles $B$ and $C$ are congruent. Circle $A$ has radius $1$ and passes through the center of $D$. What is the radius of circle $B$?

Choices

  1. A$\frac23$
  2. B$\frac34$
  3. C$\frac78$
  4. D$\frac89$
  5. E$1+\frac{\sqrt3}{3}$

Answer

D. \frac{8}{9}

Solution

Step 1: Find the radius of the large circle

Circle $A$ has radius $1$, passes through the center of $D$, and is internally tangent to $D$. Therefore the radius of $D$ is $2$.

Step 2: Use symmetry

Let the congruent circles $B$ and $C$ have radius $r$. By symmetry, put their centers at $(x,r)$ and $(x,-r)$, while the center of $D$ is at the origin and the center of $A$ is at $(-1,0)$.

Step 3: Use tangency to circle D

Since $B$ is internally tangent to $D$, the distance from its center to the origin is $2-r$. Thus \[x^2+r^2=(2-r)^2,\] so $x^2=4-4r$.

Step 4: Use tangency to circle A

Circle $B$ is externally tangent to circle $A$, so the distance between their centers is $1+r$: \[(x+1)^2+r^2=(1+r)^2.\] This simplifies to $x^2+2x=2r$.

Step 5: Solve

Using $x^2=4-4r$ in $x^2+2x=2r$ gives $x=3r-2$. Then $(3r-2)^2=4-4r$, so $9r^2-8r=0$. Since $r>0$, $r=\frac89$. The answer is $\boxed{\frac89}$.

Key Idea

Use symmetry and tangency distances between circle centers.
