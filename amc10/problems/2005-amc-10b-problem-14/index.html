2005 AMC 10B Problem 14

2005AMC 10BProblem 14几何面积与相似面积比例

Problem Statement

Equilateral $\triangle ABC$ has side length $2$, $M$ is the midpoint of $AC$, and $C$ is the midpoint of $BD$. What is the area of $\triangle CDM$?

Choices

  1. A$\frac12$
  2. B$\frac{\sqrt3}{4}$
  3. C$\frac{\sqrt3}{2}$
  4. D$1$
  5. E$2$

Answer

C. \frac{\sqrt3}{2}

Solution

Step 1: Place the equilateral triangle

Let $B=(-1,0)$, $C=(1,0)$, and $A=(0,\sqrt3)$. This gives side length $2$.

Step 2: Locate M

Point $M$ is the midpoint of $AC$, so $M=\left(\frac12,\frac{\sqrt3}{2}\right)$.

Step 3: Use C as midpoint of BD

Since $C$ is the midpoint of $BD$ and $B=(-1,0)$, point $D$ must be $(3,0)$.

Step 4: Compute the triangle area

Segment $CD$ is horizontal with length $2$. The height from $M$ to line $CD$ is $\frac{\sqrt3}{2}$.

Step 5: Answer

Thus $[CDM]=\frac12\cdot2\cdot\frac{\sqrt3}{2}=\boxed{\frac{\sqrt3}{2}}$.

Key Idea

Use coordinates for the equilateral triangle and midpoint condition.
