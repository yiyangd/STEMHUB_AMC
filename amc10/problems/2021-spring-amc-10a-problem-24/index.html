2021 Spring AMC 10A Problem 24

2021 SpringAMC 10AProblem 24几何面积与相似面积比例

Problem Statement

The interior of a quadrilateral is bounded by the graphs of $(x+ay)^2=4a^2$ and $(ax-y)^2=a^2$, where $a$ is a positive real number. What is the area of this region in terms of $a$, valid for all $a>0$?

Choices

  1. A$\frac{8a^2}{(a+1)^2}$
  2. B$\frac{4a}{a+1}$
  3. C$\frac{8a}{a+1}$
  4. D$\frac{8a^2}{a^2+1}$
  5. E$\frac{8a}{a^2+1}$

Answer

D. \frac{8a^2}{a^2+1}

Solution

Step 1: Rewrite the boundaries as linear equations

The equations represent four lines: \[x+ay=\pm2a,\qquad ax-y=\pm a.\] So the quadrilateral becomes a rectangle in suitable coordinates.

Step 2: Use a change of variables

Let \[u=x+ay,\qquad v=ax-y.\] In the $uv$-plane, the region is the rectangle \[-2a\le u\le2a,\qquad -a\le v\le a.\]

Step 3: Find the rectangle area in uv-coordinates

The rectangle has side lengths $4a$ and $2a$, so its area in the $uv$-plane is \[8a^2.\]

Step 4: Account for area scaling

The Jacobian determinant is \[\left|\begin{matrix}1&a\\ a&-1\end{matrix}\right|=a^2+1.\] Thus areas in the $uv$-plane are $(a^2+1)$ times the corresponding areas in the $xy$-plane.

Step 5: Compute the original area

The desired area is \[\frac{8a^2}{a^2+1}.\]

Step 6: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{\frac{8a^2}{a^2+1}}$.

Key Idea

Use a linear change of variables to turn the region into a rectangle.
