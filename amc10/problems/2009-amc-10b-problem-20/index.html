2009 AMC 10B Problem 20

2009AMC 10BProblem 20几何三角形三角形角度

Problem Statement

Triangle $ABC$ has a right angle at $B$, $AB=1$, and $BC=2$. The angle bisector of $\angle A$ intersects side $\overline{BC}$ at $D$. What is $BD$?

Choices

  1. A$\frac{\sqrt3-1}{2}$
  2. B$\frac{\sqrt5-1}{2}$
  3. C$\frac{\sqrt5+1}{2}$
  4. D$\frac{\sqrt6+\sqrt2}{2}$
  5. E$2\sqrt3-1$

Answer

B. \frac{\sqrt5-1}{2}

Solution

Step 1: Find the third side

The right triangle has legs $AB=1$ and $BC=2$, so $AC=\sqrt{1^2+2^2}=\sqrt5$.

Step 2: Use the angle bisector theorem

Because $AD$ bisects $\angle A$, the theorem gives $\frac{BD}{DC}=\frac{AB}{AC}=\frac1{\sqrt5}$.

Step 3: Turn the ratio into a length

Since $BD+DC=BC=2$, the part adjacent to $AB$ is $BD=\frac{2\cdot AB}{AB+AC}=\frac{2}{1+\\sqrt5}$.

Step 4: Simplify

Rationalizing gives $\frac{2}{1+\sqrt5}\cdot\frac{\sqrt5-1}{\sqrt5-1}=\frac{\sqrt5-1}{2}$.

Step 5: Answer

Therefore $BD=\boxed{\frac{\sqrt5-1}{2}}$.

Key Idea

Use the angle bisector theorem in a right triangle.
