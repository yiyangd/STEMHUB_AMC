2010 AMC 10B Problem 19

2010AMC 10BProblem 19几何圆与曲线圆曲线

Problem Statement

A circle with center $O$ has area $156\pi$. Triangle $ABC$ is equilateral, $BC$ is a chord of the circle, $OA=4\sqrt{3}$, and point $O$ is outside $\triangle ABC$. What is the side length of $\triangle ABC$?

Choices

    Answer

    B. 6

    Solution

    Step 1: Turn the area into a radius

    The circle has area $156\pi$, so its radius $R$ satisfies $\pi R^2=156\pi$. Thus $R^2=156$. We will use $R^2$ directly instead of simplifying the radical.

    Step 2: Let the triangle side be $s$

    Since $ABC$ is equilateral, the altitude from $A$ to $BC$ is $\frac{s\sqrt3}{2}$. The chord $BC$ has length $s$, so if the distance from $O$ to chord $BC$ is $d$, then the right triangle from the circle center to the midpoint of the chord gives \[\left(\frac{s}{2}\right)^2+d^2=156.\]

    Step 3: Use the position of $O$

    Point $O$ is outside the equilateral triangle, and $OA=4\sqrt3$. In this configuration the center lies beyond side $BC$ from vertex $A$, so the distance from $O$ to $BC$ is the triangle altitude plus $OA$: \[d=\frac{s\sqrt3}{2}+4\sqrt3.\]

    Step 4: Solve the equation

    Substitute this into the chord equation: \[\frac{s^2}{4}+\left(\frac{s\sqrt3}{2}+4\sqrt3\right)^2=156.\] This simplifies to $s^2+12s+48=156$, so $s^2+12s-108=0$.

    Step 5: Choose the positive root

    Factoring gives $(s-6)(s+18)=0$. A side length must be positive, so $s=6$. The answer is $\boxed{6}$.

    Key Idea

    Relate the chord length, circle radius, and equilateral-triangle height with the Pythagorean theorem.
