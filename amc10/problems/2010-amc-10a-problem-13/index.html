2010 AMC 10A Problem 13

2010AMC 10AProblem 13概率与统计平均数与数据集平均数数据

Problem Statement

Angelina drove at an average rate of $80$ km/h and then stopped $20$ minutes for gas. After the stop, she drove at an average rate of $100$ km/h. Altogether she drove $250$ km in a total trip time of $3$ hours including the stop. Which equation could be used to solve for the time $t$ in hours that she drove before her stop?

Choices

  1. A$80t+100(\frac83-t)=250$
  2. B$80t=250$
  3. C$100t=250$
  4. D$90t=250$
  5. E$80(\frac83-t)+100t=250$

Answer

A. 80t+100(\frac83-t)=250

Solution

Step 1: Remove the stop time

The total trip time was $3$ hours, but $20$ minutes, or $\frac13$ hour, was spent stopped. So the total driving time was $3-\frac13=\frac83$ hours.

Step 2: Use t for the first driving time

If Angelina drove for $t$ hours before stopping, then she drove for $\frac83-t$ hours after stopping.

Step 3: Write total distance

Distance equals rate times time, so the two driving distances are $80t$ and $100(\frac83-t)$. Their sum is $250$.

Step 4: Answer

The equation is $\boxed{80t+100(\frac83-t)=250}$.

Key Idea

Subtract the gas stop from the total time before writing the distance equation.
