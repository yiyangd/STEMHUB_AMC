2015 AMC 10A Problem 20

2015AMC 10AProblem 20几何面积与相似面积比例

Problem Statement

A rectangle with positive integer side lengths has area $A$ square centimeters and perimeter $P$ centimeters. Which of the following numbers cannot equal $A+P$?

Choices

  1. A100
  2. B102
  3. C104
  4. D106
  5. E108

Answer

B. 102

Solution

Step 1: Use the corrected integer-side version

Let the positive integer side lengths be $a$ and $b$. Then $A=ab$ and $P=2a+2b$.

Step 2: Factor A plus P

\[A+P=ab+2a+2b=(a+2)(b+2)-4.\] Thus $A+P+4$ must factor into two integers greater than $2$.

Step 3: Test the choices by adding 4

The choices become $104,106,108,110,112$ after adding $4$.

Step 4: Find the impossible one

$106=2\cdot53$ has no factorization into two integers both greater than $2$. The others do: for example, $104=4\cdot26$, $108=6\cdot18$, $110=5\cdot22$, and $112=4\cdot28$.

Step 5: Conclude

Therefore $A+P$ cannot be $\boxed{102}$.

Key Idea

Rewrite A+P as (a+2)(b+2)-4 for integer side lengths.

Notes

This page uses the corrected practice version with positive integer side lengths; the original wording was ambiguous.
