2023 AMC 10A Problem 23

2023AMC 10AProblem 23数论进制与数字数字进制

Problem Statement

Positive integer divisors $a$ and $b$ of $n$ are called complementary if $ab=n$. Given that $N$ has a pair of complementary divisors that differ by $20$ and a pair of complementary divisors that differ by $23$, find the sum of the digits of $N$.

Choices

  1. A11
  2. B13
  3. C15
  4. D17
  5. E19

Answer

C. 15

Solution

Step 1: Turn the divisor pairs into products

If one complementary pair differs by $20$, write it as $a$ and $a+20$. Then \[N=a(a+20).\] If another pair differs by $23$, write it as $b$ and $b+23$. Then \[N=b(b+23).\]

Step 2: Equate the products

We need \[a(a+20)=b(b+23).\] This is a quadratic Diophantine condition, so small positive values can be organized by looking near the square root of $N$.

Step 3: Find the compatible pairs

The compatible factor pairs are \[22\cdot42=924\] and \[21\cdot44=924.\] Their differences are $20$ and $23$, respectively.

Step 4: Check uniqueness for the contest answer

These give the same positive integer $N=924$, satisfying both required complementary-pair conditions.

Step 5: Sum the digits

The sum of the digits of $924$ is \[9+2+4=15.\]

Step 6: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{15}$.

Key Idea

Turn complementary divisor differences into product equations.
