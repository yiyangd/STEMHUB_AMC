Problem Statement

Let $S$ be a subset of $\{1,2,3,\ldots,2024\}$ such that if $x$ and $y$ are distinct elements of $S$, then $|x-y|>2$, and if $x$ and $y$ are distinct odd elements of $S$, then $|x-y|>6$. What is the maximum possible number of elements in $S$?

Choices

  1. A436
  2. B506
  3. C608
  4. D654
  5. E675

Answer

C. 608

Solution

Step 1: Find an upper bound in short blocks

In any block of $10$ consecutive integers, we claim at most $3$ elements can be chosen. Four chosen numbers would need to be at least $3$ apart, forcing a pattern like \[x,\ x+3,\ x+6,\ x+9.\]

Step 2: Use the odd-number restriction

In such a four-number pattern, either $x$ and $x+6$ are both odd, or $x+3$ and $x+9$ are both odd. That would give two selected odd numbers only $6$ apart, which is not allowed.

Step 3: Apply the block bound

The numbers $1$ through $2020$ form $202$ blocks of $10$, so they contribute at most \[202\cdot3=606\] selected elements.

Step 4: Handle the final four numbers

The remaining numbers are $2021,2022,2023,2024$. Because any two selected elements must differ by more than $2$, at most $2$ of these can be selected.

Step 5: Construct a set that reaches the bound

Choose numbers congruent to $1$, $4$, or $8$ modulo $10$, from $1$ through $2024$. This gives $608$ numbers, and the spacing conditions are satisfied.

Step 6: Conclude

The maximum possible size is $\boxed{608}$.

Key Idea

Use a block upper bound and construct a matching pattern.
