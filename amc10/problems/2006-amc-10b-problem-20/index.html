2006 AMC 10B Problem 20

2006AMC 10BProblem 20几何面积与相似面积比例

Problem Statement

In rectangle $ABCD$, we have $A=(6,-22)$, $B=(2006,178)$, and $D=(8,y)$ for some integer $y$. What is the area of rectangle $ABCD$?

Choices

  1. A$4000$
  2. B$4040$
  3. C$4400$
  4. D$40,000$
  5. E$40,400$

Answer

E. 40,400

Solution

Step 1: Use rectangle geometry

In rectangle $ABCD$, the sides $AB$ and $AD$ meet at a right angle. So the vectors $\overrightarrow{AB}$ and $\overrightarrow{AD}$ must be perpendicular.

Step 2: Write the two vectors

From $A=(6,-22)$ to $B=(2006,178)$, \ \[\overrightarrow{AB}=(2000,200).\] From $A=(6,-22)$ to $D=(8,y)$, \ \[\overrightarrow{AD}=(2,y+22).\]

Step 3: Use the dot product

Perpendicular vectors have dot product $0$, so \ \[(2000)(2)+200(y+22)=0.\] This gives $4000+200y+4400=0$, hence $200y=-8400$ and $y=-42$.

Step 4: Find side lengths

Now $AD=(2,-20)$, so $AD=\sqrt{2^2+(-20)^2}=\sqrt{404}$. Also $AB=\sqrt{2000^2+200^2}=\sqrt{4,040,000}=100\sqrt{404}$.

Step 5: Compute area

The area of the rectangle is \ \[(\sqrt{404})(100\sqrt{404})=100\cdot404=40,400.\]

Step 6: Answer

The answer is $\boxed{40,400}$.

Key Idea

Use perpendicular side vectors of a rectangle and the dot product to find the missing coordinate and area.
