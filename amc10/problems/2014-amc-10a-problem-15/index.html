Problem Statement

David drives from his home to the airport to catch a flight. He drives $35$ miles in the first hour, but realizes that he will be $1$ hour late if he continues at this speed. He increases his speed by $15$ miles per hour for the rest of the way to the airport and arrives $30$ minutes early. How many miles is the airport from his home?

Choices

  1. A140
  2. B175
  3. C210
  4. D245
  5. E280

Answer

C. 210

Solution

Step 1: Compare the same remaining distance

After the first hour, David has traveled $35$ miles. Let the total distance be $D$, so the remaining distance is $D-35$.

Step 2: Translate the time information

If he stays at $35$ mph, he is $1$ hour late. If he drives the rest at $50$ mph, he is $30$ minutes early. The difference between those remaining travel times is therefore $1.5$ hours.

Step 3: Set up the equation

\[\frac{D-35}{35}-\frac{D-35}{50}=1.5.\] This compares only the part of the trip after the first hour.

Step 4: Solve

The left side is $(D-35)\left(\frac1{35}-\frac1{50}\right)=(D-35)\frac{15}{1750}$. Therefore $D-35=175$.

Step 5: Conclude

So $D=210$, and the answer is $\boxed{210}$.

Key Idea

Compare the two remaining-travel times after the first hour.
