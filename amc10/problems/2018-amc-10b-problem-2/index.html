2018 AMC 10B Problem 2

2018AMC 10BProblem 2概率与统计平均数与数据集平均数数据

Problem Statement

Sam drove $96$ miles in $90$ minutes. His average speed during the first $30$ minutes was $60$ mph, and his average speed during the second $30$ minutes was $65$ mph. What was his average speed, in mph, during the last $30$ minutes?

Choices

  1. A64
  2. B65
  3. C66
  4. D67
  5. E68

Answer

D. 67

Solution

Step 1: Convert each 30 minutes to hours

Each $30$-minute part is $\frac12$ hour.

Step 2: Find distances in the first two parts

In the first half hour, Sam drove $60\cdot\frac12=30$ miles. In the second half hour, he drove $65\cdot\frac12=32.5$ miles.

Step 3: Find the remaining distance

He drove $96$ miles total, so the last half hour covered \[96-30-32.5=33.5\] miles.

Step 4: Compute speed

Speed is distance divided by time, so the last speed was \[\frac{33.5}{0.5}=67\text{ mph}.\]

Step 5: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{67}$.

Key Idea

Use distance equals rate times time for each half-hour segment.
