2017 AMC 10B Problem 24

2017AMC 10BProblem 24几何面积与相似面积比例

Problem Statement

The vertices of an equilateral triangle lie on the hyperbola $xy=1$, and a vertex of this hyperbola is the centroid of the triangle. What is the square of the area of the triangle?

Choices

  1. A48
  2. B60
  3. C108
  4. D120
  5. E169

Answer

C. 108

Solution

Step 1: Center at the hyperbola vertex

The vertex of the branch $xy=1$ in the first quadrant is $(1,1)$. Let this be the centroid of the equilateral triangle.

Step 2: Describe one vertex by a vector

Write one triangle vertex as $(1+u,1+v)$. Because it lies on $xy=1$, \[(1+u)(1+v)=1,\] or \[u+v+uv=0.\]

Step 3: Rotate for the other vertices

The other two vertices are obtained by rotating vector $(u,v)$ by $120^\circ$ and $240^\circ$ about the centroid. Requiring those two rotated points to also satisfy $xy=1$ gives the same type of equation for the two rotated vectors.

Step 4: Solve the system

The nondegenerate solutions give \[u^2+v^2=8.\] This is the squared distance from the centroid to each vertex.

Step 5: Convert radius to area

If the distance from the centroid to a vertex is $r$, then an equilateral triangle has side length $\sqrt3\,r$ and area \[\frac{\sqrt3}{4}(\sqrt3 r)^2=\frac{3\sqrt3}{4}r^2.\] With $r^2=8$, the area is $6\sqrt3$.

Step 6: Square the area

The square of the area is \[(6\sqrt3)^2=108.\]

Step 7: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{108}$.

Key Idea

Center the triangle at the hyperbola vertex and rotate coordinates by 120 degrees.
