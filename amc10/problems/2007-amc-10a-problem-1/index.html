Problem Statement

One ticket to a show costs $\$20$ at full price. Susan buys $4$ tickets using a coupon that gives her a $25\%$ discount. Pam buys $5$ tickets using a coupon that gives her a $30\%$ discount. How many more dollars does Pam pay than Susan?

Choices

  1. A$2$
  2. B$5$
  3. C$10$
  4. D$15$
  5. E$20$

Answer

C. 10

Solution

Step 1: Find Susan's cost

A $25\%$ discount means Susan pays $75\%$ of full price. Four tickets at $\$20$ each would cost $\$80$, so Susan pays $0.75\cdot80=60$ dollars.

Step 2: Find Pam's cost

A $30\%$ discount means Pam pays $70\%$ of full price. Five tickets at $\$20$ each would cost $\$100$, so Pam pays $0.70\cdot100=70$ dollars.

Step 3: Compare

Pam pays $70-60=10$ more dollars than Susan.

Step 4: Check the scale

Pam buys one more ticket, but also has a larger discount. A difference of $10$ dollars is reasonable.

Step 5: Answer

The answer is $\boxed{10}$.

Key Idea

Compute each discounted ticket total, then compare.
