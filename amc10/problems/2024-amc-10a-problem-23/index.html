2024AMC 10AProblem 23数论整数构造整数构造

Problem Statement

Integers $a$, $b$, and $c$ satisfy \[ab+c=100,\qquad bc+a=87,\qquad ca+b=60.\] What is $ab+bc+ca$?

Choices

  1. A212
  2. B247
  3. C258
  4. D276
  5. E284

Answer

D. 276

Solution

Step 1: Subtract two equations to reveal a factor

Subtract the second equation from the first: \[(ab+c)-(bc+a)=100-87.\] This simplifies to \[(a-c)(b-1)=13.\]

Step 2: Use the prime factor 13

Since $13$ is prime, the factor pairs for \((a-c)(b-1)\) are limited. In particular, $a-c$ must be one of \(\pm1,\pm13\).

Step 3: Test the compatible case

The case \(a-c=-1\) gives \(b-1=-13\), so \(b=-12\) and \(c=a+1\). Substituting into \(ab+c=100\) gives \[-12a+(a+1)=100,\] so \[-11a=99\] and \(a=-9\). Thus \(c=-8\).

Step 4: Check the equations

With \[(a,b,c)=(-9,-12,-8),\] the three equations are satisfied: \[ab+c=108-8=100,\quad bc+a=96-9=87,\quad ca+b=72-12=60.\]

Step 5: Compute the requested sum

Therefore \[ab+bc+ca=108+96+72=276.\]

Step 6: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{276}$.

Key Idea

Subtract equations to create factored integer constraints.
