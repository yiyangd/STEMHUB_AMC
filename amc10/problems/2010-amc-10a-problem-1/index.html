2010 AMC 10A Problem 1

2010AMC 10AProblem 1概率与统计平均数与数据集平均数数据

Problem Statement

Mary's top book shelf holds five books with the following widths, in centimeters: $6$, $\frac12$, $1$, $2.5$, and $10$. What is the average book width, in centimeters?

Choices

  1. A$1$
  2. B$2$
  3. C$3$
  4. D$4$
  5. E$5$

Answer

D. 4

Solution

Step 1: Identify the data

The five widths are $6$, $\frac12$, $1$, $2.5$, and $10$ centimeters.

Step 2: Add the widths

Their sum is $6+0.5+1+2.5+10=20$.

Step 3: Divide by the number of books

There are $5$ books, so the average width is $20\div5=4$.

Step 4: Answer

The average width is $\boxed{4}$ centimeters.

Key Idea

Add the five widths and divide by 5.
