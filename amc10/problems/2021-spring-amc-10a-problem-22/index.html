2021 Spring AMC 10A Problem 22

2021 SpringAMC 10AProblem 22概率与统计平均数与数据集平均数数据

Problem Statement

Hiram's algebra notes are $50$ pages long and are printed on $25$ sheets of paper. His roommate takes a consecutive set of sheets from the middle of the notes. The average of the page numbers on all remaining sheets is exactly $19$. How many sheets were borrowed?

Choices

  1. A10
  2. B13
  3. C15
  4. D17
  5. E20

Answer

B. 13

Solution

Step 1: Start with the total page sum

The sum of pages $1$ through $50$ is \[\frac{50\cdot51}{2}=1275.\]

Step 2: Let h be the number of sheets removed

If $h$ sheets are removed, then $2h$ pages are removed and $50-2h$ pages remain. Since the remaining average is $19$, the remaining sum is \[19(50-2h).\]

Step 3: Find the removed sum

The removed pages therefore have sum \[1275-19(50-2h)=325+38h.\]

Step 4: Use consecutive pages from consecutive sheets

If the first removed page is odd and equal to $p$, then the removed pages are $p,p+1,\ldots,p+2h-1$. Their sum is \[h(2p+2h-1).\] So \[h(2p+2h-1)=325+38h.\]

Step 5: Test divisors of 325

Rearranging gives \[h(2p+2h-39)=325.\] The possible sheet counts from the answer choices are tested through divisors of $325$. The value $h=13$ gives $2p+26-39=25$, so $p=19$, a valid first page.

Step 6: Conclude

The roommate borrowed $\boxed{13}$ sheets.

Key Idea

Use page-number sums and consecutive sheets.
