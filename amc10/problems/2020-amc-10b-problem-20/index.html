2020 AMC 10B Problem 20

2020AMC 10BProblem 20代数三角恒等式三角函数恒等式

Problem Statement

A box has dimensions $1\times3\times4$. For a real number $r>0$, let $S(r)$ be the set of points that are within distance $r$ of the box. The volume of $S(r)$ can be written as $ar^3+br^2+cr+d$, where $a,b,c,d$ are constants. What is $\frac{bc}{ad}$?

Choices

  1. A16
  2. B19
  3. C22
  4. D24
  5. E26

Answer

B. 19

Solution

Step 1: Interpret the volume expansion

The set $S(r)$ is the original box thickened by distance $r$ in every direction. Its volume can be decomposed into the box, face prisms, edge quarter-cylinders, and corner octants.

Step 2: Find d and c

The constant term is the box volume, so \[d=1\cdot3\cdot4=12.\] The coefficient of $r$ is the surface area of the box: \[c=2(1\cdot3+1\cdot4+3\cdot4)=38.\]

Step 3: Find b from the edges

The total edge length is \[4(1+3+4)=32.\] Around each edge is a quarter-cylinder, so \[b=\frac{\pi}{4}\cdot32=8\pi.\]

Step 4: Find a from the corners

The $8$ rounded corners combine to one full sphere of radius $r$, so \[a=\frac{4\pi}{3}.\]

Step 5: Compute the requested ratio

Now \[\frac{bc}{ad}=\frac{(8\pi)(38)}{\left(\frac{4\pi}{3}\right)(12)}=\frac{304\pi}{16\pi}=19.\]

Step 6: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{19}$.

Key Idea

Use the volume formula for a rounded rectangular box.
