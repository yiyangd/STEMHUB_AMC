2008 AMC 10B Problem 20

2008AMC 10BProblem 20概率与统计组合概率概率计数

Problem Statement

The faces of a cubical die are marked with the numbers $1,2,2,3,3,$ and $4$. The faces of a second cubical die are marked with the numbers $1,3,4,5,6,$ and $8$. Both dice are thrown. What is the probability that the sum of the two top numbers will be $5$, $7$, or $9$?

Choices

  1. A$\frac{5}{18}$
  2. B$\frac{7}{18}$
  3. C$\frac{11}{18}$
  4. D$\frac{3}{4}$
  5. E$\frac{8}{9}$

Answer

B. \frac{7}{18}

Solution

Step 1: Treat repeated labels as separate faces

Each die has $6$ faces, so there are $36$ equally likely ordered outcomes. The repeated numbers on the first die count as separate faces.

Step 2: Count outcomes from the first die face 1

If the first die shows $1$, the second die must show $4$, $6$, or $8$, giving $3$ favorable outcomes.

Step 3: Count outcomes from the two faces labeled 2 and 3

For each face labeled $2$, the second die can show $3$ or $5$, giving $2\cdot2=4$ outcomes. For each face labeled $3$, the second die can show $4$ or $6$, giving another $4$ outcomes.

Step 4: Count outcomes from the first die face 4

If the first die shows $4$, the second die can show $1$, $3$, or $5$, giving $3$ outcomes.

Step 5: Compute the probability

There are $3+4+4+3=14$ favorable outcomes out of $36$, so the probability is $\frac{14}{36}=\frac{7}{18}$.

Step 6: Answer

The probability is $\boxed{\frac{7}{18}}$.

Key Idea

Count favorable ordered die-face pairs with repeated labels treated as distinct faces.
