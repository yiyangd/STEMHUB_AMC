2004 AMC 10B Problem 11

2004AMC 10BProblem 11概率与统计组合概率概率计数

Problem Statement

Two eight-sided dice each have faces numbered $1$ through $8$. When the dice are rolled, each face has an equal probability of appearing on the top. What is the probability that the product of the two top numbers is greater than their sum?

Choices

  1. A$\frac12$
  2. B$\frac{47}{64}$
  3. C$\frac34$
  4. D$\frac{55}{64}$
  5. E$\frac78$

Answer

C. \frac{3}{4}

Solution

Step 1: Use the complement

There are $8\cdot8=64$ ordered outcomes. It is easier to count when the product is not greater than the sum.

Step 2: Rewrite the inequality

We want $ab>a+b$. This is equivalent to $(a-1)(b-1)>1$.

Step 3: Count the bad outcomes

If either die shows $1$, then the product is not greater than the sum; this gives $8+8-1=15$ outcomes. Among outcomes with both dice at least $2$, only $(2,2)$ fails, since $(2-1)(2-1)=1$.

Step 4: Find the favorable count

There are $16$ bad outcomes, so $64-16=48$ favorable outcomes.

Step 5: Answer

The probability is $\frac{48}{64}=\boxed{\frac34}$.

Key Idea

Count the complement of ordered die rolls whose product is not greater than their sum.
