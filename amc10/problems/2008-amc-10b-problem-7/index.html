2008 AMC 10B Problem 7

2008AMC 10BProblem 7几何三角形三角形角度

Problem Statement

An equilateral triangle of side length $10$ is completely filled in by non-overlapping equilateral triangles of side length $1$. How many small triangles are required?

Choices

  1. A$10$
  2. B$25$
  3. C$100$
  4. D$250$
  5. E$1000$

Answer

C. 100

Solution

Step 1: Use similarity instead of drawing every triangle

All the small triangles are equilateral and have side length $1$, while the large triangle has side length $10$. The triangles are similar.

Step 2: Convert side scale to area scale

Area scales by the square of the side-length scale. Since the side-length ratio is $10:1$, the area ratio is $10^2:1^2=100:1$.

Step 3: Interpret the area ratio

Because the large triangle is completely filled without overlap, the number of small triangles equals the ratio of the large area to one small area.

Step 4: Answer

The required number of small triangles is $\boxed{100}$.

Key Idea

Use area scaling for similar equilateral triangles.
