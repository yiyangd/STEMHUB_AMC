2021 Spring AMC 10A Problem 6

2021 SpringAMC 10AProblem 6概率与统计平均数与数据集平均数数据

Problem Statement

Chantal and Jean start hiking from a trailhead toward a fire tower. Jean is wearing a heavy backpack and walks slower. Chantal starts walking at $4$ miles per hour. Halfway to the tower, the trail becomes really steep, and Chantal slows down to $2$ miles per hour. After reaching the tower, she immediately turns around and descends the steep part of the trail at $3$ miles per hour. She meets Jean at the halfway point. What was Jean's average speed, in miles per hour, until they meet?

Choices

  1. A$\frac{12}{13}$
  2. B1
  3. C$\frac{13}{12}$
  4. D$\frac{24}{13}$
  5. E2

Answer

A. \frac{12}{13}

Solution

Step 1: Choose a convenient distance

Because only ratios of speed matter, choose the halfway distance to be $12$ miles. Then the full trip to the tower is $24$ miles.

Step 2: Compute Chantal's time to the halfway point

Chantal walks the first $12$ miles at $4$ miles per hour, taking \[\frac{12}{4}=3\] hours.

Step 3: Compute the steep uphill and downhill time

She then walks $12$ miles uphill at $2$ mph, taking $6$ hours, and comes back down $12$ miles at $3$ mph, taking $4$ hours.

Step 4: Find Jean's distance and time

When they meet at the halfway point, Jean has walked $12$ miles. The total time is \[3+6+4=13\] hours.

Step 5: Compute Jean's average speed

Jean's average speed is \[\frac{12}{13}\] miles per hour.

Step 6: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{\frac{12}{13}}$.

Key Idea

Choose a convenient distance and compare time traveled.
