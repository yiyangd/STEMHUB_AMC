2006 AMC 10A Problem 19

2006AMC 10AProblem 19几何面积与相似面积比例

Problem Statement

How many non-similar triangle have angles whose degree measures are distinct positive inte- gers in arithmetic progression?

Choices

  1. A0
  2. B1
  3. C59
  4. D89
  5. E178

Answer

C. 59

Solution

Step 1: Use the arithmetic progression structure

If three angles are in arithmetic progression, write them as $60-d$, $60$, and $60+d$, because their sum must be $180$.

Step 2: Apply positivity

The smallest angle must be positive, so $60-d>0$. Thus $d<60$.

Step 3: Apply distinctness

The angles are distinct, so $d$ must be a positive integer.

Step 4: Count d values

Therefore $d$ can be $1,2,\ldots,59$, giving $59$ different angle triples.

Step 5: Answer

Each different angle triple gives a non-similar triangle, so the answer is $\boxed{59}$.

Key Idea

An arithmetic progression of triangle angles must have middle angle 60 degrees.
