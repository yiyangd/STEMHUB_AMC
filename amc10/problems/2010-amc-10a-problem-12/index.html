2010 AMC 10A Problem 12

2010AMC 10AProblem 12几何空间几何空间几何体积

Problem Statement

Logan is constructing a scaled model of his town. The city's water tower stands $40$ meters high, and the top portion is a sphere that holds $100{,}000$ liters of water. Logan's miniature water tower holds $0.1$ liters. How tall, in meters, should Logan make his tower?

Choices

  1. A$0.04$
  2. B$\frac{0.4}{\pi}$
  3. C$0.4$
  4. D$\frac4\pi$
  5. E$4$

Answer

C. 0.4

Solution

Step 1: Compare volumes

The real tower holds $100{,}000$ liters, while the model holds $0.1$ liters. The volume scale factor is $\frac{0.1}{100000}=10^{-6}$.

Step 2: Convert volume scale to length scale

For similar solids, volume scale is the cube of length scale. Thus the length scale is $\sqrt[3]{10^{-6}}=10^{-2}=\frac1{100}$.

Step 3: Scale the height

The real tower is $40$ meters high, so the model should be $40\cdot\frac1{100}=0.4$ meters high.

Step 4: Answer

Logan should make the tower $\boxed{0.4}$ meters tall.

Key Idea

Volume scale is the cube of length scale.
