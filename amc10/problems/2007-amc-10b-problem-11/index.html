2007 AMC 10B Problem 11

2007AMC 10BProblem 11几何圆与曲线圆曲线

Problem Statement

A circle passes through the three vertices of an isosceles triangle that has two sides of length $3$ and a base of length $2$. What is the area of this circle?

Choices

  1. A$2\pi$
  2. B$\frac{5\pi}{2}$
  3. C$\frac{81\pi}{32}$
  4. D$3\pi$
  5. E$\frac{7\pi}{2}$

Answer

C. \frac{81\pi}{32}

Solution

Step 1: Find the triangle's height

The isosceles triangle has equal sides $3$ and base $2$. Dropping an altitude to the base splits the base into two segments of length $1$. The height is $\sqrt{3^2-1^2}=\sqrt8=2\sqrt2$.

Step 2: Find the triangle area

The area is $K=\frac12\cdot2\cdot2\sqrt2=2\sqrt2$.

Step 3: Use the circumradius formula

For a triangle with side lengths $a,b,c$ and area $K$, the circumradius is $R=\frac{abc}{4K}$. Here $a,b,c=3,3,2$, so $R=\frac{18}{4(2\sqrt2)}=\frac{9}{4\sqrt2}$.

Step 4: Compute the circle area

Thus $R^2=\frac{81}{32}$, and the circle's area is $\pi R^2=\frac{81\pi}{32}$.

Step 5: Answer

The answer is $\boxed{\frac{81\pi}{32}}$.

Key Idea

Find the circumradius of the isosceles triangle using area and side lengths.
