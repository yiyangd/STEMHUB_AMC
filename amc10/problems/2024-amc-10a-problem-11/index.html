2024AMC 10AProblem 11数论整数构造整数构造

Problem Statement

How many ordered pairs of integers $(m,n)$ satisfy \[\sqrt{n^2-49}=m?\]

Choices

  1. A1
  2. B2
  3. C3
  4. D4
  5. EInfinitely many

Answer

D. 4

Solution

Step 1: Use the nonnegative square root

The left side \(\sqrt{n^2-49}\) is always nonnegative, so $m$ must be a nonnegative integer.

Step 2: Square both sides

Squaring gives \[n^2-49=m^2,\] so \[n^2-m^2=49.\]

Step 3: Factor the difference of squares

We get \[(n-m)(n+m)=49.\] Since $49$ is positive, the two factors have the same sign.

Step 4: List the useful factor pairs

The ordered factor pairs of $49$ are \[(1,49),(7,7),(49,1),(-1,-49),(-7,-7),(-49,-1).\] For each pair, solve \[n-m=a,\quad n+m=b.\]

Step 5: Keep only m nonnegative

The pairs that give $m\ge0$ are $(1,49)$, $(7,7)$, $(-7,-7)$, and $(-49,-1)$. These produce four ordered integer pairs $(m,n)$.

Step 6: Conclude

There are $\boxed{4}$ solutions.

Key Idea

Turn the square-root equation into a difference of squares.
