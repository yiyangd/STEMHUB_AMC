2022 AMC 10A Problem 13

2022AMC 10AProblem 13几何面积与相似面积比例

Problem Statement

Let $\triangle ABC$ be a scalene triangle. Point $P$ lies on $\overline{BC}$ so that $\overline{AP}$ bisects $\angle BAC$. The line through $B$ perpendicular to $\overline{AP}$ intersects the line through $A$ parallel to $\overline{BC}$ at point $D$. Suppose $BP=2$ and $PC=3$. What is $AD$?

Choices

  1. A8
  2. B9
  3. C10
  4. D11
  5. E12

Answer

C. 10

Solution

Step 1: Add helpful intersection points

Let the perpendicular line through $B$ meet $\overline{AP}$ at $X$ and meet $\overline{AC}$ at $Y$. This turns the configuration into right triangles and similar triangles.

Step 2: Use the angle bisector

Because $\overline{AP}$ bisects $\angle BAC$, the right triangles $\triangle ABX$ and $\triangle AYX$ have two equal angles and the shared side $AX$. Therefore they are congruent, so $AB=AY$.

Step 3: Apply the angle-bisector theorem

The angle-bisector theorem gives \[\frac{AB}{AC}=\frac{BP}{PC}=\frac23.\] Write $AB=AY=2x$ and $AC=3x$. Then \[YC=AC-AY=3x-2x=x.\]

Step 4: Use the parallel line

Since $AD\parallel BC$, triangles $\triangle ADY$ and $\triangle CBY$ are similar. Their scale factor is \[\frac{AY}{CY}=\frac{2x}{x}=2.\]

Step 5: Finish with the known base

Thus $AD=2\cdot CB$. Since $CB=BP+PC=2+3=5$, we get \[AD=2\cdot5=10.\]

Step 6: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{10}$.

Key Idea

Use angle-bisector ratio plus similar triangles from parallel lines.
