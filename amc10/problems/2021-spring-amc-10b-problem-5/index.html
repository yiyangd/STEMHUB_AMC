2021 Spring AMC 10B Problem 5

2021 SpringAMC 10BProblem 5数论进制与数字数字进制

Problem Statement

The ages of Jonie's four cousins are distinct single-digit positive integers. Two of the cousins' ages multiplied together give $24$, while the other two multiply to $30$. What is the sum of the ages of Jonie's four cousins?

Choices

  1. A21
  2. B22
  3. C23
  4. D24
  5. E25

Answer

B. 22

Solution

Step 1: List factor pairs carefully

The distinct single-digit positive factors of $24$ could be paired as $(3,8)$ or $(4,6)$, while $30$ could be $(5,6)$ or $(3,10)$, but $10$ is not single digit.

Step 2: Respect distinct ages

The pair for $30$ must be $(5,6)$. Then the pair for $24$ cannot use $6$, so it must be $(3,8)$.

Step 3: Collect the ages

The four ages are \[3,5,6,8.\] They are distinct and satisfy the two product conditions.

Step 4: Add

The sum is \[3+5+6+8=22.\]

Step 5: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{22}$.

Key Idea

Factor the products into distinct one-digit ages.
