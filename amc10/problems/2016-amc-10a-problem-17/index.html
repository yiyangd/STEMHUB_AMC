2016 AMC 10A Problem 17

2016AMC 10AProblem 17概率与统计平均数与数据集平均数数据

Problem Statement

Let $N$ be a positive multiple of $5$. One red ball and $N$ green balls are arranged in a line in random order. Let $P(N)$ be the probability that at least $\frac35$ of the green balls are on the same side of the red ball. Observe that $P(5)=1$ and that $P(N)$ approaches $\frac45$ as $N$ grows large. What is the sum of the digits of the least value of $N$ such that $P(N)<\frac{321}{400}$?

Choices

  1. A12
  2. B14
  3. C16
  4. D18
  5. E20

Answer

A. 12

Solution

Step 1: Focus on the red ball's position

Once the red ball is placed, suppose there are $k$ green balls on its left. Then there are $N-k$ green balls on its right. Each value $k=0,1,\ldots,N$ is equally likely.

Step 2: Translate the condition

At least $\frac35$ of the green balls are on the same side of the red ball exactly when \[k\ge \frac{3N}{5}\quad\text{or}\quad N-k\ge \frac{3N}{5}.\] The second inequality is $k\le\frac{2N}{5}$.

Step 3: Count favorable positions

Because $N$ is a multiple of $5$, the values $0$ through $\frac{2N}{5}$ give $\frac{2N}{5}+1$ possibilities, and the values $\frac{3N}{5}$ through $N$ give another $\frac{2N}{5}+1$ possibilities. Thus \[P(N)=\frac{\frac{4N}{5}+2}{N+1}.\]

Step 4: Solve the inequality

We need \[\frac{\frac{4N}{5}+2}{N+1}<\frac{321}{400}.\] Multiplying through gives $320N+800<321N+321$, so $N>479$.

Step 5: Use the multiple-of-5 condition

The least positive multiple of $5$ greater than $479$ is $480$. The sum of its digits is $4+8+0=12$.

Step 6: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{12}$.

Key Idea

Count possible positions of the red ball that leave enough green balls on one side.
