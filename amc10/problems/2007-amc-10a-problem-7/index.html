2007AMC 10AProblem 7代数三角恒等式三角函数恒等式

Problem Statement

Last year Mr. John Q. Public received an inheritance. He paid $20\%$ in federal taxes on the inheritance, and paid $10\%$ of what he had left in state taxes. He paid a total of $\$10,500$ for both taxes. How many dollars was the inheritance?

Choices

  1. A$30,000
  2. B$32,500
  3. C$35,000
  4. D$37,500
  5. E$40,000

Answer

D. 37,500

Solution

Step 1: Let the inheritance be $x$

The federal tax is $20\%$ of $x$, or $0.20x$. After that tax, he has $0.80x$ left.

Step 2: Compute the state tax

The state tax is $10\%$ of what remains, so it is $0.10\cdot0.80x=0.08x$.

Step 3: Use the total tax

The total tax is $0.20x+0.08x=0.28x$. We are told this equals $10,500$.

Step 4: Solve

Thus $0.28x=10500$, so $x=10500/0.28=37500$.

Step 5: Answer

The inheritance was $\boxed{37,500}$ dollars.

Key Idea

Model sequential percentage taxes as fractions of the original inheritance.
