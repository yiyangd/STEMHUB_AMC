2014 AMC 10A Problem 2

2014AMC 10AProblem 2代数运算与化简代数化简

Problem Statement

Roy's cat eats $\frac13$ of a can of cat food every morning and $\frac14$ of a can every evening. Before feeding his cat on Monday morning, Roy opened a box containing $6$ cans of cat food. On what day of the week did the cat finish eating all the cat food in the box?

Choices

  1. ATuesday
  2. BWednesday
  3. CThursday
  4. DFriday
  5. ESaturday

Answer

C. Thursday

Solution

Step 1: Combine one full day of food

Each day the cat eats $\frac13+\frac14=\frac7{12}$ of a can. Thinking in twelfths makes the arithmetic cleaner.

Step 2: Convert the supply

Six cans is $\frac{72}{12}$ of a can. After $10$ full days, the cat has eaten $10\cdot\frac7{12}=\frac{70}{12}$ cans, so there is still $\frac{2}{12}$ can left.

Step 3: Locate the day

Starting on Monday, after $10$ full days we have reached Wednesday evening of the next week. The next feeding is Thursday morning.

Step 4: Finish on the partial day

On Thursday morning the cat wants $\frac13=\frac4{12}$ of a can, but only $\frac2{12}$ remains. Therefore the cat finishes the box during that Thursday morning feeding.

Step 5: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{\text{Thursday}}$.

Key Idea

Convert the morning and evening amounts to one daily total, then track the final partial day.
