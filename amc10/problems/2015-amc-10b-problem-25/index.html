2015 AMC 10B Problem 25

2015AMC 10BProblem 25几何面积与相似面积比例

Problem Statement

A rectangular box measures $a\times b\times c$, where $a$, $b$, and $c$ are integers and $1\le a\le b\le c$. The volume and surface area of the box are numerically equal. How many ordered triples $(a,b,c)$ are possible?

Choices

  1. A4
  2. B10
  3. C12
  4. D21
  5. E26

Answer

B. 10

Solution

Step 1: Write the equation

The volume is $abc$, and the surface area is $2(ab+ac+bc)$. The condition is \[abc=2(ab+ac+bc).\]

Step 2: Solve for c

Rearrange as \[c(ab-2a-2b)=2ab,\] so \[c=\frac{2ab}{ab-2a-2b}.\] This gives a finite check because the denominator must be positive.

Step 3: Bound a and b

Since $a\le b\le c$, testing possible small $a$ values is enough. The denominator becomes positive only after $a$ and $b$ are large enough, but if $a\ge7$ the fraction is already too small to keep $c\ge b$.

Step 4: List the valid triples

The valid triples are $(3,7,42),(3,8,24),(3,9,18),(3,10,15),(3,12,12),(4,5,20),(4,6,12),(4,8,8),(5,5,10),(6,6,6)$.

Step 5: Count

There are $10$ triples.

Step 6: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{10}$.

Key Idea

Transform the volume-surface equation and enumerate bounded integer cases.
