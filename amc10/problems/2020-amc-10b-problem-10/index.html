2020 AMC 10B Problem 10

2020AMC 10BProblem 10几何空间几何空间几何体积

Problem Statement

A three-quarter sector of a circle of radius $4$ inches together with its interior can be rolled up to form the lateral surface area of a right circular cone by taping together along the two radii. What is the volume of the cone in cubic inches?

Choices

  1. A$3\pi\sqrt5$
  2. B$4\pi\sqrt3$
  3. C$3\pi\sqrt7$
  4. D$6\pi\sqrt3$
  5. E$6\pi\sqrt7$

Answer

C. 3\pi\sqrt7

Solution

Step 1: Use the sector arc length

The sector has radius $4$, so the cone's slant height will be $4$. The arc length of a three-quarter circle of radius $4$ is \[\frac34\cdot2\pi\cdot4=6\pi.\]

Step 2: Find the cone radius

When rolled into a cone, that arc becomes the base circumference. Thus \[2\pi r=6\pi,\] so $r=3$.

Step 3: Find the cone height

The slant height, radius, and height form a right triangle: \[h^2+3^2=4^2.\] Therefore \[h=\sqrt7.\]

Step 4: Compute volume

The cone volume is \[\frac13\pi r^2h=\frac13\pi\cdot9\cdot\sqrt7=3\pi\sqrt7.\]

Step 5: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{3\pi\sqrt7}$.

Key Idea

Use arc length as the cone base circumference.
