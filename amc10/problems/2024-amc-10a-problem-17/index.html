2024 AMC 10A Problem 17

2024AMC 10AProblem 17概率与统计独立性独立事件概率

Problem Statement

Two teams are in a best-two-out-of-three playoff. Team A has a $\frac23$ chance of winning at home, and its probability of winning away from home is $p$. The first game is played on Team A's home field, and the remaining games are played on Team B's home field. Outcomes are independent. The probability that Team A wins the playoff is $\frac12$. Then $p$ can be written in the form $\frac12(m-\sqrt n)$, where $m$ and $n$ are positive integers. What is $m+n$?

Choices

  1. A10
  2. B11
  3. C12
  4. D13
  5. E14

Answer

E. 14

Solution

Step 1: List the ways Team A can win

Team A wins the playoff either by winning Game 1 and at least one of the next two games, or by losing Game 1 and winning both away games.

Step 2: Write the probability

Team A wins Game 1 with probability $\frac23$. Away games are won with probability $p$. Thus \[P(A\text{ wins})=\frac23\bigl(1-(1-p)^2\bigr)+\frac13p^2.\]

Step 3: Simplify

Since \(1-(1-p)^2=2p-p^2\), the probability becomes \[\frac23(2p-p^2)+\frac13p^2=\frac{4p-p^2}{3}.\]

Step 4: Use the given probability

We are told this equals $\frac12$, so \[\frac{4p-p^2}{3}=\frac12.\] This gives \[2p^2-8p+3=0.\]

Step 5: Solve for p

Using the quadratic formula, \[p=\frac{8\pm\sqrt{64-24}}{4}=2\pm\frac{\sqrt{10}}{2}.\] Only the value between $0$ and $1$ is \[p=\frac12(4-\sqrt{10}).\]

Step 6: Conclude

Thus $m=4$ and $n=10$, so \[m+n=14.\] The answer is $\boxed{14}$.

Key Idea

Write the playoff win probability and solve a quadratic.
