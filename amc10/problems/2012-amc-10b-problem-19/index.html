2012 AMC 10B Problem 19

2012AMC 10BProblem 19几何面积与相似面积比例

Problem Statement

In rectangle $ABCD$, $AB=6$, $AD=30$, and $G$ is the midpoint of $AD$. Segment $AB$ is extended $2$ units beyond $B$ to point $E$, and $F$ is the intersection of $ED$ and $BC$. What is the area of $BFDG$?

Choices

  1. A133
  2. B\frac{67}{2}
  3. C\frac{135}{2}
  4. D68
  5. E\frac{137}{2}

Answer

C. \frac{135}{2}

Solution

Step 1: Place coordinates

Let $A=(0,0)$, $B=(6,0)$, $D=(0,30)$, and $C=(6,30)$. Then $E=(8,0)$ and $G=(0,15)$.

Step 2: Find F

Line $ED$ goes from $(8,0)$ to $(0,30)$. Its slope is $-15/4$, so at $x=6$ its height is $y=\frac{15}{2}$. Thus $F=(6,\frac{15}{2})$.

Step 3: See the trapezoid

Quadrilateral $BFDG$ has parallel vertical sides $BF=\frac{15}{2}$ and $DG=15$, separated by distance $6$.

Step 4: Compute area

The area is $\frac12(\frac{15}{2}+15)\cdot6=\frac{135}{2}$.

Key Idea

Use coordinates to find F and compute the trapezoid area.
