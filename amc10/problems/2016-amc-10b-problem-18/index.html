2016 AMC 10B Problem 18

2016AMC 10BProblem 18组合数学递推与序列计数递推序列

Problem Statement

In how many ways can $345$ be written as the sum of an increasing sequence of two or more consecutive positive integers?

Choices

  1. A1
  2. B3
  3. C5
  4. D6
  5. E7

Answer

E. 7

Solution

Step 1: Use the formula for consecutive sums

Suppose the sequence has length $k\ge2$ and first term $a$. Then \[345=a+(a+1)+\cdots+(a+k-1)=\frac{k(2a+k-1)}2.\]

Step 2: Solve for the first term

Rearranging gives \[2a=\frac{690}{k}-k+1.\] Thus $k$ must divide $690$, and the right side must be a positive even integer.

Step 3: Test possible lengths

The valid lengths are \[k=2,3,5,6,10,15,23.\] These give positive integer first terms $172,114,67,55,30,16,4$, respectively.

Step 4: Understand why this list is complete

Any longer sequence would have too large a minimum sum, and any valid length must divide $690$, so checking these divisors covers all possibilities.

Step 5: Count

There are $7$ valid lengths, hence $7$ representations.

Step 6: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{7}$.

Key Idea

Use the formula for a consecutive integer sum.
