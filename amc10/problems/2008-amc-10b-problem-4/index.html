2008 AMC 10B Problem 4

2008AMC 10BProblem 4数论进制与数字数字进制

Problem Statement

A semipro baseball league has teams with $21$ players each. League rules state that a player must be paid at least $\$15{,}000$, and that the total of all players' salaries for each team cannot exceed $\$700{,}000$. What is the maximum possible salary, in dollars, for a single player?

Choices

  1. A$270{,}000$
  2. B$385{,}000$
  3. C$400{,}000$
  4. D$430{,}000$
  5. E$700{,}000$

Answer

C. 400000

Solution

Step 1: Understand how to maximize one player

To make one player's salary as large as possible, the other $20$ players should be paid as little as the rules allow.

Step 2: Compute the minimum total for the other players

Each of the other $20$ players must receive at least $\$15{,}000$, so together they must receive at least $20\cdot15{,}000=300{,}000$ dollars.

Step 3: Use the team salary cap

The team total cannot exceed $\$700{,}000$. After reserving $\$300{,}000$ for the other players, the most left for one player is $700{,}000-300{,}000=400{,}000$.

Step 4: Check that this is possible

This salary is allowed because the other $20$ players can each be paid exactly $\$15{,}000$, making the total exactly $\$700{,}000$.

Step 5: Answer

The maximum possible salary is $\boxed{400{,}000}$ dollars.

Key Idea

Maximize one salary by minimizing the other 20 salaries.
