2015 AMC 10B Problem 13

2015AMC 10BProblem 13几何三角形三角形角度

Problem Statement

The line $12x+5y=60$ forms a triangle with the coordinate axes. What is the sum of the lengths of the altitudes of this triangle?

Choices

  1. A20
  2. B\frac{360}{17}
  3. C\frac{107}{5}
  4. D\frac{43}{2}
  5. E\frac{281}{13}

Answer

E. \frac{281}{13}

Solution

Step 1: Find the intercepts

The line $12x+5y=60$ meets the axes at $(5,0)$ and $(0,12)$, so the triangle has legs $5$ and $12$.

Step 2: Recognize the right triangle

The hypotenuse is $13$, making this a $5$-$12$-$13$ triangle.

Step 3: Use area to find altitudes

The altitudes to the two legs are $12$ and $5$. The area is $\frac12\cdot5\cdot12=30$, so the altitude to the hypotenuse is $\frac{2\cdot30}{13}=\frac{60}{13}$.

Step 4: Add the altitudes

The sum is $12+5+\frac{60}{13}=17+\frac{60}{13}=\frac{281}{13}$.

Step 5: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{\frac{281}{13}}$.

Key Idea

Use the 5-12-13 intercept triangle and area-altitude relations.
