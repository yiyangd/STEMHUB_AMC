2022 AMC 10B Problem 25

2022AMC 10BProblem 25数论整除与同余同余余数

Problem Statement

Let $x_0,x_1,x_2,\ldots$ be a sequence of numbers, where each $x_k$ is either $0$ or $1$. For each positive integer $n$, define \[S_n=\sum_{k=0}^{n-1}x_k2^k.\] Suppose $7S_n\equiv1\pmod{2^n}$ for all $n\ge1$. What is the value of $x_{2019}+2x_{2020}+4x_{2021}+8x_{2022}$?

Choices

  1. A6
  2. B7
  3. C12
  4. D14
  5. E15

Answer

A. 6

Solution

Step 1: Interpret the congruence as binary digits

The number $S_n$ is the integer formed by the first $n$ binary digits $x_0,x_1,\ldots,x_{n-1}$. The condition says these prefixes behave like the inverse of $7$ modulo powers of $2$.

Step 2: Find the 2-adic inverse pattern

Because $7=8-1$, the inverse of $7$ in the $2$-adic sense is \[\frac1{7}=1+\frac{6}{1-8}=1+6(1+8+8^2+\cdots).\] This identity is interpreted by matching binary coefficients modulo powers of $2$.

Step 3: Read off the bits

The expression \[1+6(1+8+8^2+\cdots)\] gives binary bits \[1,1,1,0,1,1,0,1,1,0,\ldots.\] After $x_0=1$, the pattern $1,1,0$ repeats.

Step 4: Locate the needed indices

For $k\ge1$, the repeated pattern says $x_k=0$ when $k$ is divisible by $3$, and $x_k=1$ otherwise.

Step 5: Apply this to 2019 through 2022

We have \[2019\equiv0,\quad2020\equiv1,\quad2021\equiv2,\quad2022\equiv0\pmod3.\] Thus \[x_{2019},x_{2020},x_{2021},x_{2022}=0,1,1,0.\]

Step 6: Conclude

The requested value is \[0+2(1)+4(1)+8(0)=6.\] The answer is $\boxed{6}$.

Key Idea

Recognize the binary digits of the 2-adic inverse of 7.
