2014 AMC 10A Problem 18

2014AMC 10AProblem 18几何坐标几何坐标几何

Problem Statement

A square in the coordinate plane has vertices whose $y$-coordinates are $0$, $1$, $4$, and $5$. What is the area of the square?

Choices

  1. A16
  2. B17
  3. C25
  4. D26
  5. E27

Answer

B. 17

Solution

Step 1: Think in side vectors

A square can be described by one side vector $(u,v)$ and the perpendicular side vector $(-v,u)$. Starting from one vertex, the four $y$-coordinates differ by $0$, $v$, $u$, and $u+v$.

Step 2: Match the given y-coordinates

The set of $y$-coordinates is $\{0,1,4,5\}$. This matches $0$, $1$, $4$, and $1+4$, so the vertical components of the two side vectors can be $1$ and $4$.

Step 3: Find the side length squared

If perpendicular side vectors have vertical components $1$ and $4$, their horizontal components can be arranged so the side length squared is $1^2+4^2=17$.

Step 4: Connect area and side length

The area of a square is the square of its side length. Therefore the area is $17$.

Step 5: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{17}$.

Key Idea

Use the vertical components of perpendicular side vectors of a square.
