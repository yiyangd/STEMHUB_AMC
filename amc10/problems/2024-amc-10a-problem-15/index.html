2024 AMC 10A Problem 15

2024AMC 10AProblem 15几何三角形三角形角度

Problem Statement

Let $M$ be the greatest integer such that both $M+1213$ and $M+3773$ are perfect squares. What is the units digit of $M$?

Choices

  1. A1
  2. B2
  3. C3
  4. D6
  5. E8

Answer

E. 8

Solution

Step 1: Represent the two squares

Let \[M+1213=u^2,\quad M+3773=v^2,\] where $v>u$ because $3773>1213$.

Step 2: Subtract the equations

Subtracting gives \[v^2-u^2=3773-1213=2560.\]

Step 3: Factor the difference of squares

So \[(v-u)(v+u)=2560.\] Both factors must have the same parity, because their sum is $2v$ and their difference is $2u$.

Step 4: Maximize the smaller square root

Since \[u=\frac{(v+u)-(v-u)}{2},\] we make $u$ as large as possible by making $v-u$ as small as possible. The factor $1$ does not work because $1$ and $2560$ have different parity. The smallest working value is \[v-u=2,\quad v+u=1280.\]

Step 5: Find M

Solving gives \[u=\frac{1280-2}{2}=639.\] Therefore \[M=639^2-1213=408321-1213=407108.\]

Step 6: Conclude

The units digit of $M$ is $\boxed{8}$.

Key Idea

Use the difference between two squares.
