2002 AMC 10A Problem 19

2002AMC 10AProblem 19几何面积与相似面积比例

Problem Statement

Spot’s doghouse has a regular hexagonal base that measures one yard on each side. He is tethered to a vertex with a two-yard rope. What is the area, in square yards, of the region outside of the doghouse that Spot can reach?

Choices

  1. A2π/3
  2. B2π
  3. C5π/2
  4. D8π/3
  5. E3π

Answer

E. 3\\pi

Solution

Step 1: Use sectors

The reachable region outside the regular hexagon is made of circular sectors as the rope wraps around vertices.

Step 2: Main sector

At the tethered vertex, the outside angle is $240^\circ$. With radius $2$, the sector area is $\frac{240}{360}\pi(2)^2=\frac{8\pi}{3}$.

Step 3: Adjacent sectors

After wrapping around an adjacent vertex, $1$ yard of rope remains. The two adjacent contributions are $60^\circ$ sectors of radius $1$, total area $2\cdot\frac{60}{360}\pi=\frac{\pi}{3}$.

Step 4: Total

The reachable area is $\frac{8\pi}{3}+\frac{\pi}{3}=3\pi$. The answer is $\boxed{3\pi}$.

Key Idea

抓住面积比例、相似关系或周长约束建立方程。
