2004AMC 10BProblem 19代数数列与递推数列递推

Problem Statement

In the sequence 2001, 2002, 2003, . . ., each term after the third is found by subtracting the previ- ous term from the sum of the two terms that precede that term. For example, the fourth term is 2001 + 2002 − 2003 = 2000. What is the 2004 th term in this sequence?

Choices

  1. A−2004
  2. B−2
  3. C0
  4. D4003
  5. E6007

Answer

C. 0

Solution

Step 1: Generate a few terms

The recurrence gives $2001,2002,2003,2000,2005,1998,2007,1996,\ldots$. The odd-indexed terms increase by $2$, while the even-indexed terms decrease by $2$.

Step 2: Write the even-term pattern

For even index $2k$, the term is $2004-2k$. This fits $a_2=2002$, $a_4=2000$, and $a_6=1998$.

Step 3: Use the desired index

The $2004$th term has $2k=2004$, so $k=1002$.

Step 4: Compute

Thus $a_{2004}=2004-2(1002)=0$.

Step 5: Answer

The answer is $\boxed{0}$.

Key Idea

Detect the explicit odd/even pattern generated by the recurrence.
