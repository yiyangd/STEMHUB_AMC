2023 AMC 10A Problem 25

2023AMC 10AProblem 25概率与统计几何概率几何概率面积

Problem Statement

If $A$ and $B$ are vertices of a polyhedron, define $d(A,B)$ to be the minimum number of edges one must traverse to connect $A$ and $B$. Let $Q,R,$ and $S$ be randomly chosen distinct vertices of a regular icosahedron. What is the probability that $d(Q,R)>d(R,S)$?

Choices

  1. A$\frac7{22}$
  2. B$\frac13$
  3. C$\frac38$
  4. D$\frac5{12}$
  5. E$\frac12$

Answer

A. \frac{7}{22}

Solution

Step 1: Fix the middle vertex

By symmetry, we can fix the vertex $R$ first. A regular icosahedron has $12$ vertices.

Step 2: Know the distance distribution

From any fixed vertex of an icosahedron, there are $5$ adjacent vertices at distance $1$, another $5$ vertices at distance $2$, and $1$ opposite vertex at distance $3$.

Step 3: Count ordered choices

After fixing $R$, the vertices $Q$ and $S$ are chosen as distinct vertices among the other $11$. There are \[11\cdot10=110\] ordered choices.

Step 4: Count favorable choices

We need $d(Q,R)>d(R,S)$. If $S$ is at distance $1$, then $Q$ can be any of the $5+1=6$ vertices at distance $2$ or $3$, giving $5\cdot6=30$ choices. If $S$ is at distance $2$, then $Q$ must be the one vertex at distance $3$, giving $5$ choices. If $S$ is at distance $3$, there are no choices for $Q$ farther away.

Step 5: Compute the probability

The favorable count is \[30+5=35.\] Therefore the probability is \[\frac{35}{110}=\frac7{22}.\]

Step 6: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{\frac7{22}}$.

Key Idea

Use the distance distribution from a fixed vertex of an icosahedron.
