2005 AMC 10B Problem 9

2005AMC 10BProblem 9概率与统计组合概率概率计数

Problem Statement

One fair die has faces $1,1,2,2,3,3$ and another has faces $4,4,5,5,6,6$. The dice are rolled and the numbers on the top faces are added. What is the probability that the sum will be odd?

Choices

  1. A$\frac13$
  2. B$\frac49$
  3. C$\frac12$
  4. D$\frac59$
  5. E$\frac23$

Answer

D. \frac{5}{9}

Solution

Step 1: Use parity

A sum is odd when one addend is odd and the other is even.

Step 2: Find parity probabilities

The first die has four odd faces ($1,1,3,3$) and two even faces ($2,2$). So $P(\text{odd on first})=\frac46=\frac23$ and $P(\text{even on first})=\frac13$.

Step 3: Do the same for the second die

The second die has two odd faces ($5,5$) and four even faces ($4,4,6,6$). So $P(\text{odd on second})=\frac13$ and $P(\text{even on second})=\frac23$.

Step 4: Add the two favorable parity cases

The probability is \[\frac23\cdot\frac23+\frac13\cdot\frac13=\frac49+\frac19=\frac59.\]

Step 5: Answer

The answer is $\boxed{\frac59}$.

Key Idea

Use parity: odd sums occur when one die is odd and the other is even.
