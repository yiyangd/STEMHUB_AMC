2008 AMC 10B Problem 21

2008AMC 10BProblem 21组合数学排列组合排列组合计数

Problem Statement

Ten chairs are evenly spaced around a round table and numbered clockwise from $1$ through $10$. Five married couples are to sit in the chairs with men and women alternating, and no one is to sit either next to or directly across from his or her spouse. How many seating arrangements are possible?

Choices

  1. A$240$
  2. B$360$
  3. C$480$
  4. D$540$
  5. E$720$

Answer

C. 480

Solution

Step 1: Separate the gender pattern

Because the chairs are numbered, rotations are not being identified. Men and women must alternate, so there are $2$ possible gender patterns around the table.

Step 2: Seat the men

Once a gender pattern is chosen, the five men can be placed in the five male-position chairs in $5!$ ways.

Step 3: Study the wife positions for one fixed male seating

The five remaining chairs form five female positions around the circle. For each wife, the forbidden positions are the two chairs next to her husband and the chair directly across from him.

Step 4: Count the valid wife placements

After labeling the female positions cyclically relative to the husbands, each wife has only two allowed positions. A short cycle check shows that all wives must shift the same way around the table, giving exactly $2$ valid placements of the wives.

Step 5: Multiply

The total number of arrangements is $2\cdot5!\cdot2=480$.

Step 6: Answer

The answer is $\boxed{480}$.

Key Idea

Seat genders first, then count the two valid wife placements for each fixed seating of the men.
