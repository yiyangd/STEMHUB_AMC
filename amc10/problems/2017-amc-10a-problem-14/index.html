2017 AMC 10A Problem 14

2017AMC 10AProblem 14代数三角恒等式三角函数恒等式

Problem Statement

Every week Roger pays for a movie ticket and a soda out of his allowance. Last week, Roger's allowance was $A$ dollars. The cost of his movie ticket was $20\%$ of the difference between $A$ and the cost of his soda, while the cost of his soda was $5\%$ of the difference between $A$ and the cost of his movie ticket. To the nearest whole percent, what fraction of $A$ did Roger pay for his movie ticket and soda?

Choices

  1. A9%
  2. B19%
  3. C22%
  4. D23%
  5. E25%

Answer

D. 23%

Solution

Step 1: Name the costs

Let $t$ be the ticket cost and $s$ be the soda cost. The problem gives \[t=0.20(A-s),\quad s=0.05(A-t).\]

Step 2: Clear decimals

These become \[5t=A-s,\quad 20s=A-t.\] Equivalently, \[5t+s=A,\quad t+20s=A.\]

Step 3: Relate ticket and soda

Subtracting the equations gives $4t=19s$, so $t=\frac{19}{4}s$.

Step 4: Find the total fraction

Substitute into $5t+s=A$: \[5\cdot\frac{19}{4}s+s=\frac{99}{4}s=A.\] Thus $s=\frac{4A}{99}$ and $t=\frac{19A}{99}$.

Step 5: Add and round

The total is \[t+s=\frac{23A}{99}\approx0.2323A,\] which is about $23\%$ of $A$.

Step 6: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{23\%}$.

Key Idea

Set up two linear equations for ticket and soda costs.
