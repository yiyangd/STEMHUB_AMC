2013 AMC 10A Problem 12

2013AMC 10AProblem 12几何面积与相似面积比例

Problem Statement

In $\triangle ABC$, $AB=AC=28$ and $BC=20$. Points $D,E,F$ are on sides $AB,BC,AC$, respectively, such that $DE\parallel AC$ and $EF\parallel AB$. What is the perimeter of parallelogram $ADEF$?

Choices

  1. A48
  2. B52
  3. C56
  4. D60
  5. E72

Answer

C. 56

Solution

Step 1: Name one side of the parallelogram

Let $AD=x$. Since $AB=28$, we have $DB=28-x$.

Step 2: Use the triangle near B

Because $DE\parallel AC$, triangle $DBE$ is similar to triangle $ABC$. Thus $\frac{BE}{BC}=\frac{DB}{AB}=\frac{28-x}{28}$.

Step 3: Find the other side indirectly

This gives $BE=20\cdot\frac{28-x}{28}$. Hence $CE=20-BE=20\cdot\frac{x}{28}$. Since $EF\parallel AB$, triangle $CEF$ is similar to $CBA$, so $CF=28\cdot\frac{CE}{20}=x$.

Step 4: Finish the perimeter

Then $AF=AC-CF=28-x$. The parallelogram sides are $x$ and $28-x$, so its perimeter is $2(x+28-x)=56$. The answer is $\boxed{56}$.

Key Idea

Use similarity on the two small triangles inside the isosceles triangle.
