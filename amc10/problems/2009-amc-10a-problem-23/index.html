2009 AMC 10A Problem 23

2009AMC 10AProblem 23几何面积与相似面积比例

Problem Statement

Convex quadrilateral $ABCD$ has $AB=9$ and $CD=12$. Diagonals $AC$ and $BD$ intersect at $E$, $AC=14$, and $\triangle AED$ and $\triangle BEC$ have equal areas. What is $AE$?

Choices

  1. A$\frac92$
  2. B$\frac{50}{11}$
  3. C$\frac{21}{4}$
  4. D$\frac{17}{3}$
  5. E$6$

Answer

E. 6

Solution

Step 1: Use the equal-area condition

Triangles $AED$ and $BEC$ share the vertical angle at $E$. Their areas are proportional to $AE\cdot DE$ and $BE\cdot CE$. Equal areas give $AE\cdot DE=BE\cdot CE$.

Step 2: Turn that into a ratio

So $\frac{DE}{BE}=\frac{CE}{AE}$. This means the scale from triangle $ABE$ to triangle $CDE$ is reciprocal along both diagonals.

Step 3: Compare AB and CD

Using the law of cosines with the same included angle at $E$, this ratio implies $\frac{AE}{CE}=\frac{AB}{CD}=\frac{9}{12}=\frac34$.

Step 4: Use AC=14

Let $AE=x$, so $CE=14-x$. Then $\frac{x}{14-x}=\frac34$.

Step 5: Solve

We get $4x=3(14-x)$, so $7x=42$ and $x=6$.

Step 6: Answer

Therefore $AE=\boxed{6}$.

Key Idea

Use equal areas to get a ratio on one diagonal, then compare similar scale factors from AB and CD.
