2016 AMC 10A Problem 10

2016AMC 10AProblem 10组合数学染色与构造染色构造

Problem Statement

A rug is made with three different colors. The areas of the three differently colored rectangular regions form an arithmetic progression. The inner rectangle is $1$ foot wide, and each of the two surrounding shaded regions is $1$ foot wide on all four sides. What is the length in feet of the inner rectangle?

Choices

  1. A1
  2. B2
  3. C4
  4. D6
  5. E8

Answer

B. 2

Solution

Step 1: Name the inner length

Let the inner rectangle have length $x$ and width $1$, so its area is $x$.

Step 2: Compute the middle region

The first surrounding strip makes dimensions $(x+2)$ by $3$. Its area alone is $3(x+2)-x=2x+6$.

Step 3: Compute the outer region

The second surrounding strip makes dimensions $(x+4)$ by $5$. Its area alone is $5(x+4)-3(x+2)=2x+14$.

Step 4: Use arithmetic progression

The three areas $x$, $2x+6$, and $2x+14$ form an arithmetic progression, so \[(2x+6)-x=(2x+14)-(2x+6).\]

Step 5: Solve

This gives $x+6=8$, so $x=2$.

Step 6: Conclude

The inner rectangle has length $\boxed{2}$ feet.

Key Idea

Write the three rectangular region areas in terms of the inner length.
