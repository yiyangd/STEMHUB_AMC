Problem Statement

Marvin had a birthday on Tuesday, May $27$ in the leap year $2008$. In what year will his birthday next fall on a Saturday?

Choices

  1. A$2011$
  2. B$2012$
  3. C$2013$
  4. D$2015$
  5. E$2017$

Answer

E. 2017

Solution

Step 1: Track yearly shifts

From one May $27$ to the next, the weekday usually moves forward one day. It moves forward two days if the interval includes February $29$.

Step 2: List the birthdays

Starting from Tuesday in $2008$: $2009$ is Wednesday, $2010$ is Thursday, $2011$ is Friday, and because $2012$ is a leap year with February $29$ before May $27$, $2012$ is Sunday.

Step 3: Continue until Saturday

Then $2013$ is Monday, $2014$ Tuesday, $2015$ Wednesday, $2016$ Friday, and $2017$ Saturday.

Step 4: Answer

His birthday next falls on a Saturday in $\boxed{2017}$.

Key Idea

Track weekday shifts from birthday to birthday, including leap-year shifts.
