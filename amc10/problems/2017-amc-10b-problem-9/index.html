2017 AMC 10B Problem 9

2017AMC 10BProblem 9概率与统计组合概率概率计数

Problem Statement

A radio program has a quiz consisting of $3$ multiple-choice questions, each with $3$ choices. A contestant wins if he or she gets $2$ or more of the questions right. The contestant answers randomly to each question. What is the probability of winning?

Choices

  1. A$\frac1{27}$
  2. B$\frac19$
  3. C$\frac29$
  4. D$\frac7{27}$
  5. E$\frac12$

Answer

D. \frac7{27}

Solution

Step 1: Identify success cases

The contestant wins by getting exactly $2$ questions right or exactly $3$ questions right.

Step 2: Find single-question probabilities

Each question has probability $\frac13$ of being correct and $\frac23$ of being wrong.

Step 3: Count exactly two correct

There are $\binom32=3$ ways to choose which two questions are correct, so the probability is \[3\left(\frac13\right)^2\left(\frac23\right)=\frac{6}{27}.\]

Step 4: Count exactly three correct

The probability of all three correct is \[\left(\frac13\right)^3=\frac1{27}.\]

Step 5: Add

The winning probability is \[\frac6{27}+\frac1{27}=\frac7{27}.\]

Step 6: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{\frac7{27}}$.

Key Idea

Use binomial counting for exactly two or three correct answers.
