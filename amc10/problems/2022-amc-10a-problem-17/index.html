2022 AMC 10A Problem 17

2022AMC 10AProblem 17数论进制与数字数字进制

Problem Statement

How many three-digit positive integers $\overline{abc}$ are there whose nonzero digits $a,b,$ and $c$ satisfy \[0.\overline{abc}=\frac13(0.\overline{a}+0.\overline{b}+0.\overline{c})?\] The bar indicates repetition.

Choices

  1. A9
  2. B10
  3. C11
  4. D13
  5. E14

Answer

D. 13

Solution

Step 1: Translate repeating decimals

A repeating three-digit decimal is \[0.\overline{abc}=\frac{100a+10b+c}{999}.\] Also $0.\overline{a}=\frac a9$, and similarly for $b$ and $c$.

Step 2: Set up the digit equation

The condition becomes \[\frac{100a+10b+c}{999}=\frac13\left(\frac a9+\frac b9+\frac c9\right).\] Multiplying by $999$ gives \[100a+10b+c=37a+37b+37c,\] so \[7a=3b+4c.\]

Step 3: Use modular arithmetic

Taking the equation modulo $7$ gives \[3b+4c\equiv 0\pmod7.\] Since $4\equiv-3\pmod7$, this means $3(b-c)\equiv0\pmod7$, so $b\equiv c\pmod7$.

Step 4: Count the equal-digit cases

If $b=c$, then $7a=7b$, so $a=b=c$. This gives $9$ solutions: $(1,1,1)$ through $(9,9,9)$.

Step 5: Count the digit pairs differing by 7

The other possibilities are $(b,c)=(1,8),(2,9),(8,1),(9,2)$. They give $(a,b,c)=(5,1,8),(6,2,9),(4,8,1),(5,9,2)$.

Step 6: Add

There are $9+4=13$ valid three-digit integers. The answer is $\boxed{13}$.

Key Idea

Convert repeating decimals to fractions and count digit triples.
