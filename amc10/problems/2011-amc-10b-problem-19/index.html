2011 AMC 10B Problem 19

2011AMC 10BProblem 19代数多项式与根多项式根

Problem Statement

What is the product of all the roots of the equation \[\sqrt{5|x|+8}=\sqrt{x^2-16}?\]

Choices

  1. A-64
  2. B-24
  3. C-9
  4. D24
  5. E576

Answer

A. -64

Solution

Step 1: Notice the role of absolute value

Both sides are square roots, so after squaring we should try to express everything in terms of $|x|$. This is natural because $x^2=|x|^2$.

Step 2: Square both sides

Squaring gives $5|x|+8=x^2-16$. Replacing $x^2$ by $|x|^2$ gives \[|x|^2-5|x|-24=0.\]

Step 3: Solve the quadratic in $|x|$

Factor: \[(|x|-8)(|x|+3)=0.\] Since $|x|$ cannot be negative, the only possible value is $|x|=8$.

Step 4: Convert back to x

If $|x|=8$, then $x=8$ or $x=-8$. Both work in the original equation because both sides become $\sqrt{48}$.

Step 5: Compute the product

The product of all roots is $8\cdot(-8)=-64$. The answer is $\boxed{-64}$.

Key Idea

Square both sides and reduce the equation to a quadratic in $|x|$.
