2024 AMC 10B Problem 15

2024AMC 10BProblem 15概率与统计平均数与数据集平均数数据

Problem Statement

A list of $9$ real numbers consists of $1,2.2,3.2,5.2,6.2,7$ as well as $x,y,z$ with $x\le y\le z$. The range of the list is $7$, and the mean and median are both positive integers. How many ordered triples $(x,y,z)$ are possible?

Choices

  1. A1
  2. B2
  3. C3
  4. D4
  5. Einfinitely many

Answer

C. 3

Solution

Step 1: Start with the fixed data

The six fixed numbers have sum \[1+2.2+3.2+5.2+6.2+7=24.8.\] Since the mean of all $9$ numbers is a positive integer, the total must be a multiple of $9$.

Step 2: Case 1: the maximum remains 7

Then the range condition forces the minimum to be $0$, so $x=0$. The only possible integer total is $36$, so \[y+z=36-24.8=11.2.\] The median is an integer only when \[(x,y,z)=(0,5,6.2).\]

Step 3: Case 2: the minimum remains 1

Then the maximum must be $8$, so $z=8$. The total can be $36$ or $45$. The total $36$ case does not give an integer median, while the total $45$ case gives \[x+y=45-24.8-8=12.2.\] The valid integer-median choice is \[(x,y,z)=(6,6.2,8).\]

Step 4: Case 3: both endpoints are new

Write \[x=t,\quad z=t+7,\quad 0\le t\le1.\] In this case the only possible integer total is $36$, so \[t+y+(t+7)=11.2,\] and hence \[y=4.2-2t.\]

Step 5: Find the remaining integer median

For the median to be an integer in this moving-endpoint case, the fifth value must be \(y=4\). Then \(4=4.2-2t\), so \(t=0.1\), giving \[(0.1,4,7.1).\]

Step 6: Conclude

The three possible triples are \[(0,5,6.2),\quad(6,6.2,8),\quad(0.1,4,7.1).\] Thus the answer is $\boxed{3}$.

Key Idea

Use range endpoints, mean integrality, and median position.
