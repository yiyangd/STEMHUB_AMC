2018 AMC 10A Problem 15

2018AMC 10AProblem 15几何圆与曲线圆曲线

Problem Statement

Two circles of radius $5$ are externally tangent to each other and are internally tangent to a circle of radius $13$ at points $A$ and $B$. The distance $AB$ can be written in the form $\frac{m}{n}$, where $m$ and $n$ are relatively prime positive integers. What is $m+n$?

Choices

  1. A21
  2. B29
  3. C58
  4. D69
  5. E93

Answer

D. 69

Solution

Step 1: Use the centers

Let $X$ be the center of the large circle and $Y,Z$ the centers of the two smaller circles. Since the small circles are internally tangent to the large circle, $XY=XZ=13-5=8$.

Step 2: Use external tangency

The two small circles are externally tangent, so $YZ=5+5=10$.

Step 3: Relate center triangle to tangent-point triangle

The tangent points $A$ and $B$ lie on rays $XY$ and $XZ$. Thus $\triangle XAB$ is similar to $\triangle XYZ$.

Step 4: Scale lengths

The scale factor from $\triangle XYZ$ to $\triangle XAB$ is \[\frac{XA}{XY}=\frac{13}{8}.\] Therefore \[\frac{AB}{YZ}=\frac{13}{8},\quad AB=10\cdot\frac{13}{8}=\frac{65}{4}.\]

Step 5: Find m plus n

Thus $m=65$ and $n=4$, so $m+n=69$.

Step 6: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{69}$.

Key Idea

Use similar triangles formed by the centers and tangency points.
