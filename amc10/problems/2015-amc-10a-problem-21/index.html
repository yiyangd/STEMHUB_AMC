2015 AMC 10A Problem 21

2015AMC 10AProblem 21几何空间几何空间几何体积

Problem Statement

Tetrahedron $ABCD$ has $AB=5$, $AC=3$, $BC=4$, $BD=4$, $AD=3$, and $CD=\frac{12\sqrt2}{5}$. What is the volume of the tetrahedron?

Choices

  1. A3\sqrt2
  2. B2\sqrt5
  3. C\frac{24}{5}
  4. D3\sqrt3
  5. E\frac{24\sqrt2}{5}

Answer

C. \frac{24}{5}

Solution

Step 1: Place one face in the xy-plane

Let $A=(0,0,0)$ and $B=(5,0,0)$. Since $AC=3$ and $BC=4$, we can put $C=\left(\frac95,\frac{12}{5},0\right)$.

Step 2: Use the equal distances to A and B

Because $AD=3$ and $BD=4$, point $D$ has the same $x$-coordinate as $C$, namely $\frac95$. Write $D=\left(\frac95,y,z\right)$.

Step 3: Use AD and CD

From $AD=3$, we get $y^2+z^2=\left(\frac{12}{5}\right)^2$. From $CD=\frac{12\sqrt2}{5}$, we get \[(y-\frac{12}{5})^2+z^2=\frac{288}{25}.\]

Step 4: Solve for the height

Subtracting the equations gives $y=0$, so $z=\frac{12}{5}$. This $z$-value is the height above the plane of $\triangle ABC$.

Step 5: Compute volume

The area of $\triangle ABC$ is $\frac12\cdot3\cdot4=6$. Thus the tetrahedron volume is \[\frac13\cdot6\cdot\frac{12}{5}=\frac{24}{5}.\]

Step 6: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{\frac{24}{5}}$.

Key Idea

Use coordinates for the two 3-4-5 faces and compute height.
