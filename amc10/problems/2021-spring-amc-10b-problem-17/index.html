2021 Spring AMC 10B Problem 17

2021 SpringAMC 10BProblem 17概率与统计组合概率概率计数

Problem Statement

Five players are each given two cards from the cards numbered $1$ through $10$. Their scores are the sums of their cards: Ravon $11$, Oscar $4$, Aditi $7$, Tyrone $16$, and Kim $17$. Which statement is true?

Choices

  1. ARavon was given card 3.
  2. BAditi was given card 3.
  3. CRavon was given card 4.
  4. DAditi was given card 4.
  5. ETyrone was given card 7.

Answer

C. Ravon was given card 4.

Solution

Step 1: Start with the forced small sum

Oscar's score is $4$, so Oscar must have cards $1$ and $3$.

Step 2: Find Aditi's cards

Aditi's score is $7$. The possible pairs are $(1,6)$, $(2,5)$, and $(3,4)$. Since $1$ and $3$ are already used by Oscar, Aditi must have $(2,5)$.

Step 3: Use the large scores

Kim's score is $17$, so Kim has either $(7,10)$ or $(8,9)$. Tyrone's score is $16$, so Tyrone has either $(6,10)$ or $(7,9)$.

Step 4: Avoid overlaps

If Kim had $(7,10)$, Tyrone could not make $16$ without reusing a card. Thus Kim has $(8,9)$ and Tyrone has $(6,10)$.

Step 5: Determine Ravon's cards

The remaining cards are $4$ and $7$, and they sum to Ravon's score $11$. Therefore Ravon was given card $4$.

Step 6: Conclude

The true statement is $\boxed{\text{Ravon was given card 4}}$.

Key Idea

Use forced card pairs from the smallest and largest sums.
