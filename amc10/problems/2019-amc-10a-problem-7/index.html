2019 AMC 10A Problem 7

2019AMC 10AProblem 7几何坐标几何坐标几何

Problem Statement

Two lines with slopes $\frac12$ and $2$ intersect at $(2,2)$. What is the area of the triangle enclosed by these two lines and the line $x+y=10$?

Choices

  1. A4
  2. B$4\sqrt2$
  3. C6
  4. D8
  5. E$6\sqrt2$

Answer

C. 6

Solution

Step 1: Write equations for the two sloped lines

The line with slope $\frac12$ through $(2,2)$ is \[y-2=\frac12(x-2),\] so \[y=\frac{x}{2}+1.\] The line with slope $2$ is \[y-2=2(x-2),\] so \[y=2x-2.\]

Step 2: Find intersections with the third line

The third line is $x+y=10$, or $y=10-x$. Intersecting with $y=\frac{x}{2}+1$ gives $(6,4)$. Intersecting with $y=2x-2$ gives $(4,6)$.

Step 3: Identify the triangle

The three vertices are \[(2,2),\quad(6,4),\quad(4,6).\] This coordinate setup makes the area straightforward.

Step 4: Compute area by determinant

Using vectors from $(2,2)$, \[\vec u=(4,2),\qquad \vec v=(2,4).\] The area is \[\frac12|4\cdot4-2\cdot2|=\frac12(12)=6.\]

Step 5: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{6}$.

Key Idea

Find the three line intersections and compute triangle area.
