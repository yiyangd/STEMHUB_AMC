2009 AMC 10A Problem 9

2009AMC 10AProblem 9数论整数构造整数构造

Problem Statement

Positive integers $a$, $b$, and $2009$, with $a<b<2009$, form a geometric sequence with an integer ratio. What is $a$?

Choices

  1. A$7$
  2. B$41$
  3. C$49$
  4. D$289$
  5. E$2009$

Answer

B. 41

Solution

Step 1: Use the geometric sequence form

Since $a,b,2009$ form a geometric sequence with integer ratio $r$, we have $b=ar$ and $2009=ar^2$.

Step 2: Factor 2009

We factor $2009=7^2\cdot41$. Therefore the square factor $r^2$ must be $7^2$, so $r=7$.

Step 3: Find a

Then $a=\frac{2009}{7^2}=41$. This also gives $b=287$, which satisfies $a<b<2009$.

Step 4: Answer

The value of $a$ is $\boxed{41}$.

Key Idea

Use prime factorization of 2009 and the integer common ratio.
