2004 AMC 10A Problem 25

2004AMC 10AProblem 25几何空间几何空间几何体积

Problem Statement

Three mutually tangent spheres of radius $1$ rest on a horizontal plane. A sphere of radius $2$ rests on them. What is the distance from the plane to the top of the larger sphere?

Choices

  1. A$3+\frac{\sqrt{30}}{2}$
  2. B$3+\frac{\sqrt{69}}{3}$
  3. C$3+\frac{\sqrt{123}}{4}$
  4. D$\frac{\sqrt{52}}{2}$
  5. E$3+\frac{2\sqrt9}{3}$

Answer

B. 3+\frac{\sqrt{69}}{3}

Solution

Step 1: Look at the centers

The centers of the three small spheres form an equilateral triangle of side $2$ in a horizontal plane one unit above the floor.

Step 2: Place the large center above the triangle center

By symmetry, the center of the radius-$2$ sphere lies directly above the centroid of that equilateral triangle.

Step 3: Find the horizontal distance

In an equilateral triangle of side $2$, the distance from the centroid to a vertex is $\frac{2}{\sqrt3}$.

Step 4: Use the center-to-center distance

The large sphere is tangent to each small sphere, so the distance between their centers is $1+2=3$. If the vertical distance between centers is $h$, then \[h^2+\left(\frac{2}{\sqrt3}\right)^2=3^2.\] Thus $h^2=9-\frac43=\frac{23}{3}=\frac{69}{9}$, so $h=\frac{\sqrt{69}}{3}$.

Step 5: Add heights

The small-sphere centers are $1$ unit above the plane, so the large-sphere center is $1+\frac{\sqrt{69}}{3}$ above the plane. The top of the large sphere is $2$ more units up, giving $3+\frac{\sqrt{69}}{3}$. The answer is $\boxed{3+\frac{\sqrt{69}}{3}}$.

Key Idea

Model the sphere centers as a tetrahedral arrangement over an equilateral triangle.
