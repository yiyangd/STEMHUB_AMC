2012 AMC 10A Problem 1

2012AMC 10AProblem 1代数运算与化简代数化简

Problem Statement

Cagney can frost a cupcake every $20$ seconds and Lacey can frost a cupcake every $30$ seconds. Working together, how many cupcakes can they frost in $5$ minutes?

Choices

  1. A10
  2. B15
  3. C20
  4. D25
  5. E30

Answer

D. 25

Solution

Step 1: Convert the time

Five minutes is $5\cdot60=300$ seconds. Working in seconds matches the frosting rates in the problem.

Step 2: Find each person's output

Cagney frosts one cupcake every $20$ seconds, so in $300$ seconds Cagney can frost $300/20=15$ cupcakes. Lacey frosts one every $30$ seconds, so Lacey can frost $300/30=10$ cupcakes.

Step 3: Add the outputs

Since they work together on separate cupcakes, their outputs add. The total is $15+10=25$.

Step 4: Conclude

Together they can frost $\boxed{25}$ cupcakes.

Key Idea

Add work rates and multiply by the total time.
