2019 AMC 10B Problem 8

2019AMC 10BProblem 8几何面积与相似面积比例

Problem Statement

A square and four equilateral triangles are arranged so that each triangle has a side lying on a side of the square, each triangle has side length $2$, and the third vertices of the triangles meet at the center of the square. The region inside the square but outside the triangles is shaded. What is the area of the shaded region?

Choices

  1. A4
  2. B$12-4\sqrt3$
  3. C$3\sqrt3$
  4. D$4\sqrt3$
  5. E$16-\sqrt3$

Answer

B. 12-4\sqrt3

Solution

Step 1: Use the triangle height

Each equilateral triangle has side length $2$, so its height is \[\frac{\sqrt3}{2}\cdot2=\sqrt3.\] Since the third vertices meet at the center of the square, the distance from the center to each side of the square is $\sqrt3$.

Step 2: Find the square area

The square's side length is twice that distance: \[2\sqrt3.\] So the area of the square is \[(2\sqrt3)^2=12.\]

Step 3: Find the triangle areas

Each equilateral triangle has area \[\frac{\sqrt3}{4}\cdot2^2=\sqrt3.\] Four such triangles have total area $4\sqrt3$.

Step 4: Subtract

The shaded region is inside the square but outside the four triangles, so its area is \[12-4\sqrt3.\]

Step 5: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{12-4\sqrt3}$.

Key Idea

Subtract the four equilateral triangle areas from the square area.
