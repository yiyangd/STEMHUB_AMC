2009 AMC 10A Problem 10

2009AMC 10AProblem 10几何面积与相似面积比例

Problem Statement

Triangle $ABC$ has a right angle at $B$. Point $D$ is the foot of the altitude from $B$ to $AC$, $AD=3$, and $DC=4$. What is the area of $\triangle ABC$?

Choices

  1. A$4\sqrt3$
  2. B$7\sqrt3$
  3. C$21$
  4. D$14\sqrt3$
  5. E$42$

Answer

B. 7\sqrt3

Solution

Step 1: Use the altitude-to-hypotenuse relation

In a right triangle, the altitude from the right angle to the hypotenuse satisfies $BD^2=AD\cdot DC$. Here $AD=3$ and $DC=4$, so $BD^2=12$.

Step 2: Find the altitude

Thus $BD=2\sqrt3$.

Step 3: Use AC as the base

The hypotenuse is split into $AD+DC=3+4=7$, so $AC=7$.

Step 4: Compute area

Using base $AC$ and height $BD$, the area is $\frac12\cdot7\cdot2\sqrt3=7\sqrt3$.

Step 5: Answer

The area is $\boxed{7\sqrt3}$.

Key Idea

Use the right-triangle altitude relation BD squared equals AD times DC.
