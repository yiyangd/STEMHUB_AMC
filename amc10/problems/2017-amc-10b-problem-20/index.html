2017 AMC 10B Problem 20

2017AMC 10BProblem 20概率与统计组合概率概率计数

Problem Statement

The number $21!$ has over $60,000$ positive integer divisors. One of them is chosen at random. What is the probability that it is odd?

Choices

  1. A$\frac1{21}$
  2. B$\frac1{19}$
  3. C$\frac1{18}$
  4. D$\frac12$
  5. E$\frac{11}{21}$

Answer

B. \frac1{19}

Solution

Step 1: Factor only what matters

A divisor of $21!$ is odd exactly when it uses no factor of $2$. So the probability is controlled by the exponent of $2$ in $21!$.

Step 2: Find the exponent of 2

The exponent of $2$ in $21!$ is \[\left\lfloor\frac{21}{2}\right\rfloor+\left\lfloor\frac{21}{4}\right\rfloor+\left\lfloor\frac{21}{8}\right\rfloor+\left\lfloor\frac{21}{16}\right\rfloor=10+5+2+1=18.\]

Step 3: Compare odd divisors to all divisors

For every choice of the odd-prime exponents, there are $19$ choices for the exponent of $2$, namely $0$ through $18$.

Step 4: Find the probability

Only one of those $19$ choices, exponent $0$, gives an odd divisor. Therefore the probability is \[\frac1{19}.\]

Step 5: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{\frac1{19}}$.

Key Idea

Only the exponent of 2 determines whether a divisor is odd.
