2006 AMC 10B Problem 4

2006AMC 10BProblem 4几何圆与曲线圆曲线

Problem Statement

Circles of diameter $1$ inch and $3$ inches have the same center. The smaller circle is painted red, and the portion outside the smaller circle and inside the larger circle is painted blue. What is the ratio of the blue-painted area to the red-painted area?

Choices

  1. A$2$
  2. B$3$
  3. C$6$
  4. D$8$
  5. E$9$

Answer

D. 8

Solution

Step 1: Convert diameters to radii

Area formulas use radius, not diameter. The red circle has radius $\frac12$, and the larger circle has radius $\frac32$.

Step 2: Compute the red area

The red area is the area of the smaller circle: \[\pi\left(\frac12\right)^2=\frac{\pi}{4}.\]

Step 3: Compute the blue area as a difference

The blue region is the larger circle minus the smaller circle: \[\pi\left(\frac32\right)^2-\pi\left(\frac12\right)^2=\frac{9\pi}{4}-\frac{\pi}{4}=2\pi.\]

Step 4: Form the requested ratio

The ratio of blue area to red area is \[\frac{2\pi}{\pi/4}=8.\] The factor of $\pi$ cancels, as it often does in ratios of circular areas.

Step 5: Answer

Therefore the answer is $\boxed{8}$.

Key Idea

Compare annulus area to inner circle area using radii from the given diameters.
