2008 AMC 10B Problem 12

2008AMC 10BProblem 12代数运算与化简代数化简

Problem Statement

Postman Pete has a pedometer to count his steps. The pedometer records up to $99999$ steps, then flips over to $00000$ on the next step. Pete plans to determine his mileage for a year. On January 1 Pete sets the pedometer to $00000$. During the year, the pedometer flips from $99999$ to $00000$ forty-four times. On December 31 the pedometer reads $50000$. Pete takes $1800$ steps per mile. Which of the following is closest to the number of miles Pete walked during the year?

Choices

  1. A$2500$
  2. B$3000$
  3. C$3500$
  4. D$4000$
  5. E$4500$

Answer

A. 2500

Solution

Step 1: Understand one rollover

The pedometer flips after recording $99999$ and taking one more step, so each full flip represents $100000$ steps.

Step 2: Count total steps

There were $44$ full flips and then the display ended at $50000$. That means Pete walked $44\cdot100000+50000=4{,}450{,}000$ steps.

Step 3: Convert steps to miles

At $1800$ steps per mile, the distance is $\frac{4{,}450{,}000}{1800}\approx2472.2$ miles.

Step 4: Choose the closest option

Among the choices, $2500$ is closest to $2472.2$.

Step 5: Answer

The closest number of miles is $\boxed{2500}$.

Key Idea

Translate pedometer rollovers into total steps, then divide by steps per mile.
