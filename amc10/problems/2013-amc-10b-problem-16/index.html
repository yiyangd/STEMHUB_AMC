2013 AMC 10B Problem 16

2013AMC 10BProblem 16概率与统计平均数与数据集平均数数据

Problem Statement

In $\triangle ABC$, medians $AD$ and $CE$ intersect at $P$. Given $PE=1.5$, $PD=2$, and $DE=2.5$, what is the area of quadrilateral $AEDC$?

Choices

  1. A13
  2. B13.5
  3. C14
  4. D14.5
  5. E15

Answer

B. 13.5

Solution

Step 1: Use centroid ratios

Since $AD$ and $CE$ are medians, their intersection $P$ is the centroid. A centroid divides each median in a $2:1$ ratio, so $AD=3\cdot PD=6$ and $CE=3\cdot PE=4.5$.

Step 2: Look at triangle PDE

The sides of $\triangle PDE$ are $1.5$, $2$, and $2.5$, so it is a right triangle. Its area is $\frac12\cdot1.5\cdot2=1.5$.

Step 3: Relate this to triangle ABC

In any triangle, the triangle formed by the centroid and two side midpoints has area $\frac1{12}$ of the whole triangle. Thus $[ABC]=12\cdot1.5=18$.

Step 4: Find the requested quadrilateral

Since $D$ and $E$ are midpoints, triangle $BDE$ is similar to $BAC$ with scale factor $\frac12$, so $[BDE]=\frac14[ABC]$. Therefore $[AEDC]=\frac34[ABC]=\frac34\cdot18=13.5$.

Step 5: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{13.5}$.

Key Idea

Use centroid ratios and the area ratio between triangle PDE and triangle ABC.
