2023 AMC 10A Problem 22

2023AMC 10AProblem 22几何圆与曲线圆曲线

Problem Statement

Circles $C_1$ and $C_2$ each have radius $1$, and the distance between their centers is $\frac12$. Circle $C_3$ is the largest circle internally tangent to both $C_1$ and $C_2$. Circle $C_4$ is internally tangent to both $C_1$ and $C_2$ and externally tangent to $C_3$. What is the radius of $C_4$?

Choices

  1. A$\frac1{14}$
  2. B$\frac1{12}$
  3. C$\frac1{10}$
  4. D$\frac3{28}$
  5. E$\frac19$

Answer

D. \frac{3}{28}

Solution

Step 1: Use symmetry

Put the centers of the two unit circles at $\left(-\frac14,0\right)$ and $\left(\frac14,0\right)$. By symmetry, the centers of $C_3$ and $C_4$ lie on the perpendicular bisector, the $y$-axis.

Step 2: Find the radius of C3

The largest circle internally tangent to both unit circles is centered at the midpoint. Its center is $\frac14$ from either unit-circle center, so its radius is \[1-\frac14=\frac34.\]

Step 3: Set up the small circle

Let the radius of $C_4$ be $r$, and let its center be $(0,y)$ on the same axis. Internal tangency to either unit circle gives \[\sqrt{y^2+\left(\frac14\right)^2}=1-r.\]

Step 4: Use tangency to C3

Since $C_4$ is externally tangent to $C_3$, the distance between their centers is \[y=\frac34+r.\]

Step 5: Solve the equation

Substitute this into the right triangle equation: \[\left(\frac34+r\right)^2+\left(\frac14\right)^2=(1-r)^2.\] Expanding gives \[\frac{9}{16}+\frac32r+r^2+\frac1{16}=1-2r+r^2.\] Thus \[\frac58+\frac72r=1,\] so \[r=\frac3{28}.\]

Step 6: Conclude

The radius of $C_4$ is $\boxed{\frac3{28}}$.

Key Idea

Use symmetry and a right triangle between circle centers.
