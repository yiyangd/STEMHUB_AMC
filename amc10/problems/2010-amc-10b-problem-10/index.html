2010 AMC 10B Problem 10

2010AMC 10BProblem 10代数运算与化简代数化简

Problem Statement

Shelby drives her scooter at $30$ miles per hour if it is not raining, and $20$ miles per hour if it is raining. Today she drove in the sun in the morning and in the rain in the evening, for a total of $16$ miles in $40$ minutes. How many minutes did she drive in the rain?

Choices

  1. A$18$
  2. B$21$
  3. C$24$
  4. D$27$
  5. E$30$

Answer

C. 24

Solution

Step 1: Use hours

Forty minutes is $\frac23$ hour. Let $t$ be the number of hours Shelby drove in the rain.

Step 2: Write the sunny time

Then she drove for $\frac23-t$ hours in the sun.

Step 3: Write the distance equation

The total distance is $20t+30(\frac23-t)=16$.

Step 4: Solve

This becomes $20t+20-30t=16$, so $10t=4$ and $t=0.4$ hours.

Step 5: Convert to minutes

The time in rain was $0.4\cdot60=24$ minutes.

Step 6: Answer

She drove in the rain for $\boxed{24}$ minutes.

Key Idea

Use time variables and distance equals rate times time.
