2008 AMC 10A Problem 10

2008AMC 10AProblem 10几何面积与相似面积比例

Problem Statement

Each of the sides of a square $S_1$ with area $16$ is bisected, and a smaller square $S_2$ is constructed using the bisection points as vertices. The same process is carried out on $S_2$ to construct an even smaller square $S_3$. What is the area of $S_3$?

Choices

  1. A$\frac12$
  2. B$1$
  3. C$2$
  4. D$3$
  5. E$4$

Answer

E. 4

Solution

Step 1: Understand the midpoint square

When the side midpoints of a square are connected, the new square has diagonals equal to the side length of the original square.

Step 2: Compare areas

A square whose diagonal is $d$ has area $\frac{d^2}{2}$. Therefore the midpoint square has half the area of the original square.

Step 3: Apply twice

Starting from area $16$, the first new square has area $8$, and the second new square has area $4$.

Step 4: Answer

The area of $S_3$ is $\boxed{4}$.

Key Idea

Joining side midpoints of a square creates a new square with half the area.
