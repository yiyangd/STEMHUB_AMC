2019 AMC 10B Problem 5

2019AMC 10BProblem 5几何坐标几何坐标几何

Problem Statement

Triangle $ABC$ lies in the first quadrant. Points $A,B,C$ are reflected across the line $y=x$ to points $A',B',C'$, respectively. Assume that none of the vertices lie on $y=x$. Which statement is not always true?

Choices

  1. ATriangle $A'B'C'$ lies in the first quadrant.
  2. BTriangles $ABC$ and $A'B'C'$ have the same area.
  3. CThe slope of line $AA'$ is $-1$.
  4. DThe slopes of lines $AA'$ and $CC'$ are the same.
  5. ELines $AB$ and $A'B'$ are perpendicular to each other.

Answer

E. Lines $AB$ and $A'B'$ are perpendicular to each other.

Solution

Step 1: Understand the reflection

Reflecting across $y=x$ swaps coordinates: $(u,v)$ becomes $(v,u)$. This preserves distances, area, and the first quadrant.

Step 2: Check the always-true statements

Because reflection is an isometry, the reflected triangle has the same area. Also the segment from a point to its reflection is perpendicular to $y=x$, so each line like $AA'$ has slope $-1$.

Step 3: Analyze slopes of reflected lines

If a line has slope $m$, then after reflecting across $y=x$, its slope becomes $\frac1m$ when both slopes are defined.

Step 4: Test perpendicularity

For two lines to be perpendicular, their slopes should multiply to $-1$. But \[m\cdot\frac1m=1,\] not $-1$.

Step 5: Conclude

Thus lines $AB$ and $A'B'$ are not always perpendicular. The answer is $\boxed{\text{E}}$.

Key Idea

Understand how reflection across $y=x$ affects slopes.
