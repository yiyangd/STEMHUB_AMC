2015 AMC 10B Problem 20

2015AMC 10BProblem 20组合数学图形计数网格计数

Problem Statement

Erin the ant starts at a given corner of a cube and crawls along exactly $7$ edges in such a way that she visits every corner exactly once and then finds that she is unable to return along an edge to her starting point. How many paths are there meeting these conditions?

Choices

  1. A6
  2. B9
  3. C12
  4. D18
  5. E24

Answer

A. 6

Solution

Step 1: Translate to cube vertices

Visiting every corner exactly once along $7$ edges is a Hamiltonian path on the cube graph starting from a fixed vertex.

Step 2: Use the final condition

After $7$ edge moves, the endpoint has odd parity relative to the start. Among odd-parity vertices, the only one not adjacent to the start is the opposite vertex.

Step 3: Count paths to the opposite vertex

From a fixed start to the opposite vertex, a direct case check on the first two moves shows there are $6$ Hamiltonian paths. Equivalently, they correspond to the $3!$ orders in which the three coordinate directions are first introduced.

Step 4: Check the condition

Each such path ends at the opposite corner, which is not connected to the starting corner by an edge, so Erin cannot return directly.

Step 5: Conclude

There are $\boxed{6}$ paths.

Key Idea

Count Hamiltonian paths on the cube ending at the opposite vertex.
