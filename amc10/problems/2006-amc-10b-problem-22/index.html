2006 AMC 10B Problem 22

2006AMC 10BProblem 22数论整数构造整数构造

Problem Statement

Elmo makes $N$ sandwiches for a fundraiser. For each sandwich he uses $B$ globs of peanut butter at $4$ cents per glob and $J$ blobs of jam at $5$ cents per blob. The cost of the peanut butter and jam to make all the sandwiches is $\$2.53$. Assume that $B$, $J$, and $N$ are all positive integers with $N>1$. What is the cost of the jam Elmo uses to make the sandwiches?

Choices

  1. A$1.05
  2. B$1.25
  3. C$1.45
  4. D$1.65
  5. E$1.85

Answer

D. $1.65

Solution

Step 1: Write the cost equation

For one sandwich, the peanut butter and jam cost $4B+5J$ cents. For $N$ sandwiches, the total is \[N(4B+5J)=253.\]

Step 2: Factor the total

Since $253=11\cdot23$ and $N>1$, the possible values of $N$ are $11$ or $23$ if the per-sandwich cost is an integer factor greater than $1$.

Step 3: Test the per-sandwich costs

If $N=23$, then $4B+5J=11$, but positive integers $B,J$ cannot make $11$ because the smallest possible cost is $4+5=9$ and the next possibilities skip $11$. If $N=11$, then $4B+5J=23$.

Step 4: Solve for positive integers

The equation $4B+5J=23$ has the positive solution $B=2$, $J=3$, since $4\cdot2+5\cdot3=8+15=23$.

Step 5: Find the total jam cost

Each sandwich uses $3$ blobs of jam at $5$ cents each, so jam costs $15$ cents per sandwich. For $11$ sandwiches, the jam cost is $11\cdot15=165$ cents, or $\$1.65$.

Step 6: Answer

The answer is $\boxed{\$1.65}$.

Key Idea

Use integer factorization and modular constraints on the per-sandwich cost.
