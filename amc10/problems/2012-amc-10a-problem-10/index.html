2012 AMC 10A Problem 10

2012AMC 10AProblem 10几何圆与曲线圆曲线

Problem Statement

Mary divides a circle into $12$ sectors. The central angles of these sectors, measured in degrees, are all integers and they form an arithmetic sequence. What is the degree measure of the smallest possible sector angle?

Choices

  1. A5
  2. B6
  3. C8
  4. D10
  5. E12

Answer

C. 8

Solution

Step 1: Use the sum of all central angles

The $12$ sector angles add to $360^\circ$. Since they form an arithmetic sequence, let the first angle be $a$ and common difference be $d$, where $a$ and $d$ are integers.

Step 2: Apply the arithmetic-sequence sum

The sum is \[\frac{12}{2}(2a+11d)=360,\] so $2a+11d=60$.

Step 3: Minimize the first angle

To make $a$ small, make $d$ as large as possible while keeping $a$ positive and integral. Since $2a=60-11d$, $d$ must be even. The largest even $d$ with positive $a$ is $d=4$.

Step 4: Compute a

Then $2a=60-44=16$, so $a=8$.

Step 5: Conclude

The smallest possible sector angle is $\boxed{8^\circ}$.

Key Idea

Use the arithmetic-sequence sum formula and integer constraints.
