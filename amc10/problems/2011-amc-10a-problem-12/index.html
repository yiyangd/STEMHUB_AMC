Problem Statement

The players on a basketball team made some three-point shots, some two-point shots, and some one-point free throws. They scored as many points with two-point shots as with three-point shots. Their number of successful free throws was one more than their number of successful two-point shots. The team's total score was $61$ points. How many free throws did they make?

Choices

  1. A13
  2. B14
  3. C15
  4. D16
  5. E17

Answer

A. 13

Solution

Step 1: Name the made shots

Let $x$ be the number of made three-point shots and $y$ be the number of made two-point shots. The number of free throws is then $y+1$.

Step 2: Use the equal point condition

The team scored as many points from two-point shots as from three-point shots, so $2y=3x$. This means the points from three-point shots are also $2y$.

Step 3: Write the total score

The total score is points from threes plus points from twos plus free throws: $2y+2y+(y+1)=61$.

Step 4: Solve

This gives $5y+1=61$, so $5y=60$ and $y=12$. Therefore the number of free throws is $y+1=13$.

Step 5: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{13}$.

Key Idea

Translate point totals into variables for made shots and solve a linear equation.
