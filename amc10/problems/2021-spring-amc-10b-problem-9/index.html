Problem Statement

The point $P(a,b)$ is first rotated counterclockwise by $90^\circ$ around $(1,5)$ and then reflected about the line $y=-x$. The image of $P$ is $(-6,3)$. What is $b-a$?

Choices

  1. A1
  2. B3
  3. C5
  4. D7
  5. E9

Answer

D. 7

Solution

Step 1: Undo the final reflection

Reflection across $y=-x$ sends $(u,v)$ to $(-v,-u)$. Since the final image is $(-6,3)$, the point just before reflection was $(-3,6)$.

Step 2: Write the rotation formula

Rotating $P(a,b)$ counterclockwise by $90^\circ$ around $(1,5)$ means rotate the vector $(a-1,b-5)$ to $(5-b,a-1)$.

Step 3: Add back the center

After rotation, the point is \[(1+5-b,\;5+a-1)=(6-b,\;a+4).\]

Step 4: Set equal to the pre-reflection point

So \[(6-b,\;a+4)=(-3,6).\] This gives $b=9$ and $a=2$.

Step 5: Conclude

Therefore \[b-a=9-2=7.\] The answer is $\boxed{7}$.

Key Idea

Undo coordinate transformations.
