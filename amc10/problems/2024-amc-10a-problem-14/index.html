2024 AMC 10A Problem 14

2024AMC 10AProblem 14几何圆与曲线圆曲线

Problem Statement

One side of an equilateral triangle of height $24$ lies on line $\ell$. A circle of radius $12$ is tangent to $\ell$ and is externally tangent to the triangle. The area of the region exterior to the triangle and the circle and bounded by the triangle, the circle, and line $\ell$ can be written as $a\sqrt b-c\pi$, where $a$, $b$, and $c$ are positive integers and $b$ is squarefree. What is $a+b+c$?

Choices

  1. A72
  2. B73
  3. C74
  4. D75
  5. E76

Answer

D. 75

Solution

Step 1: Identify the useful bounded region

The desired region is bounded by one side of the equilateral triangle, the line $\ell$, and an arc of the circle. A good way to see the area is as a kite-shaped region minus a circular sector.

Step 2: Use the equilateral triangle height

An equilateral triangle of height $24$ has side length \[\frac{2\cdot24}{\sqrt3}=16\sqrt3.\] The tangency geometry creates a $30$-$60$-$90$ triangle near the circle.

Step 3: Find the straight-edged area

The relevant kite-shaped region has diagonals $12$ and $8\sqrt3$, so its area is \[\frac12\cdot12\cdot8\sqrt3=48\sqrt3.\]

Step 4: Find the circular sector

The angle of the corresponding sector is $60^\circ$, and the circle has radius $12$. Thus the sector area is \[\frac{60}{360}\pi(12)^2=24\pi.\]

Step 5: Subtract

The desired area is \[48\sqrt3-24\pi.\] Hence $a=48$, $b=3$, and $c=24$.

Step 6: Conclude

Therefore \[a+b+c=48+3+24=75,\] so the answer is $\boxed{75}$.

Key Idea

Subtract a 60-degree circular sector from a kite-shaped region.
