2011 AMC 10B Problem 4

2011AMC 10BProblem 4代数三角恒等式三角函数恒等式

Problem Statement

LeRoy and Bernardo went on a week-long trip together and agreed to share the costs equally. At the end of the trip LeRoy had paid $A$ dollars and Bernardo had paid $B$ dollars, where $A<B$. How many dollars must LeRoy give to Bernardo so that they share the costs equally?

Choices

  1. A\frac{A+B}{2}
  2. B\frac{A-B}{2}
  3. C\frac{B-A}{2}
  4. DB-A
  5. EA+B

Answer

C. \frac{B-A}{2}

Solution

Step 1: Find each person's fair share

Together they paid $A+B$ dollars. If they share equally, each person should pay $\frac{A+B}{2}$.

Step 2: Compare LeRoy's payment to his share

LeRoy has already paid $A$ dollars. Since $A<B$, he paid less than half, so he must pay Bernardo the difference between his fair share and what he already paid.

Step 3: Compute the difference

That amount is $\frac{A+B}{2}-A=\frac{A+B-2A}{2}=\frac{B-A}{2}$.

Step 4: Conclude

LeRoy must give Bernardo $\boxed{\frac{B-A}{2}}$ dollars.

Key Idea

Equal sharing means each person should end up paying half the total cost.
