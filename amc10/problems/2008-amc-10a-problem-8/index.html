2008 AMC 10A Problem 8

2008AMC 10AProblem 8代数运算与化简代数化简

Problem Statement

Heather compares the price of a new computer at two different stores. Store A offers $15\%$ off the sticker price followed by a $\$90$ rebate, and store B offers $25\%$ off the same sticker price with no rebate. Heather saves $\$15$ by buying the computer at store A instead of store B. What is the sticker price of the computer, in dollars?

Choices

  1. A$750$
  2. B$900$
  3. C$1000$
  4. D$1050$
  5. E$1500$

Answer

A. 750

Solution

Step 1: Let the sticker price be x

Store A's final price is $0.85x-90$. Store B's final price is $0.75x$.

Step 2: Use the savings statement

Heather saves $15$ by buying at Store A, so Store B's price is $15$ more than Store A's price: $0.75x-(0.85x-90)=15$.

Step 3: Solve

This gives $-0.10x+90=15$, so $0.10x=75$, and $x=750$.

Step 4: Answer

The sticker price is $\boxed{750}$ dollars.

Key Idea

Translate discounts and rebates into equations in the sticker price.
