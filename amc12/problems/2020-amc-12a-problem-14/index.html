2020 AMC 12A Problem 14

2020AMC 12AProblem 14几何面积与相似面积比例

Problem Statement

Regular octagon $ABCDEFGH$ has area $n$. Let $m$ be the area of quadrilateral $ACEG$. What is $\frac mn$?

Choices

  1. A$\frac{\sqrt2}{4}$
  2. B$\frac{\sqrt2}{2}$
  3. C$\frac34$
  4. D$\frac{3\sqrt2}{5}$
  5. E$\frac{2\sqrt2}{3}$

Answer

B. \frac{\sqrt2}{2}

Solution

Step 1: Use a circumradius model

Place the regular octagon on a circle of radius $R$. The area of a regular $8$-gon is \[\frac82R^2\sin45^\circ=2\sqrt2\,R^2.\]

Step 2: Identify quadrilateral ACEG

Vertices $A,C,E,G$ are every other vertex of the octagon. They form a square inscribed in the same circle.

Step 3: Find the square area

An inscribed square with circumradius $R$ has diagonal $2R$, so its area is \[\frac{(2R)^2}{2}=2R^2.\]

Step 4: Take the ratio

Thus \[\frac mn=\frac{2R^2}{2\sqrt2\,R^2}=\frac1{\sqrt2}=\frac{\sqrt2}{2}.\]

Step 5: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{\frac{\sqrt2}{2}}$.

Key Idea

Compare the area of an inscribed square with the area of the regular octagon.
