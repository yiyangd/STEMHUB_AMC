Problem Statement

What is the volume of the region in three-dimensional space defined by the inequalities \[|x|+|y|+|z|\le1\qquad\text{and}\qquad |x|+|y|+|z-1|\le1?\]

Choices

  1. A$\frac16$
  2. B$\frac13$
  3. C$\frac12$
  4. D$\frac23$
  5. E1

Answer

A. \frac16

Solution

Step 1: Separate the xy part

Let \[r=|x|+|y|.\] The two inequalities become \[r+|z|\le1\quad\text{and}\quad r+|z-1|\le1.\]

Step 2: Find the allowed z interval

For a fixed value of \(r\), the first inequality gives \[-(1-r)\le z\le1-r,\] and the second gives \[r\le z\le2-r.\] Their intersection has length \[1-2r,\] provided \(0\le r\le\frac12\).

Step 3: Measure the xy slices

In the $xy$-plane, the region \(|x|+|y|\le r\) is a diamond with area \(2r^2\). Therefore the thin layer where \(|x|+|y|\) is between \(r\) and \(r+dr\) has area approximately \(4r\,dr\).

Step 4: Integrate the slice volumes

The total volume is \[\int_0^{1/2}(1-2r)(4r)\,dr.\]

Step 5: Evaluate

We get \[\int_0^{1/2}(4r-8r^2)\,dr=\left[2r^2-\frac83r^3\right]_0^{1/2}=\frac12-\frac13=\frac16.\]

Step 6: Conclude

The volume is \[\boxed{\frac16}.\]

Key Idea

Slice the intersection of two octahedra by the value of |x| plus |y|.
