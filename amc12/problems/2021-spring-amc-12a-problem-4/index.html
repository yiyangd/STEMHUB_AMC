Problem Statement

Tom has a collection of $13$ snakes, $4$ of which are purple and $5$ of which are happy. He observes that all happy snakes can add, none of his purple snakes can subtract, and all snakes that cannot subtract also cannot add. Which conclusion can be drawn?

Choices

  1. APurple snakes can add.
  2. BPurple snakes are happy.
  3. CSnakes that can add are purple.
  4. DHappy snakes are not purple.
  5. EHappy snakes cannot subtract.

Answer

D. Happy snakes are not purple.

Solution

Step 1: Translate the statements

The observations say: happy implies can add, purple implies cannot subtract, and cannot subtract implies cannot add.

Step 2: Chain the purple implication

If a snake is purple, then it cannot subtract. Therefore it cannot add.

Step 3: Compare with happy snakes

Every happy snake can add. A purple snake cannot add, so a happy snake cannot be purple.

Step 4: Conclude

The conclusion that must be true is $\boxed{\text{Happy snakes are not purple.}}$

Key Idea

Use implications and contrapositives carefully.
