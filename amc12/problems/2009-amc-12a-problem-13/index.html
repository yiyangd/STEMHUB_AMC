2009 AMC 12A Problem 13

2009AMC 12AProblem 13几何三角形三角形角度

Problem Statement

A ship sails \(10\) miles in a straight line from \(A\) to \(B\), turns through an angle between \(45^\circ\) and \(60^\circ\), and then sails another \(20\) miles to \(C\). Which interval contains \(AC^2\)?

Choices

  1. A[400,500]
  2. B[500,600]
  3. C[600,700]
  4. D[700,800]
  5. E[800,900]

Answer

D. [700,800]

Solution

Step 1: Convert the turn to the triangle angle

The ship turns between \(45^\circ\) and \(60^\circ\), so the interior angle \(\angle ABC\) of triangle \(ABC\) is between \(120^\circ\) and \(135^\circ\).

Step 2: Apply the Law of Cosines

\[AC^2=10^2+20^2-2(10)(20)\cos\angle ABC=500-400\cos\angle ABC.\]

Step 3: Bound the cosine

For angles between \(120^\circ\) and \(135^\circ\), the cosine is between \(-\frac12\) and \(-\frac{\sqrt2}{2}\).

Step 4: Bound AC squared

Therefore \[700=500+200\le AC^2\le 500+200\sqrt2<800.\]

Step 5: Conclude

So \(AC^2\) lies in \(\boxed{[700,800]}\).

Key Idea

Apply the Law of Cosines with the turn angle converted to the interior angle.
