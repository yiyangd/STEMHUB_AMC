2009 AMC 12A Problem 17

2009AMC 12AProblem 17代数运算与化简代数化简

Problem Statement

Let \(a+ar_1+ar_1^2+\cdots\) and \(a+ar_2+ar_2^2+\cdots\) be two different infinite geometric series of positive numbers with the same first term. The sum of the first series is \(r_1\), and the sum of the second series is \(r_2\). What is \(r_1+r_2\)?

Choices

  1. A0
  2. B\(\frac12\)
  3. C1
  4. D\(\frac{1+\sqrt5}{2}\)
  5. E2

Answer

C. 1

Solution

Step 1: Write the sum formula

For an infinite geometric series with first term \(a\) and ratio \(r\), the sum is \(\frac{a}{1-r}\). The problem says this sum equals \(r\).

Step 2: Create the equation for each ratio

Thus each ratio \(r\) satisfies \[\frac{a}{1-r}=r,\] or \[r^2-r+a=0.\]

Step 3: Use the fact that the two ratios are different

The two different ratios \(r_1\) and \(r_2\) are the two roots of the same quadratic equation \(r^2-r+a=0\).

Step 4: Use sum of roots

The sum of the roots is \(1\). Therefore \[r_1+r_2=1.\]

Step 5: Conclude

The answer is \(\boxed{1}\).

Key Idea

Both ratios are roots of the same quadratic.
