2022 AMC 12A Problem 23

2022AMC 12AProblem 23数论质因数与整除质因数整除

Problem Statement

Let $h_n$ and $k_n$ be the unique relatively prime positive integers such that \[\frac11+\frac12+\frac13+\cdots+\frac1n=\frac{h_n}{k_n}.\] Let $L_n$ denote the least common multiple of the numbers $1,2,3,\ldots,n$. For how many integers $n$ with $1\le n\le22$ is $k_n<L_n$?

Choices

  1. A0
  2. B3
  3. C7
  4. D8
  5. E10

Answer

D. 8

Solution

Step 1: Understand what must be checked

The denominator $k_n$ is the denominator after the harmonic sum is reduced. The denominator $L_n$ is a natural common denominator before reducing. Thus $k_n<L_n$ exactly when cancellation occurs after writing the sum over denominator $L_n$.

Step 2: Use a running computation

Because $n\le22$, a systematic table is safe and short. At each step, update \[H_n=H_{n-1}+\frac1n\] and reduce the fraction, while also updating \[L_n=\operatorname{lcm}(1,2,\ldots,n).\]

Step 3: Record the first cancellation interval

For $n=1,2,3,4,5$, the reduced denominator equals $L_n$. For \[n=6,7,8,\] cancellation occurs, giving \[k_6=20<60,\quad k_7=140<420,\quad k_8=280<840.\]

Step 4: Record the middle interval

From $n=9$ through $n=17$, the reduced denominator again equals $L_n$. This contributes no additional values.

Step 5: Record the final interval

From $n=18$ through $n=22$, cancellation occurs again: \[k_{18}<L_{18},\ k_{19}<L_{19},\ k_{20}<L_{20},\ k_{21}<L_{21},\ k_{22}<L_{22}.\]

Step 6: Count

The successful values are \[6,7,8,18,19,20,21,22,\] for a total of $8$.

Step 7: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{8}$.

Key Idea

Track reduced harmonic denominators against the running LCM.
