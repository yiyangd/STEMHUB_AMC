2006 AMC 12B Problem 12

2006AMC 12BProblem 12几何三角形三角形角度

Problem Statement

The parabola \(y=ax^2+bx+c\) has vertex \((p,p)\) and \(y\)-intercept \((0,-p)\), where \(p\ne0\). What is \(b\)?

Choices

  1. A\(-p\)
  2. B0
  3. C2
  4. D4
  5. E\(p\)

Answer

D. 4

Solution

Step 1: Use vertex form

A parabola with vertex \((p,p)\) can be written as \[y=a(x-p)^2+p.\] This form uses the vertex directly.

Step 2: Use the y-intercept

The y-intercept is \((0,-p)\), so substitute \(x=0\) and \(y=-p\): \[-p=ap^2+p.\]

Step 3: Solve for a

Since \(p\ne0\), \[ap^2=-2p,\] so \[a=-\frac2p.\]

Step 4: Expand to find b

Expanding vertex form gives \[y=ax^2-2apx+ap^2+p.\] Therefore \(b=-2ap\).

Step 5: Substitute a

\[b=-2\left(-\frac2p\right)p=4.\]

Step 6: Conclude

The value of \(b\) is \(\boxed{4}\).

Key Idea

Use vertex form and the y-intercept to determine the quadratic coefficient.
