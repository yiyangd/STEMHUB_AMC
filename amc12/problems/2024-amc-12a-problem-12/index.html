2024 AMC 12A Problem 12

2024AMC 12AProblem 12代数数列与递推等比数列整数最小值

Problem Statement

The first three terms of a geometric sequence are the integers $a$, $720$, and $b$, where $a<720<b$. What is the sum of the digits of the least possible value of $b$?

Choices

  1. A9
  2. B12
  3. C16
  4. D18
  5. E21

Answer

E. 21

Solution

Step 1: Use the structure of a geometric sequence

For three consecutive terms $a,720,b$ in a geometric sequence, the middle term squared equals the product of the two outside terms. Therefore

\[a b=720^2.\]

We need $a$ and $b$ to be integers with $a<720<b$, so $b$ must be a divisor of $720^2$ that is just larger than $720$.

Step 2: Factor the central term

Prime factorization helps us understand the divisors:

\[720=2^4\cdot3^2\cdot5,\qquad 720^2=2^8\cdot3^4\cdot5^2.\]

The least possible $b$ greater than $720$ should be the smallest divisor of this number that exceeds $720$.

Step 3: Look for the nearest divisor above 720

A natural candidate is to keep a large power of $2$ and a small factor of $3$:

\[2^8\cdot3=256\cdot3=768.\]

This is greater than $720$, and it divides $720^2$. The paired value is

\[a=\frac{720^2}{768}=675,\]

which is less than $720$, so the order condition works.

Step 4: Check why this is minimal

The divisors just above $720$ are limited by the prime factors $2$, $3$, and $5$. Checking the nearby divisor structure confirms there is no divisor of $720^2$ strictly between $720$ and $768$.

So the least possible value of $b$ is $768$.

Step 5: Answer the digit-sum question

The sum of the digits of $768$ is

\[7+6+8=21.\]

Thus the answer is $21$.

Key Idea

Use the fact that consecutive terms in a geometric sequence satisfy middle squared equals product of neighbors.
