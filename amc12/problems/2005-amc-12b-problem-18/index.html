2005 AMC 12B Problem 18

2005AMC 12BProblem 18几何面积与相似面积比例

Problem Statement

Let \(A(2,2)\) and \(B(7,7)\) be points in the plane. Define \(R\) as the region in the first quadrant consisting of those points \(C\) such that \(\triangle ABC\) is an acute triangle. What is the closest integer to the area of the region \(R\)?

Choices

  1. A25
  2. B39
  3. C51
  4. D60
  5. E80

Answer

C. 51

Solution

Step 1: Translate acute conditions at A and B

For angle \(A\) to be acute, \((B-A)\cdot(C-A)>0\), which gives \(x+y>4\). For angle \(B\) to be acute, \((A-B)\cdot(C-B)>0\), which gives \(x+y<14\).

Step 2: Translate the condition at C

The angle at \(C\) is right on the circle with diameter \(\overline{AB}\). It is acute outside that circle. The circle has center \((4.5,4.5)\) and radius \(\frac{5\sqrt2}{2}\).

Step 3: Find the strip area

In the first quadrant, the region \(4<x+y<14\) has area \[\frac12(14)^2-\frac12(4)^2=98-8=90.\]

Step 4: Subtract the circle

The circle lies inside this strip and is tangent to the two boundary lines. Its area is \[\pi\left(\frac{5\sqrt2}{2}\right)^2=\frac{25\pi}{2}.\]

Step 5: Approximate

The desired area is \[90-\frac{25\pi}{2}\approx50.7,\] whose closest integer is \(51\).

Step 6: Conclude

The answer is \(\boxed{51}\).

Key Idea

Use acute-angle inequalities and subtract the Thales circle.
