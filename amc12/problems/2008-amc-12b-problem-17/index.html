2008 AMC 12B Problem 17

2008AMC 12BProblem 17几何坐标几何坐标几何

Problem Statement

Let \(A\), \(B\), and \(C\) be three distinct points on the graph of \(y=x^2\) such that line \(\overline{AB}\) is parallel to the \(x\)-axis and \(\triangle ABC\) is a right triangle with area \(2008\). What is the sum of the digits of the \(y\)-coordinate of \(C\)?

Choices

  1. A16
  2. B17
  3. C18
  4. D19
  5. E20

Answer

C. 18

Solution

Step 1: Use symmetry from the horizontal chord

Since \(A\) and \(B\) are on \(y=x^2\) and \(\overline{AB}\) is horizontal, we can write \(A=(-p,p^2)\) and \(B=(p,p^2)\). Let \(C=(q,q^2)\).

Step 2: Use the right angle condition

For \(\triangle ABC\) to be right with the right angle at \(C\), the slopes of \(CA\) and \(CB\) multiply to \(-1\). These slopes simplify to \(q-p\) and \(q+p\), so \[(q-p)(q+p)=q^2-p^2=-1.\]

Step 3: Relate this to the area

Thus \(p^2-q^2=1\). The base \(AB\) has length \(2p\), and the vertical distance from \(C\) to line \(AB\) is \(p^2-q^2=1\).

Step 4: Use the given area

The area is \[\frac12\cdot2p\cdot1=p.\] Since the area is \(2008\), we get \(p=2008\).

Step 5: Find the y-coordinate of C

From \(p^2-q^2=1\), \[q^2=2008^2-1=4{,}032{,}063.\] This is the \(y\)-coordinate of \(C\).

Step 6: Sum the digits

The digit sum of \(4{,}032{,}063\) is \[4+0+3+2+0+6+3=18.\]

Step 7: Conclude

The answer is \(\boxed{18}\).

Key Idea

Use symmetry on the parabola and the right-triangle slope condition.
