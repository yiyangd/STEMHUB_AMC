2023 AMC 12B Problem 16

2023AMC 12BProblem 16概率与统计组合概率概率计数

Problem Statement

In Coinland, coins have values $6$, $10$, and $15$. Suppose $x$ is the value of the most expensive item that cannot be purchased using these coins with exact change. What is the sum of the digits of $x$?

Choices

  1. A8
  2. B10
  3. C7
  4. D11
  5. E9

Answer

D. 11

Solution

Step 1: Find a large impossible value

Any amount made with all three coin types is at least \[6+10+15=31.\] Amounts made with only two or fewer coin types are restricted by divisibility patterns. In particular, $29$ cannot be made.

Step 2: Show everything after 29 works

It is enough to make six consecutive amounts, because then adding more $6$-coin values makes all larger amounts. We have \[30=15+15,\] \[31=6+10+15,\] \[32=6+6+10+10,\] \[33=6+6+6+15,\] \[34=6+6+6+6+10,\] \[35=10+10+15.\]

Step 3: Conclude the maximum impossible amount

Since $30$ through $35$ are possible, every amount greater than $29$ is possible by adding $6$s.

Step 4: Find the digit sum

The maximum impossible amount is $29$, whose digit sum is \[2+9=11.\]

Step 5: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{11}$.

Key Idea

Show 29 is impossible and all larger values are possible by six consecutive amounts.
