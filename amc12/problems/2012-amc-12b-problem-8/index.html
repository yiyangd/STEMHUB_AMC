Problem Statement

A dessert chef prepares the dessert for every day of a week starting with Sunday. The dessert each day is either cake, pie, ice cream, or pudding. The same dessert may not be served two days in a row. There must be cake on Friday because of a birthday. How many different dessert menus for the week are possible?

Choices

  1. A729
  2. B972
  3. C1024
  4. D2187
  5. E2304

Answer

A. 729

Solution

Step 1: Split around Friday

Friday is fixed as cake. The dessert on Thursday cannot be cake, and the dessert on Saturday cannot be cake.

Step 2: Count Sunday through Thursday

Without the Thursday restriction, Sunday through Thursday would have \[4\cdot3^4=324\] possibilities. By symmetry, exactly one fourth of these end with cake on Thursday, so \[324-\frac{324}{4}=243\] end with something other than cake.

Step 3: Count Saturday

Saturday can be any dessert except cake, giving $3$ choices.

Step 4: Multiply

The total number of menus is \[243\cdot3=729.\]

Step 5: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{729}$.

Key Idea

Count menus with a fixed Friday dessert and no adjacent repeats.
