2011 AMC 12A Problem 12

2011AMC 12AProblem 12代数三角恒等式三角函数恒等式

Problem Statement

A power boat and a raft both left dock $A$ on a river and headed downstream. The raft drifted at the speed of the river current. The power boat maintained a constant speed with respect to the river. The power boat reached dock $B$ downriver, then immediately turned and traveled back upriver. It eventually met the raft on the river $9$ hours after leaving dock $A$. How many hours did it take the power boat to go from $A$ to $B$?

Choices

  1. A3
  2. B3.5
  3. C4
  4. D4.5
  5. E5

Answer

D. 4.5

Solution

Step 1: Name the speeds

Let the river current speed be $c$ and the boat's speed relative to the water be $v$. Let $t$ be the time, in hours, that the boat takes to travel from $A$ to $B$ downstream.

Step 2: Write the distance to B

The downstream speed of the boat is $v+c$, so the distance from $A$ to $B$ is \[D=(v+c)t.\]

Step 3: Use the meeting after 9 hours

After $9$ hours, the raft has drifted $9c$ from dock $A$. The boat has returned upstream for $9-t$ hours at speed $v-c$, so its position is \[D-(v-c)(9-t).\]

Step 4: Set the positions equal

At the meeting point, \[(v+c)t-(v-c)(9-t)=9c.\] Expanding gives \[vt+ct-9v+9c+vt-ct=9c,\] so \[2vt-9v=0.\]

Step 5: Solve for t

Since $v>0$, we get \[2t=9,\] so \[t=4.5.\]

Step 6: Conclude

The boat took $\boxed{4.5}$ hours to go from $A$ to $B$.

Key Idea

Use downstream and upstream positions with current speed and boat speed.
