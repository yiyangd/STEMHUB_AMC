2005 AMC 12B Problem 15

2005AMC 12BProblem 15数论进制与数字数字进制

Problem Statement

The sum of four two-digit numbers is \(221\). None of the eight digits is \(0\), and no two of them are the same. Which of the following is not included among the eight digits?

Choices

  1. A1
  2. B2
  3. C3
  4. D4
  5. E5

Answer

D. 4

Solution

Step 1: Use digit sums

Let \(T\) be the sum of the four tens digits and \(U\) the sum of the four units digits. The sum of the four two-digit numbers is \(10T+U=221\).

Step 2: Use the missing digit

The eight used digits are \(1\) through \(9\) except for one missing digit \(d\). Thus \(T+U=45-d\).

Step 3: Subtract equations

Subtracting gives \[9T=221-(45-d)=176+d.\]

Step 4: Use divisibility by 9

The right side must be divisible by \(9\). Since \(176\equiv5\pmod9\), we need \(d\equiv4\pmod9\). The missing digit is \(4\).

Step 5: Conclude

The answer is \(\boxed{4}\).

Key Idea

Use digit sums modulo \(9\).
