2003 AMC 12B Problem 19

2003AMC 12BProblem 19概率与统计组合概率概率计数

Problem Statement

Let \(S\) be the set of permutations of \(1,2,3,4,5\) for which the first term is not \(1\). A permutation is chosen randomly from \(S\). The probability that the second term is \(2\), in lowest terms, is \(\frac ab\). What is \(a+b\)?

Choices

  1. A5
  2. B6
  3. C11
  4. D16
  5. E19

Answer

E. 19

Solution

Step 1: Count the sample space

There are \(5!=120\) permutations of \(1,2,3,4,5\). Those with first term \(1\) number \(4!=24\). So \[|S|=120-24=96.\]

Step 2: Count favorable permutations

Now require the second term to be \(2\). There are \(4!=24\) permutations with second term \(2\).

Step 3: Remove forbidden first term

Among those, the permutations with first term \(1\) have first two terms \(1,2\), and the remaining \(3\) terms can be arranged in \(3!=6\) ways. Thus favorable permutations in \(S\) number \(24-6=18\).

Step 4: Reduce the probability

The probability is \[\frac{18}{96}=\frac{3}{16}.\]

Step 5: Conclude

Thus \(a+b=3+16=\boxed{19}\).

Key Idea

Count conditional permutations.
