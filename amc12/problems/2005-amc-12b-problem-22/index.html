2005 AMC 12B Problem 22

2005AMC 12BProblem 22代数数列与递推数列递推

Problem Statement

A sequence of complex numbers \(z_0,z_1,z_2,\ldots\) is defined by the rule \[z_{n+1}=\frac{i z_n}{\overline{z_n}},\] where \(\overline{z_n}\) is the complex conjugate of \(z_n\) and \(i^2=-1\). Suppose that \(|z_0|=1\) and \(z_{2005}=1\). How many possible values are there for \(z_0\)?

Choices

  1. A1
  2. B2
  3. C4
  4. D2005
  5. E\(2^{2005}\)

Answer

E. 2^{2005}

Solution

Step 1: Use the unit circle

Because \(|z_0|=1\), the recurrence keeps every \(z_n\) on the unit circle. For \(|z_n|=1\), we have \(\overline{z_n}=1/z_n\).

Step 2: Simplify the recurrence

Thus \[z_{n+1}=\frac{i z_n}{\overline{z_n}}=i z_n^2.\]

Step 3: Write arguments

Let \(z_n=e^{i\theta_n}\). Then \[\theta_{n+1}\equiv 2\theta_n+\frac{\pi}{2}\pmod{2\pi}.\]

Step 4: Iterate the formula

After \(N\) steps, \[\theta_N\equiv 2^N\theta_0+(2^N-1)\frac{\pi}{2}\pmod{2\pi}.\]

Step 5: Count preimages of 1

The condition \(z_{2005}=1\) means \(\theta_{2005}\equiv0\pmod{2\pi}\). Multiplication by \(2^{2005}\) on the circle has exactly \(2^{2005}\) solutions for \(\theta_0\).

Step 6: Conclude

There are \(\boxed{2^{2005}}\) possible values of \(z_0\).

Key Idea

Track arguments of complex numbers on the unit circle.
