2005 AMC 12B Problem 19

2005AMC 12BProblem 19数论进制与数字数字进制

Problem Statement

Let \(x\) and \(y\) be two-digit integers such that \(y\) is obtained by reversing the digits of \(x\). The integers \(x\) and \(y\) satisfy \(x^2-y^2=m^2\) for some positive integer \(m\). What is \(x+y+m\)?

Choices

  1. A88
  2. B112
  3. C116
  4. D144
  5. E154

Answer

E. 154

Solution

Step 1: Write the reversed numbers

Let \(x=10a+b\) and \(y=10b+a\), with \(a>b\) so that \(x^2-y^2\) is positive.

Step 2: Factor the difference of squares

We have \[x-y=9(a-b),\qquad x+y=11(a+b),\] so \[x^2-y^2=99(a-b)(a+b)=99(a^2-b^2).\]

Step 3: Force a square

Since \(99=9\cdot11\), we need \(11(a^2-b^2)\) to be a square. With digit values, the only possibility is \(a^2-b^2=11\).

Step 4: Find the digits

The digit pair satisfying \(a^2-b^2=11\) is \(a=6,b=5\). Thus \(x=65\) and \(y=56\).

Step 5: Find m and finish

Then \(x^2-y^2=1089=33^2\), so \(m=33\). Therefore \[x+y+m=65+56+33=154.\]

Step 6: Conclude

The answer is \(\boxed{154}\).

Key Idea

Use reversed digits and force a square.
