2004 AMC 12B Problem 4

2004AMC 12BProblem 4概率与统计组合概率概率计数

Problem Statement

An integer \(x\), with \(10\le x\le99\), is to be chosen. If all choices are equally likely, what is the probability that at least one digit of \(x\) is a \(7\)?

Choices

  1. A\(\frac19\)
  2. B\(\frac15\)
  3. C\(\frac{19}{90}\)
  4. D\(\frac29\)
  5. E\(\frac13\)

Answer

B. \frac15

Solution

Step 1: Count the sample space

The integers from \(10\) through \(99\) are exactly the \(90\) two-digit integers.

Step 2: Count units digit 7

There are \(10\) numbers with units digit \(7\): \(17,27,\ldots,97\).

Step 3: Count tens digit 7

There are \(10\) numbers from \(70\) through \(79\), but this count includes \(77\), which was already counted in the units-digit group.

Step 4: Apply inclusion-exclusion

The number with at least one digit \(7\) is \(10+10-1=19\) if we include \(07\), but \(07\) is not a two-digit number. Equivalently, tens digit \(7\) gives \(10\) numbers and units digit \(7\) among two-digit numbers gives \(9\) additional numbers outside the 70s, for \(18\) total.

Step 5: Compute probability

The probability is \[\frac{18}{90}=\frac15.\]

Step 6: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{\frac15}$.

Key Idea

Use complement or inclusion-exclusion on two-digit numbers.
