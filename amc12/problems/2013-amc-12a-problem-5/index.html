2013 AMC 12A Problem 5

2013AMC 12AProblem 5代数三角恒等式三角函数恒等式

Problem Statement

Tom, Dorothy, and Sammy went on a vacation and agreed to split the costs evenly. During their trip Tom paid $\$105$, Dorothy paid $\$125$, and Sammy paid $\$175$. In order to share the costs equally, Tom gave Sammy $t$ dollars, and Dorothy gave Sammy $d$ dollars. What is $t-d$?

Choices

  1. A15
  2. B20
  3. C25
  4. D30
  5. E35

Answer

B. 20

Solution

Step 1: Find the equal share

The total cost was \[105+125+175=405.\] Split equally among three people, each person should pay \[405/3=135.\]

Step 2: Find what Tom owes

Tom paid $105$, so he owes \[135-105=30\] dollars. Thus $t=30$.

Step 3: Find what Dorothy owes

Dorothy paid $125$, so she owes \[135-125=10\] dollars. Thus $d=10$.

Step 4: Subtract

\[t-d=30-10=20.\]

Step 5: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{20}$.

Key Idea

Compare each person's payment to the equal share.
