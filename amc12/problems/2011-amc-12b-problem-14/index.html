2011 AMC 12B Problem 14

2011AMC 12BProblem 14几何三角形三角形角度

Problem Statement

A segment through the focus $F$ of a parabola with vertex $V$ is perpendicular to $\overline{FV}$ and intersects the parabola in points $A$ and $B$. What is $\cos(\angle AVB)$?

Choices

  1. A$-\frac{3\sqrt5}{7}$
  2. B$-\frac{2\sqrt5}{5}$
  3. C$-\frac45$
  4. D$-\frac35$
  5. E$-\frac12$

Answer

D. -\frac35

Solution

Step 1: Use a standard coordinate model

Place the vertex at the origin and the focus at $(p,0)$. Then the parabola has equation \[y^2=4px.\]

Step 2: Find A and B

The segment through the focus perpendicular to $\overline{FV}$ is the vertical line $x=p$. Substituting into the parabola gives \[y^2=4p^2,\] so \[y=\pm2p.\] Thus $A=(p,2p)$ and $B=(p,-2p)$.

Step 3: Use vectors from V

The vectors from $V$ to these points are \[\vec{VA}=(p,2p),\qquad \vec{VB}=(p,-2p).\]

Step 4: Compute the cosine

The dot product is \[p^2-4p^2=-3p^2.\] Each vector has length \[p\sqrt5.\] Therefore \[\cos(\angle AVB)=\frac{-3p^2}{(p\sqrt5)(p\sqrt5)}=-\frac35.\]

Step 5: Conclude

The answer is \[\boxed{-\frac35}.\]

Key Idea

Use the standard parabola and latus rectum endpoints.
