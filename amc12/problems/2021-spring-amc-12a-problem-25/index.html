2021 Spring AMC 12A Problem 25

2021 SpringAMC 12AProblem 25数论进制与数字数字进制

Problem Statement

Let $d(n)$ denote the number of positive integers that divide $n$, including $1$ and $n$. Let \[f(n)=\frac{d(n)}{\sqrt[3]{n}}.\] There is a unique positive integer $N$ such that $f(N)>f(n)$ for all positive integers $n\ne N$. What is the sum of the digits of $N$?

Choices

  1. A5
  2. B6
  3. C7
  4. D8
  5. E9

Answer

E. 9

Solution

Step 1: Write the function prime by prime

If

\[n=\prod p^{e_p},\]

then

\[d(n)=\prod(e_p+1)\]

and

\[\sqrt[3]{n}=\prod p^{e_p/3}.\]

Therefore

\[f(n)=\prod_p \frac{e_p+1}{p^{e_p/3}}.\]

Step 2: Maximize one prime at a time

For each fixed prime $p$, we choose the exponent $e$ that maximizes

\[\frac{e+1}{p^{e/3}}.\]

This works because the full expression is a product of independent prime contributions.

Step 3: Check the small primes

For $p=2$, the best exponent is $e=3$, giving contribution

\[\frac4{2}=2.\]

For $p=3$, the best exponent is $e=2$. For $p=5$ and $p=7$, the best exponent is $e=1$.

Step 4: Show larger primes should not appear

For $p\ge11$, even using exponent $1$ gives

\[\frac2{\sqrt[3]{p}}<1.\]

Higher exponents only become worse after the peak. So no prime $p\ge11$ appears in the maximizing integer.

Step 5: Compute N and its digit sum

Thus

\[N=2^3\cdot3^2\cdot5\cdot7=2520.\]

The sum of the digits of $2520$ is

\[2+5+2+0=9.\]

So the answer is $9$.

Key Idea

Factor n and maximize each prime's contribution independently.
