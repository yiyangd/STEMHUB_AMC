2003 AMC 12B Problem 22

2003AMC 12BProblem 22代数运算与化简代数化简

Problem Statement

Let \(ABCD\) be a rhombus with \(AC=16\) and \(BD=30\). Let \(N\) be a point on \(\overline{AB}\), and let \(P\) and \(Q\) be the feet of the perpendiculars from \(N\) to \(\overline{AC}\) and \(\overline{BD}\), respectively. Which is closest to the minimum possible value of \(PQ\)?

Choices

  1. A6.5
  2. B6.75
  3. C7
  4. D7.25
  5. E7.5

Answer

C. 7

Solution

Step 1: Use diagonal coordinates

In a rhombus, the diagonals are perpendicular and bisect each other. Put their intersection at the origin, let \(AC\) be horizontal with endpoints \((-8,0)\) and \((8,0)\), and \(BD\) be vertical with endpoints \((0,-15)\) and \((0,15)\).

Step 2: Interpret P and Q

If \(N=(x,y)\), then the foot to \(AC\) is \(P=(x,0)\), and the foot to \(BD\) is \(Q=(0,y)\). Therefore \[PQ=\sqrt{x^2+y^2},\] the distance from \(N\) to the origin.

Step 3: Minimize over side AB

Point \(N\) lies on side \(AB\), the line through \((-8,0)\) and \((0,15)\). The closest point on this side to the origin gives the minimum \(PQ\).

Step 4: Compute the distance

The line has equation \[15x-8y+120=0.\] The distance from the origin to this line is \[\frac{120}{\sqrt{15^2+8^2}}=\frac{120}{17}\approx7.06.\]

Step 5: Choose the closest answer

The closest listed value is \(\boxed{7}\).

Key Idea

Model the rhombus with perpendicular diagonals and minimize a distance to a side.
