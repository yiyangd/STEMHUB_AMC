2020 AMC 12B Problem 14

2020AMC 12BProblem 14数论整数构造整数构造

Problem Statement

Bela and Jenn play the following game on the closed interval $[0,n]$ of the real number line, where $n$ is a fixed integer greater than $4$. They take turns playing, with Bela going first. On his first turn, Bela chooses any real number in the interval $[0,n]$. Thereafter, the player whose turn it is chooses a real number that is more than one unit away from all numbers previously chosen by either player. A player unable to choose such a number loses. Using optimal strategy, which player will win the game?

Choices

  1. ABela will always win.
  2. BJenn will always win.
  3. CBela will win if and only if $n$ is odd.
  4. DJenn will win if and only if $n$ is odd.
  5. EJenn will win if and only if $n>8$.

Answer

A. Bela will always win.

Solution

Step 1: Look for symmetry

The interval $[0,n]$ is symmetric about its midpoint $\frac n2$. In games on symmetric boards, a common first-player strategy is to take the center and then mirror the opponent.

Step 2: Choose Bela's first move

Bela chooses

\[\frac n2\]

on his first turn. This point is now unavailable, and every other point has a distinct mirror image across the midpoint.

Step 3: Describe the mirror response

Whenever Jenn chooses a point $x$, Bela responds with

\[n-x.\]

This point is not equal to $x$ unless $x=\frac n2$, which Jenn cannot choose because Bela already chose it.

Step 4: Check that the mirror response is legal

The previously chosen points come in mirror pairs, together with the midpoint. If Jenn's point $x$ is more than one unit away from all previously chosen points, then its mirror $n-x$ is also more than one unit away from all mirror images of those points. It is also more than one unit from the midpoint because $x$ was legal.

Step 5: Conclude who gets stuck first

So every legal Jenn move gives Bela a legal response. Since only finitely many points can be chosen with mutual distances more than $1$ in a bounded interval, the game must eventually end. Jenn is the first player who can fail to move. Therefore Bela always wins.

Key Idea

Bela takes the midpoint first, then mirrors every move Jenn makes.
