2021 Spring AMC 12A Problem 23

2021 SpringAMC 12AProblem 23概率与统计几何概率几何概率面积

Problem Statement

Frieda the frog begins a sequence of hops on a $3\times3$ grid of squares, moving one square on each hop and choosing randomly among up, down, left, and right. If a hop would take Frieda off the grid, she wraps around to the opposite edge. Frieda starts from the center square, makes at most four hops, and stops if she lands on a corner square. What is the probability that she reaches a corner square on one of the four hops?

Choices

  1. A$\frac9{16}$
  2. B$\frac58$
  3. C$\frac34$
  4. D$\frac{25}{32}$
  5. E$\frac{13}{16}$

Answer

D. \frac{25}{32}

Solution

Step 1: Classify the positions

On a $3\times3$ toroidal grid, starting at the center, after one hop Frieda is on an edge-middle square. Corners are absorbing because she stops when she reaches one.

Step 2: Track the first two hops

After one hop, she has not reached a corner. From an edge-middle square, two of the four moves go to corners, so by the second hop the probability of having reached a corner is \[\frac12.\]

Step 3: Continue only with non-corner positions

If she has not stopped after two hops, she is either back at the center or at an edge-middle square. Tracking these remaining probabilities through hops three and four gives additional corner-reaching probability \[\frac18+\frac5{32}.\]

Step 4: Add the probabilities

The total probability is \[\frac12+\frac18+\frac5{32}=\frac{16}{32}+\frac4{32}+\frac5{32}=\frac{25}{32}.\]

Step 5: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{\frac{25}{32}}$.

Key Idea

Track probabilities by grid position type under wraparound movement.
