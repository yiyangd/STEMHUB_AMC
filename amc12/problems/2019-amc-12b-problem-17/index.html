2019 AMC 12B Problem 17

2019AMC 12BProblem 17几何三角形三角形角度

Problem Statement

How many nonzero complex numbers $z$ have the property that $0$, $z$, and $z^3$, when represented by points in the complex plane, are the three distinct vertices of an equilateral triangle?

Choices

  1. A0
  2. B1
  3. C2
  4. D4
  5. Einfinitely many

Answer

D. 4

Solution

Step 1: Use the geometry of an equilateral triangle

The points are $0$, $z$, and $z^3$. From the vertex $0$, the two vectors to the other vertices are $z$ and $z^3$. For an equilateral triangle, these vectors must have equal length and differ in direction by $60^\circ$.

Step 2: Translate to a complex equation

The ratio of the two nonzero vectors is \[\frac{z^3}{z}=z^2.\] Therefore $z^2$ must be one of the two complex numbers with magnitude $1$ and argument $\pm60^\circ$: \[z^2=e^{i\pi/3}\quad\text{or}\quad z^2=e^{-i\pi/3}.\]

Step 3: Count square roots

Each of these two nonzero complex numbers has exactly two square roots.

Step 4: Check distinctness

Neither value of $z^2$ is $1$, so $z^3\ne z$. Also $z\ne0$ by construction. Thus the three vertices are distinct.

Step 5: Conclude

There are \[2+2=4\] possible values of $z$, so the answer is $\boxed{4}$.

Key Idea

Translate the equilateral condition into multiplication by a complex cube root of unity.
