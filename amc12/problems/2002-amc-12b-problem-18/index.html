2002 AMC 12B Problem 18

2002AMC 12BProblem 18概率与统计组合概率概率计数

Problem Statement

A point \(P\) is randomly selected from the rectangular region with vertices \((0,0)\), \((2,0)\), \((2,1)\), and \((0,1)\). What is the probability that \(P\) is closer to the origin than it is to the point \((3,1)\)?

Choices

  1. A$\frac12$
  2. B$\frac23$
  3. C$\frac35$
  4. D$\frac34$
  5. E$\frac45$

Answer

D. \frac34

Solution

Step 1: Translate closer-than condition

A point \((x,y)\) is closer to \((0,0)\) than to \((3,1)\) when \[x^2+y^2<(x-3)^2+(y-1)^2.\]

Step 2: Simplify

Expanding and canceling gives \[6x+2y<10,\] or \[3x+y<5.\]

Step 3: Find the favorable area

In the rectangle \(0\le x\le2\), \(0\le y\le1\), the line \(3x+y=5\) crosses the top edge at \(x=\frac43\) and the bottom edge at \(x=\frac53\). The favorable area is a rectangle of area \(\frac43\) plus a right triangle of area \(\frac16\).

Step 4: Compute probability

The favorable area is \[\frac43+\frac16=\frac32.\] The rectangle area is \(2\), so the probability is \[\frac{3/2}{2}=\frac34.\]

Step 5: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{\frac34}$.

Key Idea

Use the perpendicular-bisector half-plane and area in a rectangle.
