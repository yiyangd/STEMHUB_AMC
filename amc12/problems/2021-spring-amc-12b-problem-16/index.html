2021 Spring AMC 12B Problem 16

2021 SpringAMC 12BProblem 16代数多项式与根多项式根

Problem Statement

Let $g(x)$ be a monic polynomial whose three roots are the reciprocals of the three roots of \[f(x)=x^3+ax^2+bx+c,\] where $1<a<b<c$. What is $g(1)$ in terms of $a,b,$ and $c$?

Choices

  1. A$\frac{1+a+b+c}{c}$
  2. B$1+a+b+c$
  3. C$\frac{1+a+b+c}{c^2}$
  4. D$\frac{a+b+c}{c^2}$
  5. E$\frac{1+a+b+c}{a+b+c}$

Answer

A. \frac{1+a+b+c}{c}

Solution

Step 1: Let the roots of f be r1, r2, r3

Write the roots of $f$ as $r_1,r_2,r_3$. Since $f$ is monic, \[f(x)=(x-r_1)(x-r_2)(x-r_3).\]

Step 2: Build g from reciprocal roots

The roots of $g$ are \[\frac1{r_1},\frac1{r_2},\frac1{r_3},\] so \[g(1)=\prod_{i=1}^3\left(1-\frac1{r_i}\right)=\frac{\prod_{i=1}^3(r_i-1)}{r_1r_2r_3}.\]

Step 3: Relate to f(1)

We have \[f(1)=\prod_{i=1}^3(1-r_i).\] Because there are three factors, \[\prod(r_i-1)=-f(1).\] Also, for $x^3+ax^2+bx+c$, \[r_1r_2r_3=-c.\]

Step 4: Simplify

Therefore \[g(1)=\frac{-f(1)}{-c}=\frac{f(1)}{c}.\] Since \[f(1)=1+a+b+c,\]

Step 5: Conclude

We get \[g(1)=\boxed{\frac{1+a+b+c}{c}}.\]

Key Idea

Use roots and reciprocal roots to evaluate the monic transformed polynomial at 1.
