2015 AMC 12A Problem 7

2015AMC 12AProblem 7几何空间几何空间几何体积

Problem Statement

Two right circular cylinders have the same volume. The radius of the second cylinder is $10\%$ more than the radius of the first. What is the relationship between the heights of the two cylinders?

Choices

  1. AThe second height is $10\%$ less than the first.
  2. BThe first height is $10\%$ more than the second.
  3. CThe second height is $21\%$ less than the first.
  4. DThe first height is $21\%$ more than the second.
  5. EThe second height is $80\%$ of the first.

Answer

D. The first height is 21% more than the second.

Solution

Step 1: Use the volume formula

A cylinder has volume \[V=\pi r^2h.\] Equal volumes mean \[r_1^2h_1=r_2^2h_2.\]

Step 2: Use the radius relationship

The second radius is $10\%$ more than the first, so \[r_2=1.1r_1.\]

Step 3: Solve for the height relationship

\[h_2=\frac{r_1^2}{(1.1r_1)^2}h_1=\frac{1}{1.21}h_1=\frac{100}{121}h_1.\]

Step 4: Interpret the comparison

This means \[h_1=\frac{121}{100}h_2=1.21h_2.\] So the first height is $21\%$ more than the second.

Step 5: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{\text{D}}$.

Key Idea

Equal cylinder volumes imply height is inversely proportional to radius squared.
