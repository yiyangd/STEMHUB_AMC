2006 AMC 12A Problem 5

2006AMC 12AProblem 5数论整除与同余同余余数

Problem Statement

Doug and Dave shared a pizza with \(8\) equally-sized slices. Doug wanted a plain pizza, but Dave wanted anchovies on half the pizza. The cost of a plain pizza was \(\$8\), and there was an additional cost of \(\$2\) for putting anchovies on one half. Dave ate all the slices of anchovy pizza and one plain slice. Doug ate the remainder. Each paid for what he had eaten. How many more dollars did Dave pay than Doug?

Choices

  1. A1
  2. B2
  3. C3
  4. D4
  5. E5

Answer

D. 4

Solution

Step 1: Split the pizza into two halves

The topping is placed on exactly half of the pizza. So it is natural to price the anchovy half and the plain half separately.

Step 2: Price the plain half

A plain pizza costs \(\$8\), so half of the plain base cost is \(\$4\). The plain half has \(4\) slices, so each plain slice costs \(\$1\).

Step 3: Price the anchovy half

The anchovy half has the same \(\$4\) base cost plus \(\$2\) for anchovies, for a total of \(\$6\). Since it has \(4\) slices, those slices together cost \(\$6\).

Step 4: Find what Dave paid

Dave ate all \(4\) anchovy slices, costing \(\$6\), and one plain slice, costing \(\$1\). So Dave paid \(\$7\).

Step 5: Find what Doug paid and compare

Doug ate the remaining \(3\) plain slices, costing \(\$3\). The difference is \[7-3=4.\]

Step 6: Conclude

Dave paid \(\boxed{4}\) more dollars than Doug.

Key Idea

Price the anchovy half and plain half separately, then assign slices to each person.
