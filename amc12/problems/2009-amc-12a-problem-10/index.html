2009 AMC 12A Problem 10

2009AMC 12AProblem 10几何三角形三角形角度

Problem Statement

In quadrilateral \(ABCD\), \(AB=5\), \(BC=17\), \(CD=5\), \(DA=9\), and \(BD\) is an integer. What is \(BD\)?

Choices

  1. A11
  2. B12
  3. C13
  4. D14
  5. E15

Answer

C. 13

Solution

Step 1: Let BD be x

Diagonal \(\overline{BD}\) forms triangles \(ABD\) and \(BCD\). Let \(BD=x\).

Step 2: Use triangle BCD

In triangle \(BCD\), the sides are \(17\), \(5\), and \(x\). The triangle inequality requires \[5+x>17,\] so \(x>12\).

Step 3: Use triangle ABD

In triangle \(ABD\), the sides are \(5\), \(9\), and \(x\). The triangle inequality requires \[5+9>x,\] so \(x<14\).

Step 4: Use the integer condition

The only integer satisfying \(12<x<14\) is \(x=13\).

Step 5: Conclude

\(BD=\boxed{13}\).

Key Idea

Use triangle inequalities on triangles \(ABD\) and \(BCD\).
