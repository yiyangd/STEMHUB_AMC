2018 AMC 12A Problem 14

2018AMC 12AProblem 14数论质因数与整除质因数整除

Problem Statement

The solution to the equation \[\log_{3x}4=\log_{2x}8,\] where \(x\) is a positive real number other than \(\frac13\) or \(\frac12\), can be written as \(\frac pq\), where \(p\) and \(q\) are relatively prime positive integers. What is \(p+q\)?

Choices

  1. A5
  2. B13
  3. C17
  4. D31
  5. E35

Answer

D. \frac4{27}

Solution

Step 1: Change to natural logarithms

The equation is \[\frac{\ln4}{\ln(3x)}=\frac{\ln8}{\ln(2x)}.\] Since \(\ln4=2\ln2\) and \(\ln8=3\ln2\), we can cancel \(\ln2\).

Step 2: Cross-multiply

We get \[2\ln(2x)=3\ln(3x).\]

Step 3: Rewrite as one logarithm

This means \[\ln((2x)^2)=\ln((3x)^3).\] Therefore \[(2x)^2=(3x)^3.\]

Step 4: Solve for x

Since \(x>0\), \[4x^2=27x^3,\] so \[x=\frac4{27}.\]

Step 5: Add numerator and denominator

Thus \(p=4\), \(q=27\), and \[p+q=31.\]

Step 6: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{31}$.

Key Idea

Use logarithm change of base and compare powers.
