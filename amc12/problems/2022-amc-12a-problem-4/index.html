2022 AMC 12A Problem 4

2022AMC 12AProblem 4数论进制与数字数字进制

Problem Statement

The least common multiple of a positive integer $n$ and $18$ is $180$, and the greatest common divisor of $n$ and $45$ is $15$. What is the sum of the digits of $n$?

Choices

  1. A3
  2. B6
  3. C8
  4. D9
  5. E12

Answer

B. 6

Solution

Step 1: Factor the fixed numbers

Prime factorization keeps both LCM and GCD information visible: \[18=2\cdot3^2,\quad 180=2^2\cdot3^2\cdot5,\quad 45=3^2\cdot5,\quad 15=3\cdot5.\]

Step 2: Use the LCM condition

For $\operatorname{lcm}(n,18)$ to be $180$, $n$ must contribute a second factor of $2$ and one factor of $5$. Also, $n$ cannot have any prime factor not already appearing in $180$.

Step 3: Use the GCD condition

The condition $\gcd(n,45)=15$ means $n$ has exactly one factor of $3$ in common with $45$, and it has a factor of $5$.

Step 4: Determine n

Putting the prime exponents together gives \[n=2^2\cdot3\cdot5=60.\]

Step 5: Conclude

The sum of the digits of $60$ is $6+0=6$, so the answer is $\boxed{6}$.

Key Idea

Use prime exponents to satisfy both LCM and GCD conditions.
