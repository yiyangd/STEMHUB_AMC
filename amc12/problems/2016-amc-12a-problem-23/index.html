2016 AMC 12A Problem 23

2016AMC 12AProblem 23概率与统计几何概率几何概率面积

Problem Statement

Three numbers in the interval $[0,1]$ are chosen independently and at random. What is the probability that the chosen numbers are the side lengths of a triangle with positive area?

Choices

  1. A$\frac16$
  2. B$\frac13$
  3. C$\frac12$
  4. D$\frac23$
  5. E$\frac56$

Answer

C. \frac12

Solution

Step 1: Use the triangle inequality

Three positive lengths form a triangle exactly when no one length is at least the sum of the other two. Since the numbers are chosen continuously, equality has probability $0$, so we can use $>$ or $\ge$ without changing the probability.

Step 2: Count the complement for one side

Let the three chosen numbers be $x,y,z$. Consider the bad event \(x\ge y+z\). For fixed $y$ and $z$ with $y+z\le1$, the variable $x$ can range from $y+z$ to $1$, a length of \(1-y-z\).

Step 3: Compute that volume

The probability volume for \(x\ge y+z\) is \[\int_0^1\int_0^{1-y}(1-y-z)\,dz\,dy=\frac16.\]

Step 4: Use symmetry

The same probability applies to \(y\ge x+z\) and to \(z\ge x+y\). These three bad events cannot overlap except on boundary cases of probability $0$.

Step 5: Subtract from 1

The total bad probability is \[3\cdot\frac16=\frac12.\] Therefore the good probability is \[1-\frac12=\frac12.\]

Step 6: Conclude

The probability is \[\boxed{\frac12}.\]

Key Idea

Use the complement: one chosen length is at least the sum of the other two.
