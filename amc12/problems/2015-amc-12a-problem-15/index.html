2015 AMC 12A Problem 15

2015AMC 12AProblem 15数论进制与数字数字进制

Problem Statement

What is the minimum number of digits to the right of the decimal point needed to express the fraction \[\frac{123456789}{2^{26}\cdot5^4}\] as a decimal?

Choices

  1. A4
  2. B22
  3. C26
  4. D30
  5. E104

Answer

C. 26

Solution

Step 1: Check whether the fraction reduces by 2 or 5

The numerator $123456789$ is not divisible by $2$ or $5$, so the denominator keeps the factors \[2^{26}\cdot5^4.\]

Step 2: Make the denominator a power of 10

A decimal with $n$ digits after the decimal point has denominator dividing $10^n=2^n5^n$. We need at least $26$ factors of $2$ and at least $4$ factors of $5$.

Step 3: Choose the larger exponent

The limiting exponent is $26$, so $10^{26}$ is enough after multiplying by extra factors of $5$.

Step 4: Conclude

The minimum number of digits needed is $\boxed{26}$.

Key Idea

A terminating decimal denominator needs only powers of 2 and 5.
