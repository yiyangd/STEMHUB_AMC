2020 AMC 12B Problem 23

2020AMC 12BProblem 23几何圆与曲线圆曲线

Problem Statement

How many integers $n\ge2$ are there such that whenever $z_1,z_2,\ldots,z_n$ are complex numbers satisfying \[|z_1|=|z_2|=\cdots=|z_n|=1\] and \[z_1+z_2+\cdots+z_n=0,\] then the numbers $z_1,z_2,\ldots,z_n$ are equally spaced on the unit circle in the complex plane?

Choices

  1. A1
  2. B2
  3. C3
  4. D4
  5. E5

Answer

B. 2

Solution

Step 1: Check n = 2

If $z_1+z_2=0$, then $z_2=-z_1$. These two points are opposite endpoints of a diameter, so they are equally spaced on the unit circle.

Step 2: Check n = 3

If three unit complex numbers add to $0$, their vector sum forms a closed equilateral triangle. Equivalently, after rotating the picture, the only possibility is

\[1,\omega,\omega^2,\]

where $\omega$ is a primitive cube root of unity. Thus the three points are equally spaced.

Step 3: Build counterexamples for even n at least 4

If $n$ is even and $n\ge4$, take $\frac n2$ copies of $1$ and $\frac n2$ copies of $-1$. The sum is $0$, and every number has absolute value $1$. But because values are repeated, the points are not $n$ equally spaced points around the circle.

Step 4: Build counterexamples for odd n at least 5

If $n$ is odd and $n\ge5$, use the three cube roots

\[1,\omega,\omega^2\]

whose sum is $0$, and then add $\frac{n-3}{2}$ pairs $1,-1$. The total sum is still $0$, but the resulting list has repeated points and is not equally spaced.

Step 5: Count the valid n-values

The property holds for $n=2$ and $n=3$, and fails for every $n\ge4$. Therefore the number of integers $n\ge2$ with the stated property is

\[2.\]

Key Idea

Only $n=2$ and $n=3$ force a regular configuration; all larger n have simple counterexamples.
