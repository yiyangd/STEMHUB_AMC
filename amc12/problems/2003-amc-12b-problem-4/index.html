2003 AMC 12B Problem 4

2003AMC 12BProblem 4代数三角恒等式三角函数恒等式

Problem Statement

Moe uses a mower to cut his rectangular \(90\)-foot by \(150\)-foot lawn. The swath he cuts is \(28\) inches wide, but he overlaps each cut by \(4\) inches. He walks at \(5000\) feet per hour while pushing the mower. Which is closest to the number of hours it will take?

Choices

  1. A0.75
  2. B0.8
  3. C1.35
  4. D1.5
  5. E3

Answer

C. 1.35

Solution

Step 1: Find the effective width

The mower cuts \(28\) inches, but each pass overlaps by \(4\) inches, so each new pass covers \(24\) inches, or \(2\) feet.

Step 2: Compute total walking distance

To cover a \(90\)-foot width, Moe needs \(90/2=45\) passes. Each pass is \(150\) feet, so he walks \[45\cdot150=6750\] feet.

Step 3: Convert to time

At \(5000\) feet per hour, the time is \[\frac{6750}{5000}=1.35\] hours.

Step 4: Conclude

The closest choice is $\boxed{1.35}$.

Key Idea

Use the effective mower width after overlap.
