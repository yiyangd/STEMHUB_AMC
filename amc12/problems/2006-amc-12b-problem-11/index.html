2006 AMC 12B Problem 11

2006AMC 12BProblem 11代数运算与化简代数化简

Problem Statement

Joe and JoAnn each bought \(12\) ounces of coffee in a \(16\)-ounce cup. Joe drank \(2\) ounces of his coffee and then added \(2\) ounces of cream. JoAnn added \(2\) ounces of cream, stirred the coffee well, and then drank \(2\) ounces. What is the resulting ratio of the amount of cream in Joe's coffee to that in JoAnn's coffee?

Choices

  1. A\(\frac67\)
  2. B\(\frac{13}{14}\)
  3. C1
  4. D\(\frac{14}{13}\)
  5. E\(\frac76\)

Answer

E. \frac76

Solution

Step 1: Track Joe first

Joe starts with \(12\) ounces of coffee. He drinks \(2\) ounces of coffee, leaving \(10\) ounces of coffee, and then adds \(2\) ounces of cream. So Joe has \(2\) ounces of cream.

Step 2: Track JoAnn's mixture

JoAnn first adds \(2\) ounces of cream to \(12\) ounces of coffee, making \(14\) ounces total. The cream fraction is \(\frac{2}{14}=\frac17\).

Step 3: Find how much cream JoAnn drinks

When JoAnn drinks \(2\) ounces of the well-stirred mixture, she drinks \[2\cdot\frac17=\frac27\] ounces of cream.

Step 4: Find JoAnn's remaining cream

She started with \(2\) ounces of cream, so she has \[2-\frac27=\frac{12}{7}\] ounces of cream left.

Step 5: Form the ratio

The ratio of Joe's cream to JoAnn's cream is \[2:\frac{12}{7}=\frac{2}{12/7}=\frac76.\]

Step 6: Conclude

The answer is \(\boxed{\frac76}\).

Key Idea

Track cream amounts after mixing and drinking.
