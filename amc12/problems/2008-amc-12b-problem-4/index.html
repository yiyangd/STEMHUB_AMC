2008 AMC 12B Problem 4

2008AMC 12BProblem 4几何圆与曲线圆曲线

Problem Statement

On circle \(O\), points \(C\) and \(D\) are on the same side of diameter \(\overline{AB}\), \(\angle AOC=30^\circ\), and \(\angle DOB=45^\circ\). What is the ratio of the area of the smaller sector \(COD\) to the area of the circle?

Choices

  1. A\(\frac29\)
  2. B\(\frac14\)
  3. C\(\frac5{18}\)
  4. D\(\frac7{24}\)
  5. E\(\frac3{10}\)

Answer

D. \frac7{24}

Solution

Step 1: Use the semicircle

Since \(\overline{AB}\) is a diameter, the central angle from \(OA\) to \(OB\) on one side is \(180^\circ\). Points \(C\) and \(D\) lie on that same side.

Step 2: Subtract the given end angles

We are given \(\angle AOC=30^\circ\) and \(\angle DOB=45^\circ\). Therefore the central angle between \(OC\) and \(OD\) is \[180^\circ-30^\circ-45^\circ=105^\circ.\]

Step 3: Convert sector angle to area ratio

A sector's area is the same fraction of the circle's area as its central angle is of \(360^\circ\).

Step 4: Compute the ratio

\[\frac{105}{360}=\frac7{24}.\]

Step 5: Conclude

The answer is \(\boxed{\frac7{24}}\).
