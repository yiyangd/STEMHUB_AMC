2002 AMC 12B Problem 17

2002AMC 12BProblem 17几何面积与相似面积比例

Problem Statement

Andy’s lawn has twice as much area as Beth’s lawn and three times as much area as Carlos’ lawn. Carlos’ lawn mower cuts half as fast as Beth’s mower and one third as fast as Andy’s mower. If they all start to mow their lawns at the same time, who will finish first?

Choices

  1. AAndy
  2. BBeth
  3. CCarlos
  4. DAndy and Carlos tie for first
  5. EAll three tie

Answer

B. Beth

Solution

Step 1: Choose units

Let Beth's lawn area be \(1\). Then Andy's area is \(2\), and Carlos' area is \(\frac23\).

Step 2: Choose mowing rates

Let Carlos' mowing rate be \(c\). Then Beth's rate is \(2c\), and Andy's rate is \(3c\).

Step 3: Compute times

Time equals area divided by rate. Andy's time is \[\frac{2}{3c},\] Beth's time is \[\frac{1}{2c},\] and Carlos' time is \[\frac{2/3}{c}=\frac{2}{3c}.\]

Step 4: Compare

Beth's time \(\frac{1}{2c}\) is less than \(\frac{2}{3c}\), so Beth finishes first.

Step 5: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{\text{Beth}}$.

Key Idea

Compare mowing time as area divided by rate.
