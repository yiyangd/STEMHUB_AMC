2018 AMC 12A Problem 20

2018AMC 12AProblem 20几何面积与相似面积比例

Problem Statement

Triangle $ABC$ is an isosceles right triangle with $AB=AC=3$. Let $M$ be the midpoint of hypotenuse $\overline{BC}$. Points $I$ and $E$ lie on sides $\overline{AC}$ and $\overline{AB}$, respectively, so that $AI>AE$ and $AIME$ is a cyclic quadrilateral. Given that triangle $EMI$ has area $2$, the length $CI$ can be written as $\frac{a-\sqrt b}{c}$, where $a$, $b$, and $c$ are positive integers and $b$ is not divisible by the square of any prime. What is the value of $a+b+c$?

Choices

  1. A$9$
  2. B$10$
  3. C$11$
  4. D$12$
  5. E$13$

Answer

D. $12$

Solution

Step 1: Set up coordinates that match the right triangle

Place

\[A=(0,0),\quad B=(3,0),\quad C=(0,3).\]

Then the midpoint of the hypotenuse is

\[M=\left(\frac32,\frac32\right).\]

Let

\[E=(e,0),\qquad I=(0,i).\]

The condition $AI>AE$ means $i>e$.

Step 2: Use the cyclic condition

The circle through $A$, $E$, and $I$ has equation

\[x^2+y^2-ex-iy=0.\]

Since $M$ is also on this circle,

\[\left(\frac32\right)^2+\left(\frac32\right)^2-\frac32e-\frac32i=0.\]

This simplifies to

\[e+i=3.\]

Step 3: Use the area of triangle EMI

The area of $\triangle EMI$ can be computed from coordinates:

\[\text{Area}=\frac12\left(\frac32(e+i)-ei\right).\]

Since $e+i=3$ and the area is $2$,

\[2=\frac12\left(\frac92-ei\right).\]

Thus

\[ei=\frac12.\]

Step 4: Solve for e and i

Now $e$ and $i$ are the two roots of

\[t^2-3t+\frac12=0.\]

Using the quadratic formula,

\[t=\frac{3\pm\sqrt7}{2}.\]

Because $i>e$, we have

\[i=\frac{3+\sqrt7}{2}.\]

Step 5: Find CI

The length $CI$ is the distance from $C=(0,3)$ to $I=(0,i)$, so

\[CI=3-i.\]

Therefore

\[CI=3-\frac{3+\sqrt7}{2}=\frac{3-\sqrt7}{2}.\]

Step 6: Read off the requested sum

The expression has the form

\[\frac{a-\sqrt b}{c}=\frac{3-\sqrt7}{2}.\]

So $a=3$, $b=7$, and $c=2$, giving

\[a+b+c=3+7+2=12.\]

Key Idea

Use coordinates and the cyclic condition to relate AE and AI, then use the area condition.
