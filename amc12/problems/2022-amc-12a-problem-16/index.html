2022 AMC 12A Problem 16

2022AMC 12AProblem 16几何三角形三角形角度

Problem Statement

A triangular number is a positive integer that can be expressed in the form $t_n=1+2+3+\cdots+n$ for some positive integer $n$. The three smallest triangular numbers that are also perfect squares are $t_1=1=1^2$, $t_8=36=6^2$, and $t_{49}=1225=35^2$. What is the sum of the digits of the fourth smallest triangular number that is also a perfect square?

Choices

  1. A6
  2. B9
  3. C12
  4. D18
  5. E27

Answer

D. 18

Solution

Step 1: Convert to a Pell equation

If $t_n$ is a square, write \[\frac{n(n+1)}2=m^2.\] Multiplying by $8$ and completing the square gives \[(2n+1)^2-8m^2=1.\]

Step 2: Use the recurrence for solutions

Solutions to $u^2-8m^2=1$ are generated by multiplying $u+m\sqrt8$ by $3+\sqrt8$. The third given square triangular number has \[n=49,\quad m=35,\quad u=2n+1=99.\]

Step 3: Generate the next solution

Multiplying gives \[(99+35\sqrt8)(3+\sqrt8)=577+204\sqrt8.\] Thus the next square triangular number has $m=204$.

Step 4: Find the triangular number

The fourth square triangular number is therefore \[204^2=41616.\]

Step 5: Sum its digits

The digit sum is \[4+1+6+1+6=18.\]

Step 6: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{18}$.

Key Idea

Transform square triangular numbers into a Pell-type recurrence.
