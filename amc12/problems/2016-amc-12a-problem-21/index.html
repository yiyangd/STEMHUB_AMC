2016 AMC 12A Problem 21

2016AMC 12AProblem 21几何圆与曲线圆曲线

Problem Statement

A quadrilateral is inscribed in a circle of radius $200\sqrt2$. Three of the sides of this quadrilateral have length $200$. What is the length of the fourth side?

Choices

  1. A200
  2. B$200\sqrt2$
  3. C$200\sqrt3$
  4. D$300\sqrt2$
  5. E500

Answer

E. 500

Solution

Step 1: Convert the repeated side length to a central angle

Each side of a cyclic quadrilateral is a chord. Let the central angle for a chord of length $200$ be $\theta$. Since the radius is $200\sqrt2$, \[200=2(200\sqrt2)\sin\frac{\theta}{2},\] so \[\sin\frac{\theta}{2}=\frac{1}{2\sqrt2}.\]

Step 2: Use the arcs around the circle

The three equal sides use three equal central angles, each equal to $\theta$. The fourth side corresponds to the remaining central angle \[2\pi-3\theta.\]

Step 3: Write the fourth chord length

The fourth chord length is \[2(200\sqrt2)\sin\left(\frac{2\pi-3\theta}{2}\right)=400\sqrt2\sin\left(\pi-\frac{3\theta}{2}\right).\] Since $\sin(\pi-u)=\sin u$, this is \[400\sqrt2\sin\frac{3\theta}{2}.\]

Step 4: Apply the triple-angle identity

Let $\alpha=\frac{\theta}{2}$. We know \(\sin\alpha=\frac{1}{2\sqrt2}\). Then \[\sin3\alpha=3\sin\alpha-4\sin^3\alpha=\frac{5}{4\sqrt2}.\]

Step 5: Compute the chord

Thus the fourth side has length \[400\sqrt2\cdot\frac{5}{4\sqrt2}=500.\]

Step 6: Conclude

The length of the fourth side is $\boxed{500}$.

Key Idea

Convert equal chord lengths into equal central angles and use a triple-angle identity.
