2022 AMC 12B Problem 23

2022AMC 12BProblem 23数论整除与同余同余余数

Problem Statement

Let $x_0,x_1,x_2,\ldots$ be a sequence of numbers, where each $x_k$ is either $0$ or $1$. For each positive integer $n$, define \[S_n=\sum_{k=0}^{n-1}x_k2^k.\] Suppose $7S_n\equiv1\pmod{2^n}$ for all $n\ge1$. What is \[x_{2019}+2x_{2020}+4x_{2021}+8x_{2022}?\]

Choices

  1. A6
  2. B7
  3. C12
  4. D14
  5. E15

Answer

A. 6

Solution

Step 1: Interpret the congruence

The number $S_n$ is the integer whose first $n$ binary digits are $x_0,x_1,\ldots,x_{n-1}$. The condition says that these prefixes represent the inverse of $7$ modulo powers of $2$.

Step 2: Find the inverse pattern

Since $7=8-1$, we have formally \[\frac1{7}=\frac1{8-1}=-\frac1{1-8}.\] In modulo powers of $2$, this produces a repeating binary pattern after adjustment: \[1+6(1+8+8^2+\cdots).\]

Step 3: Read the bits

The binary digits are \[1,1,1,0,1,1,0,1,1,0,\ldots.\] After the first digit, the pattern $1,1,0$ repeats.

Step 4: Locate the needed indices

For $k\ge1$, the digit $x_k$ is $0$ exactly when $k$ is divisible by $3$, and is $1$ otherwise.

Step 5: Apply this to the four indices

We have \[2019\equiv0,\quad2020\equiv1,\quad2021\equiv2,\quad2022\equiv0\pmod3.\] Thus \[x_{2019},x_{2020},x_{2021},x_{2022}=0,1,1,0.\]

Step 6: Conclude

The requested value is \[0+2(1)+4(1)+8(0)=6.\] The answer is $\boxed{6}$.

Key Idea

Interpret the congruence as the binary expansion of the 2-adic inverse of 7.
