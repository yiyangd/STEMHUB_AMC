2021 Spring AMC 12A Problem 24

2021 SpringAMC 12AProblem 24几何圆与曲线圆曲线

Problem Statement

Semicircle $\Gamma$ has diameter $AB$ of length $14$. Circle $\Omega$ lies tangent to $AB$ at a point $P$ and intersects $\Gamma$ at points $Q$ and $R$. If $QR=3\sqrt3$ and $\angle QPR=60^\circ$, then the area of $\triangle PQR$ is $\frac{a\sqrt b}{c}$, where $a$ and $c$ are relatively prime positive integers, and $b$ is a positive integer not divisible by the square of any prime. What is $a+b+c$?

Choices

  1. A$110$
  2. B$114$
  3. C$118$
  4. D$122$
  5. E$126$

Answer

D. $122$

Solution

Step 1: Find the radius of circle Omega

The points $P,Q,R$ all lie on circle $\Omega$, and $\angle QPR=60^\circ$ subtends chord $\overline{QR}$.

If the radius of $\Omega$ is $r$, then

\[QR=2r\sin60^\circ=r\sqrt3.\]

Since $QR=3\sqrt3$, we get

\[r=3.\]

Step 2: Set up coordinates

Put the center of the semicircle at $C=(0,0)$ and let $AB$ be the $x$-axis. The semicircle has radius $7$.

Since $\Omega$ is tangent to $AB$ at $P$, write

\[P=(p,0),\qquad O=(p,3),\]

where $O$ is the center of $\Omega$.

Step 3: Use the common chord distance

The common chord $QR$ has length $3\sqrt3$. In circle $\Omega$, the distance from $O$ to the chord is

\[\sqrt{3^2-\left(\frac{3\sqrt3}{2}\right)^2}=\frac32.\]

In the larger circle $\Gamma$, the distance from $C$ to the same chord is

\[\sqrt{7^2-\left(\frac{3\sqrt3}{2}\right)^2}=\frac{13}{2}.\]

Step 4: Find the distance between the centers

The chord lies on the far side of $O$ from $C$, so the distance between the centers is

\[CO=\frac{13}{2}-\frac32=5.\]

But

\[CO=\sqrt{p^2+3^2},\]

so

\[p^2+9=25,\qquad p=4\]

up to symmetry.

Step 5: Find the distance from P to QR

The line $CO$ has direction vector $(4,3)$, so the common chord $QR$ is perpendicular to that direction. Its foot from $C$ is $\frac{13}{2}$ units along the direction from $C$ to $O$, giving the chord line

\[4x+3y=\frac{65}{2}.\]

The distance from $P=(4,0)$ to this line is

\[\frac{|4\cdot4+3\cdot0-\frac{65}{2}|}{\sqrt{4^2+3^2}}=\frac{33}{10}.\]

Step 6: Compute the area

Now use base $QR=3\sqrt3$ and height $\frac{33}{10}$:

\[\text{Area}=\frac12\cdot3\sqrt3\cdot\frac{33}{10}=\frac{99\sqrt3}{20}.\]

Thus $a=99$, $b=3$, and $c=20$, so

\[a+b+c=99+3+20=122.\]

Key Idea

Use the chord length in circle Omega to find its radius, then locate the common chord of the two circles.
