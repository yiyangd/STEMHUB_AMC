2009 AMC 12B Problem 23

2009AMC 12BProblem 23概率与统计组合概率概率计数

Problem Statement

A region $S$ in the complex plane is defined by \[S=\{x+iy:-1\le x\le1,\ -1\le y\le1\}.\] A complex number $z=x+iy$ is chosen uniformly at random from $S$. What is the probability that \[\left(\frac34+\frac34 i\right)z\] is also in $S$?

Choices

  1. A$\frac12$
  2. B$\frac23$
  3. C$\frac34$
  4. D$\frac79$
  5. E$\frac78$

Answer

D. \frac79

Solution

Step 1: Interpret complex multiplication geometrically

Multiplying by $\frac34+\frac34 i$ sends \[x+iy\mapsto \frac34(x-y)+i\frac34(x+y).\] So the new point stays in $S$ exactly when \[\left|\frac34(x-y)\right|\le1\quad\text{and}\quad \left|\frac34(x+y)\right|\le1.\]

Step 2: Rewrite the inequalities

These conditions are \[|x-y|\le\frac43,\qquad |x+y|\le\frac43.\] We need the fraction of the original square $-1\le x,y\le1$ that also satisfies these two inequalities.

Step 3: Use a rotated coordinate system

Let $u=x+y$ and $v=x-y$. The original square becomes the diamond \[|u+v|\le2,\qquad |u-v|\le2,\] while the new conditions become the square \[|u|\le\frac43,\qquad |v|\le\frac43.\]

Step 4: Find the intersection area in uv-coordinates

The square $|u|,|v|\le\frac43$ has area $\frac{64}{9}$. At each of its four corners, a right triangle of leg length $\frac23$ lies outside the diamond. Each such triangle has area $\frac12\cdot\frac23\cdot\frac23=\frac29$, so the intersection has area \[\frac{64}{9}-4\cdot\frac29=\frac{56}{9}.\]

Step 5: Convert back to probability

The change of variables has area scale factor $2$, so the favorable area in the original $xy$-plane is $\frac12\cdot\frac{56}{9}=\frac{28}{9}$. The original square has area $4$, giving probability \[\frac{28/9}{4}=\frac79.\]

Step 6: Conclude

The answer is \[\boxed{\frac79}.\]

Key Idea

Transform the square under complex multiplication and compute an area ratio.
