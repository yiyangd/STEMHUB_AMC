2016 AMC 12B Problem 19

2016AMC 12BProblem 19概率与统计组合概率概率计数

Problem Statement

Tom, Dick, and Harry are playing a game. Starting at the same time, each of them flips a fair coin repeatedly until he gets his first head, at which point he stops. What is the probability that all three flip their coins the same number of times?

Choices

  1. A$\frac18$
  2. B$\frac17$
  3. C$\frac16$
  4. D$\frac14$
  5. E$\frac13$

Answer

B. \frac17

Solution

Step 1: Find one person's stopping probability

The probability that a player first gets heads on flip \(n\) is \[\left(\frac12\right)^n,\] because the first \(n-1\) flips must be tails and the \(n\)th flip must be heads.

Step 2: Require all three to stop on the same n

For a fixed \(n\), the probability that all three stop on flip \(n\) is \[\left(\frac1{2^n}\right)^3=\frac1{8^n}.\]

Step 3: Sum over all possible n

The desired probability is \[\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac1{8^n}.\] This is a geometric series with first term \(\frac18\) and ratio \(\frac18\).

Step 4: Evaluate the series

The sum is \[\frac{1/8}{1-1/8}=\frac{1/8}{7/8}=\frac17.\]

Step 5: Conclude

The probability is \[\boxed{\frac17}.\]

Key Idea

Sum the probabilities that all three first heads occur on the same flip number.
