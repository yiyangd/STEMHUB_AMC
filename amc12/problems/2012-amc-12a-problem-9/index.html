2012 AMC 12A Problem 9

2012AMC 12AProblem 9数论质因数与整除质因数整除

Problem Statement

A year is a leap year if and only if the year number is divisible by $400$ or is divisible by $4$ but not by $100$. The $200$th anniversary of the birth of novelist Charles Dickens was celebrated on February $7,2012$, a Tuesday. On what day of the week was Dickens born?

Choices

  1. AFriday
  2. BSaturday
  3. CSunday
  4. DMonday
  5. ETuesday

Answer

A. Friday

Solution

Step 1: Count ordinary days

From February $7,1812$ to February $7,2012$ is $200$ years. Ignoring leap days, that is \[200\cdot365\] days.

Step 2: Count leap days

The leap years contributing February $29$ in this interval are $1812,1816,\ldots,2008$, except $1900$ because it is divisible by $100$ but not by $400$. This gives $49$ leap days.

Step 3: Find the weekday shift

The total number of days is \[200\cdot365+49=73049.\] Modulo $7$, this is congruent to $4$.

Step 4: Move backward from Tuesday

February $7,2012$ was a Tuesday. Dickens's birthday was $4$ weekdays earlier in the cycle, which is Friday.

Step 5: Conclude

Dickens was born on a $\boxed{\text{Friday}}$.

Key Idea

Count day shifts across 200 years including leap years.
