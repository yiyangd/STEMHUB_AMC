Problem Statement

Of the following complex numbers $z$, which one has the property that $z^5$ has the greatest real part?

Choices

  1. A$-\sqrt2$
  2. B$-\sqrt3+i$
  3. C$-\sqrt2+\sqrt2 i$
  4. D$-1+\sqrt3 i$
  5. E$2i$

Answer

B. -\sqrt3+i

Solution

Step 1: Use polar form

The real part of $z^5$ depends on both the magnitude and angle of $z$. The candidates have magnitudes $\sqrt2$, $2$, $2$, $2$, and $2$ respectively.

Step 2: Check the larger-magnitude candidates

The four candidates of magnitude $2$ give fifth powers with magnitude $32$. Computing their angles shows the real parts are: \[-\sqrt3+i:\ 16\sqrt3,\quad -\sqrt2+\sqrt2i:\ 16\sqrt2,\quad -1+\sqrt3i:\ -16,\quad 2i:\ 0.\]

Step 3: Compare with the remaining candidate

The candidate $-\sqrt2$ has fifth power with real part \[-4\sqrt2,\] which is negative.

Step 4: Choose the greatest

The largest real part is $16\sqrt3$, from \[-\sqrt3+i.\]

Step 5: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{-\sqrt3+i}$.

Key Idea

Compute powers using polar form of the candidate complex numbers.
