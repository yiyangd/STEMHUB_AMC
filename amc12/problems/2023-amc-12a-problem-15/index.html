2023 AMC 12A Problem 15

2023AMC 12AProblem 15几何三角形三角形角度

Problem Statement

Usain is walking across a $100$-meter by $30$-meter rectangular field by zigzagging. The path makes the same angle $\theta$ with the horizontal direction on each segment. What angle $\theta$ will produce a total walking length of $120$ meters?

Choices

  1. A$\arccos\frac56$
  2. B$\arccos\frac45$
  3. C$\arccos\frac3{10}$
  4. D$\arcsin\frac45$
  5. E$\arcsin\frac56$

Answer

A. $\arccos\frac56$

Solution

Step 1: Focus on horizontal progress

No matter how many zigzag segments Usain uses, he starts on the left side and ends on segment $BC$, which is on the right side of the rectangle. So his total horizontal displacement is $100$ meters.

Step 2: Relate path length to horizontal component

Each segment makes angle $\theta$ with the horizontal, so its horizontal component is its length times $\cos\theta$. Adding over the whole path gives \[(\text{total length})\cos\theta=100.\]

Step 3: Use the desired total length

The total length should be $120$, so \[120\cos\theta=100.\]

Step 4: Solve for theta

Thus \[\cos\theta=\frac{100}{120}=\frac56.\]

Step 5: Conclude

The required angle is $\boxed{\arccos\frac56}$.

Key Idea

Use the horizontal component of the total zigzag path.
