2003 AMC 12A Problem 20

2003AMC 12AProblem 20组合数学递推与序列计数递推序列

Problem Statement

How many \(15\)-letter arrangements of \(5\) A's, \(5\) B's, and \(5\) C's have no A's in the first \(5\) letters, no B's in the next \(5\) letters, and no C's in the last \(5\) letters?

Choices

  1. A\(\sum_{k=0}^5\binom5k^3\)
  2. B\(3^5\cdot2^5\)
  3. C\(2^{15}\)
  4. D\(\frac{15!}{(5!)^3}\)
  5. E\(3^{15}\)

Answer

A. \sum_{k=0}^5\binom5k^3

Solution

Step 1: Break the word into three blocks

Divide the \(15\) positions into three blocks of length \(5\). Block 1 cannot contain A, block 2 cannot contain B, and block 3 cannot contain C.

Step 2: Condition on A's in the middle block

Let \(k\) be the number of A's placed in the middle block. Choose those positions in \(\binom5k\) ways.

Step 3: Match the forced counts

Then block 1 must contain \(k\) B's and \(5-k\) C's, and block 3 must contain \(5-k\) A's and \(k\) B's. These choices contribute another \(\binom5k\) and \(\binom5k\).

Step 4: Sum over k

For each \(k=0,1,\ldots,5\), the number of arrangements is \(\binom5k^3\).

Step 5: Conclude

The total is \[\boxed{\sum_{k=0}^{5}\binom5k^3}.\]

Key Idea

Condition on how many A's go in the middle block.
