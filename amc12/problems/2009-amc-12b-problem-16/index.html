2009 AMC 12B Problem 16

2009AMC 12BProblem 16代数运算与化简代数化简

Problem Statement

Trapezoid $ABCD$ has $\overline{AD}\parallel\overline{BC}$, $BD=1$, $\angle DBA=23^\circ$, $\angle BDC=46^\circ$, and $BC:AD=9:5$. What is $CD$?

Choices

  1. A$\frac35$
  2. B$\frac45$
  3. C1
  4. D$\frac65$
  5. E$\frac43$

Answer

B. \frac45

Solution

Step 1: Use the diagonal to create two triangles

The diagonal $\overline{BD}$ splits the trapezoid into triangles $ABD$ and $BCD$. Since $AD\parallel BC$, the angle that $\overline{BD}$ makes with $AD$ equals the angle it makes with $BC$ by alternate interior angles. Let this common angle be $\alpha$.

Step 2: Express AD and BC with the Law of Sines

In triangle $ABD$, the angles at $B$ and $D$ are $23^\circ$ and $\alpha$, so \[AD=\frac{\sin 23^\circ}{\sin(23^\circ+\alpha)}.\] In triangle $BCD$, the angles at $D$ and $B$ are $46^\circ$ and $\alpha$, so \[BC=\frac{\sin 46^\circ}{\sin(46^\circ+\alpha)}.\] We used $BD=1$ in both formulas.

Step 3: Use the given ratio

The ratio $BC:AD=9:5$ gives \[\frac{\sin46^\circ\sin(23^\circ+\alpha)}{\sin23^\circ\sin(46^\circ+\alpha)}=\frac95.\] Since $\sin46^\circ=2\sin23^\circ\cos23^\circ$, this relation becomes manageable.

Step 4: Turn the ratio into a useful tangent relation

Let $A=23^\circ+\alpha$. The equation simplifies to \[10\cos23^\circ\sin A=9\sin(A+23^\circ).\] Expanding the sine on the right gives \[\sin A\cos23^\circ=9\cos A\sin23^\circ.\]

Step 5: Find CD from the same angles

In triangle $BCD$, \[CD=\frac{\sin\alpha}{\sin(46^\circ+\alpha)}=\frac{\sin(A-23^\circ)}{\sin(A+23^\circ)}.\] Using the relation from the previous step, the numerator-to-denominator ratio becomes \[\frac{9-1}{9+1}=\frac45.\]

Step 6: Conclude

The length is \[\boxed{\frac45}.\]

Key Idea

Apply the Law of Sines in the two triangles formed by the diagonal.
