2008 AMC 12B Problem 11

2008AMC 12BProblem 11几何空间几何空间几何体积

Problem Statement

A cone-shaped mountain has its base on the ocean floor and has a height of \(8000\) feet. The top \(\frac18\) of the volume of the mountain is above water. What is the depth of the ocean at the base of the mountain, in feet?

Choices

  1. A4000
  2. B\(2000(4-\sqrt2)\)
  3. C6000
  4. D6400
  5. E7000

Answer

A. 4000

Solution

Step 1: Use similarity

The part above water is a smaller cone similar to the whole mountain. For similar solids, volume ratio is the cube of the linear scale factor.

Step 2: Find the linear scale

The top cone has volume \(\frac18\) of the whole cone, so its linear scale factor is \[\sqrt[3]{\frac18}=\frac12.\]

Step 3: Find the height above water

The whole cone has height \(8000\) feet, so the above-water smaller cone has height \[\frac12\cdot8000=4000\] feet.

Step 4: Find the ocean depth

The base is on the ocean floor, and the top of the mountain is \(8000\) feet above the base. If \(4000\) feet are above water, then the water depth at the base is \[8000-4000=4000\] feet.

Step 5: Conclude

The answer is \(\boxed{4000}\).

Key Idea

Use similar cones: volume scales as the cube of linear scale.
