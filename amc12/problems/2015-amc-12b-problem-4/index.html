2015 AMC 12B Problem 4

2015AMC 12BProblem 4代数运算与化简代数化简

Problem Statement

David, Hikmet, Jack, Marta, Rand, and Todd were in a 12-person race with 6 other people. Rand finished 6 places ahead of Hikmet. Marta finished 1 place behind Jack. David finished 2 places behind Hikmet. Jack finished 2 places behind Todd. Todd finished 1 place behind Rand. Marta finished in 6th place. Who finished in 8th place?

Choices

  1. ADavid
  2. BHikmet
  3. CJack
  4. DRand
  5. ETodd

Answer

B. Hikmet

Solution

Step 1: Start from Marta

Marta finished in $6^\text{th}$ place. Since Marta finished $1$ place behind Jack, Jack finished $5^\text{th}$.

Step 2: Work backward through the chain

Jack finished $2$ places behind Todd, so Todd finished $3^\text{rd}$. Todd finished $1$ place behind Rand, so Rand finished $2^\text{nd}$.

Step 3: Place Hikmet

Rand finished $6$ places ahead of Hikmet, so Hikmet finished \[2+6=8^\text{th}.\]

Step 4: Check David

David finished $2$ places behind Hikmet, so David finished $10^\text{th}$.

Step 5: Conclude

The person in $8^\text{th}$ place was $\boxed{\text{Hikmet}}$.

Key Idea

Use relative positions from Marta's fixed sixth-place finish.
