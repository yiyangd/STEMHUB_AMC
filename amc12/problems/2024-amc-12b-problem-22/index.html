2024 AMC 12B Problem 22

2024AMC 12BProblem 22几何三角形倍角整数边正弦定理

Problem Statement

Let $\triangle ABC$ have integer side lengths and the property that $\angle B=2\angle A$. What is the least possible perimeter of such a triangle?

Choices

  1. A13
  2. B14
  3. C15
  4. D16
  5. E17

Answer

C. 15

Solution

Step 1: Name the sides opposite the angles

Let $a,b,c$ be the side lengths opposite angles $A,B,C$, respectively. Since $B=2A$, the Law of Sines gives

\[\frac ba=\frac{\sin B}{\sin A}=\frac{\sin 2A}{\sin A}=2\cos A.\]

Step 2: Use the Law of Cosines for cos A

The Law of Cosines gives

\[\cos A=\frac{b^2+c^2-a^2}{2bc}.\]

Substituting this into $\frac ba=2\cos A$ gives a relation among the side lengths.

Step 3: Simplify the side relation

After substitution and simplification, the nondegenerate integer triangles must satisfy

\[b^2=a(a+c).\]

This condition is much easier to search than the original angle condition.

Step 4: Find the smallest integer solution

Trying small side lengths, the first valid solution is

\[a=4,\qquad b=6,\qquad c=5.\]

Indeed, $b^2=36$ and $a(a+c)=4(9)=36$. The triangle inequalities also hold.

Step 5: Compute the perimeter

The perimeter is

\[4+6+5=15.\]

No smaller perimeter gives a valid integer triangle satisfying the side relation, so the answer is $15$.

Key Idea

Use the Law of Sines and Law of Cosines to derive a side-length condition, then find the smallest integer triangle.
