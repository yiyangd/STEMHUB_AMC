2008 AMC 12B Problem 16

2008AMC 12BProblem 16几何面积与相似面积比例

Problem Statement

A rectangular floor measures \(a\) by \(b\) feet, where \(a\) and \(b\) are positive integers with \(b>a\). An artist paints a rectangle on the floor with the sides of the rectangle parallel to the sides of the floor. The unpainted part of the floor forms a border of width \(1\) foot around the painted rectangle and occupies half of the area of the entire floor. How many possibilities are there for the ordered pair \((a,b)\)?

Choices

  1. A1
  2. B2
  3. C3
  4. D4
  5. E5

Answer

B. 2

Solution

Step 1: Write the painted dimensions

A \(1\)-foot border around all sides means the painted rectangle has dimensions \((a-2)\) by \((b-2)\).

Step 2: Use the border area condition

The unpainted border occupies half the total floor area, so the painted area also occupies half. Thus \[(a-2)(b-2)=\frac{ab}{2}.\]

Step 3: Simplify

Expanding gives \[ab-2a-2b+4=\frac{ab}{2}.\] Multiplying by \(2\), \[ab-4a-4b+8=0.\]

Step 4: Factor

Add \(8\) to complete the factorization: \[(a-4)(b-4)=8.\]

Step 5: Count positive integer possibilities

Since \(b>a\), the positive factor pairs give \((a,b)=(5,12)\) and \((6,8)\).

Step 6: Conclude

There are \(\boxed{2}\) possible ordered pairs.

Key Idea

Translate the border area into a factor equation.
