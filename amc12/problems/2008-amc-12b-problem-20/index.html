2008 AMC 12B Problem 20

2008AMC 12BProblem 20代数运算与化简代数化简

Problem Statement

Michael walks at the rate of \(5\) feet per second on a long straight path. Trash pails are located every \(200\) feet along the path. A garbage truck travels at \(10\) feet per second in the same direction as Michael and stops for \(30\) seconds at each pail. As Michael passes a pail, he notices the truck ahead of him just leaving the next pail. How many times will Michael and the truck meet?

Choices

  1. A4
  2. B5
  3. C6
  4. D7
  5. E8

Answer

B. 5

Solution

Step 1: Track the situation at t=0

When Michael passes a pail, the truck is \(200\) feet ahead and just leaving the next pail. The truck moves for \(20\) seconds to the next pail, then stops for \(30\) seconds.

Step 2: Track the gap by cycles

During each \(20\)-second moving interval, the truck gains \(5\cdot20=100\) feet on Michael. During each \(30\)-second stop, Michael closes \(5\cdot30=150\) feet.

Step 3: List the first gaps

The starting gap is \(200\). After successive move-stop cycles, the gap at the start of stops becomes \(300,250,200,150,100,\ldots\), so Michael first catches the truck at a stop when the gap can be closed during a \(30\)-second stop.

Step 4: Count actual meetings

The unique meeting times occur at \(t=200,240,260,280,\) and \(320\) seconds. Some occur exactly when the truck starts or ends a stop, so those boundary contacts should be counted only once.

Step 5: Stop when Michael stays ahead

After the last contact, Michael's average speed over the truck's move-stop cycle is larger than the truck's average speed, so no further meetings occur.

Step 6: Conclude

They meet \(\boxed{5}\) times.

Key Idea

Track the truck by move-stop cycles and merge boundary meetings.
