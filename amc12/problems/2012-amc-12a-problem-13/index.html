2012 AMC 12A Problem 13

2012AMC 12AProblem 13代数三角恒等式三角函数恒等式

Problem Statement

Paula the painter and her two helpers each paint at constant, but different, rates. They always start at $8{:}00$ AM, and all three always take the same amount of time to eat lunch. On Monday the three of them painted $50\%$ of a house, quitting at $4{:}00$ PM. On Tuesday, when Paula wasn't there, the two helpers painted only $24\%$ of the house and quit at $2{:}12$ PM. On Wednesday Paula worked by herself and finished the house by working until $7{:}12$ PM. How long, in minutes, was each day's lunch break?

Choices

  1. A30
  2. B36
  3. C42
  4. D48
  5. E60

Answer

D. 48

Solution

Step 1: Measure work time after lunch

Let the daily lunch break be $L$ hours. Monday's available time is $8-L$ hours, Tuesday's is $6.2-L=\frac{31}{5}-L$, and Wednesday's is $11.2-L=\frac{56}{5}-L$.

Step 2: Write the rates

Let Paula's rate be $p$ houses per hour and the two helpers' combined rate be $h$. The data give \[(p+h)(8-L)=\frac12,\] \[h\left(\frac{31}{5}-L\right)=\frac{6}{25},\] and \[p\left(\frac{56}{5}-L\right)=\frac{13}{50},\] because after Monday and Tuesday, $26\%$ of the house remains.

Step 3: Use the rates in Monday's equation

From Tuesday and Wednesday, \[h=\frac{6/25}{31/5-L},\qquad p=\frac{13/50}{56/5-L}.\] Substitute these into \[(p+h)(8-L)=\frac12.\]

Step 4: Solve for L

The resulting equation simplifies to \[L=\frac45\] hour.

Step 5: Convert to minutes

\[\frac45\] hour is \[\frac45\cdot60=48\] minutes.

Step 6: Conclude

Each lunch break was $\boxed{48}$ minutes.

Key Idea

Set up work-rate equations with an unknown lunch break.
