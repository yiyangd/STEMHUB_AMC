2006 AMC 12B Problem 13

2006AMC 12BProblem 13几何面积与相似面积比例

Problem Statement

Rhombus \(ABCD\) is similar to rhombus \(BFDE\). The area of rhombus \(ABCD\) is \(24\), and \(\angle BAD=60^\circ\). What is the area of rhombus \(BFDE\)?

Choices

  1. A6
  2. B\(4\sqrt3\)
  3. C8
  4. D9
  5. E\(6\sqrt3\)

Answer

C. 8

Solution

Step 1: Use the 60-degree rhombus

In rhombus \(ABCD\), \(AB=AD\) and \(\angle BAD=60^\circ\). Therefore \(\triangle ABD\) is equilateral, so diagonal \(BD\) has the same length as a side of the large rhombus.

Step 2: Compare corresponding lengths

The smaller rhombus \(BFDE\) is similar to the larger rhombus. In the diagram, the corresponding long diagonal of the smaller rhombus is \(BD\), while the corresponding long diagonal of the larger rhombus is \(AC\).

Step 3: Find the diagonal ratio

For a \(60^\circ\) rhombus, the long diagonal is \(\sqrt3\) times the side. Since \(BD\) equals the side of \(ABCD\), the linear scale factor from large to small is \[\frac{BD}{AC}=\frac1{\sqrt3}.\]

Step 4: Square the scale factor for area

Areas scale by the square of the linear factor, so \[\frac{[BFDE]}{[ABCD]}=\left(\frac1{\sqrt3}\right)^2=\frac13.\]

Step 5: Compute the smaller area

The larger area is \(24\), so the smaller area is \[24\cdot\frac13=8.\]

Step 6: Conclude

The area is \(\boxed{8}\).

Key Idea

Use similarity and diagonal ratios in a 60-degree rhombus.
