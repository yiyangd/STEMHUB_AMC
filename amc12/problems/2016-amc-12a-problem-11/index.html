2016 AMC 12A Problem 11

2016AMC 12AProblem 11代数三角恒等式三角函数恒等式

Problem Statement

Each of the $100$ students in a certain summer camp can either sing, dance, or act. Some students have more than one talent, but no student has all three talents. There are $42$ students who cannot sing, $65$ students who cannot dance, and $29$ students who cannot act. How many students have two of these talents?

Choices

  1. A16
  2. B25
  3. C36
  4. D49
  5. E64

Answer

E. 64

Solution

Step 1: Translate the negative statements

If $42$ students cannot sing, then $100-42=58$ students can sing. Similarly, $35$ students can dance and $71$ students can act.

Step 2: Count total talent memberships

Adding these gives the total number of reported talents: \[58+35+71=164.\] This is not the number of students, because a student with two talents is counted twice.

Step 3: Introduce exactly-one and exactly-two groups

Let $x$ be the number of students with exactly two talents, and let $y$ be the number with exactly one talent. Since no student has all three talents, these are the only two possibilities, so \[x+y=100.\]

Step 4: Count memberships a second way

The students with one talent contribute $1$ membership each, and the students with two talents contribute $2$ memberships each. Hence \[2x+y=164.\]

Step 5: Subtract the equations

Subtracting $x+y=100$ from $2x+y=164$ gives \[x=64.\]

Step 6: Conclude

Therefore $\boxed{64}$ students have two talents.

Key Idea

Count talent memberships in two ways to find the number of students with exactly two talents.
