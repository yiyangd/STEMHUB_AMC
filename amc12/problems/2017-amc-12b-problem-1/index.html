Problem Statement

Kymbrea's comic book collection currently has $30$ comic books, and she is adding to her collection at the rate of $2$ comic books per month. LaShawn's collection currently has $10$ comic books, and he is adding to his collection at the rate of $6$ comic books per month. After how many months will LaShawn's collection have twice as many comic books as Kymbrea's?

Choices

  1. A1
  2. B4
  3. C5
  4. D20
  5. E25

Answer

E. 25

Solution

Step 1: Write both collection sizes

After \(m\) months, Kymbrea has \[30+2m\] comic books, and LaShawn has \[10+6m\] comic books.

Step 2: Set up the twice-as-many condition

We need LaShawn's collection to be twice Kymbrea's collection: \[10+6m=2(30+2m).\]

Step 3: Solve for m

This gives \[10+6m=60+4m,\] so \[2m=50,\quad m=25.\]

Step 4: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{25}$ months.

Key Idea

Set up linear growth expressions and solve when one is twice the other.
