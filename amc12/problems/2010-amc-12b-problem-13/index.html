2010AMC 12BProblem 13代数三角恒等式三角函数恒等式

Problem Statement

In $\triangle ABC$, \[\cos(2A-B)+\sin(A+B)=2\] and $AB=4$. What is $BC$?

Choices

  1. A2
  2. B$\sqrt3$
  3. C$2\sqrt2$
  4. D$2\sqrt3$
  5. E4

Answer

A. 2

Solution

Step 1: Use the maximum possible values

Both $\cos(2A-B)$ and $\sin(A+B)$ are at most $1$. Their sum is $2$, so both must equal $1$.

Step 2: Use the sine condition

Since $\sin(A+B)=1$ and $A+B$ is an angle sum in a triangle, we must have \[A+B=90^\circ.\] Therefore \[C=90^\circ.\]

Step 3: Use the cosine condition

Since $\cos(2A-B)=1$, the angle $2A-B$ must be $0^\circ$ in this triangle setting. Thus \[B=2A.\]

Step 4: Find the triangle angles

Now \[A+B=90^\circ\] and $B=2A$, so $3A=90^\circ$. Hence \[A=30^\circ,\quad B=60^\circ,\quad C=90^\circ.\]

Step 5: Use the side AB

The side $AB$ is opposite the right angle $C$, so it is the hypotenuse. Since $AB=4$, the side opposite $30^\circ$ is half the hypotenuse, so \[BC=2.\]

Step 6: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{2}$.

Key Idea

Use the fact that two trigonometric terms can sum to 2 only when both equal 1.
