2013 AMC 12A Problem 2

2013AMC 12AProblem 2代数运算与化简代数化简

Problem Statement

A softball team played ten games, scoring $1,2,3,4,5,6,7,8,9,$ and $10$ runs. They lost by one run in exactly five games. In each of the other games, they scored twice as many runs as their opponent. How many total runs did their opponents score?

Choices

  1. A35
  2. B40
  3. C45
  4. D50
  5. E55

Answer

C. 45

Solution

Step 1: Identify the doubled-score games

If the team scored twice as many runs as its opponent, then the team's score in that game must be even. Among $1,2,\ldots,10$, the five even scores are $2,4,6,8,10$.

Step 2: Score the opponents in those games

For those games, the opponent scores were \[1,2,3,4,5,\] with total $15$.

Step 3: Use the one-run losses

The remaining five scores, $1,3,5,7,9$, must be the games they lost by one run. The opponents scored \[2,4,6,8,10,\] with total $30$.

Step 4: Add

The opponents scored \[15+30=45\] total runs.

Step 5: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{45}$.

Key Idea

Separate odd-score one-run losses from even-score games where the team doubled the opponent.
