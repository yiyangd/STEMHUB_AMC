2013 AMC 12A Problem 19

2013AMC 12AProblem 19几何圆与曲线圆曲线

Problem Statement

In $\triangle ABC$, $AB=86$ and $AC=97$. A circle with center $A$ and radius $AB$ intersects $\overline{BC}$ at points $B$ and $X$. Moreover, $\overline{BX}$ and $\overline{CX}$ have integer lengths. What is $BC$?

Choices

  1. A11
  2. B28
  3. C33
  4. D61
  5. E72

Answer

D. 61

Solution

Step 1: Use power of a point

From point $C$, the secant line through $B$ and $X$ intersects the circle at $X$ and $B$. The line through $C$ and $A$ intersects the circle at distances $AC-86$ and $AC+86$ from $C$.

Step 2: Compute the product

Since $AC=97$, those two distances are \[97-86=11,\qquad 97+86=183.\] Therefore \[CX\cdot CB=11\cdot183=2013.\]

Step 3: Use integer lengths

Both $CX$ and $BX$ are integers, so $CB=CX+BX$ is also an integer. We need factor pairs \[(CX,CB)\] with product $2013$ and $CB\ge CX$.

Step 4: List possible factor pairs

The relevant factor pairs are \[(1,2013),\ (3,671),\ (11,183),\ (33,61).\]

Step 5: Apply triangle inequality

In $\triangle ACX$, the sides are $AC=97$, $AX=86$, and $CX$. The triangle inequality gives \[CX>97-86=11.\] Thus only $CX=33$ is possible among the listed smaller factors.

Step 6: Conclude

Then \[BC=CB=61,\] so the answer is $\boxed{61}$.

Key Idea

Use power of a point from C with respect to the circle centered at A.
