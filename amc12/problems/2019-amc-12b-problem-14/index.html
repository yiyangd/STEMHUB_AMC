2019 AMC 12B Problem 14

2019AMC 12BProblem 14数论整数构造整数构造

Problem Statement

Let $S$ be the set of all positive integer divisors of $100{,}000$. How many numbers are the product of two distinct elements of $S$?

Choices

  1. A98
  2. B100
  3. C117
  4. D119
  5. E121

Answer

C. 117

Solution

Step 1: Represent each divisor by exponents

Since \[100{,}000=10^5=2^5\cdot5^5,\] every divisor has the form $2^a5^b$ where $0\le a\le5$ and $0\le b\le5$.

Step 2: Translate products into exponent sums

The product of two divisors has the form $2^A5^B$, where $A$ and $B$ each range from $0$ to $10$. If repeated divisors were allowed, that would give $11\cdot11=121$ possible exponent pairs.

Step 3: Find when distinct divisors are impossible

We must use two distinct divisors. Most exponent sums can be made in more than one way, but the four corner sums \[(0,0),(10,0),(0,10),(10,10)\] force the same divisor to be used twice.

Step 4: Check a typical edge case

For example, $(0,2)$ can be made as $(0,0)+(0,2)$, so it is allowed. Only the corners are forced to be double copies of a single divisor.

Step 5: Count

Therefore the number of products of two distinct divisors is \[121-4=117.\] The answer is $\boxed{117}$.

Key Idea

Represent divisors by exponent pairs and count attainable products of two distinct divisors.
