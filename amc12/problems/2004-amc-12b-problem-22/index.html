2004 AMC 12B Problem 22

2004AMC 12BProblem 22几何三角形三角形角度

Problem Statement

The square \[\begin{matrix}50&b&c\\ d&e&f\\ g&h&2\end{matrix}\] is a multiplicative magic square. That is, the product of the numbers in each row, column, and diagonal is the same. If all the entries are positive integers, what is the sum of the possible values of \(g\)?

Choices

  1. A10
  2. B25
  3. C35
  4. D62
  5. E136

Answer

C. 35

Solution

Step 1: Use the magic product

Let the common product be \(P\). The diagonal \(50,e,2\) gives \(P=100e\).

Step 2: Use the other diagonal

The other diagonal gives \(ceg=100e\), so \[cg=100.\]

Step 3: Relate entries using rows and columns

From the first row, \(50bc=100e\), so \(bc=2e\). From the middle column, \(beh=100e\), so \(bh=100\). From the bottom row, \(2gh=100e\), so \(gh=50e\).

Step 4: Derive g in terms of b

Using \(gh=50e\) and \(bh=100\), divide the first by the second to get \(\frac{g}{b}=\frac{e}{2}\). Also \(bc=2e\) and \(cg=100\) imply \(\frac{g}{b}=\frac{50}{e}\). Hence \(e=10\) and \(g=5b\).

Step 5: Apply integrality

Now \(c=100/g=20/b\), \(d=2e/g=4/b\), and \(h=100/b\). Thus \(b\) must divide \(4\), so \(b=1,2,4\). The corresponding \(g\)-values are \(5,10,20\).

Step 6: Conclude

Their sum is \(5+10+20=\boxed{35}\).

Key Idea

Translate equal products into divisibility conditions.
