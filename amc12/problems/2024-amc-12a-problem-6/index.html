2024 AMC 12A Problem 6

2024AMC 12AProblem 6数论整数构造整数因子最值符号

Problem Statement

The product of three integers is $60$. What is the least possible positive sum of the three integers?

Choices

  1. A2
  2. B3
  3. C5
  4. D6
  5. E13

Answer

B. 3

Solution

Step 1: Think about signs

The product is positive. To make the sum small but positive, a useful strategy is one positive integer and two negative integers.

Step 2: Search by factor triples

Write the factors as $a,-b,-c$, where $abc=60$. The sum is \[a-b-c,\] so we want this positive and as small as possible.

Step 3: Try balanced factor pairs

The triple \[10,-6,-1\] has product $60$ and sum \[10-6-1=3.\]

Step 4: Check why smaller positive sums do not occur

Using a positive factor smaller than $10$ makes the two negative factors have sum too large in magnitude, producing a nonpositive total. The next feasible positive total is therefore $3$.

Step 5: Conclude

The least possible positive sum is $\boxed{3}$.

Key Idea

Use two negative factors and one positive factor to make a small positive sum.
