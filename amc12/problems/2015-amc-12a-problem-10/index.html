2015 AMC 12A Problem 10

2015AMC 12AProblem 10数论整数构造整数构造

Problem Statement

Integers $x$ and $y$ with $x>y>0$ satisfy \[x+y+xy=80.\] What is $x$?

Choices

  1. A8
  2. B10
  3. C15
  4. D18
  5. E26

Answer

E. 26

Solution

Step 1: Add 1 to factor

The expression \[xy+x+y\] is close to a product. Add $1$ to both sides: \[xy+x+y+1=81.\]

Step 2: Factor

This gives \[(x+1)(y+1)=81.\]

Step 3: Use positive integers and order

Since $x>y>0$, we need factor pairs of $81$ with the larger factor equal to $x+1$ and the smaller equal to $y+1$.

Step 4: Choose the valid pair

The factor pair \[27\cdot3=81\] gives \[x+1=27,\quad y+1=3.\] Thus \[x=26,\quad y=2.\]

Step 5: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{26}$.

Key Idea

Factor xy+x+y by adding 1 to both variables.
