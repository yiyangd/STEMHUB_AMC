2013 AMC 12A Problem 12

2013AMC 12AProblem 12几何三角形三角形角度

Problem Statement

The angles in a particular triangle are in arithmetic progression, and the side lengths are $4,5,x$. The sum of the possible values of $x$ equals $a+\sqrt b+\sqrt c$, where $a,b,$ and $c$ are positive integers. What is $a+b+c$?

Choices

  1. A36
  2. B38
  3. C40
  4. D42
  5. E44

Answer

A. 36

Solution

Step 1: Use the angle progression

If three angles are in arithmetic progression and sum to $180^\circ$, the middle angle must be $60^\circ$. The side opposite the middle angle is the middle-length side.

Step 2: Case 1: side 5 is opposite 60 degrees

If side $5$ is opposite the $60^\circ$ angle, then by the Law of Cosines, \[5^2=4^2+x^2-2(4)(x)\cos60^\circ.\] This becomes \[x^2-4x-9=0,\] so the positive solution is \[x=2+\sqrt{13}.\]

Step 3: Case 2: side x is opposite 60 degrees

If side $x$ is opposite the $60^\circ$ angle, then \[x^2=4^2+5^2-2(4)(5)\cos60^\circ=21,\] so \[x=\sqrt{21}.\]

Step 4: Reject the last case

If side $4$ were opposite the $60^\circ$ angle, then \[4^2=5^2+x^2-2(5)(x)\cos60^\circ,\] giving \[x^2-5x+9=0.\] Its discriminant is $25-36=-11$, so there is no real side length.

Step 5: Add the possible values

The possible values sum to \[2+\sqrt{13}+\sqrt{21}.\] Thus $a=2$, $b=13$, and $c=21$.

Step 6: Conclude

\[a+b+c=2+13+21=36.\] The answer is $\boxed{36}$.

Key Idea

Use the fact that the middle angle of an arithmetic progression is 60 degrees.
