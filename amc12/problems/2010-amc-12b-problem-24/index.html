Problem Statement

The set of real numbers $x$ for which \[\frac1{x-2009}+\frac1{x-2010}+\frac1{x-2011}\ge1\] is the union of intervals of the form $a<x\le b$. What is the sum of the lengths of these intervals?

Choices

  1. A$\frac{1003}{335}$
  2. B$\frac{1004}{335}$
  3. C3
  4. D$\frac{403}{134}$
  5. E$\frac{202}{67}$

Answer

C. 3

Solution

Step 1: Shift the variable

Let \[t=x-2010.\] Then the inequality becomes \[\frac1{t+1}+\frac1t+\frac1{t-1}\ge1.\] This centers the three excluded points at $t=-1,0,1$.

Step 2: Combine the fractions

Moving $1$ to the left and combining gives \[-\frac{t^3-3t^2-t+1}{t(t-1)(t+1)}\ge0.\] The interval endpoints occur at the three roots of \[t^3-3t^2-t+1=0\] and at the vertical asymptotes $t=-1,0,1$.

Step 3: Name the cubic roots

Let the three roots of the cubic be \[r_1<r_2<r_3.\] A sign chart shows that the solution intervals have lengths \[(r_1+1),\quad r_2,\quad (r_3-1).\]

Step 4: Add the lengths

The total length is \[(r_1+1)+r_2+(r_3-1)=r_1+r_2+r_3.\]

Step 5: Use Vieta's formula

For \[t^3-3t^2-t+1=0,\] the sum of the roots is $3$.

Step 6: Conclude

The sum of the interval lengths is $\boxed{3}$.

Key Idea

Shift the variable and use the roots of a cubic to sum interval lengths.
