2020 AMC 12B Problem 25

2020AMC 12BProblem 25概率与统计独立性独立事件概率

Problem Statement

For each real number $a$ with $0\le a\le1$, let numbers $x$ and $y$ be chosen independently at random from the intervals $[0,a]$ and $[0,1]$, respectively, and let $P(a)$ be the probability that \[\sin^2(\pi x)+\sin^2(\pi y)>1.\] What is the maximum value of $P(a)$?

Choices

  1. A$\frac7{12}$
  2. B$2-\sqrt2$
  3. C$\frac{1+\sqrt2}{4}$
  4. D$\frac{\sqrt5-1}{2}$
  5. E$\frac58$

Answer

B. $2-\sqrt2$

Solution

Step 1: Understand the probability geometrically

The pair $(x,y)$ is uniformly distributed over the rectangle

\[0\le x\le a,\qquad 0\le y\le1.\]

So $P(a)$ is an area inside this rectangle divided by its total area $a$.

Step 2: Find the boundary of the inequality

The inequality

\[\sin^2(\pi x)+\sin^2(\pi y)>1\]

is equivalent to

\[\sin^2(\pi x)>\cos^2(\pi y).\]

On the unit square, the boundary lines are

\[y=\frac12-x,\quad y=x-\frac12,\quad y=x+\frac12,\quad y=\frac32-x.\]

These form a diamond with vertices $(0,\frac12)$, $(\frac12,0)$, $(1,\frac12)$, and $(\frac12,1)$.

Step 3: Compute the area for a at most one-half

For $0\le a\le\frac12$, the vertical slice of the diamond at position $x$ has length $2x$. Thus the favorable area is

\[\int_0^a 2x\,dx=a^2.\]

Since the rectangle area is $a$, we get

\[P(a)=a.\]

Step 4: Compute the area for a at least one-half

For $\frac12\le a\le1$, the area up to $x=\frac12$ is $\frac14$. From $x=\frac12$ to $x=a$, the vertical slice length is $2(1-x)$. Hence the favorable area is

\[\frac14+\int_{1/2}^a2(1-x)\,dx=2a-a^2-\frac12.\]

Therefore

\[P(a)=\frac{2a-a^2-\frac12}{a}=2-a-\frac1{2a}.\]

Step 5: Maximize the expression

For $a\le\frac12$, the maximum is at most $\frac12$. For $a\ge\frac12$, maximize

\[2-a-\frac1{2a}.\]

Its derivative is

\[-1+\frac1{2a^2},\]

which is $0$ when $a=\frac1{\sqrt2}$. The maximum value is

\[2-\frac1{\sqrt2}-\frac{\sqrt2}{2}=2-\sqrt2.\]

Key Idea

Convert the trigonometric inequality into a diamond-shaped region in the unit square.
