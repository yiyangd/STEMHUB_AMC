2012 AMC 12B Problem 15

2012AMC 12BProblem 15几何空间几何空间几何体积

Problem Statement

Jesse cuts a circular paper disk of radius $12$ along two radii to form two sectors, the smaller having a central angle of $120^\circ$. He makes two circular cones, using each sector to form the lateral surface of a cone. What is the ratio of the volume of the smaller cone to that of the larger?

Choices

  1. A$\frac18$
  2. B$\frac14$
  3. C$\frac{\sqrt{10}}{10}$
  4. D$\frac{\sqrt5}{6}$
  5. E$\frac{\sqrt{10}}5$

Answer

C. \frac{\sqrt{10}}{10}

Solution

Step 1: Convert sector arcs to cone base circumferences

The disk radius $12$ becomes the slant height of each cone. The smaller sector has angle $120^\circ$, one third of a full circle, so its arc length is \[\frac13\cdot2\pi\cdot12=8\pi.\] Thus the smaller cone has base radius $4$.

Step 2: Find the larger cone radius

The larger sector has angle $240^\circ$, so its arc length is \[16\pi,\] giving base radius $8$.

Step 3: Compute cone heights

For the smaller cone, height is \[\sqrt{12^2-4^2}=\sqrt{128}=8\sqrt2.\] For the larger cone, height is \[\sqrt{12^2-8^2}=\sqrt{80}=4\sqrt5.\]

Step 4: Form the volume ratio

Cone volume is \[\frac13\pi r^2h.\] Therefore the ratio is \[\frac{4^2\cdot8\sqrt2}{8^2\cdot4\sqrt5}=\frac{\sqrt2}{2\sqrt5}=\frac{\sqrt{10}}{10}.\]

Step 5: Conclude

The answer is \[\boxed{\frac{\sqrt{10}}{10}}.\]

Key Idea

Convert sector arc lengths into cone base radii and use volume.
