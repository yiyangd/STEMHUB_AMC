2013 AMC 12A Problem 16

2013AMC 12AProblem 16概率与统计平均数与数据集平均数数据

Problem Statement

$A$, $B$, and $C$ are three piles of rocks. The mean weight of the rocks in $A$ is $40$ pounds, the mean weight of the rocks in $B$ is $50$ pounds, the mean weight of the rocks in the combined piles $A$ and $B$ is $43$ pounds, and the mean weight of the rocks in the combined piles $A$ and $C$ is $44$ pounds. What is the greatest possible integer value for the mean in pounds of the rocks in the combined piles $B$ and $C$?

Choices

  1. A55
  2. B56
  3. C57
  4. D58
  5. E59

Answer

E. 59

Solution

Step 1: Set variables for pile sizes

Let the numbers of rocks in piles $A,B,C$ be $a,b,c$. The total weights of $A$ and $B$ are $40a$ and $50b$.

Step 2: Use the A and B combined mean

The condition \[\frac{40a+50b}{a+b}=43\] gives \[40a+50b=43a+43b,\] so \[7b=3a.\] Thus $a=\frac73b$, so $b$ must be a multiple of $3$.

Step 3: Use the A and C combined mean

If the total weight of pile $C$ is $W_C$, then \[\frac{40a+W_C}{a+c}=44.\] Hence \[W_C=44c+4a.\]

Step 4: Write the B and C mean

The combined mean of $B$ and $C$ is \[\frac{50b+W_C}{b+c}=44+\frac{6b+4a}{b+c}.\] Substitute $a=\frac73b$ to get \[44+\frac{46b/3}{b+c}.\]

Step 5: Maximize an integer value

Write $b=3t$. Then the mean is \[44+\frac{46t}{3t+c}.\] This is less than $44+\frac{46}{3}$, so it is less than $60$. The greatest possible integer is therefore at most $59$.

Step 6: Show 59 is possible

To get at least $59$, we need \[\frac{46t}{3t+c}\ge15,\] or \[t\ge15c.\] Taking $c=1$ and $t=15$ makes the mean exactly $59$.

Step 7: Conclude

The greatest possible integer value is $\boxed{59}$.

Key Idea

Use weighted averages to relate pile sizes, then maximize the combined mean.
