2009 AMC 12B Problem 11

2009AMC 12BProblem 11代数运算与化简代数化简

Problem Statement

On Monday Millie puts a quart of birdseed, $25\%$ of which is millet, into a bird feeder. Each successive day she adds another quart of the same mixture without removing any seeds that are left. Each day the birds eat only $25\%$ of the millet in the feeder, but they eat all of the other seeds. On which day, just after Millie has placed the seeds in the feeder, will the birds find that more than half the seeds in the feeder are millet?

Choices

  1. ATuesday
  2. BWednesday
  3. CThursday
  4. DFriday
  5. ESaturday

Answer

D. Friday

Solution

Step 1: Track the part that can accumulate

The non-millet seeds do not accumulate because the birds eat all of them each day. Just after Millie adds a quart, the non-millet amount is always $0.75$ quart. The millet is different: only $25\%$ of it is eaten, so $75\%$ remains for the next day.

Step 2: Write a recurrence for the millet

Let $M$ be the amount of millet just after a daily addition. The next day, $75\%$ of the previous millet is still present, and Millie adds $0.25$ quart more millet. Thus \[M_{\text{next}}=0.75M+0.25.\]

Step 3: Translate the more-than-half condition

Just after an addition, the total amount of seed is $M+0.75$. Millet is more than half exactly when \[M>\frac{M+0.75}{2}.\] This simplifies to $M>0.75$. So we only need to know when the accumulated millet first exceeds $0.75$ quart.

Step 4: Compute day by day

On Monday, $M=0.25$. Then Tuesday gives $0.75(0.25)+0.25=0.4375$, Wednesday gives $0.75(0.4375)+0.25=0.578125$, and Thursday gives about $0.6836$. These are still below $0.75$.

Step 5: Find the first successful day

On Friday the millet amount is \[0.75(0.6836)+0.25\approx0.7627,\] which is greater than $0.75$. Therefore Friday is the first day when millet is more than half of the seed in the feeder.

Step 6: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{\text{Friday}}$.

Key Idea

Model the remaining millet with a recurrence and compare it to the fixed non-millet amount.
