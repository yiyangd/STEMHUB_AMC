2018 AMC 12A Problem 22

2018AMC 12AProblem 22几何面积与相似面积比例

Problem Statement

The solutions to the equations $z^2=4+4\sqrt{15}i$ and $z^2=2+2\sqrt3 i$, where $i=\sqrt{-1}$, form the vertices of a parallelogram in the complex plane. The area of this parallelogram can be written in the form $p\sqrt q-r\sqrt s$, where $p,q,r,$ and $s$ are positive integers and neither $q$ nor $s$ is divisible by the square of any prime number. What is $p+q+r+s$?

Choices

  1. A$20$
  2. B$21$
  3. C$22$
  4. D$23$
  5. E$24$

Answer

A. $20$

Solution

Step 1: Find the first pair of roots

Let $u^2=4+4\sqrt{15}i$. Since

\[|4+4\sqrt{15}i|=16,\]

we have $|u|=4$. If $u=x+yi$, then

\[x^2+y^2=16,\qquad x^2-y^2=4.\]

Solving gives $x^2=10$ and $y^2=6$, so one root is

\[u=\sqrt{10}+i\sqrt6.\]

Step 2: Find the second pair of roots

Similarly, let $v^2=2+2\sqrt3i$. Then $|v|=2$, and

\[x^2+y^2=4,\qquad x^2-y^2=2.\]

Thus one root is

\[v=\sqrt3+i.\]

Step 3: Understand the parallelogram

The four roots are

\[u,\ -u,\ v,\ -v.\]

These naturally form a parallelogram centered at the origin. Its area is twice the absolute value of the determinant formed by $u$ and $v$:

\[\text{Area}=2|\operatorname{Im}(u\overline v)|.\]

Step 4: Compute the determinant

Using coordinates, this determinant is

\[\sqrt{10}\cdot1-\sqrt6\cdot\sqrt3=\sqrt{10}-3\sqrt2.\]

Its absolute value is

\[3\sqrt2-\sqrt{10}.\]

Step 5: Match the required form

Therefore the area is

\[2(3\sqrt2-\sqrt{10})=6\sqrt2-2\sqrt{10}.\]

So $p=6$, $q=2$, $r=2$, and $s=10$, giving

\[p+q+r+s=20.\]

Key Idea

Find the square roots explicitly and use a determinant for the parallelogram area.
