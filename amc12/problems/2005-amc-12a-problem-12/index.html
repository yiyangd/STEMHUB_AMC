2005 AMC 12A Problem 12

2005AMC 12AProblem 12数论整数构造整数构造

Problem Statement

A line passes through \(A(1,1)\) and \(B(100,1000)\). How many other points with integer coordinates are on the line and strictly between \(A\) and \(B\)?

Choices

  1. A0
  2. B2
  3. C3
  4. D8
  5. E9

Answer

D. 8

Solution

Step 1: Look at coordinate differences

From \(A(1,1)\) to \(B(100,1000)\), the changes are \[\Delta x=99,\qquad \Delta y=999.\]

Step 2: Use the lattice-point fact

The number of equal steps between lattice points on a segment is \(\gcd(|\Delta x|,|\Delta y|)\).

Step 3: Compute the gcd

\(\gcd(99,999)=9\). So the segment is divided into \(9\) equal lattice steps.

Step 4: Count interior points

That gives \(9+1\) lattice points including both endpoints, so the number strictly between them is \(9-1=8\).

Step 5: Conclude

The answer is \(\boxed{8}\).

Key Idea

Use the gcd of coordinate differences to count lattice points on a segment.
