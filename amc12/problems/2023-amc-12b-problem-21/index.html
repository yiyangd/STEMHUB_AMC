2023 AMC 12B Problem 21

2023AMC 12BProblem 21几何空间几何空间几何体积

Problem Statement

A lampshade is made in the form of the lateral surface of the frustum of a right circular cone. The height of the frustum is $3\sqrt3$ inches, its top diameter is $6$ inches, and its bottom diameter is $12$ inches. A bug is at the bottom of the lampshade, and there is a glob of honey on the top edge of the lampshade at the spot farthest from the bug. The bug wants to crawl to the honey, but it must stay on the surface of the lampshade. What is the length in inches of its shortest path to the honey?

Choices

  1. A$6+3\pi$
  2. B$6+6\pi$
  3. C$6\sqrt3$
  4. D$6\sqrt5$
  5. E$6\sqrt3+\pi$

Answer

E. $6\sqrt3+\pi$

Solution

Step 1: Find the slant lengths of the full cone

The bottom radius is $6$, the top radius is $3$, and the vertical height between them is $3\sqrt3$. The frustum's slant height is

\[\sqrt{3^2+(3\sqrt3)^2}=6.\]

By similarity, the slant distance from the cone apex to the top circle is $6$, and to the bottom circle is $12$.

Step 2: Unroll the cone

When the cone is unrolled, the lateral surface becomes a sector. The outer radius is $12$, and the outer arc length is the bottom circumference $12\pi$. Therefore the sector angle is

\[\frac{12\pi}{12}=\pi.\]

Step 3: Locate the two points in the sector

The honey is opposite the bug around the circular frustum. A half-turn around the original cone becomes half of the sector angle, so the angular separation in the unrolled picture is

\[\frac{\pi}{2}.\]

Thus the bug is at radius $12$, and the honey is on the inner circle of radius $6$, separated by angle $\frac\pi2$.

Step 4: Notice why the straight line is not allowed

The straight segment between these two points would pass inside the removed inner disk of radius $6$. That part of the cone is not part of the frustum, so the valid shortest path must touch the inner circle tangentially and then follow the inner circle to the honey.

Step 5: Compute tangent plus arc

From the outer point at radius $12$, the tangent length to the inner circle of radius $6$ is

\[\sqrt{12^2-6^2}=6\sqrt3.\]

The tangent point is $60^\circ$ from the bug's radius, while the honey is $90^\circ$ away, leaving an arc angle of $30^\circ=\frac\pi6$. The arc length is

\[6\cdot\frac\pi6=\pi.\]

Step 6: Add the two pieces

Therefore the shortest surface path has length

\[6\sqrt3+\pi.\]

So the answer is $6\sqrt3+\pi$.

Key Idea

Unroll the frustum as an annular sector; the shortest path must be tangent to the removed inner circle.
