2003 AMC 12A Problem 17

2003AMC 12AProblem 17几何圆与曲线圆曲线

Problem Statement

Square \(ABCD\) has sides of length \(4\), and \(M\) is the midpoint of \(\overline{CD}\). A circle with radius \(2\) and center \(M\) intersects a circle with radius \(4\) and center \(A\) at points \(P\) and \(D\). What is the distance from \(P\) to \(\overline{AD}\)?

Choices

  1. A3
  2. B\(\frac{16}{5}\)
  3. C\(\frac{13}{4}\)
  4. D\(2\sqrt3\)
  5. E\(\frac72\)

Answer

B. \frac{16}{5}

Solution

Step 1: Set coordinates

Let \(D=(0,0)\), \(C=(4,0)\), \(A=(0,4)\), and \(B=(4,4)\). Then \(M=(2,0)\).

Step 2: Write the circle equations

The circle centered at \(A\) with radius \(4\) is \[x^2+(y-4)^2=16.\] The circle centered at \(M\) with radius \(2\) is \[(x-2)^2+y^2=4.\]

Step 3: Solve for the second intersection

The two circles intersect at \(D=(0,0)\) and at another point \(P\). Solving the equations gives \[P=\left(\frac{16}{5},\frac85\right).\]

Step 4: Find the distance to AD

The line \(AD\) is the \(y\)-axis, so the distance from \(P\) to \(AD\) is the \(x\)-coordinate of \(P\), namely \(\frac{16}{5}\).

Step 5: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{\frac{16}{5}}$.

Key Idea

Solve two circle equations in coordinates.
