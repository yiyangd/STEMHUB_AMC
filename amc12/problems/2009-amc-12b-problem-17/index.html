2009 AMC 12B Problem 17

2009AMC 12BProblem 17概率与统计独立性独立事件概率

Problem Statement

Each face of a cube is painted with a narrow stripe that joins the midpoints of two opposite edges of that face. For each face, one of the two possible directions for the stripe is chosen independently at random. What is the probability that the stripes form one continuous stripe encircling the cube?

Choices

  1. A$\frac18$
  2. B$\frac3{16}$
  3. C$\frac14$
  4. D$\frac38$
  5. E$\frac12$

Answer

B. \frac3{16}

Solution

Step 1: Understand the random choices

On each face of the cube there are exactly two possible stripe directions. The six faces choose independently, so a condition involving four specified faces has probability $(1/2)^4$.

Step 2: Think about what a continuous belt means

A stripe that encircles the cube must wrap around one of the three main directions of the cube. You can imagine a belt going around the cube left-right, front-back, or top-bottom.

Step 3: Count one fixed belt

For one chosen belt direction, the four faces around that belt must all choose the correct stripe orientation. The two remaining faces do not matter for making that belt continuous. Therefore one fixed belt has probability \[\left(\frac12\right)^4=\frac1{16}.\]

Step 4: Count the possible belt directions

There are $3$ possible belt directions. A complete belt in two different directions at once is impossible because it would force conflicting stripe choices on some faces.

Step 5: Add the disjoint cases

Thus the probability is \[3\cdot\frac1{16}=\frac3{16}.\]

Step 6: Conclude

The answer is \[\boxed{\frac3{16}}.\]

Key Idea

Count the possible complete belts around the cube.
