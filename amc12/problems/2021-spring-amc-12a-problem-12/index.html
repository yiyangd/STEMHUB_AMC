2021 SpringAMC 12AProblem 12数论整数构造整数构造

Problem Statement

All the roots of \[z^6-10z^5+Az^4+Bz^3+Cz^2+Dz+16\] are positive integers. What is the value of $B$?

Choices

  1. A-88
  2. B-80
  3. C-64
  4. D-41
  5. E-40

Answer

A. -88

Solution

Step 1: Use product and sum of roots

The polynomial is monic of degree $6$. The sum of the six positive integer roots is $10$, and their product is $16$.

Step 2: Find the root multiset

Since $16=2^4$, all roots are powers of $2$ or $1$. Six positive integers with product $16$ and sum $10$ must be \[1,1,2,2,2,2.\]

Step 3: Relate B to elementary symmetric sums

In a monic degree-$6$ polynomial, the coefficient of $z^3$ is \[-e_3,\] where $e_3$ is the sum of all products of three roots.

Step 4: Compute e3

For roots $1,1,2,2,2,2$, the sum of all triple products is \[88.\] Therefore $B=-88$.

Step 5: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{-88}$.

Key Idea

Use integer roots, product, and sum to determine the multiset of roots.
