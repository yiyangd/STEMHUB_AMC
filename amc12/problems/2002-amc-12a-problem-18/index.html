2002 AMC 12A Problem 18

2002AMC 12AProblem 18几何圆与曲线圆曲线

Problem Statement

Let $C_1$ and $C_2$ be circles defined by \[(x-10)^2+y^2=36\] and \[(x+15)^2+y^2=81,\] respectively. What is the length of the shortest line segment $\overline{PQ}$ that is tangent to $C_1$ at $P$ and to $C_2$ at $Q$?

Choices

  1. A15
  2. B18
  3. C20
  4. D21
  5. E24

Answer

C. 20

Solution

Step 1: Identify centers and radii

The first circle has center \((10,0)\) and radius \(6\). The second has center \((-15,0)\) and radius \(9\). The distance between centers is \(25\).

Step 2: Choose the shortest common tangent

The shortest segment tangent to both circles is along an internal common tangent, because the circles lie on opposite sides of that tangent segment.

Step 3: Use the right triangle

For an internal common tangent, the relevant perpendicular offset is the sum of the radii, \(6+9=15\). The center distance \(25\) is the hypotenuse of a right triangle, and the tangent segment is the other leg.

Step 4: Compute

Thus the tangent length is \[\sqrt{25^2-15^2}=\sqrt{625-225}=\sqrt{400}=20.\]

Step 5: Conclude

The shortest possible length is $\boxed{20}$.

Key Idea

Use the internal common tangent length between two circles.
