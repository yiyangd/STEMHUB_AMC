2005 AMC 12A Problem 25

2005AMC 12AProblem 25几何坐标几何坐标几何

Problem Statement

Let \(S\) be the set of all points with coordinates \((x,y,z)\), where \(x,y,\) and \(z\) are each chosen from the set \(\{0,1,2\}\). How many equilateral triangles have all their vertices in \(S\)?

Choices

  1. A72
  2. B76
  3. C80
  4. D84
  5. E88

Answer

C. 80

Solution

Step 1: Classify by side length

All vertices lie in the \(3\times3\times3\) lattice. A coordinate-difference check shows that equilateral triangles can occur only with squared side lengths \(2,6,\) or \(8\).

Step 2: Count side squared 2

Triangles with side squared \(2\) lie inside one of the \(8\) unit cubes. Each unit cube contains \(8\) such triangles, the faces of two complementary regular tetrahedra. This gives \(8\cdot8=64\) triangles.

Step 3: Count side squared 8

Scaling the same unit-cube configuration to the whole \(2\times2\times2\) cube gives \(8\) triangles with side squared \(8\).

Step 4: Count side squared 6

For side squared \(6\), each coordinate \(0,1,2\) appears once among the three vertices, so the centroid is \((1,1,1)\). Ordering the vertices by their \(x\)-coordinate, a direct permutation check for the \(y\)- and \(z\)-coordinates gives \(8\) such triangles.

Step 5: Add the cases

The total number is \[64+8+8=80.\]

Step 6: Conclude

The answer is \(\boxed{80}\).

Key Idea

Classify lattice equilateral triangles by squared side length.
