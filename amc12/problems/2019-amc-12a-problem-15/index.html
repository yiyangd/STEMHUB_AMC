2019 AMC 12A Problem 15

2019AMC 12AProblem 15数论进制与数字数字进制

Problem Statement

Positive real numbers \(a\) and \(b\) have the property that \[\sqrt{\log a}+\sqrt{\log b}+\log\sqrt a+\log\sqrt b=100,\] and all four terms on the left are positive integers, where \(\log\) denotes base \(10\) logarithm. What is \(ab\)?

Choices

  1. A$10^{52}$
  2. B$10^{100}$
  3. C$10^{144}$
  4. D$10^{164}$
  5. E$10^{200}$

Answer

D. 10^{164}

Solution

Step 1: Introduce integer variables

Let \[\sqrt{\log a}=x,\qquad \sqrt{\log b}=y.\] These are positive integers. Since \(\log\sqrt a=\frac12\log a=\frac{x^2}{2}\) is also an integer, \(x\) must be even; similarly \(y\) is even.

Step 2: Write x and y as even numbers

Let \(x=2m\) and \(y=2n\), where \(m,n\) are positive integers.

Step 3: Translate the sum condition

The equation becomes \[2m+2n+2m^2+2n^2=100,\] or \[m(m+1)+n(n+1)=50.\]

Step 4: Find m and n

Testing positive integers gives \[(m,n)=(4,5)\] or \((5,4)\), because \(4\cdot5+5\cdot6=20+30=50\).

Step 5: Compute ab

We have \[\log a=x^2=4m^2,\qquad \log b=y^2=4n^2.\] Therefore \[\log(ab)=4m^2+4n^2=4(16+25)=164.\]

Step 6: Conclude

So \(ab=10^{164}\), and the answer is \(\boxed{10^{164}}\).

Key Idea

Convert the integer log conditions into triangular-number equations.
