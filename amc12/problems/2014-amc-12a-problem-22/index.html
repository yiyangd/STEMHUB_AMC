2014 AMC 12A Problem 22

2014AMC 12AProblem 22数论整数构造整数构造

Problem Statement

The number $5^{867}$ is between $2^{2013}$ and $2^{2014}$. How many pairs of integers $(m,n)$ are there such that $1\le m\le2012$ and \[5^n<2^m<2^{m+2}<5^{n+1}?\]

Choices

  1. A278
  2. B279
  3. C280
  4. D281
  5. E282

Answer

B. 279

Solution

Step 1: Interpret the inequality visually on a number line

The inequality

\[5^n<2^m<2^{m+2}<5^{n+1}\]

means that the three consecutive powers $2^m,2^{m+1},2^{m+2}$ all lie strictly between two consecutive powers of $5$. So each valid pair corresponds to an interval $(5^n,5^{n+1})$ that contains three powers of $2$.

Step 2: Notice that each interval contains only two or three powers of 2

Between $5^n$ and $5^{n+1}$, the ratio of the endpoints is $5$. Since

\[2^2=4<5<8=2^3,\]

there are at least two powers of $2$ in such intervals often, but there can never be four consecutive powers of $2$ inside one interval. Thus each interval contributes either two or three powers of $2$.

Step 3: Count all powers of 2 before $5^{867}$

We are told

\[2^{2013}<5^{867}<2^{2014}.\]

Therefore the powers $2^1,2^2,\ldots,2^{2013}$ are exactly the positive powers of $2$ below $5^{867}$. There are $2013$ such powers.

Step 4: Set up a total-count equation

There are $867$ intervals from $5^0$ to $5^{867}$:

\[(5^0,5^1),(5^1,5^2),\ldots,(5^{866},5^{867}).\]

Let $T$ be the number of these intervals that contain three powers of $2$. The other $867-T$ intervals contain two powers of $2$. Since the total number of powers counted is $2013$,

\[3T+2(867-T)=2013.\]

Step 5: Solve and connect back to pairs

Solving gives

\[3T+1734-2T=2013,\]

so

\[T=279.\]

Each interval with three powers of $2$ determines exactly one starting exponent $m$, namely the first of those three exponents. Therefore the number of valid pairs $(m,n)$ is $279$.

Key Idea

Count how often three consecutive powers of 2 fit between two consecutive powers of 5.
