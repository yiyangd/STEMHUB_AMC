2004 AMC 12A Problem 10

2004AMC 12AProblem 10概率与统计平均数与数据集平均数数据

Problem Statement

The sum of \(49\) consecutive integers is \(7^5\). What is their median?

Choices

  1. A\(7\)
  2. B\(7^2\)
  3. C\(7^3\)
  4. D\(7^4\)
  5. E\(7^5\)

Answer

C. 7^3

Solution

Step 1: Recognize the structure

The numbers are consecutive and there are \(49\) of them, an odd number. That means the middle term is also the median.

Step 2: Connect median and average

For an odd list of consecutive integers, terms pair symmetrically around the middle. Each pair has average equal to the middle term, so the average of all \(49\) integers equals the median.

Step 3: Compute the average

There are \(49=7^2\) integers, and their sum is \(7^5\). Thus the average is \[\frac{7^5}{7^2}=7^3.\]

Step 4: Use the fact to answer

Because the average equals the median for this consecutive odd-length list, the median is \(7^3\).

Step 5: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{7^3}$.

Key Idea

For consecutive integers, the median equals the average.
