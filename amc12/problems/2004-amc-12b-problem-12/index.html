2004 AMC 12B Problem 12

2004AMC 12BProblem 12代数数列与递推数列递推

Problem Statement

In the sequence \(2001,2002,2003,\ldots\), each term after the third is found by subtracting the previous term from the sum of the two terms that precede that term. For example, the fourth term is \(2001+2002-2003=2000\). What is the \(2004\)th term in this sequence?

Choices

  1. A-2004
  2. B-2
  3. C0
  4. D4003
  5. E6007

Answer

C. 0

Solution

Step 1: Compute a few terms

After \(2001,2002,2003\), the next terms are \(2000,2005,1998,2007,\ldots\). This suggests the odd and even positions behave separately.

Step 2: Track odd terms

The odd-indexed terms are \(2001,2003,2005,2007,\ldots\), increasing by \(2\).

Step 3: Track even terms

The even-indexed terms are \(2002,2000,1998,1996,\ldots\), decreasing by \(2\).

Step 4: Locate the 2004th term

The \(2004\)th term is the \(1002\)nd even-indexed term.

Step 5: Compute it

Starting at \(2002\) and subtracting \(2\) for each step, we get \[2002-2(1002-1)=2002-2002=0.\]

Step 6: Conclude

The answer is \(\boxed{0}\).

Key Idea

Separate the recurrence into odd and even subsequences.
