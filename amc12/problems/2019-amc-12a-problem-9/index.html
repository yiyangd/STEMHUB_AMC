2019 AMC 12A Problem 9

2019AMC 12AProblem 9数论质因数与整除质因数整除

Problem Statement

A sequence is defined recursively by \(a_1=1\), \(a_2=\frac37\), and \[a_n=\frac{a_{n-2}a_{n-1}}{2a_{n-2}-a_{n-1}}\] for all \(n\ge3\). Then \(a_{2019}\) can be written as \(\frac pq\), where \(p\) and \(q\) are relatively prime positive integers. What is \(p+q\)?

Choices

  1. A2020
  2. B4039
  3. C6057
  4. D6061
  5. E8078

Answer

E. 8078

Solution

Step 1: Take reciprocals

The recurrence is easier after taking reciprocals. Let \[b_n=\frac1{a_n}.\] Then \[b_n=\frac{2a_{n-2}-a_{n-1}}{a_{n-2}a_{n-1}}=2b_{n-1}-b_{n-2}.\]

Step 2: Compute the first two b-values

Since \(a_1=1\), \(b_1=1\). Since \(a_2=\frac37\), \(b_2=\frac73\).

Step 3: Recognize an arithmetic sequence

The recurrence \(b_n=2b_{n-1}-b_{n-2}\) means the first differences are constant. The difference is \[\frac73-1=\frac43.\]

Step 4: Find b 2019

Thus \[b_n=1+(n-1)\frac43=\frac{4n-1}{3}.\] For \(n=2019\), \[b_{2019}=\frac{8075}{3}.\]

Step 5: Invert

Therefore \[a_{2019}=\frac3{8075}.\] The numerator and denominator are relatively prime.

Step 6: Conclude

So \(p+q=3+8075=\boxed{8078}\).

Key Idea

Take reciprocals to turn the recurrence into an arithmetic sequence.
