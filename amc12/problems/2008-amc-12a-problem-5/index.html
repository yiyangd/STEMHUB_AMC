2008 AMC 12A Problem 5

2008AMC 12AProblem 5数论整数构造整数构造

Problem Statement

Suppose that \[\frac{2x}{3}-\frac{x}{6}\] is an integer. Which of the following statements must be true about \(x\)?

Choices

  1. AIt is negative.
  2. BIt is even, but not necessarily a multiple of \(3\).
  3. CIt is a multiple of \(3\), but not necessarily even.
  4. DIt is a multiple of \(6\), but not necessarily a multiple of \(12\).
  5. EIt is a multiple of \(12\).

Answer

B. It is even, but not necessarily a multiple of 3.

Solution

Step 1: Simplify the expression

The expression is \[\frac{2x}{3}-\frac{x}{6}=\frac{4x}{6}-\frac{x}{6}=\frac{3x}{6}=\frac{x}{2}.\]

Step 2: Use the integer condition

If \(\frac{x}{2}\) is an integer, then \(x\) must be even.

Step 3: Check that stronger statements are not forced

An even number does not have to be a multiple of \(3\). For example, \(x=4\) makes \(\frac{x}{2}=2\), but \(4\) is not a multiple of \(3\), \(6\), or \(12\).

Step 4: Choose the must-be-true statement

The only statement that must always be true is that \(x\) is even, but not necessarily a multiple of \(3\).

Step 5: Conclude

The answer is \(\boxed{\text{B}}\).

Key Idea

Simplify the expression to \( rac{x}{2}\).
