2009 AMC 12A Problem 23

2009AMC 12AProblem 23几何坐标几何坐标几何

Problem Statement

Functions \(f\) and \(g\) are quadratic, \(g(x)=-f(100-x)\), and the graph of \(g\) contains the vertex of the graph of \(f\). The four \(x\)-intercepts on the two graphs have \(x\)-coordinates \(x_1,x_2,x_3,x_4\), in increasing order, and \(x_3-x_2=150\). The value of \(x_4-x_1\) is \(m+n\sqrt p\), where \(m,n,p\) are positive integers and \(p\) is squarefree. What is \(m+n+p\)?

Choices

  1. A602
  2. B652
  3. C702
  4. D752
  5. E802

Answer

D. 752

Solution

Step 1: Use the symmetry between f and g

The relation \(g(x)=-f(100-x)\) means the graph of \(g\) is obtained from the graph of \(f\) by a half-turn around \((50,0)\). Therefore the roots of \(g\) are \(100\) minus the roots of \(f\).

Step 2: Place the middle two roots

By symmetry, the middle two intercepts satisfy \[x_2+x_3=100.\] Since \(x_3-x_2=150\), we get \(x_2=-25\) and \(x_3=125\).

Step 3: Let h be the vertex x-coordinate of f

Assume the roots of \(f\) are \(x_1\) and \(x_3=125\). Then the vertex of \(f\) has \(x\)-coordinate \[h=\frac{x_1+125}{2},\] so \(x_1=2h-125\). By symmetry, \(x_4=100-x_1=225-2h\).

Step 4: Use that g contains the vertex of f

Write \(f(x)=A(x-x_1)(x-125)\). Then \(g(x)=-f(100-x)\). Substituting the vertex point \((h,f(h))\) into \(g\) gives an equation that simplifies to \[h=-25-75\sqrt2.\] The other sign places the roots in the wrong order.

Step 5: Find x4 minus x1

Now \[x_4-x_1=(225-2h)-(2h-125)=350-4h.\] With \(h=-25-75\sqrt2\), this becomes \[350-4(-25-75\sqrt2)=450+300\sqrt2.\]

Step 6: Finish

Thus \(m=450\), \(n=300\), and \(p=2\), so \[m+n+p=752.\]

Step 7: Conclude

The answer is \(\boxed{752}\).

Key Idea

Use the symmetry \(g(x)=-f(100-x)\) to relate the two pairs of roots.
