2003 AMC 12B Problem 25

2003AMC 12BProblem 25概率与统计几何概率几何概率面积

Problem Statement

Three points are chosen randomly and independently on a circle. What is the probability that all three pairwise distances between the points are less than the radius of the circle?

Choices

  1. A\(\frac1{36}\)
  2. B\(\frac1{24}\)
  3. C\(\frac1{18}\)
  4. D\(\frac1{12}\)
  5. E\(\frac19\)

Answer

D. \frac1{12}

Solution

Step 1: Fix one point

By rotational symmetry, fix the first point on the circle. The other two points are independently and uniformly chosen.

Step 2: Translate chord length to angle

A chord is shorter than the radius exactly when its smaller central angle is less than \(60^\circ\), or \(\frac16\) of a full circle.

Step 3: Describe the allowed region

Each of the other two points must lie within \(60^\circ\) of the fixed point. In angular coordinates scaled so the full circle has length \(1\), both lie in an interval \((-1/6,1/6)\).

Step 4: Enforce the third pair

The two moving points must also be within \(1/6\) of each other. Inside the square of side \(1/3\), this removes two right triangles of side \(1/6\), leaving area \[3\left(\frac16\right)^2=\frac1{12}.\]

Step 5: Conclude

The probability is $\boxed{\frac1{12}}$.

Key Idea

Fix one point and measure the allowable angular region for the other two.
