2024 AMC 12B Problem 23

2024AMC 12BProblem 23几何空间几何正八边形棱锥空间垂直高度

Problem Statement

A right pyramid has regular octagon $ABCDEFGH$ with side length $1$ as its base and apex $V$. Segments $AV$ and $DV$ are perpendicular. What is the square of the height of the pyramid?

Choices

  1. A$\frac12$
  2. B$\frac{1+\sqrt2}{2}$
  3. C$\frac23$
  4. D$\sqrt3$
  5. E$2+\sqrt2$

Answer

B. $\frac{1+\sqrt2}{2}$

Solution

Step 1: Place the pyramid in coordinates

Because this is a right pyramid, the apex $V$ lies directly above the center of the regular octagon. Put the octagon in the plane $z=0$ with center at the origin, and let the height be $h$.

The vectors from $V$ to base vertices $A$ and $D$ are then $(\vec a,-h)$ and $(\vec d,-h)$, where $\vec a$ and $\vec d$ are base-plane vectors from the center.

Step 2: Use the perpendicular condition

The condition $AV\perp DV$ means the dot product is zero:

\[(\vec a,-h)\cdot(\vec d,-h)=\vec a\cdot\vec d+h^2=0.\]

So

\[h^2=-\vec a\cdot\vec d.\]

Step 3: Find the angle between the base vectors

In a regular octagon, vertices $A$ and $D$ are separated by three side steps, so the central angle between them is

\[3\cdot45^\circ=135^\circ.\]

If $R$ is the circumradius of the octagon, then

\[\vec a\cdot\vec d=R^2\cos135^\circ=-\frac{R^2\sqrt2}{2}.\]

Step 4: Compute the circumradius

For a regular octagon with side length $1$,

\[1=2R\sin22.5^\circ.\]

This gives

\[R^2=\frac{1}{2-\sqrt2}=\frac{2+\sqrt2}{2}.\]

Step 5: Finish h squared

Therefore

\[h^2=\frac{R^2\sqrt2}{2}=\frac{1+\sqrt2}{2}.\]

So the square of the height is $\frac{1+\sqrt2}{2}$.

Key Idea

Place the right pyramid over the center of the octagon and use a vector dot product.
