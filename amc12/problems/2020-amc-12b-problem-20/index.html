2020 AMC 12B Problem 20

2020AMC 12BProblem 20概率与统计独立性独立事件概率

Problem Statement

Two different cubes of the same size are to be painted, with the color of each face chosen independently and at random to be either black or white. What is the probability that after they are painted, the cubes can be rotated to be identical in appearance?

Choices

  1. A$\frac9{64}$
  2. B$\frac{289}{2048}$
  3. C$\frac{73}{512}$
  4. D$\frac{147}{1024}$
  5. E$\frac{589}{4096}$

Answer

D. \frac{147}{1024}

Solution

Step 1: Think in rotation classes

Each painted cube is one of $2^6=64$ face colorings. Two cubes match after rotation exactly when their colorings lie in the same rotation orbit.

Step 2: List orbit sizes

Under the $24$ rotations of a cube, the $64$ colorings split into orbits of sizes \[1,1,3,3,6,6,8,12,12,12.\]

Step 3: Convert orbit sizes to probability

If an orbit has size $s$, the probability that both random cubes land in that orbit is \[\left(\frac{s}{64}\right)^2.\] Therefore the desired probability is \[\frac{1^2+1^2+3^2+3^2+6^2+6^2+8^2+12^2+12^2+12^2}{64^2}.\]

Step 4: Simplify

The numerator is \[588,\] so the probability is \[\frac{588}{4096}=\frac{147}{1024}.\]

Step 5: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{\frac{147}{1024}}$.

Key Idea

Group cube colorings into rotation orbits and sum orbit-size probabilities.
