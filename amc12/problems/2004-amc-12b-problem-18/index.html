2004 AMC 12B Problem 18

2004AMC 12BProblem 18几何三角形三角形角度

Problem Statement

Points \(A\) and \(B\) are on the parabola \(y=4x^2+7x-1\), and the origin is the midpoint of \(\overline{AB}\). What is the length of \(\overline{AB}\)?

Choices

  1. A\(2\sqrt5\)
  2. B\(5+\frac{\sqrt2}{2}\)
  3. C\(5+\sqrt2\)
  4. D7
  5. E\(5\sqrt2\)

Answer

E. 5\sqrt2

Solution

Step 1: Use midpoint symmetry

If \(A=(x,y)\) and the origin is the midpoint of \(\overline{AB}\), then \(B=(-x,-y)\). Both points lie on the parabola.

Step 2: Write both equations

Since \(A\) is on the parabola, \[y=4x^2+7x-1.\] Since \(B=(-x,-y)\) is on it, \[-y=4x^2-7x-1.\]

Step 3: Eliminate y

The second equation gives \(y=-4x^2+7x+1\). Equating the two expressions for \(y\), \[4x^2+7x-1=-4x^2+7x+1,\] so \(8x^2=2\) and \(x=\pm\frac12\).

Step 4: Find one point

Using \(x=\frac12\), \[y=4\left(\frac14\right)+7\left(\frac12\right)-1=\frac72.\] One endpoint is \((\frac12,\frac72)\), and the other is its negative.

Step 5: Compute the length

The distance from the origin to one endpoint is \[\sqrt{\left(\frac12\right)^2+\left(\frac72\right)^2}=\frac{5\sqrt2}{2}.\] Therefore \(AB\) is twice this, or \(5\sqrt2\).

Step 6: Conclude

The answer is \(\boxed{5\sqrt2}\).

Key Idea

Use midpoint symmetry to pair \((x,y)\) with \((-x,-y)\).
