2019 AMC 12B Problem 22

2019AMC 12BProblem 22数论整数构造整数构造

Problem Statement

Define a sequence recursively by $x_0=5$ and \[x_{n+1}=\frac{x_n^2+5x_n+4}{x_n+6}\] for all nonnegative integers $n$. Let $m$ be the least positive integer such that \[x_m\le4+\frac1{2^{20}}.\] In which of the following intervals does $m$ lie?

Choices

  1. A$[9,26]$
  2. B$[27,80]$
  3. C$[81,242]$
  4. D$[243,728]$
  5. E$[729,\infty)$

Answer

C. [81,242]

Solution

Step 1: Shift the sequence toward the fixed point

The expression suggests that the sequence is approaching $4$. Let \[y_n=x_n-4.\] Then $y_0=1$ and a direct substitution gives \[y_{n+1}=y_n\cdot\frac{y_n+9}{y_n+10}.\]

Step 2: Bound the shrink factor

Since $y_n$ stays positive and starts at $1$, the factor \[\frac{y_n+9}{y_n+10}\] is always between $\frac9{10}$ and $\frac{10}{11}$. Therefore \[\left(\frac9{10}\right)^n<y_n\le\left(\frac{10}{11}\right)^n.\]

Step 3: Show m is greater than 80

Using the lower bound, \[y_{80}>\left(\frac9{10}\right)^{80}.\] This is greater than $2^{-20}$, so $x_{80}>4+\frac1{2^{20}}$. Hence $m>80$.

Step 4: Show m is at most 242

Using the upper bound, \[y_{242}\le\left(\frac{10}{11}\right)^{242}.\] This is less than $2^{-20}$, so $x_{242}\le4+\frac1{2^{20}}$. Hence $m\le242$.

Step 5: Choose the interval

We have shown \[81\le m\le242.\]

Step 6: Conclude

The correct interval is $\boxed{[81,242]}$.

Key Idea

Study the distance from the fixed point 4 and bound the contraction ratio.
