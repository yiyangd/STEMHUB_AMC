Problem Statement

Last summer $30\%$ of the birds living on Town Lake were geese, $25\%$ were swans, $10\%$ were herons, and $35\%$ were ducks. What percent of the birds that were not swans were geese?

Choices

  1. A20
  2. B30
  3. C40
  4. D50
  5. E60

Answer

C. 40

Solution

Step 1: Identify the relevant group

The birds that were not swans make up \[100\%-25\%=75\%\] of all the birds.

Step 2: Use the geese percentage

Geese make up $30\%$ of all the birds.

Step 3: Compute the conditional percentage

Among the non-swans, the fraction that were geese is \[\frac{30}{75}=\frac25.\]

Step 4: Convert to percent

The value $\frac25$ is $40\%$.

Step 5: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{40}$.

Key Idea

Condition on the birds that were not swans.
