2021 Spring AMC 12A Problem 20

2021 SpringAMC 12AProblem 20几何三角形三角形角度

Problem Statement

Suppose that on a parabola with vertex $V$ and focus $F$ there exists a point $A$ such that $AF=20$ and $AV=21$. What is the sum of all possible values of the length $FV$?

Choices

  1. A$13$
  2. B$\frac{40}{3}$
  3. C$\frac{41}{3}$
  4. D$14$
  5. E$\frac{43}{3}$

Answer

B. $\frac{40}{3}$

Solution

Step 1: Set up a standard parabola

Put the vertex at the origin and the focus at $(0,d)$, where $d=FV$. Then the parabola has equation

\[x^2=4dy.\]

Step 2: Use the distance to the directrix

The directrix is the line $y=-d$. If $A=(u,v)$ is on the parabola, then its distance to the focus equals its distance to the directrix. Since $AF=20$,

\[v+d=20,\]

so

\[v=20-d.\]

Step 3: Use the distance to the vertex

The condition $AV=21$ gives

\[u^2+v^2=441.\]

Because $A$ is on the parabola, $u^2=4dv$. Substitute $v=20-d$:

\[4d(20-d)+(20-d)^2=441.\]

Step 4: Solve for d

Simplifying gives

\[3d^2-40d+41=0.\]

This quadratic has two positive roots, and both correspond to possible configurations of the point $A$.

Step 5: Use the sum of roots

The sum of the two possible values of $d$ is

\[\frac{40}{3}\]

by Vieta's formula. Therefore the requested sum is $\frac{40}{3}$.

Key Idea

Use the standard parabola model with vertex at the origin and focus distance d.
