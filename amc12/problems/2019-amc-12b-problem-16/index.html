2019 AMC 12B Problem 16

2019AMC 12BProblem 16概率与统计组合概率概率计数

Problem Statement

There are lily pads in a row numbered $0$ to $11$, in that order. There are predators on lily pads $3$ and $6$, and a morsel of food on lily pad $10$. Fiona the frog starts on pad $0$, and from any given lily pad, has a $\frac12$ chance to hop to the next pad, and an equal chance to jump $2$ pads. What is the probability that Fiona reaches pad $10$ without landing on either pad $3$ or pad $6$?

Choices

  1. A$\frac{15}{256}$
  2. B$\frac1{16}$
  3. C$\frac{15}{128}$
  4. D$\frac18$
  5. E$\frac14$

Answer

A. \frac{15}{256}

Solution

Step 1: Define a hitting probability

Let $p_i$ be the probability that Fiona reaches pad $10$ before landing on pads $3$ or $6$, starting from pad $i$. We know \[p_{10}=1,\quad p_3=p_6=0.\] If she jumps past $10$ to pad $11$, she has not landed on the food, so use $p_{11}=0$.

Step 2: Write the recurrence

From any other pad, Fiona moves one or two pads with equal probability, so \[p_i=\frac12p_{i+1}+\frac12p_{i+2}.\] It is easiest to compute backward from pad $10$.

Step 3: Compute after the second predator

We get \[p_9=\frac12,\quad p_8=\frac34,\quad p_7=\frac58,\quad p_6=0.\] Then \[p_5=\frac{5}{16},\quad p_4=\frac{5}{32},\quad p_3=0.\]

Step 4: Compute back to the start

Now \[p_2=\frac12p_3+\frac12p_4=\frac5{64},\] and \[p_1=\frac12p_2+\frac12p_3=\frac5{128}.\] Finally, \[p_0=\frac12p_1+\frac12p_2=\frac{15}{256}.\]

Step 5: Conclude

The probability is $\boxed{\frac{15}{256}}$.

Key Idea

Work backward with hitting probabilities on the lily-pad line.
