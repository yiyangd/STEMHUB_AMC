2009 AMC 12B Problem 22

2009AMC 12BProblem 22几何面积与相似面积比例

Problem Statement

Parallelogram $ABCD$ has area $1{,}000{,}000$. Vertex $A$ is at $(0,0)$ and all other vertices are in the first quadrant. Vertices $B$ and $D$ are lattice points on the lines $y=x$ and $y=kx$ for some integer $k>1$, respectively. How many such parallelograms are there?

Choices

  1. A49
  2. B720
  3. C784
  4. D2009
  5. E2048

Answer

C. 784

Solution

Step 1: Write the two key vertices

Because $B$ is a lattice point on $y=x$, write $B=(b,b)$ with $b$ a positive integer. Because $D$ is a lattice point on $y=kx$, write $D=(d,kd)$ with $d$ a positive integer and integer $k>1$.

Step 2: Use the determinant for area

The area of parallelogram $ABCD$ is the absolute value of the determinant of the two side vectors: \[\left|\begin{vmatrix}b&d\\ b&kd\end{vmatrix}\right|=bkd-bd=bd(k-1).\] This must equal $1{,}000{,}000$.

Step 3: Turn the problem into factoring

Let $m=k-1$. Then $m$ is a positive integer, and we need \[bdm=1{,}000{,}000.\] Every ordered factorization into the three positive integers $b,d,m$ gives exactly one parallelogram.

Step 4: Count factor distributions

Since \[1{,}000{,}000=10^6=2^6\cdot5^6,\] distribute the exponent $6$ of $2$ among $b,d,m$ in \[\binom{6+3-1}{3-1}=\binom82=28\] ways. The exponent $6$ of $5$ can be distributed independently in another $28$ ways.

Step 5: Multiply

The total number is \[28\cdot28=784.\]

Step 6: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{784}$.

Key Idea

Use the determinant area formula and count factorizations of 1,000,000.
