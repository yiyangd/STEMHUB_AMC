2024 AMC 12B Problem 1 代数 运算与化简 位置计数线性关系

Problem Statement

In a long line of people, the 1013th person from the left is also the 1010th person from the right. How many people are in the line?

Choices

  1. A2021
  2. B2022
  3. C2023
  4. D2024
  5. E2025

Answer

B. 2022

Solution

Step 1

The named person has $1013-1=1012$ people to the left and $1010-1=1009$ people to the right.

Step 2

Counting everyone gives $1012$ people on the left, the person themself, and $1009$ people on the right.

Step 3

Thus the total is $1012+1+1009=2022$, so the answer is $\boxed{2022}$.

Key Idea

Use position counting and subtract the person counted from both sides once.
