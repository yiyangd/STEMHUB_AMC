2024AMC 12BProblem 1代数运算与化简位置计数线性关系

Problem Statement

In a long line of people, the $1013$th person from the left is also the $1010$th person from the right. How many people are in the line?

Choices

  1. A2021
  2. B2022
  3. C2023
  4. D2024
  5. E2025

Answer

B. 2022

Solution

Step 1: Identify what the two positions count

The $1013$th person from the left means there are $1012$ people to that person's left. The $1010$th person from the right means there are $1009$ people to that person's right.

The same person is being described from two directions.

Step 2: Avoid double-counting the same person

If we add the two position numbers directly, that middle person is counted twice. The total number of people is therefore

\[1013+1010-1.\]

The $-1$ removes the duplicate count.

Step 3: Compute the total

Now calculate

\[1013+1010-1=2022.\]

So there are $2022$ people in the line.

Step 4: Check the answer logically

If there are $2022$ people, then the person in position $1013$ from the left has $2022-1013+1=1010$ as the position from the right. This matches the problem, so the answer is $2022$.

Key Idea

Combine the two position counts and subtract the person counted twice.
