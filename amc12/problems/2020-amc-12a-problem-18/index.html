2020 AMC 12A Problem 18

2020AMC 12AProblem 18几何面积与相似面积比例

Problem Statement

Quadrilateral $ABCD$ satisfies $\angle ABC=\angle ACD=90^\circ$, $AC=20$, and $CD=30$. Diagonals $AC$ and $BD$ intersect at point $E$, and $AE=5$. What is the area of quadrilateral $ABCD$?

Choices

  1. A330
  2. B340
  3. C350
  4. D360
  5. E370

Answer

D. 360

Solution

Step 1: Choose coordinates

Put $A=(0,0)$ and $C=(20,0)$. Since $\angle ACD=90^\circ$ and $CD=30$, take $D=(20,30)$; the reflected case gives the same area.

Step 2: Use the diagonal intersection

Because $AE=5$, point $E$ is $(5,0)$. Since $E$ lies on diagonal $BD$, point $B$ lies on the line through $D=(20,30)$ and $E=(5,0)$.

Step 3: Use the right angle at B

The condition $\angle ABC=90^\circ$ means $B$ lies on the circle with diameter $\overline{AC}$. Solving this with the line through $D$ and $E$ gives the convex configuration $B=(2,-6)$.

Step 4: Compute the area

Using the shoelace formula on \[A=(0,0),\ B=(2,-6),\ C=(20,0),\ D=(20,30),\] the area is \[360.\]

Step 5: Conclude

The area of quadrilateral $ABCD$ is $\boxed{360}$.

Key Idea

Use coordinates with AC as an axis and the diagonal intersection condition.
