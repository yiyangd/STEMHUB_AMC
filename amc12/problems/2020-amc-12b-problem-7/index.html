2020 AMC 12B Problem 7

2020AMC 12BProblem 7几何坐标几何坐标几何

Problem Statement

Two nonhorizontal, nonvertical lines in the $xy$-coordinate plane intersect to form a $45^\circ$ angle. One line has slope equal to $6$ times the slope of the other line. What is the greatest possible value of the product of the slopes of the two lines?

Choices

  1. A$\frac16$
  2. B$\frac23$
  3. C1
  4. D$\frac32$
  5. E6

Answer

D. \frac32

Solution

Step 1: Use the angle formula for slopes

If two lines have slopes $m_1$ and $m_2$, then the tangent of the angle between them is \[\left|\frac{m_2-m_1}{1+m_1m_2}\right|.\] Here the angle is $45^\circ$, so this value is $1$.

Step 2: Set the slopes

Let the slopes be $m$ and $6m$. Then \[\left|\frac{6m-m}{1+6m^2}\right|=1.\] Since $1+6m^2>0$, this becomes \[5|m|=1+6m^2.\]

Step 3: Solve for |m|

Let $t=|m|$. Then \[6t^2-5t+1=0,\] so \[(3t-1)(2t-1)=0.\] Thus $t=\frac13$ or $t=\frac12$.

Step 4: Maximize the product

The product of the slopes is \[6m^2=6t^2.\] The larger value occurs at $t=\frac12$, giving \[6\cdot\frac14=\frac32.\]

Step 5: Conclude

The greatest possible product is $\boxed{\frac32}$.

Key Idea

Use the tangent formula for the angle between two lines.
