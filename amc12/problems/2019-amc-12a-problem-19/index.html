2019 AMC 12A Problem 19

2019AMC 12AProblem 19几何面积与相似面积比例

Problem Statement

In $\triangle ABC$ with integer side lengths, $\cos A=\frac{11}{16}$, $\cos B=\frac78$, and $\cos C=-\frac14$. What is the least possible perimeter for $\triangle ABC$?

Choices

  1. A$9$
  2. B$12$
  3. C$23$
  4. D$27$
  5. E$44$

Answer

A. $9$

Solution

Step 1: Convert cosines to sines

The side lengths of a triangle are proportional to the sines of the opposite angles. Since all triangle angles have positive sine,

\[\sin A=\sqrt{1-\left(\frac{11}{16}\right)^2}=\frac{3\sqrt{15}}{16},\]

\[\sin B=\sqrt{1-\left(\frac78\right)^2}=\frac{\sqrt{15}}{8},\]

\[\sin C=\sqrt{1-\left(-\frac14\right)^2}=\frac{\sqrt{15}}{4}.\]

Step 2: Find the side ratio

By the Law of Sines,

\[a:b:c=\sin A:\sin B:\sin C.\]

Substituting the values gives

\[\frac{3\sqrt{15}}{16}:\frac{\sqrt{15}}8:\frac{\sqrt{15}}4.\]

Multiplying by $\frac{16}{\sqrt{15}}$ gives the ratio

\[3:2:4.\]

Step 3: Check that the ratio makes sense

The angle $C$ is obtuse because $\cos C<0$, and the side opposite it should be the largest side. In the ratio $3:2:4$, the largest side is indeed opposite $C$, so the ratio is consistent with the angle data.

Step 4: Use integer side lengths

All side lengths are integers, so the triangle must be an integer multiple of

\[3,2,4.\]

The least possible positive integer side lengths are exactly $3,2,4$.

Step 5: Finish

The least possible perimeter is

\[3+2+4=9.\]

So the answer is $9$.

Key Idea

Use the sine values and the Law of Sines to get the side ratio.
