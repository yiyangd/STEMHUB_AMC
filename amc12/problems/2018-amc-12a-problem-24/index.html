2018 AMC 12A Problem 24

2018AMC 12AProblem 24概率与统计组合概率概率计数

Problem Statement

Alice, Bob, and Carol play a game in which each chooses a real number. The winner is the one whose number is between the other two. Alice chooses uniformly at random from \([0,1]\), and Bob chooses uniformly at random from \(\left[\frac12,\frac23\right]\). What number should Carol choose to maximize her chance of winning?

Choices

  1. A$\frac12$
  2. B$\frac{13}{24}$
  3. C$\frac{7}{12}$
  4. D$\frac58$
  5. E$\frac23$

Answer

B. \frac{13}{24}

Solution

Step 1: Let Carol choose c

Carol's number \(c\) should lie in Bob's interval \(\left[\frac12,\frac23\right]\); outside that interval the winning probability is only linear and is not optimal.

Step 2: Write the two ways Carol can win

Carol wins if Alice chooses below \(c\) and Bob chooses above \(c\), or if Bob chooses below \(c\) and Alice chooses above \(c\).

Step 3: Compute probabilities in the middle interval

For \(\frac12\le c\le\frac23\), Alice is below \(c\) with probability \(c\), and above \(c\) with probability \(1-c\). Bob's interval length is \(\frac16\), so Bob is above \(c\) with probability \(6\left(\frac23-c\right)\), and below \(c\) with probability \(6\left(c-\frac12\right)\).

Step 4: Build the win probability

Thus \[P(c)=6c\left(\frac23-c\right)+6\left(c-\frac12\right)(1-c).\] Simplifying, \[P(c)=-12c^2+13c-3.\]

Step 5: Maximize the quadratic

This downward-opening quadratic is maximized at \[c=\frac{-13}{2(-12)}=\frac{13}{24}.\] This value lies between \(\frac12\) and \(\frac23\).

Step 6: Conclude

Carol should choose \(\boxed{\frac{13}{24}}\).

Key Idea

Write Carol's win probability as a function of her chosen number and maximize it.
