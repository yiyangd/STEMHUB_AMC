2006 AMC 12B Problem 17

2006AMC 12BProblem 17概率与统计组合概率概率计数

Problem Statement

For a particular peculiar pair of dice, the probabilities of rolling \(1,2,3,4,5,\) and \(6\) on each die are in the ratio \(1:2:3:4:5:6\). What is the probability of rolling a total of \(7\) on the two dice?

Choices

  1. A\(\frac4{63}\)
  2. B\(\frac18\)
  3. C\(\frac8{63}\)
  4. D\(\frac16\)
  5. E\(\frac27\)

Answer

C. \frac8{63}

Solution

Step 1: Turn the ratio into probabilities

The weights for faces \(1\) through \(6\) are \(1,2,3,4,5,6\), whose total is \(21\). Thus the probability of rolling face \(i\) is \(\frac{i}{21}\).

Step 2: List ordered pairs summing to 7

The possible ordered pairs are \((1,6),(2,5),(3,4),(4,3),(5,2),(6,1)\).

Step 3: Add weighted probabilities

The probability is \[\frac{1\cdot6+2\cdot5+3\cdot4+4\cdot3+5\cdot2+6\cdot1}{21^2}.\]

Step 4: Compute the numerator

The numerator is \[6+10+12+12+10+6=56.\]

Step 5: Simplify

\[\frac{56}{441}=\frac8{63}.\]

Step 6: Conclude

The probability is \(\boxed{\frac8{63}}\).

Key Idea

Use weighted probabilities for each ordered pair that sums to 7.
