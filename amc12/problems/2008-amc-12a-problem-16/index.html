2008 AMC 12A Problem 16

2008AMC 12AProblem 16代数对数与指数对数指数

Problem Statement

The numbers \(\log(a^3b^7)\), \(\log(a^5b^{12})\), and \(\log(a^8b^{15})\) are the first three terms of an arithmetic sequence, and the \(12\)th term of the sequence is \(\log b^n\). What is \(n\)?

Choices

  1. A40
  2. B56
  3. C76
  4. D112
  5. E143

Answer

D. 112

Solution

Step 1: Rewrite the logarithms linearly

Let \(A=\log a\) and \(B=\log b\). Then the first three terms are \[3A+7B,\quad 5A+12B,\quad 8A+15B.\]

Step 2: Use the arithmetic sequence condition

For three numbers to be consecutive terms of an arithmetic sequence, the middle term must be the average of the first and third: \[2(5A+12B)=(3A+7B)+(8A+15B).\]

Step 3: Solve the relation between A and B

This gives \[10A+24B=11A+22B,\] so \(A=2B\).

Step 4: Find the first term and common difference

Substitute \(A=2B\). The first term is \(3(2B)+7B=13B\), and the second is \(5(2B)+12B=22B\). The common difference is \(9B\).

Step 5: Find the 12th term

The \(12\)th term is \[13B+11(9B)=112B.\] Since \(B=\log b\), this is \(\log b^{112}\).

Step 6: Conclude

Thus \(n=\boxed{112}\).

Key Idea

Turn logarithms into linear expressions in \(\log a\) and \(\log b\).
