2015 AMC 12B Problem 9

2015AMC 12BProblem 9概率与统计独立性独立事件概率

Problem Statement

Larry and Julius are playing a game, taking turns throwing a ball at a bottle sitting on a ledge. Larry throws first. The winner is the first person to knock the bottle off the ledge. At each turn the probability that a player knocks the bottle off the ledge is $\frac12$, independently of what has happened before. What is the probability that Larry wins the game?

Choices

  1. A$\frac12$
  2. B$\frac35$
  3. C$\frac23$
  4. D$\frac34$
  5. E$\frac45$

Answer

C. \frac23

Solution

Step 1: Let P be Larry's winning probability

Larry can win immediately on his first throw with probability $\frac12$.

Step 2: Consider what happens if both miss

If Larry misses and then Julius misses, which has probability \[\frac12\cdot\frac12=\frac14,\] the game returns to the same situation with Larry to throw.

Step 3: Set up the recurrence

Therefore \[P=\frac12+\frac14P.\]

Step 4: Solve

\[\frac34P=\frac12,\] so \[P=\frac23.\]

Step 5: Conclude

The answer is \[\boxed{\frac23}.\]

Key Idea

Set up a recurrence after both players miss.
