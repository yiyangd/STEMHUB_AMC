2015 AMC 12A Problem 12

2015AMC 12AProblem 12几何面积与相似面积比例

Problem Statement

The parabolas $y=ax^2-2$ and $y=4-bx^2$ intersect the coordinate axes in exactly four points, and these four points are the vertices of a kite of area $12$. What is $a+b$?

Choices

  1. A1
  2. B1.5
  3. C2
  4. D2.5
  5. E3

Answer

B. 1.5

Solution

Step 1: Identify the intercepts

The first parabola has $y$-intercept $(0,-2)$ and the second has $y$-intercept $(0,4)$. For the four axis intercepts to form a kite, the two parabolas must share the same pair of $x$-intercepts.

Step 2: Use the kite area

The vertical diagonal from $(0,-2)$ to $(0,4)$ has length $6$. Let the shared $x$-intercepts be $(\pm t,0)$, so the horizontal diagonal has length $2t$.

Step 3: Solve for t

The kite area is \[\frac12\cdot6\cdot2t=6t.\] Since the area is $12$, we get \[t=2.\]

Step 4: Find a and b

For $y=ax^2-2$, the $x$-intercepts satisfy \[0=at^2-2,\] so with $t=2$, \[a=\frac12.\] For $y=4-bx^2$, \[0=4-bt^2,\] so \[b=1.\]

Step 5: Conclude

\[a+b=\frac12+1=1.5.\] The answer is $\boxed{1.5}$.

Key Idea

Use the kite diagonals formed by the shared axis intercepts.
