2008 AMC 12B Problem 9

2008AMC 12BProblem 9几何圆与曲线圆曲线

Problem Statement

Points \(A\) and \(B\) are on a circle of radius \(5\), and \(AB=6\). Point \(C\) is the midpoint of the minor arc \(AB\). What is the length of the line segment \(AC\)?

Choices

  1. A\(\sqrt{10}\)
  2. B\(\sqrt7\)
  3. C\(\sqrt{14}\)
  4. D\(\sqrt{15}\)
  5. E\(4\sqrt2\)

Answer

A. \sqrt{10}

Solution

Step 1: Use the chord midpoint

Let \(M\) be the midpoint of chord \(\overline{AB}\). Since \(AB=6\), \(AM=3\). The radius to the midpoint of a chord is perpendicular to the chord.

Step 2: Find the distance from the center to the chord

The circle radius is \(5\), so in right triangle \(OMA\), \[OM=\sqrt{5^2-3^2}=\sqrt{25-9}=4.\]

Step 3: Use the midpoint of the minor arc

Point \(C\), the midpoint of the minor arc, lies on the same perpendicular line through \(O\) and \(M\). Since \(OC=5\), the distance \(MC\) is \[5-4=1.\]

Step 4: Compute AC

Triangle \(AMC\) is right, with legs \(AM=3\) and \(MC=1\). Therefore \[AC=\sqrt{3^2+1^2}=\sqrt{10}.\]

Step 5: Conclude

The answer is \(\boxed{\sqrt{10}}\).

Key Idea

Use the perpendicular from the circle center to the chord.
