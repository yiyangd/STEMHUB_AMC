2024AMC 12BProblem 13代数函数与图像配方二次函数最值

Problem Statement

There are real numbers $x,y,h$, and $k$ that satisfy $x^2+y^2-6x-8y=h$ and $x^2+y^2-10x+4y=k$. What is the minimum possible value of $h+k$?

Choices

  1. A-54
  2. B-46
  3. C-34
  4. D-16
  5. E16

Answer

C. -34

Solution

Step 1: Add the two expressions

The variables $h$ and $k$ are defined by the two equations, so

\[h+k=(x^2+y^2-6x-8y)+(x^2+y^2-10x+4y).\]

This simplifies to

\[h+k=2x^2+2y^2-16x-4y.\]

Step 2: Complete the square in x

For the $x$ terms,

\[2x^2-16x=2(x^2-8x)=2((x-4)^2-16).\]

So this contributes

\[2(x-4)^2-32.\]

Step 3: Complete the square in y

For the $y$ terms,

\[2y^2-4y=2(y^2-2y)=2((y-1)^2-1).\]

So this contributes

\[2(y-1)^2-2.\]

Step 4: Read off the minimum

Therefore

\[h+k=2(x-4)^2+2(y-1)^2-34.\]

The squared terms are always nonnegative, so the minimum occurs at $x=4$ and $y=1$. The minimum value is $-34$.

Key Idea

Add the two expressions and complete the square.
