2016 AMC 12A Problem 12

2016AMC 12AProblem 12代数运算与化简代数化简

Problem Statement

In $\triangle ABC$, $AB=6$, $BC=7$, and $CA=8$. Point $D$ lies on $\overline{BC}$, and $\overline{AD}$ bisects $\angle BAC$. Point $E$ lies on $\overline{AC}$, and $\overline{BE}$ bisects $\angle ABC$. The bisectors intersect at $F$. What is the ratio $AF:FD$?

Choices

  1. A$3:2$
  2. B$5:3$
  3. C$2:1$
  4. D$7:3$
  5. E$5:2$

Answer

C. 2:1

Solution

Step 1: Use the angle-bisector theorem

Since $\overline{AD}$ bisects $\angle A$, the angle-bisector theorem gives \[BD:DC=AB:AC=6:8=3:4.\] Because $BC=7$, we get $BD=3$ and $DC=4$.

Step 2: Find the length of the angle bisector

The angle-bisector length formula gives \[AD^2=bc\left(1-\frac{a^2}{(b+c)^2}\right),\] where $a=BC=7$, $b=CA=8$, and $c=AB=6$. Thus \[AD^2=48\left(1-\frac{49}{196}\right)=36,\] so $AD=6$.

Step 3: Compute the inradius

By Heron's formula, the area of the $6$-$7$-$8$ triangle is \[\sqrt{\frac{21}{2}\cdot\frac{9}{2}\cdot\frac{7}{2}\cdot\frac{5}{2}}=\frac{21\sqrt{15}}4.\] The semiperimeter is $\frac{21}{2}$, so the inradius is \[r=\frac{K}{s}=\frac{\sqrt{15}}2.\]

Step 4: Relate AF to the inradius

The incenter $F$ lies on the angle bisector $\overline{AD}$. If $AF=t$, then the perpendicular distance from $F$ to side $AB$ is $t\sin(\angle BAD)$. From triangle $ABD$ with sides $AB=AD=6$ and $BD=3$, we have \[\sin(\angle BAD)=\frac{\sqrt{15}}8.\]

Step 5: Solve for AF and FD

Since that perpendicular distance is the inradius, \[t\cdot\frac{\sqrt{15}}8=\frac{\sqrt{15}}2,\] so $t=4$. Therefore $AF=4$ and $FD=AD-AF=2.

Step 6: Conclude

The ratio is \[AF:FD=4:2=\boxed{2:1}.\]

Key Idea

Use the angle-bisector theorem, the angle-bisector length, and the inradius along the same line.
