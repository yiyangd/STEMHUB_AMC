2018 AMC 12A Problem 18

2018AMC 12AProblem 18几何面积与相似面积比例

Problem Statement

Triangle \(ABC\) with \(AB=50\) and \(AC=10\) has area \(120\). Let \(D\) be the midpoint of \(AB\), and let \(E\) be the midpoint of \(AC\). The angle bisector of \(\angle BAC\) intersects \(DE\) and \(BC\) at \(F\) and \(G\), respectively. What is the area of quadrilateral \(FDBG\)?

Choices

  1. A60
  2. B65
  3. C70
  4. D75
  5. E80

Answer

D. 75

Solution

Step 1: Use the angle-bisector theorem

The angle bisector from \(A\) meets \(BC\) at \(G\), so \[BG:GC=AB:AC=50:10=5:1.\] Therefore \([ABG]=\frac56[ABC]=\frac56\cdot120=100.\]

Step 2: Use the midpoint segment

Since \(D\) and \(E\) are midpoints of \(AB\) and \(AC\), segment \(DE\) is parallel to \(BC\). The line \(DE\) is the image of \(BC\) under a homothety centered at \(A\) with scale factor \(\frac12\).

Step 3: Locate F by scaling

Because \(F\) and \(G\) lie on the same angle bisector from \(A\), point \(F\) is the midpoint-scale image of \(G\). Thus triangle \(ADF\) is similar to triangle \(ABG\) with scale factor \(\frac12\).

Step 4: Subtract areas

So \([ADF]=\frac14[ABG]=25.\] The quadrilateral \(FDBG\) is triangle \(ABG\) with triangle \(ADF\) removed, so its area is \[100-25=75.\]

Step 5: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{75}$.

Key Idea

Use the angle-bisector theorem and the midline homothety.
