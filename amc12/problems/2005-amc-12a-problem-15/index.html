2005 AMC 12A Problem 15

2005AMC 12AProblem 15几何圆与曲线圆曲线

Problem Statement

Let \(\overline{AB}\) be a diameter of a circle and \(C\) be a point on \(\overline{AB}\) with \(2\cdot AC=BC\). Let \(D\) and \(E\) be points on the circle such that \(\overline{DC}\perp\overline{AB}\) and \(\overline{DE}\) is a second diameter. What is the ratio of the area of \(\triangle DCE\) to the area of \(\triangle ABD\)?

Choices

  1. A\(\frac16\)
  2. B\(\frac14\)
  3. C\(\frac13\)
  4. D\(\frac12\)
  5. E\(\frac23\)

Answer

C. \frac13

Solution

Step 1: Choose convenient coordinates

Let \(A=(0,0)\), \(B=(3,0)\). Since \(2AC=BC\), we have \(AC=1\) and \(BC=2\), so \(C=(1,0)\).

Step 2: Find D

The circle has center \((\frac32,0)\) and radius \(\frac32\). Since \(DC\perp AB\), point \(D\) has \(x=1\). The circle equation gives \[DC=\sqrt{\left(\frac32\right)^2-\left(\frac12\right)^2}=\sqrt2.\]

Step 3: Find E from the diameter

Because \(\overline{DE}\) is a diameter, \(E\) is the point opposite \(D\) through the center. Thus \(E=(2,-\sqrt2)\).

Step 4: Compute triangle areas

Triangle \(DCE\) has base \(DC=\sqrt2\) and horizontal height \(1\), so its area is \(\frac{\sqrt2}{2}\). Triangle \(ABD\) has base \(AB=3\) and height \(\sqrt2\), so its area is \(\frac{3\sqrt2}{2}\).

Step 5: Take the ratio

The ratio is \[\frac{\sqrt2/2}{3\sqrt2/2}=\frac13.\]

Step 6: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{\frac13}$.

Key Idea

Use coordinates on a circle with a diameter.
