2022 AMC 12A Problem 6

2022AMC 12AProblem 6概率与统计平均数与数据集平均数数据

Problem Statement

A data set consists of $6$ not necessarily distinct positive integers: $1,7,5,2,5,$ and $X$. The average of the $6$ numbers equals a value in the data set. What is the sum of all possible positive values of $X$?

Choices

  1. A10
  2. B26
  3. C32
  4. D36
  5. E40

Answer

D. 36

Solution

Step 1: Write the mean in terms of X

The known five numbers have sum \[1+7+5+2+5=20.\] So the average of all six numbers is \[\frac{20+X}{6}.\]

Step 2: Use the key condition

The average must equal one of the values in the data set. That means it could be $1,2,5,7,$ or $X$.

Step 3: Test the fixed values

If the mean is $m$, then \[\frac{20+X}{6}=m,\] so $X=6m-20$. For $m=1,2,5,7$, this gives $X=-14,-8,10,22$. The positive values from these are $10$ and $22$.

Step 4: Test the possibility that the mean equals X

If the mean equals $X$, then \[\frac{20+X}{6}=X.\] This gives $20=5X$, so $X=4$.

Step 5: Add all possible positive values

The possible positive values are $4,10,$ and $22$, whose sum is \[4+10+22=36.\]

Step 6: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{36}$.

Key Idea

List possible data-set values for the mean and solve for X.
