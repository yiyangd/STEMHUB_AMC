2024 AMC 12A Problem 8

2024AMC 12AProblem 8代数三角恒等式对数三角函数定义域方程

Problem Statement

How many angles $\theta$ with $0\le\theta\le2\pi$ satisfy \[\log(\sin(3\theta))+\log(\cos(2\theta))=0?\]

Choices

  1. A0
  2. B1
  3. C2
  4. D3
  5. E4

Answer

A. 0

Solution

Step 1: Use the logarithm rule carefully

The logarithms are real only when \[\sin(3\theta)>0\quad\text{and}\quad \cos(2\theta)>0.\] Then the equation becomes \[\log(\sin(3\theta)\cos(2\theta))=0.\]

Step 2: Translate log equals zero

Since $\log(1)=0$, we need \[\sin(3\theta)\cos(2\theta)=1.\]

Step 3: Use the maximum possible product

Both factors are at most $1$, and both are positive. Their product can be $1$ only if \[\sin(3\theta)=1\quad\text{and}\quad \cos(2\theta)=1.\]

Step 4: Compare the angle conditions

The first condition gives \[\theta=\frac{\pi}{6}+\frac{2\pi k}{3},\] while the second gives \[\theta=m\pi.\] These two forms cannot agree.

Step 5: Conclude

There are no such angles, so the answer is $\boxed{0}$.

Key Idea

Use logarithm domain and the fact that both sine and cosine factors are at most 1.
