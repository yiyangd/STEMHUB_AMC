2006 AMC 12A Problem 13

2006AMC 12AProblem 13几何圆与曲线圆曲线

Problem Statement

The vertices of a \(3\)-\(4\)-\(5\) right triangle are the centers of three mutually externally tangent circles. What is the sum of the areas of the three circles?

Choices

  1. A\(12\pi\)
  2. B\(\frac{25\pi}{2}\)
  3. C\(13\pi\)
  4. D\(\frac{27\pi}{2}\)
  5. E\(14\pi\)

Answer

E. 14\pi

Solution

Step 1: Name the radii

Let the radii of the circles at the three triangle vertices be \(a,b,c\). Externally tangent circles have center distance equal to the sum of their radii.

Step 2: Translate the side lengths

The side lengths \(3,4,5\) give equations \[a+b=3,\quad b+c=4,\quad a+c=5,\] after labeling the circles appropriately.

Step 3: Solve for the radii

Adding all three equations gives \[2(a+b+c)=12,\] so \(a+b+c=6\). Then \(c=6-3=3\), \(a=6-4=2\), and \(b=6-5=1\).

Step 4: Compute the total area

The sum of the areas is \[\pi(a^2+b^2+c^2)=\pi(2^2+1^2+3^2)=14\pi.\]

Step 5: Conclude

The answer is \(\boxed{14\pi}\).

Key Idea

Use pairwise tangency to turn side lengths into sums of circle radii.
