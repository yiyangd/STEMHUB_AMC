2006 AMC 12B Problem 9

2006AMC 12BProblem 9数论进制与数字数字进制

Problem Statement

How many even three-digit integers have the property that their digits, read left to right, are in strictly increasing order?

Choices

  1. A21
  2. B34
  3. C51
  4. D72
  5. E150

Answer

B. 34

Solution

Step 1: Use the last digit

The integer must be even, so its last digit must be \(2,4,6,\) or \(8\). Because the digits are strictly increasing, the first two digits must be positive digits smaller than the last digit.

Step 2: Count for last digit 2

If the last digit is \(2\), the only smaller positive digit is \(1\), so there are not enough digits to make a three-digit number. This gives \(0\).

Step 3: Count for last digit 4

Choose the first two digits from \(\{1,2,3\}\), giving \(\binom32=3\) numbers.

Step 4: Count for last digits 6 and 8

For last digit \(6\), choose two digits from \(\{1,2,3,4,5\}\), giving \(\binom52=10\). For last digit \(8\), choose two from \(\{1,2,\ldots,7\}\), giving \(\binom72=21\).

Step 5: Add the cases

The total is \[0+3+10+21=34.\]

Step 6: Conclude

There are \(\boxed{34}\) such integers.

Key Idea

Count choices by the final even digit.
