2018 AMC 12B Problem 16

2018AMC 12BProblem 16几何面积与相似面积比例

Problem Statement

The solutions to the equation $(z+6)^8=81$ are connected in the complex plane to form a convex regular polygon, three of whose vertices are labeled $A,B,$ and $C$. What is the least possible area of $\triangle ABC$?

Choices

  1. A$\frac{\sqrt6}{6}$
  2. B$\frac{3\sqrt2}{2}-\frac32$
  3. C$2\sqrt3-3\sqrt2$
  4. D$\frac{\sqrt2}{2}$
  5. E$\sqrt3-1$

Answer

B. $\frac{3\sqrt2}{2}-\frac32$

Solution

Step 1: Identify the polygon

The equation can be written as

\[(z+6)^8=81=3^4.\]

Thus the eight solutions for $z+6$ have magnitude

\[81^{1/8}=\sqrt3.\]

So the solutions form a regular octagon of radius $\sqrt3$, translated left by $6$. Translation does not change area.

Step 2: Choose the smallest triangle

Among triangles formed by vertices of a regular octagon, the smallest nonzero area comes from three consecutive vertices. Any wider spacing increases either the base or the height.

Step 3: Use convenient consecutive vertices

Take the vertices at angles $45^\circ$, $90^\circ$, and $135^\circ$. Their coordinates are

\[\left(\frac{\sqrt6}{2},\frac{\sqrt6}{2}\right),\quad (0,\sqrt3),\quad \left(-\frac{\sqrt6}{2},\frac{\sqrt6}{2}\right).\]

Step 4: Compute base and height

The base between the two lower points has length $\sqrt6$. The height from the middle point to that base is

\[\sqrt3-\frac{\sqrt6}{2}.\]

Therefore the area is

\[\frac12\sqrt6\left(\sqrt3-\frac{\sqrt6}{2}\right).\]

Step 5: Simplify

This becomes

\[\frac{3\sqrt2}{2}-\frac32.\]

So the least possible area is $\frac{3\sqrt2}{2}-\frac32$.

Key Idea

The roots form a regular octagon; the least nonzero triangle uses three consecutive vertices.
