2013 AMC 12B Problem 9

2013AMC 12BProblem 9几何三角形三角形角度

Problem Statement

What is the sum of the exponents of the prime factors of the square root of the largest perfect square that divides $12!$?

Choices

  1. A5
  2. B7
  3. C8
  4. D10
  5. E12

Answer

C. 8

Solution

Step 1: Factor 12 factorial

The prime exponents in $12!$ are \[v_2=6+3+1=10,\quad v_3=4+1=5,\quad v_5=2,\quad v_7=1,\quad v_{11}=1.\]

Step 2: Build the largest square divisor

A perfect square can use only even exponents. So the largest square divisor of $12!$ has exponents \[2^{10}3^4 5^2.\]

Step 3: Take the square root

The square root of that square has exponents half as large: \[2^5 3^2 5^1.\]

Step 4: Add the exponents

The requested sum is \[5+2+1=8.\]

Step 5: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{8}$.

Key Idea

Factor 12! and take the even exponents that form the largest square.
