2004 AMC 12B Problem 21

2004AMC 12BProblem 21几何圆与曲线圆曲线

Problem Statement

The graph of \(2x^2+xy+3y^2-11x-20y+40=0\) is an ellipse in the first quadrant of the \(xy\)-plane. Let \(a\) and \(b\) be the maximum and minimum values of \(\frac{y}{x}\) over all points \((x,y)\) on the ellipse. What is the value of \(a+b\)?

Choices

  1. A3
  2. B\(\sqrt{10}\)
  3. C\(\frac72\)
  4. D\(\frac92\)
  5. E\(2\sqrt{14}\)

Answer

C. \frac72

Solution

Step 1: Introduce the slope

The quantity \(\frac{y}{x}\) is the slope of the line from the origin to \((x,y)\). Let \(t=\frac{y}{x}\), so \(y=tx\).

Step 2: Substitute into the ellipse

Substituting \(y=tx\) gives \[(2+t+3t^2)x^2-(11+20t)x+40=0.\] For a given \(t\), this line intersects the ellipse when this quadratic has a real solution.

Step 3: Use the discriminant

The extreme values of \(t\) occur when the line is tangent, so the discriminant is \(0\): \[(11+20t)^2-160(2+t+3t^2)=0.\]

Step 4: Simplify

This becomes \[-80t^2+280t-199=0,\] or \[80t^2-280t+199=0.\]

Step 5: Use sum of roots

The maximum and minimum slopes are the two roots of this quadratic. Their sum is \(\frac{280}{80}=\frac72\).

Step 6: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{\frac72}$.

Key Idea

Use the slope \(t=y/x\) and require the line \(y=tx\) to touch the ellipse.
