2018 AMC 12B Problem 23

2018AMC 12BProblem 23几何空间几何空间几何体积

Problem Statement

Ajay is standing at point \(A\) near Pontianak, Indonesia, \(0^\circ\) latitude and \(110^\circ\) E longitude. Billy is standing at point \(B\) near Big Baldy Mountain, Idaho, USA, \(45^\circ\) N latitude and \(115^\circ\) W longitude. Assume that Earth is a perfect sphere with center \(C\). What is the degree measure of \(\angle ACB\)?

Choices

  1. A105
  2. B$112\frac12$
  3. C120
  4. D135
  5. E150

Answer

C. 120

Solution

Step 1: Represent the locations on a unit sphere

For latitude \(\phi\) and longitude \(\lambda\), a point on the unit sphere has vector \[(\cos\phi\cos\lambda,\ \cos\phi\sin\lambda,\ \sin\phi).\] The central angle is found from the dot product of the two unit vectors.

Step 2: Use Ajay's coordinates

Ajay is at latitude \(0^\circ\), longitude \(110^\circ\) E. His vector is \[(\cos110^\circ,\ \sin110^\circ,\ 0).\]

Step 3: Use Billy's coordinates

Billy is at latitude \(45^\circ\) N and longitude \(115^\circ\) W, which is \(-115^\circ\). His vector has horizontal scale \(\cos45^\circ=\frac{\sqrt2}{2}\).

Step 4: Compute the dot product

The longitude difference is \[110^\circ-(-115^\circ)=225^\circ.\] Thus \[\cos\angle ACB=\cos0^\circ\cos45^\circ\cos225^\circ+\sin0^\circ\sin45^\circ.\]

Step 5: Simplify

This equals \[1\cdot\frac{\sqrt2}{2}\cdot\left(-\frac{\sqrt2}{2}\right)+0=-\frac12.\]

Step 6: Find the angle

The angle whose cosine is \(-\frac12\) is \(120^\circ\).

Step 7: Conclude

The degree measure of \(\angle ACB\) is \(\boxed{120}\).

Key Idea

Use spherical-coordinate unit vectors and the dot product to find the central angle.
