2009 AMC 12B Problem 19

2009AMC 12BProblem 19数论质因数与整除质因数整除

Problem Statement

Let $f(n)=n^4-360n^2+400$. What is the sum of all values of $f(n)$ that are prime numbers, where $n$ is an integer?

Choices

  1. A0
  2. B41
  3. C401
  4. D761
  5. E802

Answer

E. 802

Solution

Step 1: Look for a factorization

A quartic that is quadratic in $n^2$ often factors after completing a square-like pattern. Here we can write \[f(n)=n^4-360n^2+400=(n^2-20n+20)(n^2+20n+20).\]

Step 2: Use the meaning of prime

If $f(n)$ is a positive prime, it cannot be a product of two integers both larger than $1$. The second factor $n^2+20n+20$ is positive and larger than $1$ for the relevant positive solutions, so the first factor must be $1$.

Step 3: Solve the first-factor condition

Set \[n^2-20n+20=1.\] Then \[n^2-20n+19=0,\] so \[(n-1)(n-19)=0.\] Thus $n=1$ or $n=19$.

Step 4: Evaluate the prime values

We get \[f(1)=1-360+400=41,\] and \[f(19)=19^4-360\cdot19^2+400=761.\] Both are prime.

Step 5: Add the values

The required sum is \[41+761=802.\]

Step 6: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{802}$.

Key Idea

Factor the quartic and use the fact that a positive prime has only one nontrivial factor.
