2011 AMC 12A Problem 9

2011AMC 12AProblem 9代数运算与化简代数化简

Problem Statement

At a twins and triplets convention, there were $9$ sets of twins and $6$ sets of triplets, all from different families. Each twin shook hands with all the twins except his or her sibling and with half the triplets. Each triplet shook hands with all the triplets except his or her siblings and half the twins. How many handshakes took place?

Choices

  1. A324
  2. B441
  3. C630
  4. D648
  5. E882

Answer

B. 441

Solution

Step 1: Count the people

There are $9$ sets of twins, so $18$ twins. There are $6$ sets of triplets, so $18$ triplets.

Step 2: Count twin-twin handshakes

Among all twins there are \[\binom{18}{2}=153\] pairs, but the $9$ sibling pairs do not shake hands. So twin-twin handshakes contribute \[153-9=144.\]

Step 3: Count triplet-triplet handshakes

Among all triplets there are \[\binom{18}{2}=153\] pairs. In each triplet family, the $3$ sibling pairs do not shake hands, so subtract \[6\cdot3=18.\] This gives \[153-18=135.\]

Step 4: Count twin-triplet handshakes

Each of the $18$ twins shook hands with half of the $18$ triplets, so the cross handshakes contribute \[18\cdot9=162.\]

Step 5: Add all types

The total is \[144+135+162=441.\]

Step 6: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{441}$.

Key Idea

Count twin-twin, triplet-triplet, and cross handshakes separately.
