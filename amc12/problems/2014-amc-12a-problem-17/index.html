2014 AMC 12A Problem 17

2014AMC 12AProblem 17几何空间几何空间几何体积

Problem Statement

A $4\times4\times h$ rectangular box contains a sphere of radius $2$ and eight smaller spheres of radius $1$. The smaller spheres are each tangent to three sides of the box, and the larger sphere is tangent to each of the smaller spheres. What is $h$?

Choices

  1. A$2+2\sqrt7$
  2. B$3+2\sqrt5$
  3. C$4+2\sqrt7$
  4. D$4\sqrt5$
  5. E$4\sqrt7$

Answer

A. $2+2\sqrt7$

Solution

Step 1: Place the box in coordinates

Let the box be

\[0\le x\le4,\qquad 0\le y\le4,\qquad 0\le z\le h.\]

A small sphere tangent to the three coordinate-side faces has center $(1,1,1)$.

Step 2: Locate the large sphere by symmetry

The eight small spheres sit symmetrically near the eight corners of the box. Therefore the large sphere's center must lie on the center line of the box:

\[\left(2,2,\frac h2\right).\]

This avoids needing a diagram; the symmetry forces the horizontal coordinates to be $2$ and $2$.

Step 3: Translate tangency into a distance equation

A sphere of radius $2$ tangent to a sphere of radius $1$ has center-to-center distance

\[2+1=3.\]

So the distance from $(1,1,1)$ to $\left(2,2,\frac h2\right)$ must be $3$:

\[\sqrt{(2-1)^2+(2-1)^2+\left(\frac h2-1\right)^2}=3.\]

Step 4: Solve for h

Squaring gives

\[1+1+\left(\frac h2-1\right)^2=9,\]

so

\[\left(\frac h2-1\right)^2=7.\]

Since the height is greater than $2$, we take the positive value:

\[\frac h2-1=\sqrt7.\]

Step 5: Finish

Thus

\[h=2+2\sqrt7.\]

The answer is $2+2\sqrt7$.

Key Idea

Place sphere centers in 3D coordinates and use the tangency distance between centers.
