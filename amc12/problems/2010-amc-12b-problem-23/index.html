2010 AMC 12B Problem 23

2010AMC 12BProblem 23代数多项式与根多项式根

Problem Statement

Monic quadratic polynomials $P(x)$ and $Q(x)$ have the property that $P(Q(x))$ has zeroes at $x=-23,-21,-17,$ and $-15$, and $Q(P(x))$ has zeroes at $x=-59,-57,-51,$ and $-49$. What is the sum of the minimum values of $P(x)$ and $Q(x)$?

Choices

  1. A$-100$
  2. B$-82$
  3. C$-73$
  4. D$-64$
  5. E0

Answer

A. -100

Solution

Step 1: Use the symmetry of a quadratic

A monic quadratic takes the same value at two inputs exactly when those inputs are symmetric about its axis of symmetry.

Step 2: Pair the roots for Q

The four zeros of $P(Q(x))$ are $-23,-21,-17,-15$. The pairing that gives a single symmetry axis is \[-23\text{ with }-15,\qquad -21\text{ with }-17,\] both centered at $-19$. Thus $Q$ has axis $x=-19$, so write \[Q(x)=(x+19)^2+q.\]

Step 3: Use the roots of P

Then \[Q(-23)=Q(-15)=16+q,\] and \[Q(-21)=Q(-17)=4+q.\] These two values are the roots of $P$.

Step 4: Use the symmetry of P

The zeros of $Q(P(x))$ are $-59,-57,-51,-49$. Pairing \[-59\text{ with }-49,\qquad -57\text{ with }-51\] gives center $-54$, so the axis of $P$ is $x=-54$.

Step 5: Determine q and the minima

The roots of $P$ are $q+16$ and $q+4$, whose average is the axis value $-54$. Hence \[q+10=-54,\] so $q=-64$. Therefore the minimum of $Q$ is $-64$. The roots of $P$ are $-48$ and $-60$, so \[P(x)=(x+54)^2-36,\] and the minimum of $P$ is $-36$.

Step 6: Conclude

The sum of the two minimum values is \[-64+(-36)=-100.\] The answer is $\boxed{-100}$.

Key Idea

Use symmetry of roots of a monic quadratic composition.
