2007 AMC 12B Problem 21

2007AMC 12BProblem 21数论进制与数字数字进制

Problem Statement

The first \(2007\) positive integers are each written in base \(3\). How many of these base-\(3\) representations are palindromes? A palindrome is a number that reads the same forward and backward.

Choices

  1. A100
  2. B101
  3. C102
  4. D103
  5. E104

Answer

A. 100

Solution

Step 1: Convert the upper bound to base 3

Since \(3^6=729\) and \(3^7=2187\), the number \(2007\) has \(7\) base-\(3\) digits. In fact, \[2007_{10}=2202100_3.\]

Step 2: Count palindromes with fewer than 7 digits

A base-\(3\) palindrome cannot begin with \(0\). For lengths \(1,2,3,4,5,6\), the counts are \[2,\ 2,\ 6,\ 6,\ 18,\ 18.\] Their total is \(52\).

Step 3: Count 7-digit palindromes beginning with 1

Every 7-digit palindrome beginning with \(1\) is less than \(2202100_3\). The middle three freely chosen digits give \(3^3=27\) such palindromes.

Step 4: Count 7-digit palindromes beginning with 2

For a 7-digit palindrome \(2abcba2\), compare it with \(2202100_3\). If \(a=0\) or \(1\), there are \(2\cdot3^2=18\) choices. If \(a=2\), then \(b\) must be \(0\), and the middle digit has \(3\) choices.

Step 5: Add the 7-digit count

The number of allowed 7-digit palindromes is \[27+18+3=48.\]

Step 6: Finish

The total number is \[52+48=100.\]

Step 7: Conclude

The answer is \(\boxed{100}\).

Key Idea

Count base-3 palindromes by length and compare the 7-digit ones with \(2007_{10}=2202100_3\).
