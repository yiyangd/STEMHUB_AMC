2004 AMC 12A Problem 22

2004AMC 12AProblem 22几何空间几何空间几何体积

Problem Statement

Three mutually tangent spheres of radius \(1\) rest on a horizontal plane. A sphere of radius \(2\) rests on them. What is the distance from the plane to the top of the larger sphere?

Choices

  1. A\(3+\frac{\sqrt{30}}2\)
  2. B\(3+\frac{\sqrt{69}}3\)
  3. C\(3+\frac{\sqrt{123}}4\)
  4. D\(\frac{52}{9}\)
  5. E\(3+2\sqrt2\)

Answer

B. 3+\frac{\sqrt{69}}3

Solution

Step 1: Locate the small sphere centers

The three unit spheres rest on the plane, so their centers are \(1\) unit above the plane. Since they are mutually tangent, their centers form an equilateral triangle of side \(2\).

Step 2: Place the large sphere center

The radius-\(2\) sphere is tangent to each unit sphere, so its center is \(1+2=3\) units from each small-sphere center. By symmetry, it lies above the centroid of the equilateral triangle.

Step 3: Find the horizontal distance

In an equilateral triangle of side \(2\), the distance from the centroid to a vertex is \(\frac{2}{\sqrt3}\).

Step 4: Use a right triangle

Let \(h\) be the vertical distance from the small-sphere centers to the large-sphere center. Then \[h^2+\left(\frac{2}{\sqrt3}\right)^2=3^2,\] so \[h^2=9-\frac43=\frac{23}{3}\] and \(h=\frac{\sqrt{69}}3\).

Step 5: Add heights

The top of the large sphere is \(1+h+2\) units above the plane.

Step 6: Conclude

The distance is \(3+\frac{\sqrt{69}}3\), so the answer is $\boxed{3+\frac{\sqrt{69}}3}$.

Key Idea

Use the tetrahedral distance from the centroid of three tangent unit spheres.
