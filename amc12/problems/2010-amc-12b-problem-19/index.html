2010 AMC 12B Problem 19

2010AMC 12BProblem 19代数三角恒等式三角函数恒等式

Problem Statement

A high school basketball game between the Raiders and Wildcats was tied at the end of the first quarter. The number of points scored by the Raiders in each of the four quarters formed an increasing geometric sequence, and the number of points scored by the Wildcats in each of the four quarters formed an increasing arithmetic sequence. At the end of the fourth quarter, the Raiders had won by one point. Neither team scored more than $100$ points. What was the total number of points scored by the two teams in the first half?

Choices

  1. A30
  2. B31
  3. C32
  4. D33
  5. E34

Answer

E. 34

Solution

Step 1: Set up the sequences

Let the Raiders' quarter scores be an increasing geometric sequence and the Wildcats' scores be an increasing arithmetic sequence. Since the first quarter was tied, both teams scored the same first-quarter value.

Step 2: Use the Wildcats' form

If the first-quarter score is $a$ and the Wildcats' common difference is $d$, then their scores are \[a,\ a+d,\ a+2d,\ a+3d,\] with total \[4a+6d.\]

Step 3: Search the Raiders' integer geometric scores efficiently

The Raiders' scores are increasing positive integers in a geometric sequence and have total less than $100$. A bounded check of such integer geometric sequences, together with the condition that the Raiders' total is one more than the Wildcats' total, leaves the unique possibility \[5,10,20,40.\]

Step 4: Find the Wildcats' sequence

The Raiders' total is $75$, so the Wildcats' total is $74$. Their first quarter score is also $5$, so \[4\cdot5+6d=74.\] Thus $6d=54$ and $d=9$, giving Wildcats scores \[5,14,23,32.\]

Step 5: Compute the first-half total

In the first half, the Raiders scored \[5+10=15,\] and the Wildcats scored \[5+14=19.\] The combined first-half total is \[15+19=34.\]

Step 6: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{34}$.

Key Idea

Parametrize the integer geometric sequence and test the arithmetic-sequence condition.
