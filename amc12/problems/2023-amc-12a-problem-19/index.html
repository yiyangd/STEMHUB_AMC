2023 AMC 12A Problem 19

2023AMC 12AProblem 19代数对数与指数对数指数

Problem Statement

What is the product of all the solutions to \[\log_{7x}2023\cdot\log_{289x}2023=\log_{2023x}2023?\]

Choices

  1. A$(\log_{2023}7\cdot\log_{2023}289)^2$
  2. B$\log_{2023}7\cdot\log_{2023}289$
  3. C1
  4. D$\log_7 2023\cdot\log_{289}2023$
  5. E$(\log_7 2023\cdot\log_{289}2023)^2$

Answer

C. 1

Solution

Step 1: Convert to natural logs

Let \[a=\ln2023,\quad b=\ln7,\quad c=\ln289,\quad t=\ln x.\] The equation becomes \[\frac{a}{b+t}\cdot\frac{a}{c+t}=\frac{a}{a+t}.\]

Step 2: Clear denominators

After dividing by $a$ and clearing denominators, \[a(a+t)=(b+t)(c+t).\] This is a quadratic equation in $t$.

Step 3: Use the sum of roots

Expanding gives \[t^2+(b+c-a)t+(bc-a^2)=0.\] Therefore the sum of all $t$-solutions is \[a-b-c.\]

Step 4: Convert back to x

The product of all $x$-solutions is \[\exp(a-b-c)=\frac{2023}{7\cdot289}.\]

Step 5: Use 2023 equals 7 times 289

Since \[2023=7\cdot289,\] the product is $1$.

Step 6: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{1}$.

Key Idea

Convert logarithms to a quadratic in ln x and use the sum of roots.
