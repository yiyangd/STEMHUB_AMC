2016 AMC 12B Problem 13

2016AMC 12BProblem 13几何三角形三角形角度

Problem Statement

Alice and Bob live $10$ miles apart. One day Alice looks due north from her house and sees an airplane. At the same time Bob looks due west from his house and sees the same airplane. The angle of elevation of the airplane is $30^\circ$ from Alice's position and $60^\circ$ from Bob's position. Which of the following is closest to the airplane's altitude, in miles?

Choices

  1. A3.5
  2. B4
  3. C4.5
  4. D5
  5. E5.5

Answer

E. 5.5

Solution

Step 1: Set up the ground projection

Let the airplane's ground projection be directly north of Alice and directly west of Bob. If the airplane's altitude is $h$, then Alice's horizontal distance to the projection is \(h/\tan30^\circ=\sqrt3h\).

Step 2: Use Bob's angle

Bob's angle of elevation is \(60^\circ\), so Bob's horizontal distance to the projection is \[h/\tan60^\circ=\frac{h}{\sqrt3}.\]

Step 3: Use the distance between Alice and Bob

These two horizontal distances are perpendicular because Alice looks north and Bob looks west. Since Alice and Bob are $10$ miles apart, \[(\sqrt3h)^2+\left(\frac{h}{\sqrt3}\right)^2=10^2.\]

Step 4: Solve for h

This gives \[3h^2+\frac{h^2}{3}=100,\] so \[\frac{10}{3}h^2=100,\quad h^2=30.\] Hence \[h=\sqrt{30}\approx5.48.\]

Step 5: Choose the closest answer

The closest listed value is $5.5$.

Step 6: Conclude

The airplane's altitude is closest to $\boxed{5.5}$ miles.

Key Idea

Set up the horizontal right triangle formed by the airplane's ground projection.
