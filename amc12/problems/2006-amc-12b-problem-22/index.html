2006 AMC 12B Problem 22

2006AMC 12BProblem 22数论质因数与整除质因数整除

Problem Statement

Suppose \(a,b,\) and \(c\) are positive integers with \(a+b+c=2006\), and \(a!b!c!=m\cdot10^n\), where \(m\) and \(n\) are integers and \(m\) is not divisible by \(10\). What is the smallest possible value of \(n\)?

Choices

  1. A489
  2. B492
  3. C495
  4. D498
  5. E501

Answer

B. 492

Solution

Step 1: Connect trailing zeros to factors of 5

The exponent \(n\) of \(10\) in \(a!b!c!\) is the number of factors of \(5\), because factorials contain more factors of \(2\) than factors of \(5\).

Step 2: Use Legendre's digit-sum formula

For base \(5\), \[v_5(t!)=\frac{t-s_5(t)}{4},\] where \(s_5(t)\) is the sum of the base-\(5\) digits of \(t\). Thus \[n=\frac{2006-\big(s_5(a)+s_5(b)+s_5(c)\big)}4.\]

Step 3: Turn minimization into maximization

To minimize \(n\), we maximize \(s_5(a)+s_5(b)+s_5(c)\) while \(a+b+c=2006\). Since \(2006=(31011)_5\), the digit sum of the total is \(6\). Carries in base \(5\) can raise the sum of the three digit sums by \(4\) per carry.

Step 4: Maximize the digit sum

A carry check shows that the maximum possible total digit sum is \(38\). This is achieved, for example, by \[133=(1013)_5,\quad624=(4444)_5,\quad1249=(14444)_5,\] whose sum is \(2006\) and whose digit sums add to \(5+16+17=38\).

Step 5: Compute n

Therefore \[n_{\min}=\frac{2006-38}{4}=492.\]

Step 6: Conclude

The smallest possible value is \(\boxed{492}\).

Key Idea

Minimize the number of factors of 5 in \(a!b!c!\) using base-5 digit sums.
