2007 AMC 12B Problem 25

2007AMC 12BProblem 25几何面积与相似面积比例

Problem Statement

Points \(A\), \(B\), \(C\), \(D\), and \(E\) are located in three-dimensional space with \(AB=BC=CD=DE=EA=2\) and \(\angle ABC=\angle CDE=\angle DEA=90^\circ\). The plane of \(\triangle ABC\) is parallel to \(\overline{DE}\). What is the area of \(\triangle BDE\)?

Choices

  1. A\(2\sqrt2\)
  2. B\(3\sqrt3\)
  3. C2
  4. D\(5\sqrt5\)
  5. E\(6\sqrt6\)

Answer

C. 2

Solution

Step 1: Set up coordinates

Place \(A=(0,0,0)\), \(B=(2,0,0)\), and \(C=(2,2,0)\). This satisfies \(AB=BC=2\) and \(\angle ABC=90^\circ\), with \(\triangle ABC\) in the plane \(z=0\).

Step 2: Use the parallel condition

Since \(\overline{DE}\) is parallel to the plane of \(\triangle ABC\), we may choose coordinates so that \(\overline{DE}\) is horizontal in the \(xy\)-direction. A convenient realization is \[D=(1,2,\sqrt3),\qquad E=(1,0,\sqrt3).\]

Step 3: Verify the required lengths

We have \(DE=2\). Also \[CD^2=(1-2)^2+(2-2)^2+(\sqrt3)^2=4,\] and \[EA^2=1^2+0^2+(\sqrt3)^2=4,\] so \(CD=EA=2\).

Step 4: Verify the right angles

At \(D\), vector \(\overrightarrow{DC}=(1,0,-\sqrt3)\) is perpendicular to \(\overrightarrow{DE}=(0,-2,0)\). At \(E\), vector \(\overrightarrow{ED}=(0,2,0)\) is perpendicular to \(\overrightarrow{EA}=(-1,0,-\sqrt3)\). Thus the angle conditions are satisfied.

Step 5: Compute the area of triangle BDE

Use vectors from \(B\): \[\overrightarrow{BD}=(-1,2,\sqrt3),\qquad \overrightarrow{BE}=(-1,0,\sqrt3).\] The area is half the magnitude of their cross product.

Step 6: Evaluate the cross product

\[\overrightarrow{BD}\times\overrightarrow{BE}=(2\sqrt3,0,2),\] whose magnitude is \[\sqrt{12+4}=4.\] Therefore the area is \(\frac12\cdot4=2\).

Step 7: Conclude

The answer is \(\boxed{2}\).

Key Idea

Choose coordinates that satisfy the right-angle and parallel-plane conditions.
