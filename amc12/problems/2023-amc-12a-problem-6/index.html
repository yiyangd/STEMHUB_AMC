2023 AMC 12A Problem 6

2023AMC 12AProblem 6代数对数与指数对数指数

Problem Statement

Points $A$ and $B$ lie on the graph of $y=\log_2 x$. The midpoint of $\overline{AB}$ is $(6,2)$. What is the positive difference between the $x$-coordinates of $A$ and $B$?

Choices

  1. A$2\sqrt{11}$
  2. B$4\sqrt3$
  3. C8
  4. D$4\sqrt5$
  5. E9

Answer

D. $4\sqrt5$

Solution

Step 1: Use the midpoint x-coordinate

Let the two $x$-coordinates be $a$ and $b$. Since the midpoint has $x$-coordinate $6$, \[\frac{a+b}{2}=6,\] so \[a+b=12.\]

Step 2: Use the midpoint y-coordinate

The corresponding $y$-coordinates are $\log_2 a$ and $\log_2 b$. Their average is $2$, so \[\frac{\log_2 a+\log_2 b}{2}=2.\]

Step 3: Turn logs into a product

This gives \[\log_2(ab)=4,\] so \[ab=16.\]

Step 4: Find the difference

Using \[(a-b)^2=(a+b)^2-4ab,\] we get \[(a-b)^2=12^2-4\cdot16=144-64=80.\]

Step 5: Conclude

The positive difference is \[\sqrt{80}=4\sqrt5.\] The answer is $\boxed{4\sqrt5}$.

Key Idea

Use midpoint coordinates to get the sum and product of the two x-coordinates.
