Problem Statement

Let \[N=3^4\cdot3^4\cdot6^3\cdot27^0.\] What is the ratio of the sum of the odd divisors of $N$ to the sum of the even divisors of $N$?

Choices

  1. A$1:16$
  2. B$1:15$
  3. C$1:14$
  4. D$1:8$
  5. E$1:3$

Answer

C. 1:14

Solution

Step 1: Focus on powers of 2

Odd and even divisors differ only by powers of $2$. The odd part of $N$ contributes the same divisor-sum factor to both sums.

Step 2: Find the power of 2 in N

The factors $3^4$, $3^4$, and $27^0$ are odd. The only factor contributing powers of $2$ is \[6^3=2^3\cdot3^3.\] Thus the greatest power of $2$ dividing $N$ is $2^3$.

Step 3: Compare divisor sums

If the sum of the odd divisors is $S$, then the even divisors are obtained from odd divisors by multiplying by $2$, $4$, or $8$. So the sum of the even divisors is \[(2+4+8)S=14S.\]

Step 4: Conclude

The requested ratio is \[S:14S=1:14.\] The answer is $\boxed{1:14}$.

Key Idea

Only the power of 2 matters when comparing odd and even divisor sums.
