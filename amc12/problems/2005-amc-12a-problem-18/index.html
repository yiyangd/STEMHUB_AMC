2005 AMC 12A Problem 18

2005AMC 12AProblem 18数论质因数与整除质因数整除

Problem Statement

Call a number \"prime-looking\" if it is composite but not divisible by \(2,3,\) or \(5\). The three smallest prime-looking numbers are \(49,77,\) and \(91\). There are \(168\) prime numbers less than \(1000\). How many prime-looking numbers are there less than \(1000\)?

Choices

  1. A100
  2. B102
  3. C104
  4. D106
  5. E108

Answer

A. 100

Solution

Step 1: Count numbers not divisible by 2, 3, or 5

By inclusion-exclusion, the positive integers less than \(1000\) not divisible by \(2,3,\) or \(5\) number \[999-499-333-199+166+99+66-33=266.\]

Step 2: Remove primes

Among the \(168\) primes less than \(1000\), the primes \(2,3,\) and \(5\) were not included in the \(266\). So \(165\) included numbers are prime.

Step 3: Remove 1

The number \(1\) is also included in the \(266\), but it is not composite.

Step 4: Compute composite count

The prime-looking numbers are therefore \[266-165-1=100.\]

Step 5: Conclude

The answer is \(\boxed{100}\).

Key Idea

Count numbers not divisible by \(2,3,5\), then remove primes and \(1\).
