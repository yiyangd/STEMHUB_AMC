2012 AMC 12A Problem 10

2012AMC 12AProblem 10概率与统计平均数与数据集平均数数据

Problem Statement

A triangle has area $30$, one side of length $10$, and the median to that side of length $9$. Let $\theta$ be the acute angle formed by that side and the median. What is $\sin\theta$?

Choices

  1. A$\frac3{10}$
  2. B$\frac13$
  3. C$\frac9{20}$
  4. D$\frac23$
  5. E$\frac9{10}$

Answer

D. \frac23

Solution

Step 1: Use the area to find the altitude

The triangle has area $30$ and a side of length $10$. If the altitude to that side is $h$, then \[\frac12\cdot10\cdot h=30,\] so $h=6$.

Step 2: Relate the median to the altitude

The median to the side has length $9$. The acute angle $\theta$ between the side and the median forms a right triangle whose opposite height is $6$ and whose hypotenuse is the median $9$.

Step 3: Compute sine

Therefore \[\sin\theta=\frac{\text{opposite}}{\text{hypotenuse}}=\frac69=\frac23.\]

Step 4: Conclude

The answer is \[\boxed{\frac23}.\]

Key Idea

Use area to find the altitude, then use the median as the hypotenuse in a right triangle.
