2004 AMC 12A Problem 24

2004AMC 12AProblem 24几何圆与曲线圆曲线

Problem Statement

A plane contains points \(A\) and \(B\) with \(AB=1\). Let \(S\) be the union of all disks of radius \(1\) in the plane that cover \(\overline{AB}\). What is the area of \(S\)?

Choices

  1. A\(2\pi+\sqrt3\)
  2. B\(\frac{8\pi}{3}\)
  3. C\(3\pi-\frac{\sqrt3}{2}\)
  4. D\(\frac{10\pi}{3}-\sqrt3\)
  5. E\(4\pi-2\sqrt3\)

Answer

C. 3\pi-\frac{\sqrt3}{2}

Solution

Step 1: Think about disk centers

A radius-\(1\) disk covers segment \(\overline{AB}\) exactly when its center is within distance \(1\) of both \(A\) and \(B\). Thus the possible centers form the lens-shaped intersection of two unit disks centered at \(A\) and \(B\).

Step 2: Use a geometric enlargement

The set \(S\) is obtained by taking every possible center in that lens and drawing a unit disk around it. In other words, \(S\) is the lens enlarged outward by radius \(1\).

Step 3: Find the lens area

Since \(AB=1\), the two unit circles meet with central angle \(120^\circ\). The lens area is \[2\cdot\frac{120^\circ}{360^\circ}\pi-2\cdot\frac{\sqrt3}{4}=\frac{2\pi}{3}-\frac{\sqrt3}{2}.\]

Step 4: Find the lens perimeter

The boundary of the lens consists of two \(120^\circ\) arcs of radius \(1\), so its perimeter is \[2\cdot\frac{2\pi}{3}=\frac{4\pi}{3}.\]

Step 5: Add the radius-one enlargement

Enlarging a convex region by radius \(1\) adds area equal to its perimeter times \(1\), plus the area \(\pi\) of a unit disk. Thus \[|S|=\left(\frac{2\pi}{3}-\frac{\sqrt3}{2}\right)+\frac{4\pi}{3}+\pi=3\pi-\frac{\sqrt3}{2}.\]

Step 6: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{3\pi-\frac{\sqrt3}{2}}$.

Key Idea

View the possible disk centers as a lens and enlarge it by radius \(1\).
