2021 Spring AMC 12B Problem 11

2021 SpringAMC 12BProblem 11几何三角形三角形角度

Problem Statement

Triangle $ABC$ has $AB=13$, $BC=14$, and $AC=15$. Let $P$ be the point on $\overline{AC}$ such that $PC=10$. There are exactly two points $D$ and $E$ on line $BP$ such that quadrilaterals $ABCD$ and $ABCE$ are trapezoids. What is the distance $DE$?

Choices

  1. A$\frac{42}{5}$
  2. B$6\sqrt2$
  3. C$\frac{84}{5}$
  4. D$12\sqrt2$
  5. E$18$

Answer

D. $12\sqrt2$

Solution

Step 1: Choose friendly coordinates

A $13$-$14$-$15$ triangle has area $84$, so one convenient placement is

\[B=(0,0),\qquad C=(14,0),\qquad A=(5,12).\]

This gives $AB=13$, $BC=14$, and $AC=15$.

Step 2: Locate point P

Since $PC=10$ and $AC=15$, point $P$ is $\frac{10}{15}=\frac23$ of the way from $C$ to $A$. Therefore

\[P=C+\frac23(A-C)=(14,0)+\frac23(-9,12)=(8,8).\]

So line $BP$ is simply $y=x$.

Step 3: Find one trapezoid point

For one trapezoid, side $AD$ is parallel to $BC$. Since $BC$ is horizontal, $AD$ is horizontal. Thus $D$ lies on the horizontal line through $A$, namely $y=12$.

Because $D$ is also on $BP$, where $y=x$, we get

\[D=(12,12).\]

Step 4: Find the other trapezoid point

For the other trapezoid, side $CE$ is parallel to $AB$. The slope of $AB$ is

\[\frac{12-0}{5-0}=\frac{12}{5}.\]

The line through $C=(14,0)$ with this slope is

\[y=\frac{12}{5}(x-14).\]

Intersecting with $y=x$ gives $x=24$, so

\[E=(24,24).\]

Step 5: Compute DE

Now

\[DE=\sqrt{(24-12)^2+(24-12)^2}=12\sqrt2.\]

So the answer is $12\sqrt2$.

Key Idea

Place the 13-14-15 triangle in coordinates and intersect line BP with the two parallel lines forced by the trapezoid condition.
