2015 AMC 12A Problem 5

2015AMC 12AProblem 5数论整数构造整数构造

Problem Statement

Amelia needs to estimate the quantity $\frac{a}{b}-c$, where $a,b,$ and $c$ are large positive integers. She rounds each of the integers so that the calculation will be easier to do mentally. In which of these situations will her answer necessarily be greater than the exact value of $\frac{a}{b}-c$?

Choices

  1. AShe rounds all three numbers up.
  2. BShe rounds $a$ and $b$ up, and she rounds $c$ down.
  3. CShe rounds $a$ and $c$ up, and she rounds $b$ down.
  4. DShe rounds $a$ up, and she rounds $b$ and $c$ down.
  5. EShe rounds $c$ up, and she rounds $a$ and $b$ down.

Answer

D. She rounds a up, and she rounds b and c down.

Solution

Step 1: Understand what makes the expression larger

The expression is \[\frac{a}{b}-c.\] To make it larger, we want the fraction $\frac{a}{b}$ larger and the subtracted number $c$ smaller.

Step 2: Increase the fraction

The fraction $\frac{a}{b}$ increases if the numerator $a$ is rounded up and the denominator $b$ is rounded down.

Step 3: Decrease the subtracted part

Because $c$ is subtracted, rounding $c$ down makes \[-c\] larger.

Step 4: Combine the effects

Rounding $a$ up, $b$ down, and $c$ down all push the estimate upward. This guarantees the estimate is greater than the exact value.

Step 5: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{\text{D}}$.

Key Idea

Increase a/b and decrease the subtracted quantity.
