2014 AMC 12B Problem 11

2014AMC 12BProblem 11概率与统计平均数与数据集平均数数据

Problem Statement

A list of $11$ positive integers has a mean of $10$, a median of $9$, and a unique mode of $8$. What is the largest possible value of an integer in the list?

Choices

  1. A24
  2. B30
  3. C31
  4. D33
  5. E35

Answer

E. 35

Solution

Step 1: Use the total sum

The mean is $10$ for $11$ integers, so the total sum is \[11\cdot10=110.\] To make one integer as large as possible, we should make the other ten integers as small as possible.

Step 2: Respect the median

After sorting the list, the sixth number must be $9$. Therefore five numbers are at most $9$, and five numbers are at least $9$.

Step 3: Make 8 the unique mode efficiently

Using three $8$'s is efficient: it makes $8$ the mode while still allowing another value to appear twice. A small beginning is \[1,1,8,8,8,9.\]

Step 4: Minimize the remaining four numbers

The value $9$ may appear once more, and another number may appear twice without tying the three $8$'s. The smallest possible continuation is \[9,10,10,11.\]

Step 5: Compute the forced small sum

The ten smaller entries can have sum \[1+1+8+8+8+9+9+10+10+11=75.\]

Step 6: Find the largest entry

The largest possible entry is \[110-75=35.\] The answer is $\boxed{35}$.

Key Idea

Minimize the other ten entries while keeping 8 as the unique mode and 9 as the median.
