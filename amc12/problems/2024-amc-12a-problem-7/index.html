2024 AMC 12A Problem 7

2024AMC 12AProblem 7几何向量几何向量和中点等分点直角三角形

Problem Statement

In right isosceles triangle $ABC$, $\angle ABC=90^\circ$ and $BA=BC=\sqrt2$. Points $P_1,\ldots,P_{2024}$ divide hypotenuse $\overline{AC}$ into equal parts. What is the length of \[\overrightarrow{BP_1}+\overrightarrow{BP_2}+\cdots+\overrightarrow{BP_{2024}}?\]

Choices

  1. A1011
  2. B1012
  3. C2023
  4. D2024
  5. E2025

Answer

D. 2024

Solution

Step 1: Use average position

The points $P_1,\ldots,P_{2024}$ are equally spaced along $\overline{AC}$, so their average position is the midpoint of $\overline{AC}$.

Step 2: Convert vector sums to average vectors

Therefore \[\overrightarrow{BP_1}+\cdots+\overrightarrow{BP_{2024}}=2024\cdot\overrightarrow{BM},\] where $M$ is the midpoint of $\overline{AC}$.

Step 3: Find BM

In a right triangle, the midpoint of the hypotenuse is equidistant from all three vertices. Since $BA=BC=\sqrt2$, the hypotenuse has length $2$, so \[BM=1.\]

Step 4: Compute the length

The vector sum has length \[2024\cdot1=2024.\]

Step 5: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{2024}$.

Key Idea

Use the average location of equally spaced points on the hypotenuse.
