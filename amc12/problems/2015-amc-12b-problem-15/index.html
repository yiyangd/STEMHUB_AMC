2015 AMC 12B Problem 15

2015AMC 12BProblem 15概率与统计独立性独立事件概率

Problem Statement

At Rachelle's school an A counts $4$ points, a B counts $3$ points, a C counts $2$ points, and a D counts $1$ point. Her GPA on the four classes she is taking is the total sum of points divided by $4$. She is certain that she will get As in both Mathematics and Science, and at least a C in each of English and History. She has a $\frac16$ chance of getting an A in English and a $\frac14$ chance of getting a B. In History, she has a $\frac14$ chance of getting an A and a $\frac13$ chance of getting a B, independently of what she gets in English. What is the probability that Rachelle will get a GPA of at least $3.5$?

Choices

  1. A$\frac{11}{72}$
  2. B$\frac16$
  3. C$\frac{3}{16}$
  4. D$\frac{11}{24}$
  5. E$\frac12$

Answer

D. \frac{11}{24}

Solution

Step 1: Translate the GPA requirement

Math and Science are both As, contributing $8$ points. A GPA of at least $3.5$ over four classes means total points at least \[4\cdot3.5=14.\] So English plus History must contribute at least $6$ points.

Step 2: List successful grade combinations

For English and History, points are A=$4$, B=$3$, C=$2$. A total of at least $6$ occurs for \[(A,A),(A,B),(B,A),(B,B),(A,C),(C,A).\]

Step 3: Use a complement or direct count

Equivalently, failure occurs only when English and History are \[(B,C),(C,B),(C,C).\] Direct calculation of success is quick.

Step 4: Compute by English grade

If English is A, success is automatic: probability $\frac16$. If English is B, History must be A or B: probability \[\frac14\left(\frac14+\frac13\right)=\frac7{48}.\] If English is C, History must be A: probability \[\frac7{12}\cdot\frac14=\frac7{48}.\]

Step 5: Add

The total probability is \[\frac16+\frac7{48}+\frac7{48}=\frac{8+7+7}{48}=\frac{22}{48}=\frac{11}{24}.\]

Step 6: Conclude

The answer is \[\boxed{\frac{11}{24}}.\]

Key Idea

Enumerate English and History grade combinations that give total points at least 14.
