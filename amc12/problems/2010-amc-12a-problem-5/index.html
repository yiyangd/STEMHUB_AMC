2010 AMC 12A Problem 5

2010AMC 12AProblem 5组合数学排列组合排列组合计数

Problem Statement

Halfway through a $100$-shot archery tournament, Chelsea leads by $50$ points. For each shot a bullseye scores $10$ points, with other possible scores being $8,4,2,$ and $0$ points. Chelsea always scores at least $4$ points on each shot. If Chelsea's next $n$ shots are bullseyes, she will be guaranteed victory. What is the minimum value for $n$?

Choices

  1. A38
  2. B40
  3. C42
  4. D44
  5. E46

Answer

C. 42

Solution

Step 1: Focus on the worst case

To guarantee victory, Chelsea must still win even if her opponent scores the maximum possible points on every remaining shot. There are $50$ shots left.

Step 2: Compute the opponent's possible gain

The opponent can score at most \[50\cdot10=500\] points in the second half.

Step 3: Compute Chelsea's guaranteed gain

If Chelsea makes her next $n$ shots as bullseyes, those shots give $10n$ points. On the remaining $50-n$ shots, she is guaranteed at least $4$ each. Her guaranteed second-half score is \[10n+4(50-n)=200+6n.\]

Step 4: Use her current lead

Chelsea already leads by $50$, so she is guaranteed to finish ahead if \[50+(200+6n)>500.\] This simplifies to \[6n>250.\]

Step 5: Find the smallest integer n

The smallest integer satisfying $6n>250$ is $n=42$, since $6\cdot41=246$ but $6\cdot42=252$.

Step 6: Conclude

The minimum value is $\boxed{42}$.

Key Idea

Compare Chelsea's guaranteed minimum with the opponent's possible maximum.
