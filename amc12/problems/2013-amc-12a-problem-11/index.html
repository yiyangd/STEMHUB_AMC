2013 AMC 12A Problem 11

2013AMC 12AProblem 11几何面积与相似面积比例

Problem Statement

Triangle $ABC$ is equilateral with $AB=1$. Points $E$ and $G$ are on $\overline{AC}$ and points $D$ and $F$ are on $\overline{AB}$ such that both $\overline{DE}$ and $\overline{FG}$ are parallel to $\overline{BC}$. Furthermore, triangle $ADE$ and trapezoids $DFGE$ and $FBCG$ all have the same perimeter. What is $DE+FG$?

Choices

  1. A1
  2. B$\frac32$
  3. C$\frac{21}{13}$
  4. D$\frac{13}{8}$
  5. E$\frac53$

Answer

C. \frac{21}{13}

Solution

Step 1: Name the two parallel-slice lengths

Because the triangle is equilateral and $\overline{DE}\parallel\overline{BC}$, the small triangle $ADE$ is also equilateral. Let \[AD=DE=AE=x.\] Let \[AF=FG=AG=y,\] since triangle $AFG$ is also equilateral.

Step 2: Write the first perimeter

The perimeter of $\triangle ADE$ is simply \[3x.\]

Step 3: Write the middle trapezoid perimeter

In trapezoid $DFGE$, the two side pieces on $AB$ and $AC$ have length $y-x$, and the parallel bases have lengths $x$ and $y$. Its perimeter is \[x+2(y-x)+y=3y-x.\]

Step 4: Write the bottom trapezoid perimeter

In trapezoid $FBCG$, the side pieces are $1-y$ and $1-y$, and the parallel bases are $y$ and $1$. Its perimeter is \[y+2(1-y)+1=3-y.\]

Step 5: Solve the equalities

The three perimeters are equal, so \[3x=3y-x=3-y.\] From $3x=3-y$, we get $y=3-3x$. From $3x=3y-x$, we get $4x=3y$. Substituting gives \[4x=9-9x,\] so $x=\frac9{13}$ and $y=\frac{12}{13}$.

Step 6: Find the requested sum

Since $DE=x$ and $FG=y$, \[DE+FG=x+y=\frac9{13}+\frac{12}{13}=\frac{21}{13}.\] The answer is \[\boxed{\frac{21}{13}}.\]

Key Idea

Translate the equal-perimeter geometry into equations for similar equilateral slices.
