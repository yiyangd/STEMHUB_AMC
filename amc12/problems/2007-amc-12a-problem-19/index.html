2007 AMC 12A Problem 19

2007AMC 12AProblem 19几何坐标几何坐标几何

Problem Statement

Triangles \(ABC\) and \(ADE\) have areas \(2007\) and \(7002\), respectively, with \(B=(0,0)\), \(C=(223,0)\), \(D=(680,380)\), and \(E=(689,389)\). What is the sum of all possible \(x\)-coordinates of \(A\)?

Choices

  1. A282
  2. B300
  3. C600
  4. D900
  5. E1200

Answer

E. 1200

Solution

Step 1: Use triangle ABC first

Segment \(BC\) lies on the \(x\)-axis and has length \(223\). Since \([ABC]=2007\), \[\frac12\cdot223\cdot |y_A|=2007.\] Thus \(|y_A|=18\).

Step 2: Use triangle ADE area

Let \(A=(x,y)\). The vector from \(D\) to \(E\) is \((9,9)\). The area of \(\triangle ADE\) is \[\frac12|9(y-380)-9(x-680)|.\]

Step 3: Set the second area

This equals \(7002\), so \[\frac92|y-x+300|=7002.\] Hence \[|y-x+300|=1556.\]

Step 4: Find possible x values

We have \(y=18\) or \(y=-18\). For each \(y\), the equation \(y-x+300=\pm1556\) gives two possible \(x\)-values.

Step 5: Sum efficiently

For a fixed \(y\), the two \(x\)-values sum to \(2(y+300)\). Therefore the total sum is \[2(18+300)+2(-18+300)=636+564=1200.\]

Step 6: Conclude

The sum of all possible \(x\)-coordinates is \(\boxed{1200}\).

Key Idea

Use coordinate area formulas for two triangles.
