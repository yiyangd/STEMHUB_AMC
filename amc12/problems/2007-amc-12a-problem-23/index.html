2007 AMC 12A Problem 23

2007AMC 12AProblem 23几何坐标几何坐标几何

Problem Statement

Square \(ABCD\) has area \(36\), and \(\overline{AB}\) is parallel to the \(x\)-axis. Vertices \(A\), \(B\), and \(C\) are on the graphs of \(y=\log_a x\), \(y=2\log_a x\), and \(y=3\log_a x\), respectively. What is \(a\)?

Choices

  1. A\(\sqrt[6]{3}\)
  2. B\(\sqrt3\)
  3. C\(\sqrt[3]{6}\)
  4. D\(\sqrt6\)
  5. E6

Answer

A. \sqrt[6]{3}

Solution

Step 1: Use the side length of the square

The square has area \(36\), so its side length is \(6\). Since \(\overline{AB}\) is horizontal, points \(A\) and \(B\) have the same \(y\)-coordinate, and their \(x\)-coordinates differ by \(6\).

Step 2: Name the x-coordinates

Let \(A=(p,\log_a p)\) and \(B=(q,2\log_a q)\). Because \(AB\) is horizontal, \[\log_a p=2\log_a q=\log_a(q^2).\] Since the logarithm base is fixed, this gives \(p=q^2\).

Step 3: Use the horizontal distance

The length \(AB\) is \(6\), so \(|p-q|=6\). Substituting \(p=q^2\), we get \[|q^2-q|=6.\] Since the coordinates are positive and \(q^2-q=6\) gives the useful positive solution, \(q=3\).

Step 4: Bring in point C

Point \(C\) is directly above or below \(B\), and the square side length is \(6\). The \(y\)-coordinate of \(B\) is \(2\log_a 3\), while the \(y\)-coordinate of \(C\) is \(3\log_a 3\). Their vertical distance is therefore \[\log_a 3.\]

Step 5: Use the vertical side length

The vertical side of the square also has length \(6\), so \[\log_a 3=6.\] This means \(a^6=3\).

Step 6: Solve for a

Taking the sixth root gives \[a=\sqrt[6]{3}.\]

Step 7: Conclude

The answer is \(\boxed{\sqrt[6]{3}}\).

Key Idea

Use the horizontal side of the square and logarithm rules to relate \(x\)-coordinates.
