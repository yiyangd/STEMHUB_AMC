2013 AMC 12A Problem 14

2013AMC 12AProblem 14代数对数与指数对数指数

Problem Statement

The sequence \[\log_{12}162,\ \log_{12}x,\ \log_{12}y,\ \log_{12}z,\ \log_{12}1250\] is an arithmetic progression. What is $x$?

Choices

  1. A$125\sqrt3$
  2. B270
  3. C$162\sqrt5$
  4. D434
  5. E$225\sqrt6$

Answer

B. 270

Solution

Step 1: Notice what logarithms do

An arithmetic progression of logarithms means the original arguments form a geometric progression. So \[162,\ x,\ y,\ z,\ 1250\] is geometric.

Step 2: Find the fourth-root ratio

There are four equal multiplicative steps from $162$ to $1250$, so the common ratio $r$ satisfies \[r^4=\frac{1250}{162}=\frac{625}{81}.\]

Step 3: Compute the ratio

Since \[\frac{625}{81}=\left(\frac53\right)^4,\] we have \[r=\frac53.\]

Step 4: Find x

The second term is \[x=162\cdot\frac53=270.\]

Step 5: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{270}$.

Key Idea

Convert an arithmetic progression of logarithms into a geometric progression of arguments.
