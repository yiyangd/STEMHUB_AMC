2002 AMC 12B Problem 25

2002AMC 12BProblem 25几何坐标几何坐标几何

Problem Statement

Let \(f(x)=x^2+6x+1\), and let \(R\) denote the set of points \((x,y)\) in the coordinate plane such that \[f(x)+f(y)\le0\quad\text{and}\quad f(x)-f(y)\le0.\] The area of \(R\) is closest to which integer?

Choices

  1. A21
  2. B22
  3. C23
  4. D24
  5. E25

Answer

E. 25

Solution

Step 1: Complete the square

We have \[f(x)=x^2+6x+1=(x+3)^2-8.\] Let \(u=x+3\) and \(v=y+3\).

Step 2: Rewrite the first inequality

The condition \(f(x)+f(y)\le0\) becomes \[(u^2-8)+(v^2-8)\le0,\] so \[u^2+v^2\le16.\] This is a disk of radius \(4\).

Step 3: Rewrite the second inequality

The condition \(f(x)-f(y)\le0\) becomes \[u^2-v^2\le0,\] or \[|u|\le |v|.\]

Step 4: Find the area fraction

The lines \(v=u\) and \(v=-u\) divide the disk into four equal \(45^\circ\)-based sectors. The condition \(|u|\le|v|\) selects two opposite sectors, exactly half the disk.

Step 5: Compute the area

The area is \[\frac12\pi(4^2)=8\pi\approx25.13.\] The closest listed integer is \(25\).

Step 6: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{25}$.

Key Idea

Translate the quadratic and interpret the inequalities geometrically.
