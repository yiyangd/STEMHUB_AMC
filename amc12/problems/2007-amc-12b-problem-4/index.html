2007 AMC 12B Problem 4

2007AMC 12BProblem 4代数三角恒等式三角函数恒等式

Problem Statement

At Frank's Fruit Market, \(3\) bananas cost as much as \(2\) apples, and \(6\) apples cost as much as \(4\) oranges. How many oranges cost as much as \(18\) bananas?

Choices

  1. A6
  2. B8
  3. C9
  4. D12
  5. E18

Answer

B. 8

Solution

Step 1: Convert bananas to apples

The statement says \(3\) bananas cost as much as \(2\) apples. Multiplying by \(6\), \(18\) bananas cost as much as \(12\) apples.

Step 2: Convert apples to oranges

The statement also says \(6\) apples cost as much as \(4\) oranges. Therefore \(12\) apples cost twice as much, or as much as \(8\) oranges.

Step 3: Link the two conversions

Since \(18\) bananas cost as much as \(12\) apples, and \(12\) apples cost as much as \(8\) oranges, the same cost equals \(8\) oranges.

Step 4: Conclude

The answer is \(\boxed{8}\).

Key Idea

Convert bananas to apples, then apples to oranges.
