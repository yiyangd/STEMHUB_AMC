2002 AMC 12B Problem 24

2002AMC 12BProblem 24几何面积与相似面积比例

Problem Statement

A convex quadrilateral \(ABCD\) with area \(2002\) contains a point \(P\) in its interior such that \(PA=24\), \(PB=32\), \(PC=28\), and \(PD=45\). Find the perimeter of \(ABCD\).

Choices

  1. A$4\sqrt{2002}$
  2. B$2\sqrt{8465}$
  3. C$2(48+\sqrt{2002})$
  4. D$2\sqrt{8633}$
  5. E$4(36+\sqrt{113})$

Answer

E. 4(36+\sqrt{113})

Solution

Step 1: Break the quadrilateral into four triangles

The area of \(ABCD\) is the sum of the areas of \(\triangle APB\), \(\triangle BPC\), \(\triangle CPD\), and \(\triangle DPA\).

Step 2: Use the maximum possible area

For two fixed sides \(r\) and \(s\), the triangle area is at most \(\frac12rs\), with equality when the included angle is \(90^\circ\).

Step 3: Compute the maximum total

The maximum possible total area is \[\frac12(24\cdot32+32\cdot28+28\cdot45+45\cdot24)=\frac12(4004)=2002.\] This equals the given area, so all four included angles at \(P\) are right angles.

Step 4: Find the side lengths

Thus \[AB=\sqrt{24^2+32^2}=40,\quad BC=\sqrt{32^2+28^2}=4\sqrt{113},\] \[CD=\sqrt{28^2+45^2}=53,\quad DA=\sqrt{45^2+24^2}=51.\]

Step 5: Add the perimeter

The perimeter is \[40+4\sqrt{113}+53+51=144+4\sqrt{113}=4(36+\sqrt{113}).\]

Step 6: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{4(36+\sqrt{113})}$.

Key Idea

Use the maximum area of triangles with two fixed sides.
