Problem Statement

The sum of three numbers is $96$. The first number is $6$ times the third number, and the third number is $40$ less than the second number. What is the absolute value of the difference between the first and second numbers?

Choices

  1. A1
  2. B2
  3. C3
  4. D4
  5. E5

Answer

E. 5

Solution

Step 1: Name the easiest variable

The statement compares the other two numbers to the third number, so let the third number be $x$.

Step 2: Translate the relationships

The first number is $6x$. Since the third number is $40$ less than the second, the second number is $x+40$.

Step 3: Use the sum

The three numbers add to $96$, so \[6x+x+(x+40)=96.\] Thus \[8x+40=96,\] and $x=7$.

Step 4: Find the two numbers being compared

The first number is $6x=42$, and the second number is $x+40=47$.

Step 5: Conclude

The absolute value of their difference is \[|42-47|=5.\] The answer is $\boxed{5}$.

Key Idea

Translate the verbal relationships into a small linear equation.
