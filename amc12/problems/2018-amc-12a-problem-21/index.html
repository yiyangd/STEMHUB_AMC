2018 AMC 12A Problem 21

2018AMC 12AProblem 21代数多项式与根多项式根

Problem Statement

Which of the following polynomials has the greatest real root?

Choices

  1. A$x^{19}+2018x^{11}+1$
  2. B$x^{17}+2018x^{11}+1$
  3. C$x^{19}+2018x^{13}+1$
  4. D$x^{17}+2018x^{13}+1$
  5. E$2019x+2018$

Answer

B. x^{17}+2018x^{11}+1

Solution

Step 1: Notice roots must be negative

For the first four polynomials, every term is positive when \(x>0\), so there are no positive real roots. Since the degrees are odd and the leading terms dominate negatively as \(x\to-\infty\), each has one negative real root.

Step 2: Substitute x equals negative t

Let \(x=-t\) with \(t>0\). For choice B, for example, the root condition becomes \[t^{17}+2018t^{11}=1,\] or \[t^{11}(t^6+2018)=1.\] The greatest real root corresponds to the smallest positive \(t\).

Step 3: Compare A and B

For \(0<t<1\), we have \(t^6>t^8\). Therefore \[t^{11}(2018+t^6)>t^{11}(2018+t^8),\] so choice B reaches \(1\) at a smaller \(t\) than choice A.

Step 4: Compare B with C and D

Choices C and D have a factor \(t^{13}\) instead of \(t^{11}\). For \(0<t<1\), this makes the expression smaller. More directly, \[t^{11}(2018+t^6)>t^{13}(2018+t^4),\] so B also reaches \(1\) at a smaller \(t\) than D, and hence than C.

Step 5: Compare with the linear option

Choice E has root \(-\frac{2018}{2019}\), which is close to \(-1\). The root from choice B has \(t^{11}\approx\frac1{2018}\), so \(t\) is much less than $1$; its root is closer to \(0\).

Step 6: Conclude

The polynomial with the greatest real root is \(\boxed{x^{17}+2018x^{11}+1}\).

Key Idea

Compare the negative roots by substituting x equals negative t.
