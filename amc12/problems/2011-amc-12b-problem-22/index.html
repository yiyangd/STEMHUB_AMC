2011 AMC 12B Problem 22

2011AMC 12BProblem 22几何圆与曲线圆曲线

Problem Statement

Let $T_1$ be a triangle with sides $2011,2012,$ and $2013$. For $n\ge1$, if $T_n=\triangle ABC$ and $D,E,$ and $F$ are the points of tangency of the incircle of $\triangle ABC$ to the sides $\overline{AB},\overline{BC},$ and $\overline{AC}$, respectively, then $T_{n+1}$ is a triangle with side lengths $AD,BE,$ and $CF$, if it exists. What is the perimeter of the last triangle in the sequence $(T_n)$?

Choices

  1. A$\frac{1509}{8}$
  2. B$\frac{1509}{32}$
  3. C$\frac{1509}{64}$
  4. D$\frac{1509}{128}$
  5. E$\frac{1509}{256}$

Answer

D. \frac{1509}{128}

Solution

Step 1: Find the tangency lengths

In a triangle with side lengths $a,b,c$ and semiperimeter $s$, the tangent lengths from the vertices are \[s-a,\quad s-b,\quad s-c.\] So the next triangle has side lengths obtained by subtracting the old side lengths from the semiperimeter.

Step 2: Apply this to consecutive sides

If the current triangle has side lengths \[t,\ t+1,\ t+2,\] then its semiperimeter is \[\frac{3t+3}{2}.\] The next side lengths are \[\frac{t-1}{2},\quad \frac{t+1}{2},\quad \frac{t+3}{2}.\]

Step 3: Track the smallest side

Thus the smallest side follows the recurrence \[t_{n+1}=\frac{t_n-1}{2},\] starting from $t_1=2011$.

Step 4: Know when the process stops

A triangle with side lengths $t,t+1,t+2$ exists exactly when \[t+(t+1)>t+2,\] or $t>1$. So the last triangle is the last one with $t>1$.

Step 5: Iterate efficiently

Repeatedly applying \[t\mapsto\frac{t-1}{2}\] gives the last valid smallest side \[\frac{375}{128}.\] The corresponding sides are \[\frac{375}{128},\frac{503}{128},\frac{631}{128}.\]

Step 6: Compute the perimeter

The perimeter is \[\frac{375+503+631}{128}=\frac{1509}{128}.\] The answer is \[\boxed{\frac{1509}{128}}.\]

Key Idea

Iterate the incircle tangency-length transformation.
