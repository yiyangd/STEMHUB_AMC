2006 AMC 12B Problem 10

2006AMC 12BProblem 10几何面积与相似面积比例

Problem Statement

In a triangle with integer side lengths, one side is three times as long as a second side, and the length of the third side is \(15\). What is the greatest possible perimeter of the triangle?

Choices

  1. A43
  2. B44
  3. C45
  4. D46
  5. E47

Answer

A. 43

Solution

Step 1: Name the related sides

Let the shorter of the related sides be \(a\), so the longer is \(3a\). The third side is \(15\).

Step 2: Use the triangle inequality

The restrictive inequality is \[a+15>3a,\] because the two shorter sides must exceed the longest side.

Step 3: Solve the bound

This gives \(15>2a\), so \(a<7.5\). Since side lengths are integers, the largest possible \(a\) is \(7\).

Step 4: Check the triangle

With \(a=7\), the sides are \(7,21,15\). They satisfy \(7+15>21\), so this is a valid triangle.

Step 5: Compute the perimeter

The perimeter is \[7+21+15=43.\]

Step 6: Conclude

The greatest possible perimeter is \(\boxed{43}\).

Key Idea

Use triangle inequalities to maximize the integer side ratio.
