2021 Spring AMC 12B Problem 14

2021 SpringAMC 12BProblem 14几何空间几何空间几何体积

Problem Statement

Let $ABCD$ be a rectangle and let $\overline{DM}$ be a segment perpendicular to the plane of $ABCD$. Suppose that $\overline{DM}$ has integer length, and the lengths of $\overline{MA},\overline{MC},$ and $\overline{MB}$ are consecutive odd positive integers, in this order. What is the volume of pyramid $MABCD$?

Choices

  1. A$24\sqrt5$
  2. B$60$
  3. C$28\sqrt5$
  4. D$66$
  5. E$8\sqrt{70}$

Answer

A. $24\sqrt5$

Solution

Step 1: Assign coordinates

Let $D=(0,0,0)$ and $M=(0,0,h)$, where $h=DM$ is an integer. Let

\[A=(a,0,0),\quad C=(0,b,0),\quad B=(a,b,0).\]

Step 2: Translate the three distances

Suppose

\[MA=k,\quad MC=k+2,\quad MB=k+4,\]

where $k$ is an odd positive integer. Then

\[ a^2+h^2=k^2,\quad b^2+h^2=(k+2)^2,\quad a^2+b^2+h^2=(k+4)^2. \]

Step 3: Eliminate a and b

From the first two equations,

\[a^2=k^2-h^2,\qquad b^2=(k+2)^2-h^2.\]

Substitute these into the third equation:

\[k^2+(k+2)^2-h^2=(k+4)^2.\]

Step 4: Find the integer height

Simplifying gives

\[h^2=8k-12.\]

Trying odd positive $k$ with integer $h$ gives $k=7$ and $h=3$. Then

\[a^2=49-9=40,\qquad b^2=81-9=72.\]

Step 5: Compute the volume

The base area of rectangle $ABCD$ is

\[ab=\sqrt{40\cdot72}=24\sqrt5.\]

The pyramid volume is

\[\frac13\cdot h\cdot ab=\frac13\cdot3\cdot24\sqrt5=24\sqrt5.\]

Key Idea

Use 3D coordinates from the perpendicular height and the rectangle side lengths.
