Problem Statement

The players on a basketball team made some three-point shots, some two-point shots, and some one-point free throws. They scored as many points with two-point shots as with three-point shots. Their number of successful free throws was one more than their number of successful two-point shots. The team's total score was $61$ points. How many free throws did they make?

Choices

  1. A13
  2. B14
  3. C15
  4. D16
  5. E17

Answer

A. 13

Solution

Step 1: Let a variable count two-point shots

Let $t$ be the number of successful two-point shots. Then the team scored $2t$ points from two-point shots.

Step 2: Use the equal point condition

They scored as many points with three-point shots as with two-point shots, so three-point shots also contributed $2t$ points.

Step 3: Use the free-throw condition

The number of successful free throws was one more than the number of two-point shots, so they made $t+1$ free throws and scored $t+1$ free-throw points.

Step 4: Set up the total score

The total score is \[2t+2t+(t+1)=61.\] Thus \[5t+1=61,\] so $t=12$.

Step 5: Answer the question

The number of free throws was \[t+1=13.\]

Step 6: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{13}$.

Key Idea

Translate point totals into an equation.
