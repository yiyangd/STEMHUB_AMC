Problem Statement

John is walking east at a speed of \(3\) miles per hour, while Bob is also walking east, but at a speed of \(5\) miles per hour. If Bob is now \(1\) mile west of John, how many minutes will it take for Bob to catch up to John?

Choices

  1. A30
  2. B50
  3. C60
  4. D90
  5. E120

Answer

A. 30

Solution

Step 1: Focus on the gap

Both people walk east, so Bob is catching John only because Bob is faster. The initial gap is \(1\) mile.

Step 2: Find the relative speed

Bob gains on John at \[5-3=2\] miles per hour.

Step 3: Find the catch-up time

Time equals distance divided by relative speed: \[\frac{1}{2}\text{ hour}.\]

Step 4: Convert to minutes

One half hour is \(30\) minutes.

Step 5: Conclude

Bob catches John after \(\boxed{30}\) minutes.

Key Idea

Use relative speed to close a one-mile gap.
