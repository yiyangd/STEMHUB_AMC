2008 AMC 12A Problem 24

2008AMC 12AProblem 24几何三角形三角形角度

Problem Statement

Triangle \(ABC\) has \(\angle C=60^\circ\) and \(BC=4\). Point \(D\) is the midpoint of \(\overline{BC}\). What is the largest possible value of \(\tan\angle BAD\)?

Choices

  1. A\(\frac{\sqrt3}{6}\)
  2. B\(\frac{\sqrt3}{3}\)
  3. C\(\frac{\sqrt3}{2\sqrt2}\)
  4. D\(\frac{\sqrt3}{4\sqrt2-3}\)
  5. E1

Answer

D. \frac{\sqrt3}{4\sqrt2-3}

Solution

Step 1: Set up coordinates

Place \(C=(0,0)\), \(B=(2,2\sqrt3)\), so \(BC=4\) and the ray \(CB\) makes a \(60^\circ\) angle with the positive \(x\)-axis. Let \(A=(x,0)\) with \(x>0\). Since \(D\) is the midpoint of \(BC\), \(D=(1,\sqrt3)\).

Step 2: Express the angle as a difference

From point \(A\), the slopes of \(\overline{AD}\) and \(\overline{AB}\) are \[\frac{\sqrt3}{1-x}\quad\text{and}\quad \frac{2\sqrt3}{2-x}.\] The tangent of \(\angle BAD\) can be computed with the tangent subtraction formula.

Step 3: Compute the tangent expression

Using \(\tan(\alpha-\beta)=\frac{\tan\alpha-\tan\beta}{1+\tan\alpha\tan\beta}\), the expression simplifies to \[\tan\angle BAD=\frac{\sqrt3\,x}{x^2-3x+8}.\]

Step 4: Maximize the one-variable expression

Now maximize \(f(x)=\frac{x}{x^2-3x+8}\) for \(x>0\). Its derivative has numerator \[x^2-3x+8-x(2x-3)=8-x^2,\] so the maximum occurs at \(x=2\sqrt2\).

Step 5: Evaluate at the maximum

Substituting \(x=2\sqrt2\), \[\tan\angle BAD=\frac{2\sqrt6}{16-6\sqrt2}=\frac{\sqrt3}{4\sqrt2-3}.\]

Step 6: Conclude

The largest possible value is \(\boxed{\frac{\sqrt3}{4\sqrt2-3}}\).

Key Idea

Place coordinates and maximize a tangent expression in one variable.
