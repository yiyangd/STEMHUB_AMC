2014AMC 12AProblem 12几何圆与曲线圆曲线

Problem Statement

Two circles intersect at points $A$ and $B$. The minor arcs $AB$ measure $30^\circ$ on one circle and $60^\circ$ on the other circle. What is the ratio of the area of the larger circle to the area of the smaller circle?

Choices

  1. A2
  2. B$1+\sqrt3$
  3. C3
  4. D$2+\sqrt3$
  5. E4

Answer

D. 2+\sqrt3

Solution

Step 1: Use the chord formula

The same chord $AB$ belongs to both circles. If a circle has radius $R$ and central angle $\theta$ for chord $AB$, then \[AB=2R\sin\frac{\theta}{2}.\]

Step 2: Identify the larger circle

The smaller central angle corresponds to the larger radius. So the circle with $30^\circ$ minor arc is the larger circle.

Step 3: Relate the radii

Let the larger radius be $R$ and the smaller radius be $r$. Then \[2R\sin15^\circ=2r\sin30^\circ.\] Thus \[\frac{R}{r}=\frac{\sin30^\circ}{\sin15^\circ}.\]

Step 4: Square for area ratio

The area ratio is \[\left(\frac{R}{r}\right)^2=\left(\frac{\sin30^\circ}{\sin15^\circ}\right)^2.\] Using \[\sin^2 15^\circ=\frac{1-\cos30^\circ}{2}=\frac{2-\sqrt3}{4},\] this becomes \[\frac{1/4}{(2-\sqrt3)/4}=\frac1{2-\sqrt3}=2+\sqrt3.\]

Step 5: Conclude

The answer is \[\boxed{2+\sqrt3}.\]

Key Idea

Use the same chord length in two circles with different central angles.
