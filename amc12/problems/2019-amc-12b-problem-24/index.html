2019 AMC 12B Problem 24

2019AMC 12BProblem 24几何面积与相似面积比例

Problem Statement

Let \[\omega=-\frac12+\frac{\sqrt3}{2}i.\] Let $S$ denote all points in the complex plane of the form \[a+b\omega+c\omega^2,\] where $0\le a\le1$, $0\le b\le1$, and $0\le c\le1$. What is the area of $S$?

Choices

  1. A$\frac{\sqrt3}{4}$
  2. B$\frac{\sqrt3}{2}$
  3. C$\frac{3\sqrt3}{2}$
  4. D$\frac{\pi}{3}$
  5. E$\frac{\pi}{2}$

Answer

C. \frac{3\sqrt3}{2}

Solution

Step 1: Interpret the complex numbers as vectors

The three variable terms are independent segments in the directions $1$, $\omega$, and $\omega^2$. These are unit vectors separated by $120^\circ$ in the complex plane.

Step 2: Recognize the shape

The set $S$ is the Minkowski sum of three unit segments. In the plane, such a sum is a centrally symmetric hexagon, also called a zonotope.

Step 3: Use the area formula for generated segments

For a zonotope generated by vectors $v_1,v_2,v_3$, its area is \[|\det(v_1,v_2)|+|\det(v_1,v_3)|+|\det(v_2,v_3)|.\] Each determinant is the area of a parallelogram from a pair of generating segments.

Step 4: Compute each parallelogram area

Each pair of the three directions makes an angle of $120^\circ$ or $60^\circ$, so the absolute determinant is \[\sin 60^\circ=\frac{\sqrt3}{2}.\]

Step 5: Add the three contributions

There are three pairs of generating directions, so \[\text{Area}(S)=3\cdot\frac{\sqrt3}{2}=\frac{3\sqrt3}{2}.\]

Step 6: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{\frac{3\sqrt3}{2}}$.

Key Idea

View the set as a zonotope generated by three unit complex directions.
