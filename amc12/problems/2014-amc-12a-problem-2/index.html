Problem Statement

At the theater children get in for half price. The price for $5$ adult tickets and $4$ child tickets is $\$24.50$. How much would $8$ adult tickets and $6$ child tickets cost?

Choices

  1. A$\$35$
  2. B$\$38.50$
  3. C$\$40$
  4. D$\$42$
  5. E$\$42.50$

Answer

B. \$38.50

Solution

Step 1: Let the adult price be A

Since children get in for half price, each child ticket costs $\frac{A}{2}$.

Step 2: Use the first purchase

The cost of $5$ adult tickets and $4$ child tickets is \[5A+4\cdot\frac{A}{2}=7A.\] This equals $\$24.50$, so \[A=3.50.\]

Step 3: Compute the new purchase

Eight adult tickets cost \[8(3.50)=28.\] Six child tickets cost \[6(1.75)=10.50.\]

Step 4: Add

The total is \[28+10.50=38.50.\]

Step 5: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{\$38.50}$.

Key Idea

Use the half-price relationship to find the adult ticket price.
