2007 AMC 12B Problem 22

2007AMC 12BProblem 22几何面积与相似面积比例

Problem Statement

Two particles move along the edges of equilateral triangle \(\triangle ABC\) in the direction \(A\to B\to C\to A\), starting simultaneously and moving at the same speed. One starts at \(A\), and the other starts at the midpoint of \(\overline{BC}\). The midpoint of the line segment joining the two particles traces out a path that encloses a region \(R\). What is the ratio of the area of \(R\) to the area of \(\triangle ABC\)?

Choices

  1. A\(\frac1{16}\)
  2. B\(\frac1{12}\)
  3. C\(\frac19\)
  4. D\(\frac16\)
  5. E\(\frac14\)

Answer

A. \frac1{16}

Solution

Step 1: Use a convenient coordinate model

Let the equilateral triangle have side length \(1\), with \(A=(0,0)\), \(B=(1,0)\), and \(C=\left(\frac12,\frac{\sqrt3}{2}\right)\). The area ratio will not depend on the side length.

Step 2: Look only at event times

The midpoint path is made of straight segments between times when one of the particles reaches a vertex. These event times are \(0,\frac12,1,\frac32,2,\frac52,3\), measured in side-length travel units.

Step 3: Find the first three midpoint positions

At the first three distinct event positions, the midpoint is \[\left(\frac38,\frac{\sqrt3}{8}\right),\quad \left(\frac12,\frac{\sqrt3}{4}\right),\quad \left(\frac58,\frac{\sqrt3}{8}\right).\] After that, the same three points repeat, so the enclosed region is this small triangle.

Step 4: Compute the small triangle area

The base of the small triangle is \(\frac14\), and its height is \(\frac{\sqrt3}{8}\). Hence its area is \[\frac12\cdot\frac14\cdot\frac{\sqrt3}{8}=\frac{\sqrt3}{64}.\]

Step 5: Compare with the original triangle

The area of the original side-\(1\) equilateral triangle is \(\frac{\sqrt3}{4}\). Therefore the ratio is \[\frac{\sqrt3/64}{\sqrt3/4}=\frac1{16}.\]

Step 6: Conclude

The answer is \(\boxed{\frac1{16}}\).

Key Idea

Track the midpoint at the event times when a particle reaches a vertex.
