2006 AMC 12B Problem 23

2006AMC 12BProblem 23几何三角形三角形角度

Problem Statement

Isosceles \(\triangle ABC\) has a right angle at \(C\). Point \(P\) is inside \(\triangle ABC\), such that \(PA=11\), \(PB=7\), and \(PC=6\). Legs \(\overline{AC}\) and \(\overline{BC}\) have length \(s=\sqrt{a+b\sqrt2}\), where \(a\) and \(b\) are positive integers. What is \(a+b\)?

Choices

  1. A85
  2. B91
  3. C108
  4. D121
  5. E127

Answer

E. 127

Solution

Step 1: Set up an angle at C

Let \(\angle BCP=\alpha\). Since \(\triangle ABC\) is right isosceles at \(C\), the angle between \(\overline{CP}\) and \(\overline{CA}\) is \(90^\circ-\alpha\).

Step 2: Use triangle PBC

By the Law of Cosines in \(\triangle PBC\), \[PB^2=s^2+6^2-2(s)(6)\cos\alpha.\] Since \(PB=7\), \[49=s^2+36-12s\cos\alpha,\] so \[\cos\alpha=\frac{s^2-13}{12s}.\]

Step 3: Use triangle PAC

In \(\triangle PAC\), \[PA^2=s^2+6^2-2(s)(6)\cos(90^\circ-\alpha).\] Since \(\cos(90^\circ-\alpha)=\sin\alpha\) and \(PA=11\), \[\sin\alpha=\frac{s^2-85}{12s}.\]

Step 4: Use the identity

Now apply \(\sin^2\alpha+\cos^2\alpha=1\): \[\left(\frac{s^2-85}{12s}\right)^2+\left(\frac{s^2-13}{12s}\right)^2=1.\]

Step 5: Solve for s squared

This simplifies to \[s^4-170s^2+3697=0,\] so \[s^2=85\pm42\sqrt2.\] Since \(P\) is inside the triangle, the positive geometry requires \(s^2>85\), so \[s^2=85+42\sqrt2.\]

Step 6: Conclude

Thus \(a=85\), \(b=42\), and \(a+b=\boxed{127}\).

Key Idea

Use the Law of Cosines twice and \(\sin^2lpha+\cos^2lpha=1\).
