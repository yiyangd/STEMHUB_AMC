Problem Statement

A bakery owner turns on his doughnut machine at 8:30 AM. At 11:10 AM the machine has completed one third of the day's job. At what time will the doughnut machine complete the job?

Choices

  1. A1:50 PM
  2. B3:00 PM
  3. C3:30 PM
  4. D4:30 PM
  5. E5:50 PM

Answer

D. 4:30 PM

Solution

Step 1: Find the time for one third of the job

From 8:30 AM to 11:10 AM is \(2\) hours and \(40\) minutes, or \(160\) minutes. That is the time needed to complete one third of the job.

Step 2: Scale to the full job

If one third takes \(160\) minutes, then the whole job takes \[3\cdot160=480\] minutes.

Step 3: Convert to hours

\(480\) minutes is \(8\) hours.

Step 4: Add to the starting time

The machine started at 8:30 AM. Eight hours after 8:30 AM is 4:30 PM.

Step 5: Conclude

The answer is \(\boxed{\text{4:30 PM}}\).

Key Idea

Use the time for one third of the job to find the full job time.
