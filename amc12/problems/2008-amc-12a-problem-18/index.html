2008 AMC 12A Problem 18

2008AMC 12AProblem 18几何坐标几何坐标几何

Problem Statement

Triangle \(ABC\), with sides of length \(5\), \(6\), and \(7\), has one vertex on the positive \(x\)-axis, one on the positive \(y\)-axis, and one on the positive \(z\)-axis. Let \(O\) be the origin. What is the volume of tetrahedron \(OABC\)?

Choices

  1. A\(\sqrt{85}\)
  2. B\(\sqrt{90}\)
  3. C\(\sqrt{95}\)
  4. D10
  5. E\(\sqrt{105}\)

Answer

C. \sqrt{95}

Solution

Step 1: Place the vertices on the axes

Let the intercept lengths be \(x\), \(y\), and \(z\), so the vertices are \((x,0,0)\), \((0,y,0)\), and \((0,0,z)\). The volume of tetrahedron \(OABC\) is \(\frac{xyz}{6}\).

Step 2: Use pairwise distances

The squared side lengths of triangle \(ABC\) are \[x^2+y^2,\quad y^2+z^2,\quad z^2+x^2.\] These are \(25\), \(36\), and \(49\) in some order.

Step 3: Solve for the squared intercepts

Using the three equations, the squared intercepts are \[\frac{25+36-49}{2}=6,\quad \frac{25+49-36}{2}=19,\quad \frac{36+49-25}{2}=30.\]

Step 4: Find xyz

Thus \[x^2y^2z^2=6\cdot19\cdot30=3420,\] so \(xyz=\sqrt{3420}=6\sqrt{95}\).

Step 5: Compute the volume

The tetrahedron volume is \[\frac{xyz}{6}=\frac{6\sqrt{95}}{6}=\sqrt{95}.\]

Step 6: Conclude

The answer is \(\boxed{\sqrt{95}}\).

Key Idea

Use coordinate-axis distances to determine the intercept lengths.
