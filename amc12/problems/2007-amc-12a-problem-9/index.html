2007 AMC 12A Problem 9

2007AMC 12AProblem 9代数三角恒等式三角函数恒等式

Problem Statement

Yan is somewhere between his home and the stadium. To get to the stadium he can walk directly to the stadium, or else he can walk home and then ride his bicycle to the stadium. He rides \(7\) times as fast as he walks, and both choices require the same amount of time. What is the ratio of Yan's distance from his home to his distance from the stadium?

Choices

  1. A\(\frac23\)
  2. B\(\frac34\)
  3. C\(\frac45\)
  4. D\(\frac56\)
  5. E\(\frac67\)

Answer

B. \frac34

Solution

Step 1: Name the distances

Let \(H\) be Yan's distance from home and \(S\) be his distance from the stadium. His walking speed is \(v\), so his biking speed is \(7v\).

Step 2: Write the direct walking time

If Yan walks directly to the stadium, the time is \[\frac{S}{v}.\]

Step 3: Write the home-then-bike time

If he walks home first, that takes \(\frac{H}{v}\). Then he bikes the full home-to-stadium distance \(H+S\), taking \(\frac{H+S}{7v}\).

Step 4: Set times equal

The times are equal, so \[\frac{S}{v}=\frac{H}{v}+\frac{H+S}{7v}.\] Multiplying by \(7v\) gives \[7S=7H+H+S.\]

Step 5: Solve the ratio

Thus \(6S=8H\), so \[\frac{H}{S}=\frac{6}{8}=\frac34.\]

Step 6: Conclude

The ratio is \(\boxed{\frac34}\).

Key Idea

Set equal travel times using walking and biking speeds.
