2020 AMC 12A Problem 24

2020AMC 12AProblem 24几何三角形三角形角度

Problem Statement

Suppose that $\triangle ABC$ is an equilateral triangle of side length $s$, with the property that there is a unique point $P$ inside the triangle such that $AP=1$, $BP=\sqrt3$, and $CP=2$. What is $s$?

Choices

  1. A$1+\sqrt2$
  2. B$\sqrt7$
  3. C$\frac83$
  4. D$\sqrt{5+\sqrt5}$
  5. E$2\sqrt2$

Answer

B. $\sqrt7$

Solution

Step 1: Use the equilateral structure

Because $\triangle ABC$ is equilateral, a $60^\circ$ rotation is natural. The goal is to learn the angle between $\overline{AP}$ and $\overline{CP}$, because then $\triangle APC$ contains the side $AC=s$.

Step 2: Rotate point P around A

Rotate $P$ by $60^\circ$ about $A$ to a point $Q$, in the direction that sends $B$ toward $C$.

Then

\[AQ=AP=1,\qquad PQ=1,\]

because $\triangle APQ$ is equilateral. Also the rotation sends $\overline{BP}$ to a segment from $C$ to $Q$, so

\[CQ=BP=\sqrt3.\]

Step 3: Identify a simple triangle

Now look at $\triangle PCQ$. Its side lengths are

\[CP=2,\qquad CQ=\sqrt3,\qquad PQ=1.\]

These are the side lengths of a $30^\circ$-$60^\circ$-$90^\circ$ triangle. In particular, the angle opposite the side $\sqrt3$ is $60^\circ$, so

\[\angle CPQ=60^\circ.\]

Step 4: Find the angle APC

Since $\triangle APQ$ is equilateral,

\[\angle APQ=60^\circ.\]

The point $Q$ is created on the other side of $\overline{AP}$ from $C$ in the useful configuration, so the angle between $\overline{AP}$ and $\overline{CP}$ is

\[\angle APC=60^\circ+60^\circ=120^\circ.\]

Step 5: Apply the Law of Cosines

Now use $\triangle APC$, where $AP=1$, $CP=2$, and $\angle APC=120^\circ$. The side opposite this angle is $AC=s$.

\[s^2=1^2+2^2-2\cdot1\cdot2\cos120^\circ.\]

Since $\cos120^\circ=-\frac12$,

\[s^2=1+4+2=7.\]

Step 6: Choose the positive side length

A side length is positive, so

\[s=\sqrt7.\]

Therefore the answer is $\sqrt7$.

Key Idea

Rotate one segment by 60 degrees to create a 30-60-90 triangle and reveal the angle at P.
