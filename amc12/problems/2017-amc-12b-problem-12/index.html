2017 AMC 12B Problem 12

2017AMC 12BProblem 12代数多项式与根多项式根

Problem Statement

What is the sum of the roots of \(z^{12}=64\) that have a positive real part?

Choices

  1. A$\sqrt2$
  2. B$4$
  3. C$2+\sqrt{12}$
  4. D$2\sqrt2+\sqrt6$
  5. E$(1+\sqrt3)+(1+\sqrt3)i$

Answer

D. 2\sqrt2+\sqrt6

Solution

Step 1: Find the size and angles of the roots

Since \(64=2^6\), the roots of \(z^{12}=64\) have modulus \[64^{1/12}=2^{1/2}=\sqrt2.\] Their arguments are multiples of \(\frac{2\pi}{12}=\frac{\pi}{6}\).

Step 2: Select roots with positive real part

A root has positive real part when its angle has positive cosine. Among the twelve angles, these are \[0,\ \pm\frac{\pi}{6},\ \pm\frac{\pi}{3}.\]

Step 3: Add symmetrically

The imaginary parts cancel in conjugate pairs. The sum is \[\sqrt2\left(1+2\cos\frac{\pi}{6}+2\cos\frac{\pi}{3}\right).\]

Step 4: Evaluate

Using \(\cos\frac{\pi}{6}=\frac{\sqrt3}{2}\) and \(\cos\frac{\pi}{3}=\frac12\), the sum is \[\sqrt2(1+\sqrt3+1)=2\sqrt2+\sqrt6.\]

Step 5: Conclude

The answer is \[\boxed{2\sqrt2+\sqrt6}.\]

Key Idea

Represent the complex roots on a circle and sum those with positive real part.
