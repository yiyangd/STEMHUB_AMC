2018 AMC 12B Problem 19

2018AMC 12BProblem 19数论进制与数字数字进制

Problem Statement

Mary chose an even \(4\)-digit number \(n\). She wrote down all the divisors of \(n\) in increasing order. At some moment Mary wrote \(323\) as a divisor of \(n\). What is the smallest possible value of the next divisor written to the right of \(323\)?

Choices

  1. A324
  2. B330
  3. C340
  4. D361
  5. E646

Answer

C. 340

Solution

Step 1: Factor 323

We have \[323=17\cdot19.\] Since \(n\) is even and has \(323\) as a divisor, \(n\) must be a multiple of \(2\cdot17\cdot19=646\).

Step 2: Test possible next divisors

The next divisor after \(323\) must be greater than \(323\). The choices \(324\) and \(330\) would force \(n\) to be a multiple of \(\operatorname{lcm}(323,324)\) or \(\operatorname{lcm}(323,330)\), both larger than a four-digit number.

Step 3: Show 340 is possible

For \(340=2^2\cdot5\cdot17\), \[\operatorname{lcm}(323,340)=2^2\cdot5\cdot17\cdot19=6460,\] which is an even four-digit number.

Step 4: Check no smaller listed value works

The smaller listed candidates cannot occur for a four-digit \(n\), while \(340\) can occur. Thus the smallest possible next divisor is \(340\).

Step 5: Conclude

The answer is \(\boxed{340}\).

Key Idea

Use prime factorization and least common multiples with 323.
