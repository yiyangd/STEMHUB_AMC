2002 AMC 12A Problem 7

2002AMC 12AProblem 7几何圆与曲线圆曲线

Problem Statement

If an arc of $45^\circ$ on circle $A$ has the same length as an arc of $30^\circ$ on circle $B$, then what is the ratio of the area of circle $A$ to the area of circle $B$?

Choices

  1. A$\frac49$
  2. B$\frac23$
  3. C$\frac56$
  4. D$\frac32$
  5. E$\frac94$

Answer

A. \frac49

Solution

Step 1: Use the arc length formula

Arc length is proportional to \(r\theta\), where \(\theta\) is the central angle in degrees or radians as long as the same unit is used for both circles.

Step 2: Set the arc lengths equal

The two arc lengths are equal, so \[45r_A=30r_B.\] Therefore \[\frac{r_A}{r_B}=\frac{30}{45}=\frac23.\]

Step 3: Convert radius ratio to area ratio

Circle area is proportional to the square of the radius. Thus \[\frac{[A]}{[B]}=\left(\frac{r_A}{r_B}\right)^2=\left(\frac23\right)^2=\frac49.\]

Step 4: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{\frac49}$.

Key Idea

Use arc length proportional to radius times central angle.
