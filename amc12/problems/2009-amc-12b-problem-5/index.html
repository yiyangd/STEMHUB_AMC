2009AMC 12BProblem 5代数运算与化简代数化简

Problem Statement

Kiana has two older twin brothers. The product of their three ages is \(128\). What is the sum of their three ages?

Choices

  1. A10
  2. B12
  3. C16
  4. D18
  5. E24

Answer

D. 18

Solution

Step 1: Use the twin condition

Let each twin brother's age be \(t\), and let Kiana's age be \(k\). The product is \[kt^2=128=2^7.\]

Step 2: Use that Kiana is younger

Since Kiana has older twin brothers, we need \(k<t\). Testing square factors of \(128\), \(t=8\) gives \(k=2\).

Step 3: Check other possibilities

If \(t=1,2,\) or \(4\), Kiana would not be younger than both brothers, and if \(t\) is larger, \(k\) is not an integer age from this factorization.

Step 4: Add the ages

The sum is \[2+8+8=18.\]

Step 5: Conclude

The answer is \(\boxed{18}\).

Key Idea

Use the twin condition and factor \(128\).
