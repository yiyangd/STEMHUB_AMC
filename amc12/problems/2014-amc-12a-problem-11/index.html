2014 AMC 12A Problem 11

2014AMC 12AProblem 11代数运算与化简代数化简

Problem Statement

David drives from his home to the airport to catch a flight. He drives $35$ miles in the first hour, but realizes that he will be $1$ hour late if he continues at this speed. He increases his speed by $15$ miles per hour for the rest of the way to the airport and arrives $30$ minutes early. How many miles is the airport from his home?

Choices

  1. A140
  2. B175
  3. C210
  4. D245
  5. E280

Answer

C. 210

Solution

Step 1: Separate the first hour

David has already driven $35$ miles in the first hour. Let $d$ be the remaining distance after that hour.

Step 2: Compare the two arrival predictions

If he continues at $35$ mph, he will be $1$ hour late. If he drives at $50$ mph for the remaining distance, he arrives $30$ minutes early. These two predictions differ by $1.5$ hours.

Step 3: Set up the time equation

The remaining travel times are \[\frac{d}{35}\quad\text{and}\quad \frac{d}{50}.\] Their difference is \[\frac{d}{35}-\frac{d}{50}=1.5.\]

Step 4: Solve for the remaining distance

\[\frac{15d}{1750}=\frac32,\] so \[d=175.\]

Step 5: Add the first hour

The total distance is \[35+175=210.\]

Step 6: Conclude

The airport is $\boxed{210}$ miles from his home.

Key Idea

Compare the scheduled remaining time under two different speeds.
