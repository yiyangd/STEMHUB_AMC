2012 AMC 12A Problem 11

2012AMC 12AProblem 11概率与统计独立性独立事件概率

Problem Statement

Alex, Mel, and Chelsea play a game that has $6$ rounds. In each round there is a single winner, and the outcomes of the rounds are independent. For each round the probability that Alex wins is $\frac12$, and Mel is twice as likely to win as Chelsea. What is the probability that Alex wins three rounds, Mel wins two rounds, and Chelsea wins one round?

Choices

  1. A$\frac5{72}$
  2. B$\frac5{36}$
  3. C$\frac16$
  4. D$\frac13$
  5. E$\frac12$

Answer

B. \frac5{36}

Solution

Step 1: Find Mel and Chelsea's probabilities

Alex wins with probability $\frac12$, so Mel and Chelsea together have probability $\frac12$. Mel is twice as likely as Chelsea, so \[P(M)=\frac13,\qquad P(C)=\frac16.\]

Step 2: Count arrangements of winners

We need Alex to win $3$ rounds, Mel $2$ rounds, and Chelsea $1$ round. The number of orders is \[\frac{6!}{3!2!1!}=60.\]

Step 3: Multiply by one order's probability

One specific order has probability \[\left(\frac12\right)^3\left(\frac13\right)^2\left(\frac16\right).\]

Step 4: Compute

The total probability is \[60\cdot\frac1{8}\cdot\frac1{9}\cdot\frac1{6}=\frac{60}{432}=\frac5{36}.\]

Step 5: Conclude

The answer is \[\boxed{\frac5{36}}.\]

Key Idea

Use a multinomial probability with individual win probabilities.
