2024 AMC 12B Problem 25

2024AMC 12BProblem 25概率与统计独立性独立事件随机配置计数二元表

Problem Statement

Pablo will decorate each of $6$ identical white balls with either a striped or dotted pattern, using either red or blue paint. He makes the color and pattern decisions by fair coin flips. Frida randomly selects one ball from the urn and notes its color and pattern. The probability that the events "the selected ball is red" and "the selected ball is striped" are independent is $\frac mn$, in lowest terms. What is $m$?

Choices

  1. A243
  2. B245
  3. C247
  4. D249
  5. E251

Answer

A. 243

Solution

Step 1: Represent the outcome with a 2-by-2 table

After the six balls are decorated, let the counts be

\[\begin{array}{c|cc} & \text{striped} & \text{dotted}\\ \hline \text{red} & a & b\\ \text{blue} & c & d\end{array}\]

with $a+b+c+d=6$.

Step 2: Translate independence into an equation

The events red and striped are independent exactly when

\[\frac a6=\frac{a+b}{6}\cdot\frac{a+c}{6}.\]

Equivalently,

\[6a=(a+b)(a+c),\]

which is the same as

\[ad=bc.\]

Step 3: Count by row and column totals

Let $r=a+b$ be the number of red balls and $s=a+c$ be the number of striped balls. Independence forces

\[a=\frac{rs}{6}.\]

So we only keep pairs $(r,s)$ for which this and the other three table entries are integers.

Step 4: Add the labeled coin-flip outcomes

For each valid table $(a,b,c,d)$, the number of labeled outcomes is

\[\frac{6!}{a!b!c!d!}.\]

Summing over the valid independent tables gives $972$ labeled outcomes. This is a small finite enumeration over $0\le r,s\le6$, not a probability approximation.

Step 5: Reduce the probability

There are $4^6=4096$ total color-pattern outcomes for the six coin-flipped balls. Thus the probability is

\[\frac{972}{4096}=\frac{243}{1024}.\]

The numerator is $243$.

Key Idea

Translate independence into a condition on a 2-by-2 count table and count labeled coin-flip outcomes.
