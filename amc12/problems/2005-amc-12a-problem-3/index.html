2005 AMC 12A Problem 3

2005AMC 12AProblem 3几何面积与相似面积比例

Problem Statement

A rectangle with a diagonal of length \(x\) is twice as long as it is wide. What is the area of the rectangle?

Choices

  1. A\(\frac14x^2\)
  2. B\(\frac25x^2\)
  3. C\(\frac12x^2\)
  4. D\(x^2\)
  5. E\(\frac32x^2\)

Answer

B. \frac25x^2

Solution

Step 1: Name the width

Let the width be \(w\). Then the length is \(2w\).

Step 2: Use the diagonal

The diagonal is the hypotenuse of a right triangle with legs \(w\) and \(2w\), so \[x^2=w^2+(2w)^2=5w^2.\]

Step 3: Solve for w squared

Thus \(w^2=\frac{x^2}{5}\).

Step 4: Compute the area

The rectangle area is \(w(2w)=2w^2=2\cdot\frac{x^2}{5}=\frac25x^2.\]

Step 5: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{\frac25x^2}$.

Key Idea

Use the Pythagorean Theorem with side ratio \(1:2\).
