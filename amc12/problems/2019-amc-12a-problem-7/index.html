2019 AMC 12A Problem 7

2019AMC 12AProblem 7概率与统计平均数与数据集平均数数据

Problem Statement

Melanie computes the mean \(\mu\), the median \(M\), and the modes of the \(365\) values that are the dates in the months of \(2019\). Thus her data consist of twelve \(1\)'s, twelve \(2\)'s, \(\ldots\), twelve \(28\)'s, eleven \(29\)'s, eleven \(30\)'s, and seven \(31\)'s. Let \(d\) be the median of the modes. Which statement is true?

Choices

  1. A$\mu<d<M$
  2. B$M<d<\mu$
  3. C$d=M=\mu$
  4. D$d<M<\mu$
  5. E$d<\mu<M$

Answer

E. 14.5 < mean < median

Solution

Step 1: Find the modes

The most frequent dates are \(1,2,\ldots,28\), each appearing \(12\) times. Thus the modes are the numbers \(1\) through \(28\).

Step 2: Find d

The median of the modes \(1,2,\ldots,28\) is the average of the \(14\)th and \(15\)th values: \[d=\frac{14+15}{2}=14.5.\]

Step 3: Find the median M

There are \(365\) data values, so the median is the \(183\)rd value. The dates \(1\) through \(15\) account for \(15\cdot12=180\) values, so the \(183\)rd value is \(16\). Thus \(M=16\).

Step 4: Estimate the mean

The mean date is a little less than \(16\). More exactly, the total of all dates in 2019 is \(5738\), so \[\mu=\frac{5738}{365}\approx15.72.\]

Step 5: Compare

Therefore \[d=14.5<\mu\approx15.72<M=16.\]

Step 6: Conclude

The true statement is \(\boxed{d<\mu<M}\).

Key Idea

Compute the median of the modal dates, then compare with the mean and median.
