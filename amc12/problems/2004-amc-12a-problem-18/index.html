2004 AMC 12A Problem 18

2004AMC 12AProblem 18几何圆与曲线圆曲线

Problem Statement

Square \(ABCD\) has side length \(2\). A semicircle with diameter \(\overline{AB}\) is constructed inside the square, and the tangent to the semicircle from \(C\) intersects side \(\overline{AD}\) at \(E\). What is the length of \(\overline{CE}\)?

Choices

  1. A\(\frac{2+\sqrt5}{2}\)
  2. B\(\sqrt5\)
  3. C\(\sqrt6\)
  4. D\(\frac52\)
  5. E\(5-\sqrt5\)

Answer

D. \frac52

Solution

Step 1: Choose coordinates

Place \(A=(0,0)\), \(B=(2,0)\), \(D=(0,2)\), and \(C=(2,2)\). The semicircle has center \((1,0)\) and radius \(1\).

Step 2: Represent the tangent line

Let \(E=(0,e)\) on side \(AD\). The line through \(E\) and \(C\) has equation \[(2-e)x-2y+2e=0.\]

Step 3: Use the tangent condition

A tangent line is exactly one radius away from the center of the circle. Therefore the distance from \((1,0)\) to the line is \(1\): \[\frac{|2+e|}{\sqrt{(2-e)^2+4}}=1.\]

Step 4: Solve for E

Squaring gives \((e+2)^2=(2-e)^2+4\), so \(8e=4\) and \(e=\frac12\).

Step 5: Find CE

Now \(C=(2,2)\) and \(E=(0,\frac12)\), so \[CE=\sqrt{2^2+\left(2-\frac12\right)^2}=\sqrt{4+\frac94}=\frac52.\]

Step 6: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{\frac52}$.

Key Idea

Use a coordinate model and the tangent distance from a point to a circle.
