2022 AMC 12A Problem 15

2022AMC 12AProblem 15代数三角恒等式三角函数恒等式

Problem Statement

The roots of the polynomial $10x^3-39x^2+29x-6$ are the height, length, and width of a rectangular box. A new rectangular box is formed by lengthening each edge of the original box by $2$ units. What is the volume of the new box?

Choices

  1. A$\frac{24}{5}$
  2. B$\frac{42}{5}$
  3. C$\frac{81}{5}$
  4. D30
  5. E48

Answer

D. 30

Solution

Step 1: Ask for the shifted product

If the original box dimensions are the roots $r_1,r_2,r_3$, then the new volume is \[(r_1+2)(r_2+2)(r_3+2).\] We do not need to find the roots individually.

Step 2: Use Vieta's formulas

For \[10x^3-39x^2+29x-6,\] the roots satisfy \[r_1+r_2+r_3=\frac{39}{10},\] \[r_1r_2+r_1r_3+r_2r_3=\frac{29}{10},\] and \[r_1r_2r_3=\frac{6}{10}=\frac35.\]

Step 3: Expand the new volume

We have \[(r_1+2)(r_2+2)(r_3+2)=r_1r_2r_3+2(r_1r_2+r_1r_3+r_2r_3)+4(r_1+r_2+r_3)+8.\]

Step 4: Substitute the symmetric sums

This equals \[\frac35+2\cdot\frac{29}{10}+4\cdot\frac{39}{10}+8.\]

Step 5: Compute

The value is \[\frac35+\frac{29}{5}+\frac{78}{5}+8=\frac{110}{5}+8=22+8=30.\]

Step 6: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{30}$.

Key Idea

Use Vieta's formulas to compute the shifted product.
