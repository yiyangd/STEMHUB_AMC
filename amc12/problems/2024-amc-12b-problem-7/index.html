2024 AMC 12B Problem 7

2024AMC 12BProblem 7几何面积与相似矩形面积直角坐标设元

Problem Statement

In rectangle $WXYZ$ with $WX=4$ and $WZ=8$, point $M$ lies on $XY$, point $A$ lies on $YZ$, $\angle WMA$ is a right angle, and the areas of $\triangle WXM$ and $\triangle WAZ$ are equal. What is the area of $\triangle WMA$?

Choices

  1. A13
  2. B14
  3. C15
  4. D16
  5. E17

Answer

C. 15

Solution

Step 1: Set up coordinates from the rectangle

Place the rectangle so that

\[W=(0,0),\quad X=(4,0),\quad Y=(4,8),\quad Z=(0,8).\]

Let $M=(4,m)$ on side $XY$ and $A=(a,8)$ on side $YZ$. This converts the geometry into algebra.

Step 2: Translate the equal-area condition

The area of $\triangle WXM$ is

\[\frac12\cdot4\cdot m=2m.\]

The area of $\triangle WAZ$ is

\[\frac12\cdot8\cdot a=4a.\]

Since the areas are equal, $2m=4a$, so $a=\frac m2$.

Step 3: Use the right angle condition

The angle $WMA$ is right, so vectors $\overrightarrow{MW}$ and $\overrightarrow{MA}$ are perpendicular. Their dot product is $0$:

\[(-4,-m)\cdot(a-4,8-m)=0.\]

This gives

\[-4a+16-8m+m^2=0.\]

Step 4: Solve for the position

Substitute $a=\frac m2$:

\[m^2-10m+16=0.\]

So $(m-2)(m-8)=0$. The nondegenerate point gives $m=2$, and then $a=1$.

Step 5: Compute the requested area

The area of $\triangle WMA$ is

\[\frac12|4\cdot8-m a|=\frac12(32-2)=15.\]

Therefore the answer is $15$.

Key Idea

Place the rectangle on coordinates, translate the equal-area and right-angle conditions, then compute the area.
