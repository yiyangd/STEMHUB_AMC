2024AMC 12AProblem 13代数函数与图像指数函数对称轴点的反射

Problem Statement

The graph of $y=e^{x+1}+e^{-x}-2$ has an axis of symmetry. What is the reflection of the point $(-1,\frac12)$ over this axis?

Choices

  1. A$(-1,-\frac32)$
  2. B$(-1,0)$
  3. C$(-1,\frac12)$
  4. D$(0,\frac12)$
  5. E$(3,\frac12)$

Answer

D. $(0,\frac12)$

Solution

Step 1: Look for symmetry in the two exponential terms

The expression is

\[y=e^{x+1}+e^{-x}-2.\]

The exponents $x+1$ and $-x$ become equal when $x+1=-x$, so the midpoint occurs at $x=-\frac12$. This strongly suggests a vertical axis of symmetry.

Step 2: Rewrite around the midpoint

Let $u=x+\frac12$. Then $x+1=u+\frac12$ and $-x=-u+\frac12$. The expression becomes

\[y=e^{u+1/2}+e^{-u+1/2}-2.\]

The two terms are symmetric when $u$ is replaced by $-u$, so the graph is symmetric about $u=0$, or $x=-\frac12$.

Step 3: Reflect the point across a vertical line

Reflecting across the vertical line $x=-\frac12$ keeps the $y$-coordinate unchanged. The point $(-1,\frac12)$ is $\frac12$ unit to the left of the line.

Its reflection must be $\frac12$ unit to the right of the line.

Step 4: Compute the reflected coordinate

Moving $\frac12$ unit to the right of $-\frac12$ gives $0$. The $y$-coordinate remains $\frac12$.

So the reflected point is

\[(0,\tfrac12).\]

Key Idea

Rewrite the exponential terms around their midpoint to identify the vertical axis of symmetry.
