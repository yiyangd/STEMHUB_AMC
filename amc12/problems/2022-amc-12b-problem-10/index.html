2022 AMC 12B Problem 10

2022AMC 12BProblem 10几何面积与相似面积比例

Problem Statement

Regular hexagon $ABCDEF$ has side length $2$. Let $G$ be the midpoint of $\overline{AB}$, and let $H$ be the midpoint of $\overline{DE}$. What is the perimeter of $GCHF$?

Choices

  1. A$4\sqrt3$
  2. B8
  3. C$4\sqrt5$
  4. D$4\sqrt7$
  5. E12

Answer

D. $4\sqrt7$

Solution

Step 1: Place the hexagon on coordinates

Use a regular hexagon centered at the origin with side length $2$: \[A=(2,0),\ B=(1,\sqrt3),\ C=(-1,\sqrt3),\ D=(-2,0),\ E=(-1,-\sqrt3),\ F=(1,-\sqrt3).\]

Step 2: Find the midpoints

The midpoint of $\overline{AB}$ is \[G=\left(\frac32,\frac{\sqrt3}{2}\right),\] and the midpoint of $\overline{DE}$ is \[H=\left(-\frac32,-\frac{\sqrt3}{2}\right).\]

Step 3: Compute one side length

For example, \[GC^2=\left(\frac32-(-1)\right)^2+\left(\frac{\sqrt3}{2}-\sqrt3\right)^2=\left(\frac52\right)^2+\left(-\frac{\sqrt3}{2}\right)^2=7.\] So $GC=\sqrt7$.

Step 4: Use symmetry for the other sides

The same calculation, or symmetry of the regular hexagon, gives \[CH=HF=FG=\sqrt7.\]

Step 5: Compute the perimeter

Therefore the perimeter of $GCHF$ is \[4\sqrt7.\]

Step 6: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{4\sqrt7}$.

Key Idea

Place the regular hexagon on coordinates and compute the four equal side lengths.
