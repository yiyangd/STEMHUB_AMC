2004 AMC 12A Problem 23

2004AMC 12AProblem 23代数多项式与根多项式根

Problem Statement

A polynomial \[P(x)=c_{2004}x^{2004}+c_{2003}x^{2003}+\cdots+c_1x+c_0\] has real coefficients with \(c_{2004}\ne0\) and \(2004\) distinct complex zeroes \(z_k=a_k+b_ki\), \(1\le k\le2004\), with \(a_k,b_k\) real, \(a_1=b_1=0\), and \[\sum_{k=1}^{2004}a_k=\sum_{k=1}^{2004}b_k.\] Which of the following quantities can be a nonzero number?

Choices

  1. A\(c_0\)
  2. B\(c_{2003}\)
  3. C\(b_2b_3\cdots b_{2004}\)
  4. D\(\sum_{k=1}^{2004}a_k\)
  5. E\(\sum_{k=1}^{2004}c_k\)

Answer

E. \sum_{k=1}^{2004}c_k

Solution

Step 1: Use the zero root

The condition \(a_1=b_1=0\) means \(z_1=0\) is a root. Therefore the constant term \(c_0\), which is related to the product of all roots, must be \(0\).

Step 2: Use real coefficients

Because the polynomial has real coefficients, nonreal roots occur in conjugate pairs. Therefore the sum of all imaginary parts is \(0\): \[\sum_{k=1}^{2004}b_k=0.\]

Step 3: Use the given equality

The problem says \(\sum a_k=\sum b_k\), so \(\sum a_k=0\). Hence the sum of all roots is also \(0\). By Vieta's formula, \(c_{2003}=0\).

Step 4: Eliminate the product of imaginary parts

After the zero root, there are \(2003\) roots left. Nonreal roots come in pairs, so at least one of these remaining roots must be real. For a real root, its imaginary part \(b_k\) is \(0\), so \(b_2b_3\cdots b_{2004}=0\).

Step 5: Identify what can remain nonzero

The sum \(\sum_{k=1}^{2004}c_k\) is \(P(1)-c_0\), and the previous constraints do not force \(P(1)\) to be \(0\). It can be nonzero for a suitable polynomial satisfying the root conditions.

Step 6: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{\sum_{k=1}^{2004}c_k}$.

Key Idea

Use conjugate roots and Vieta's formulas to force four listed quantities to be zero.
