2015 AMC 12A Problem 16

2015AMC 12AProblem 16几何空间几何空间几何体积

Problem Statement

Tetrahedron $ABCD$ has $AB=5$, $AC=3$, $BC=4$, $BD=4$, $AD=3$, and $CD=\frac{12\sqrt2}{5}$. What is the volume of the tetrahedron?

Choices

  1. A$3\sqrt2$
  2. B$2\sqrt5$
  3. C$\frac{24}{5}$
  4. D$3\sqrt3$
  5. E$\frac{24\sqrt2}{5}$

Answer

C. \frac{24}{5}

Solution

Step 1: Place the base triangle

Since $AC=3$, $BC=4$, and $AB=5$, triangle $ABC$ is right. Put \[C=(0,0,0),\quad A=(3,0,0),\quad B=(0,4,0).\]

Step 2: Let D have coordinates

Let \[D=(x,y,z).\] The distances give \[x^2+y^2+z^2=\left(\frac{12\sqrt2}{5}\right)^2=\frac{288}{25}.\]

Step 3: Use distances to A and B

From $AD=3$, subtract the $CD$ equation from \[(x-3)^2+y^2+z^2=9\] to get \[x=\frac{48}{25}.\] From $BD=4$, similarly get \[y=\frac{36}{25}.\]

Step 4: Find the height

Then \[z^2=\frac{288}{25}-\left(\frac{48}{25}\right)^2-\left(\frac{36}{25}\right)^2=\frac{144}{25},\] so the height from $D$ to the base plane is \[\frac{12}{5}.\]

Step 5: Compute volume

The area of right triangle $ABC$ is \[\frac12\cdot3\cdot4=6.\] Thus the tetrahedron volume is \[\frac13\cdot6\cdot\frac{12}{5}=\frac{24}{5}.\]

Step 6: Conclude

The answer is \[\boxed{\frac{24}{5}}.\]

Key Idea

Place the right-triangle base in coordinates and compute the height of D.
