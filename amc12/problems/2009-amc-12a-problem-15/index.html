2009 AMC 12A Problem 15

2009AMC 12AProblem 15代数运算与化简代数化简

Problem Statement

For what value of \(n\) is \[i+2i^2+3i^3+\cdots+ni^n=48+49i?\] Here \(i=\sqrt{-1}\).

Choices

  1. A24
  2. B48
  3. C49
  4. D97
  5. E98

Answer

D. 97

Solution

Step 1: Group terms in blocks of four

The powers of \(i\) repeat every four terms. For \(k\) a multiple of \(4\), the block \[(k+1)i^{k+1}+(k+2)i^{k+2}+(k+3)i^{k+3}+(k+4)i^{k+4}\] equals \[(k+1)i-(k+2)-(k+3)i+(k+4)=2-2i.\]

Step 2: Use 24 full blocks

Through \(n=96\), there are \(24\) full blocks, so the sum is \[24(2-2i)=48-48i.\]

Step 3: Add the next term

The next term is \(97i^{97}=97i\), because \(97\equiv1\pmod4\).

Step 4: Reach the target

\[48-48i+97i=48+49i.\] Thus \(n=97\).

Step 5: Conclude

The answer is \(\boxed{97}\).

Key Idea

Group powers of \(i\) in blocks of four.
