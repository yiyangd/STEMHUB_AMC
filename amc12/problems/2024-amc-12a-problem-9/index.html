2024 AMC 12A Problem 9

2024AMC 12AProblem 9数论平方数平方差最大整数个位数

Problem Statement

Let $M$ be the greatest integer such that both $M+1213$ and $M+3773$ are perfect squares. What is the units digit of $M$?

Choices

  1. A1
  2. B2
  3. C3
  4. D6
  5. E8

Answer

E. 8

Solution

Step 1: Set up the two squares

Let \[M+1213=P^2,\quad M+3773=Q^2.\] Subtracting gives \[Q^2-P^2=2560.\]

Step 2: Factor the difference

We get \[(Q-P)(Q+P)=2560.\] To make $M$ as large as possible, we want $P$ and $Q$ as large as possible, so choose the smallest positive value for $Q-P$ with the same parity as $Q+P$.

Step 3: Choose the factors

Both factors must be even, so take \[Q-P=2,\quad Q+P=1280.\] This gives \[P=639,\quad Q=641.\]

Step 4: Find the units digit

Then \[M=P^2-1213.\] Since $639^2$ has units digit $1$ and $1213$ has units digit $3$, the units digit of $M$ is \[1-3\equiv8\pmod{10}.\]

Step 5: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{8}$.

Key Idea

Turn the two square conditions into a difference of squares.
