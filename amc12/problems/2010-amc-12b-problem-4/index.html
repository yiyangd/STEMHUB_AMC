Problem Statement

A month with $31$ days has the same number of Mondays and Wednesdays. How many of the seven days of the week could be the first day of this month?

Choices

  1. A2
  2. B3
  3. C4
  4. D5
  5. E6

Answer

B. 3

Solution

Step 1: Use the structure of a 31-day month

A $31$-day month has $4$ complete weeks plus $3$ extra days. Every weekday occurs at least $4$ times, and the first three weekdays of the month occur one extra time.

Step 2: Focus on Monday and Wednesday

The month has the same number of Mondays and Wednesdays exactly when Monday and Wednesday are either both among the three extra days or neither is among the three extra days.

Step 3: List starts where both are extra

If the month starts on Monday, the three extra days are Monday, Tuesday, and Wednesday. This works.

Step 4: List starts where neither is extra

If the month starts on Thursday, the extra days are Thursday, Friday, and Saturday. If it starts on Friday, the extra days are Friday, Saturday, and Sunday. Both work.

Step 5: Count the possibilities

The possible starting weekdays are Monday, Thursday, and Friday, for a total of $3$.

Step 6: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{3}$.

Key Idea

Use the three extra weekdays in a 31-day month.
