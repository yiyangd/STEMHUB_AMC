2024 AMC 12B Problem 9

2024AMC 12BProblem 9概率与统计几何概率几何概率面积圆环菱形

Problem Statement

A dartboard is the region $B$ in the coordinate plane consisting of points $(x,y)$ such that $|x|+|y|\le8$. A target $T$ is the region where $(x^2+y^2-25)^2\le49$. A dart is thrown uniformly at random in $B$. The probability that the dart lands in $T$ can be expressed as $\frac{m\pi}{n}$, where $m$ and $n$ are relatively prime positive integers. What is $m+n$?

Choices

  1. A39
  2. B71
  3. C73
  4. D75
  5. E135

Answer

B. 71

Solution

Step 1: Understand the two regions

The dartboard is $|x|+|y|\le8$, a diamond. Its diagonals have lengths $16$ and $16$, so its area is

\[\frac12\cdot16\cdot16=128.\]

Step 2: Simplify the target condition

The target satisfies

\[(x^2+y^2-25)^2\le49.\]

Taking square roots gives

\[-7\le x^2+y^2-25\le7,\]

so

\[18\le x^2+y^2\le32.\]

Step 3: Interpret the target geometrically

This is an annulus centered at the origin, with inner radius $\sqrt{18}$ and outer radius $\sqrt{32}$. The diamond has its smallest distance to the origin at the midpoints of its sides, also $\sqrt{32}$.

Therefore the entire annulus lies inside the dartboard.

Step 4: Compute the target area

The area of the annulus is

\[\pi(32)-\pi(18)=14\pi.\]

Since the dartboard area is $128$, the probability is

\[\frac{14\pi}{128}=\frac{7\pi}{64}.\]

Step 5: Answer the requested sum

Thus $m=7$ and $n=64$, so

\[m+n=71.\]

The answer is $71$.

Key Idea

Recognize the target as a circular annulus inside the diamond-shaped dartboard.
