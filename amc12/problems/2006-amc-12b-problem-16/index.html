2006 AMC 12B Problem 16

2006AMC 12BProblem 16几何面积与相似面积比例

Problem Statement

Regular hexagon \(ABCDEF\) has vertices \(A\) and \(C\) at \((0,0)\) and \((7,1)\), respectively. What is its area?

Choices

  1. A\(20\sqrt3\)
  2. B\(22\sqrt3\)
  3. C\(25\sqrt3\)
  4. D\(27\sqrt3\)
  5. E50

Answer

C. 25\sqrt3

Solution

Step 1: Recognize which diagonal is given

In a regular hexagon \(ABCDEF\), vertices \(A\) and \(C\) are separated by one vertex. The segment \(AC\) is not a side; it is a short diagonal.

Step 2: Compute AC

From the coordinates, \[AC=\sqrt{(7-0)^2+(1-0)^2}=\sqrt{50}.\]

Step 3: Relate AC to the side length

In a regular hexagon with side length \(s\), the diagonal from \(A\) to \(C\) has length \(s\sqrt3\). Thus \[s\sqrt3=\sqrt{50},\] so \[s^2=\frac{50}{3}.\]

Step 4: Use the area formula

A regular hexagon is made of \(6\) equilateral triangles, so its area is \[\frac{3\sqrt3}{2}s^2.\]

Step 5: Substitute

\[\frac{3\sqrt3}{2}\cdot\frac{50}{3}=25\sqrt3.\]

Step 6: Conclude

The area is \(\boxed{25\sqrt3}\).

Key Idea

Relate a two-vertex diagonal of a regular hexagon to the side length.
