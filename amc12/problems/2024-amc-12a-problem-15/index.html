2024AMC 12AProblem 15代数多项式与根韦达定理多项式复数代入

Problem Statement

The roots of $x^3+2x^2-x+3=0$ are $p$, $q$, and $r$. What is $(p^2+4)(q^2+4)(r^2+4)$?

Choices

  1. A64
  2. B75
  3. C100
  4. D125
  5. E144

Answer

D. 125

Solution

Step 1: Recognize the product over roots

Let

\[f(x)=x^3+2x^2-x+3.\]

Since $p,q,r$ are the roots, we want

\[(p^2+4)(q^2+4)(r^2+4).\]

The factor $p^2+4$ can be written as $(p-2i)(p+2i)$, which suggests evaluating the polynomial at complex numbers.

Step 2: Connect polynomial values to root products

For a monic cubic, we have

\[f(x)=(x-p)(x-q)(x-r).\]

Therefore

\[f(2i)=(2i-p)(2i-q)(2i-r),\]

and similarly for $f(-2i)$. Multiplying $f(2i)$ and $f(-2i)$ will produce exactly the desired factors.

Step 3: Evaluate the polynomial at $2i$

Compute using $i^2=-1$ and $i^3=-i$:

\[f(2i)=(2i)^3+2(2i)^2-2i+3.\]

This becomes

\[-8i-8-2i+3=-5-10i.\]

Step 4: Use the conjugate value

Because the polynomial has real coefficients,

\[f(-2i)=-5+10i.\]

Now multiply the conjugates:

\[f(2i)f(-2i)=(-5-10i)(-5+10i)=25+100=125.\]

Step 5: Conclude the desired product

The product $f(2i)f(-2i)$ equals $(p^2+4)(q^2+4)(r^2+4)$. Hence the answer is $125$.

Key Idea

Evaluate the cubic at complex numbers to compute a symmetric product over its roots.
