2002 AMC 12A Problem 15

2002AMC 12AProblem 15概率与统计平均数与数据集平均数数据

Problem Statement

The mean, median, unique mode, and range of a collection of eight integers are all equal to $8$. What is the largest integer that can be an element of this collection?

Choices

  1. A11
  2. B12
  3. C13
  4. D14
  5. E15

Answer

D. 14

Solution

Step 1: Sort the eight integers

Let the sorted integers be \[x_1\le x_2\le\cdots\le x_8.\] The mean is $8$, so their sum is \(64\). The median is $8$, so \[x_4+x_5=16.\]

Step 2: Test whether 15 can be the largest

If the largest element were $15$, then the range being $8$ would force the smallest element to be $7$.

Step 3: Find the contradiction

With \(x_1=7\), \(x_8=15\), and \(x_4+x_5=16\), the remaining four middle terms would need sum \[64-7-15-16=26.\] But \(x_2,x_3\ge7\) and \(x_6,x_7\ge8\), so their sum is at least \(7+7+8+8=30\), impossible.

Step 4: Show 14 is possible

The collection \[6,6,6,8,8,8,8,14\] has sum \(64\), median \(8\), unique mode \(8\), and range \(14-6=8\).

Step 5: Conclude

The largest possible element is $\boxed{14}$.

Key Idea

Use sorted order, median, sum, range, and mode constraints.
