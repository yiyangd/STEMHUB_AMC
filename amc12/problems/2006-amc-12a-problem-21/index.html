2006 AMC 12A Problem 21

2006AMC 12AProblem 21几何面积与相似面积比例

Problem Statement

Let \[S_1=\{(x,y)\mid \log_{10}(1+x^2+y^2)\le 1+\log_{10}(x+y)\}\] and \[S_2=\{(x,y)\mid \log_{10}(2+x^2+y^2)\le 2+\log_{10}(x+y)\}.\] What is the ratio of the area of \(S_2\) to the area of \(S_1\)?

Choices

  1. A98
  2. B99
  3. C100
  4. D101
  5. E102

Answer

E. 102

Solution

Step 1: Remove the logarithms carefully

Because \(\log_{10}\) is increasing, each inequality can be converted into an ordinary inequality after matching powers of \(10\).

Step 2: Analyze S1

For \(S_1\), \[\log_{10}(1+x^2+y^2)\le \log_{10}(10(x+y)),\] so \[1+x^2+y^2\le10x+10y.\] Completing the square gives \[(x-5)^2+(y-5)^2\le49.\] Thus \(S_1\) is a disk of radius \(7\).

Step 3: Analyze S2

For \(S_2\), \[\log_{10}(2+x^2+y^2)\le \log_{10}(100(x+y)),\] so \[2+x^2+y^2\le100x+100y.\] Completing the square gives \[(x-50)^2+(y-50)^2\le4998.\]

Step 4: Compare areas

The area of a disk is proportional to the square of its radius. Therefore \[\frac{[S_2]}{[S_1]}=\frac{4998}{49}=102.\]

Step 5: Conclude

The ratio is \(\boxed{102}\).

Key Idea

Convert logarithmic inequalities into disk inequalities and compare areas.
