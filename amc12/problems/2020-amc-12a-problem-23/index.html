2020 AMC 12A Problem 23

2020AMC 12AProblem 23概率与统计组合概率概率计数

Problem Statement

Jason rolls three fair standard six-sided dice. Then he looks at the rolls and chooses a subset of the dice, possibly empty and possibly all three dice, to reroll. After rerolling, he wins if and only if the sum of the numbers face up on the three dice is exactly $7$. Jason always plays to optimize his chances of winning. What is the probability that he chooses to reroll exactly two of the dice?

Choices

  1. A$\frac7{36}$
  2. B$\frac5{24}$
  3. C$\frac29$
  4. D$\frac{17}{72}$
  5. E$\frac14$

Answer

A. \frac7{36}

Solution

Step 1: Compare possible reroll sizes

If Jason keeps two dice and rerolls one die, the best possible success probability is $\frac16$, because one die must hit one exact value. If he rerolls all three dice, the probability of sum $7$ is $\frac{15}{216}$.

Step 2: Analyze rerolling exactly two dice

If Jason keeps a die showing $m$, then the two rerolled dice must sum to $7-m$. The number of successful ordered pairs is \[5,4,3,2,1,0\] for $m=1,2,3,4,5,6$, respectively.

Step 3: When can rerolling two be best?

Rerolling two dice beats rerolling all three only when the kept die is $1$, $2$, or $3$. It also must beat rerolling one die, so no pair of the original dice may have sum at most $6$; otherwise keeping that pair gives probability $\frac16$.

Step 4: Count the rolls

Thus the smallest die must be $1$, $2$, or $3$, and the sum of the two smallest dice must be greater than $6$. Counting ordered rolls gives \[3+15+24=42\] favorable initial rolls.

Step 5: Convert to probability

There are $6^3=216$ equally likely initial rolls, so the probability is \[\frac{42}{216}=\frac7{36}.\]

Step 6: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{\frac7{36}}$.

Key Idea

Compare the best winning probabilities for rerolling zero, one, two, or three dice.
