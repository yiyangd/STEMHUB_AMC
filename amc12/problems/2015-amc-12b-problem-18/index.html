2015 AMC 12B Problem 18

2015AMC 12BProblem 18数论质因数与整除质因数整除

Problem Statement

For every composite positive integer $n$, define $r(n)$ to be the sum of the factors in the prime factorization of $n$. For example, $r(50)=12$ because the prime factorization of $50$ is $2\cdot5^2$, and $2+5+5=12$. What is the range of the function $r$, where $n$ runs over composite positive integers?

Choices

  1. Athe set of positive integers
  2. Bthe set of composite positive integers
  3. Cthe set of even positive integers
  4. Dthe set of integers greater than 3
  5. Ethe set of integers greater than 4

Answer

D. the set of integers greater than 3

Solution

Step 1: Find the smallest possible value

A composite number has at least two prime factors counted with multiplicity. The smallest possible sum is \[2+2=4,\] from $n=4$.

Step 2: Show every integer at least 4 is possible

Every integer $m\ge4$ can be written as a sum of $2$'s and $3$'s with at least two terms: \[4=2+2,\quad5=2+3,\] and larger values follow by adding more $2$'s.

Step 3: Convert sums to composite numbers

If \[m=p_1+p_2+\cdots+p_k\] is such a sum of primes with $k\ge2$, take \[n=p_1p_2\cdots p_k.\] Then $n$ is composite and \[r(n)=m.\]

Step 4: Exclude small values

No composite number can have $r(n)=1,2,$ or $3$, because at least two prime factors are needed.

Step 5: Conclude

The range is exactly the set of integers greater than $3$, so the answer is $\boxed{\text{D}}$.

Key Idea

Represent every integer at least 4 as a sum of at least two primes, using 2s and 3s.
