2010 AMC 12A Problem 23

2010AMC 12AProblem 23数论进制与数字数字进制

Problem Statement

The number obtained from the last two nonzero digits of $90!$ is equal to $n$. What is $n$?

Choices

  1. A12
  2. B32
  3. C48
  4. D52
  5. E68

Answer

A. 12

Solution

Step 1: Separate trailing zeros from the meaningful digits

Trailing zeros come from pairs of factors $2\cdot5$. In $90!$, there are \[\left\lfloor\frac{90}{5}\right\rfloor+\left\lfloor\frac{90}{25}\right\rfloor=18+3=21\] factors of $5$, and plenty of factors of $2$. Remove $21$ pairs of $2$ and $5$.

Step 2: Work modulo 25 first

After removing the factors of $5$, the part of $90!$ relatively prime to $5$ is congruent to $1$ modulo $25$. The remaining factors that came from multiples of $5$, after dividing by $5^{21}$, are congruent to $-1$ modulo $25$.

Step 3: Account for removed powers of 2

Removing the $21$ pairs also removed $2^{21}$. Since \[2^{21}\equiv2\pmod{25},\] its inverse modulo $25$ is $13$. Therefore the last two nonzero digits $N$ satisfy \[N\equiv 13\cdot1\cdot(-1)\equiv12\pmod{25}.\]

Step 4: Use divisibility by 4

After all factors of $5$ are removed, many factors of $2$ remain, so the last two nonzero digits must be divisible by $4$.

Step 5: Combine the conditions

The two-digit numbers congruent to $12$ modulo $25$ are \[12,37,62,87.\] Of these, only $12$ is divisible by $4$.

Step 6: Conclude

The last two nonzero digits are $\boxed{12}$.

Key Idea

Remove factors causing trailing zeros and work modulo 25 and modulo 4.
