2016 AMC 12B Problem 6

2016AMC 12BProblem 6几何坐标几何坐标几何

Problem Statement

All three vertices of $\triangle ABC$ lie on the parabola defined by $y=x^2$, with $A$ at the origin and $\overline{BC}$ parallel to the $x$-axis. The area of the triangle is $64$. What is the length of $BC$?

Choices

  1. A4
  2. B6
  3. C8
  4. D10
  5. E16

Answer

C. 8

Solution

Step 1: Use the horizontal chord

Because \(BC\) is parallel to the $x$-axis, points $B$ and $C$ have the same $y$-coordinate. On \(y=x^2\), a horizontal line \(y=h\) meets the parabola at \(x=\pm\sqrt h\).

Step 2: Write the base and height

Thus \[BC=2\sqrt h.\] The height from $A=(0,0)$ to the horizontal line \(y=h\) is \(h\).

Step 3: Use the area

The triangle area is \[\frac12(2\sqrt h)(h)=h^{3/2}.\] We are told this equals $64$.

Step 4: Solve for h

So \[h^{3/2}=64=8^2,\] which gives \(\sqrt h=4\) and \(h=16\).

Step 5: Find BC

Therefore \[BC=2\sqrt{16}=8.\]

Step 6: Conclude

The answer is $\boxed{8}$.

Key Idea

Use symmetry of a horizontal chord on the parabola y equals x squared.
