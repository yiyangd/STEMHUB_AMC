2021 Spring AMC 12A Problem 19

2021 SpringAMC 12AProblem 19代数三角恒等式三角函数恒等式

Problem Statement

How many solutions does the equation \[\sin\left(\frac{\pi}{2}\cos x\right)=\cos\left(\frac{\pi}{2}\sin x\right)\] have in the closed interval $[0,\pi]$?

Choices

  1. A0
  2. B1
  3. C2
  4. D3
  5. E4

Answer

C. 2

Solution

Step 1: Convert the right side to a sine

Use $\cos u=\sin(\frac\pi2-u)$. The equation becomes

\[\sin\left(\frac\pi2\cos x\right)=\sin\left(\frac\pi2-\frac\pi2\sin x\right).\]

This puts both sides into the same trig function.

Step 2: Use the sine equality cases

If $\sin A=\sin B$, then either

\[A=B+2k\pi\]

or

\[A=\pi-B+2k\pi\]

for some integer $k$. Here the angles involved are small enough that only the basic cases can occur on $0\le x\le\pi$.

Step 3: Solve the first case

The case $A=B$ gives

\[\frac\pi2\cos x=\frac\pi2-\frac\pi2\sin x,\]

so

\[\sin x+\cos x=1.\]

On $[0,\pi]$, this has solutions $x=0$ and $x=\frac\pi2$.

Step 4: Solve the second case

The case $A=\pi-B$ gives

\[\frac\pi2\cos x=\frac\pi2+ rac\pi2\sin x,\]

so

\[\cos x-\sin x=1.\]

On $[0,\pi]$, this gives only $x=0$, which has already been counted.

Step 5: Count distinct solutions

The distinct solutions are

\[x=0\quad\text{and}\quad x=\frac\pi2.\]

Therefore the equation has $2$ solutions in the interval.

Key Idea

Rewrite the cosine as a sine and solve the resulting sine equality in the restricted interval.
