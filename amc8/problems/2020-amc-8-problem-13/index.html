Problem

Jamal has a drawer containing 6 green socks, 18 purple socks, and 12 orange socks. After adding more purple socks, Jamal noticed that there is now a 60% chance that a sock randomly selected from the drawer is purple. How many purple socks did Jamal add?

Choices

  1. A$6$
  2. B$9$
  3. C$12$
  4. D$18$
  5. E$24$

Step-by-step Solution

Calculate the total number of socks

There were originally 36 socks in the drawer, with 18 of them being purple. The numbers and variables involved are: ['6', '18', '12', '36', '18'].

Let x be the number of added purple socks

Let's assume that we add x pairs of purple socks. Then the number of purple socks will be 18 + x, and the total number of socks will be 36 + x.

Establish probability equations

The probability of wearing new purple socks is 60% = 3/5. Therefore, (18 + x) / (36 + x) = 3/5.

Solve and backtrack

The product of the cross-products is 90 + 5x = 108 + 3x. Solving this equation gives x = 9. Thus, 27/45 = 60%.

计算原袜子总数

抽屉原有 6+18+12=36 只袜子，其中紫色有 18 只。

设新增紫袜数

设加入 x 只紫色袜子，则紫袜数为 18+x，总数为 36+x。

建立概率方程

新的紫袜概率为 60%=3/5，所以 (18+x)/(36+x)=3/5。

求解并回代

交叉相乘得 90+5x=108+3x，解得 x=9；此时 27/45=60%。

Key Idea

Introduce variables, translate the problem conditions into equations, and filter out solutions that meet the criteria.
设未知数，把题目条件翻译成方程并筛选符合条件的解。
